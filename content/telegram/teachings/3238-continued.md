In these teachings, brethren, we find the blueprint for a marriage that not only survives the trials of this temporal world but thrives in the eternal love of God. May your marriages be a reflection of this divine love, a beacon of Christ’s light in the world, leading each soul closer to the ultimate union with our Creator.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
