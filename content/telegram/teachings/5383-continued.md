2. History:

It recounts events that occurred at various times in the Old and New Testaments, and it also contains prayers and mysteries along with the story. Among those who recount history, the following can be seen as examples.

2.1. As for the story of Michal:

when King David, the prophet of Israel, brought the Ark of the Covenant, he sang and pray before it with all his might. Michal(Milkol), the daughter of Saul, saw him rejoicing before the Ark of the Lord and despised him in her heart. Because of this, she was punished and remained childless for the rest of her life (2 Samuel 6:12–23).

✍🏾The author, after recalling this story, says, “I will sing to you, O Virgin, as David sang before the Ark of Zion, which is a symbol of our Lady. So I will sing to you before your image. May your praise be blessed by men and angels, and may those who despise your praise be accursed, just as Michal was cursed for scorning the praise offered before the Ark of Zion by David. May all who despise your praise and your Ark be cursed by men and angels.”

✍🏾 If Michal(milkol) was cursed for her contempt toward the praise of the Ark of Zion, which symbolized our Lady, how much more cursed are those today who despise and criticize the praise of the Ark of Zion, the faithful symbol of the Virgin Mary? These are the ones condemned by the words of Psalm 30:18: “Let the lips of the deceitful be silenced, which speak arrogantly and contemptuously against the righteous.”

The Call to True Fasting and Worship

Fasting, when done for human praise, is empty; when done in obedience and mercy, it becomes a ladder to heaven. The lesson concludes with a call:

“When we fast, let us fast not for vain praise, but for the sake of the law and order. Let us fast for mercy, where we can find help, blessings, and abundance through fasting.”

Through prayer, fasting, and the intercession of the Virgin, the faithful are renewed and strengthened to walk in humility before God.

Conclusion 

In Zemene Tsige, we are reminded that faith kindles miracles, that the Virgin is the living bush who bore divine fire, and that those who honor her with pure hearts will find mercy through her Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.
