(Continued) 👆

Living in such circumstances for a year is undoubtedly challenging, but God has a timeline and a miracle that we do not yet know. It is crucial to wait patiently and to have faith that if it is in His divine plan, He will bring about the resolution. Reflect on the patience of Job, who endured immense suffering but remained steadfast in his trust in God. In the end, God visited him with His help and restored him. God is good, and He knows when to give, how to mend the broken, and how to lift the fallen. Therefore, if there is something prepared for us that we do not yet understand, we must wait patiently, for Christianity calls us to persevere and endure.

May God grant you strength, patience, and the wisdom to understand His plan in your life. Trust in His love and His perfect timing.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
