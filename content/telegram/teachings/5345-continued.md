5.We Are Reconciled Through the Cross

We were once enemies of God because of sin, separated from His holiness and peace. But through the redemption of Christ, we have been reconciled to God and restored to fellowship with Him.

The apostle Paul explains this truth, saying:

“For if, when we were enemies, we were reconciled to God through the death of His Son, much more, having been reconciled, we shall be saved by His life. And not only that, but we also rejoice in God through our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom we have now received reconciliation.” (Romans 5:10–11)

Through His death on the Cross, Christ made peace between heaven and earth. St. Paul also writes,

“He was pleased to reconcile to Himself all things, whether on earth or in heaven, by making peace through the blood of His cross.” (Colossians 1:20)

And in his letter to the Ephesians, the apostle teaches:

“For He Himself is our peace, who has made both one and has broken down the middle wall of separation, having abolished in His flesh the law of commandments contained in ordinances, so as to create in Himself one new man from the two, thus making peace, and that He might reconcile them both to God in one body through the cross, thereby putting to death the enmity.” (Ephesians 2:14–16)

As he further says, “If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation” (2 Corinthians 5:17).

Therefore, the Cross is the holy instrument of reconciliation. Through it, all divisions are broken down, peace is restored, and humanity is united with God once again. The Cross is not only a symbol of suffering it is the bridge of peace that joins heaven and earth, God and mankind, through the love and mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ.

Conclusion: The Love and Mystery of the Cross

The Cross is the table of the Eucharist, the throne of peace, the sign of renewal, the bridge of reconciliation, and the weapon of divine power. In it we see both the justice and the mercy of God joined in perfect harmony.

Let us, therefore, bow not merely with our bodies but with contrite hearts. For through the Cross, death has lost its sting, the devil his power. May the Lord grant us grace to comprehend the love revealed on the Cross, to live in its peace, and to inherit the Kingdom prepared for those who believe.
