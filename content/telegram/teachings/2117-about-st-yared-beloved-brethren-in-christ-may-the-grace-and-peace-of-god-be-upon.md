---About St. #Yared 

Beloved brethren in Christ, may the grace and peace of God be upon you all. Today, we gather together to commemorate and celebrate the life and legacy of the esteemed Saint Yared of the Ethiopian Orthodox Church. Let us delve into the profound teachings and experiences of this great scholar and draw valuable lessons for our own spiritual journey.

Born in the year 505 A.D., near the ancient city of Axum, Saint Yared's upbringing was marked by an early struggle to grasp education. As a young child, he faced difficulties in understanding his studies, which left him disheartened. His mind, however, was captivated by the love for the Virgin Mary and her divine Son. One fateful day, his teacher remarked that he lacked color, insinuating his inability to comprehend the lessons. When tested, young Yared failed to respond and was consequently beaten.

The pain of the punishment deeply affected him, prompting Saint Yared to distance himself from his teacher. Exhausted, he sought refuge in the Mai Kirah area and, in his weariness, received a divine revelation from God. The Lord taught him the importance of perseverance and unwavering effort. Encouraged by this divine guidance, Saint Yared returned to his teacher, sought forgiveness, and resumed his education with renewed determination. He diligently applied himself to his studies and devoted himself to prayer, seeking communion with God.

In His infinite mercy, God blessed Saint Yared with a profound spiritual experience through the intercession of the Virgin Mary. The mysteries that had previously eluded him were unveiled, and he received divine insights and understanding. During his prayers, when heavenly hyms had not yet been revealed, three angels, in the form of birds, appeared before him and spoke to him, granting him their divine counsel.

Reinvigorated by this encounter, Saint Yared returned to the earthly realm, filled with inspiration and gratitude. He offered his thanksgiving in Axum Zion, where the people and the king marveled at the depth and beauty of his spiritual compositions. Saint Yared served diligently in Axum and Debre Damo, ascending to higher spiritual heights with each passing day. Eventually, he left his homeland, aspiring to join the company of the Virgin Mary and the heavenly host.

Throughout the remainder of his life, Saint Yared authored five significant books, leaving a profound legacy for future generations. He established centers of learning and spiritual instruction in Tana Kirkos, Zur Amba, and other locations, where he nurtured and mentored numerous disciples. Saint Yared's contributions to Ethiopian Orthodox Church music are characterized by three distinct types of songs: Geez, Ezl, and Ararai.

The Geez songs, meaning "found first," represent liberation, reminding us of the freedom we find in Christ. The Ezl songs, comprising thirty melodies sung in conjunction with Geez or Tazlo Ezl, inspire fortitude and endurance in the face of trials. Lastly, the Ararai songs, characterized by a melancholic and tender melody, represent the Holy Spirit, who comforts and emboldens believers.
Within Saint Yared's musical compositions, we encounter various symbols that add depth and richness to the musical expression. The "Difat" (ድፋት) symbolizes the descent of Christ from heaven, encouraging us to humbly submit to God's will. The "Hidet" (ሂደት) symbol, exemplified by the Lord's earthly ministry, urges us to continue our journey without hindrance, following in His footsteps.
Furthermore, the "Kinat" (ቅናት) symbol instructs us to lift our heads and proclaim the truth boldly, just as Christ did when confronted by His accusers. The "Yizet" (ይዘት) symbol, observed when a song is read swiftly, reminds us of the Lord's arrest by the Jews. The "Kurt" (ቁርጥ) symbol calls for strength and resilience, likening it to Christ's decision to endure suffering for the redemption of humanity.

About St. #Michael 

Beloved members of John's Repentance spiritual service, today is the monthly feast of St. Michael. Hence let us read the below text written about him and partake from his blessings. 

St. Michael, whose name echoes the divine query, "Who is like God?" stands as a beacon of divine strength and leadership. This mighty Archangel, esteemed in the heavenly hierarchy, is not merely a celestial being but also a symbol of hope and fortitude for the faithful. His role transcends the boundaries of heaven, extending his protective mantle over the faithful on earth.

Let us recall the ancient scriptures and the teachings of the Church Fathers, who spoke of St. Michael with reverence and awe. His presence in pivotal moments of Biblical history underscores his importance. When Joshua, leading the Israelites in the wilderness, encountered a divine figure with a drawn sword, it was none other than St. Michael, the captain of the Lord's host. This encounter, as narrated in the Book of Joshua, reveals the martial and protective aspects of St. Michael's character.

In the exodus from Egypt, where the Israelites were led from bondage to freedom, St. Michael was the guiding light, the angelic shepherd appointed by God Himself to safeguard His people. His role was not only as a guide but also as a guardian, ensuring the fulfillment of God's promises.

Furthermore, in the prophetic visions of Daniel, St. Michael emerges as a powerful intercessor and protector of the faithful. He is depicted as the great prince who stands guard over God's people, a testament to his enduring role in the salvation history.

In the New Testament, St. Michael's presence is equally profound. His role in the cosmic struggle against evil and his intercession for the faithful before the throne of God are themes that resonate throughout Christian theology.

The Orthodox Church, in its liturgical and theological tradition, has always held St. Michael in high esteem. His commemoration on the twelfth day of each month is not merely a ritualistic observance but a testament to his ongoing intercessory role. The Church teaches that St. Michael's prayers bring about the blessings of nature: the timely rains, the fruitful earth, the gentle winds—all are seen as manifestations of his intercession.

In our daily struggles and in the greater spiritual battle against sin and temptation, let us turn to St. Michael for guidance and protection. His example inspires us to stand firm in our faith, to confront the adversities of life with courage, and to remain steadfast in our devotion to God.

As we honor St. Michael, let us remember that his story is not just a tale from the past but a living reality that permeates the life of every Orthodox believer. In our prayers and in our actions, let us emulate his unwavering faith and his unceasing devotion to the will of God. May his intercession strengthen us, may his example guide us, and may his presence among us be a source of comfort and hope.

Thus, beloved in Christ, let us embrace the lessons and the legacy of the Archangel St. Michael. May his blessings be upon us, and may his intercession lead us closer to the divine. In his honor and memory, let us say, "Archangel Michael, pray for us, that we may be worthy of the promises of Christ." Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of The Virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
