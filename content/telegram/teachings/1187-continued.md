(Continued) 👆

The Israelites' deliverance from bondage and the rituals associated with their liberation serve as profound foreshadowings of Christ's sacrificial offering on Calvary in the New Testament. They point to the ultimate sacrifice of Christ Himself, offered for our salvation. May we embrace and spread this transformative message to all, recognizing the immense grace bestowed upon us.

Let us give thanks and praise to the God of the saints, who mercifully guides us on our spiritual journey. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
