Continued 👇🏾

✍🏾Living in the Presence of God

If God is with us, we are called to live holy lives separated from sin, walking in righteousness, and glorifying His name. When a person strives to live according to God’s will, the Lord will never abandon them.

However, when people encounter suffering, hardship, or increasing trials, they often feel as though God has forsaken them. Yet, our Creator never forgets His children. He allows trials for a divine purpose to purify, strengthen, and guide us toward holiness.

✍🏾Especially in these days of hardship and turmoil, when troubles surround us and spiritual battles rage, we Christians must remain steadfast in faith.
If we fall into murmuring and complaint against God, we will find no peace.

Instead, let us remember that God never forgets His creation. He is with all who trust in Him. What He desires from us is simple and true:

To do what is pleasing in His sight,

To worship Him faithfully,

To live a genuine Christian life,

To obey His commandments and walk in His ways.

May our merciful God never depart from us.
May His divine presence dwell continually within us.
May He make us steadfast in His holy house forever.Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
