VI. The Meaning of Prayer and Its Heavenly Ascent

Prayer is not mere speech it is a holy mystery. It is:

The earthly man revealing his heavenly nature.

The connection between man and the heavenly God.

A conversation with the Most High, as the Just King wrote.

The praying soul rises like an angel, entering the heavenly court, laying itself upon the altar not with blood, but with tears. This is the true burnt offering a heart crushed, a soul humbled, and a mouth trembling with holy love.

VII. The Conditions of Acceptable Prayer

Let not the one who prays let his eyes wander or his mind stray. Let him ascend in thought, depart from earth, and draw near to God. Let his mouth and heart be one, and his faith and love be steadfast.

The one who prays rightly:

Prays with humility

Prays with a pure conscience

Prays with sincerity of heart

Prays as a child to his Father

Such a soul shall never be turned away. For the Lord, who searches hearts and sees every tear, will say to such a one: “You are My son. Ask, and you shall receive.”

VIII. Thanksgiving in Prayer and the Confession of Sin

Saint Paul teaches:

“In everything, by prayer and supplication, with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God.” (Phil. 4:6)

Yes even when our requests are not answered as we wish, we are called to thank God. Why? Because He knows better than we do. Because He is just, even when we are judged. The ancient prayer confesses:

“We have sinned… we have gone astray… yet You have done justly.”

The one who confesses his sin and praises God for His justice, even in chastisement, is more grateful than the one who boasts in comfort.

Conclusion: A Call to Holy Mourning

O you who long for God, weep!
Weep as David wept.
Weep as Peter wept.
Weep as the saints wept.

For such tears are pearls in the hand of the Lord. They are received as offerings, stored as treasures, and answered with joy. Do not be ashamed to cry before the God who wept for you. Let your prayer be not cold and dry, but wet with tears, rich with love, and aflame with repentance.

“I waited patiently for the Lord; He heard my cry. He lifted me from the pit and set my feet upon a rock.” (Psalm 40:1–2)

May your tears be the ink with which your name is written in the Book of Life.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
