Happy New Year! #2025

Beloved in Christ,

As the sun rises on this new year, let us give thanks to our Almighty God for His boundless mercies and lovingkindness that endure forever. May this new year be a journey deeper into His embrace, a tapestry woven with threads of prayer, repentance, and acts of love. 

May the blessings of the Nativity of Our Lord Jesus Christ, the light that shines in the darkness, illuminate your path and fill your heart with peace and joy. 

Let us embark on this new year with renewed spirits, striving towards righteousness as the Holy Apostle Paul urges us, "forgetting what lies behind and straining forward to what lies ahead, I press on toward the goal for the prize of the upward call of God in Christ Jesus" (Philippians 3:13-14).

May this year be filled with spiritual growth and closeness to Our Lord.

With blessings and prayers,

Your brother in Christ.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
