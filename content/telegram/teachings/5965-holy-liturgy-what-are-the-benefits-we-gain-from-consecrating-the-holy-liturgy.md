Dear followers of the Orthodox teachings on John's Repentance website .we greet you with the peace of the Lord. Today, based on the topic mentioned above, we present to you the following important lesson. We encourage everyone to read it carefully and learn from it.

👉🏾 Forgiveness of sins

The Holy Liturgy brings forgiveness of sins because it is offered with repentance and a contrite heart.

✍🏾 Blessing of soul and body

Through the Liturgy, both our spiritual and physical lives receive grace and healing.

✍🏾 Answered prayer

The prayers offered during the Liturgy are powerful and acceptable before God.

✍🏾 Participation in angelic blessings

When we participate in and consecrate the Holy Liturgy worthily, we unite with the angels and receive eternal blessings, for through the Holy Liturgy the Body and Blood of our Lord sanctify the faithful and grant life to those who partake reverently.

✍🏾Four Major Fruits of Celebrating the Holy Liturgy

1. Education (Teaching of the Faith)

During the Holy Liturgy, the faithful learn and come to know the dogma and teachings of the Church.

👉🏾 For example, when the Liturgy of St. Mary is celebrated (written by aba hriaqos ), it teaches:

👉🏾The mystery of the Holy Trinity

👉🏾The mystery of the Incarnation

👉🏾The role and holiness of the Virgin Mary

Thus, the Holy Liturgy itself becomes a living and active school of theology.

2. History of the Church

A person who participates in all 14 Liturgies learns the history of the Church, the saints, and the progression of salvation as preserved in the liturgical tradition.

3. Spiritual Advice (Exhortation)

The Liturgy teaches us:

👉🏾Love and patience

👉🏾Unity and forgiveness

👉🏾Fasting and prayer

Therefore, anyone who participates attentively receives continual spiritual guidance.

4. Exhortation (Spiritual Instruction)

We receive exhortation within the Holy Liturgy.

👉🏾 For example, if a person has a quarrel with his brother, the Holy Liturgy (kidase egzi'e) teaches him to forgive. In this part of the Liturgy, we are instructed to submit ourselves to the Creator, to renew our commitment before God, and to preserve our covenant of communion with one another. By sanctifying and participating in the Holy Liturgy, we learn these foundational principles that are beneficial for all humanity.

✍🏾 The Holy Liturgy as Communal Prayer and Offering

The Holy Liturgy is a sacred prayer in which Scripture readings and hymns are offered. All participate together the priest, the deacon, and the faithful.

The Church teaches:
“Do not stand naked before the prophet of God.”

This means that no one should stand before God during the Holy Liturgy without an offering or alms in hand. Even if we have little, we should approach Him with what we are able to offer, without fear or shame. If possible, it is appropriate to bring incense, raisins, candles, or other offerings.

During the Holy Liturgy, the priest prays specifically for the offerings brought by the faithful. If no offering is brought, we must ask ourselves, “For whom is this prayer offered?”

👉🏾 Therefore, we must prepare in advance for the celebration of the Holy Liturgy. Even if we are unable to participate in other spiritual services, we should always set aside time to attend and sanctify the Holy Liturgy.

✍🏾Our Responsibility When We Come to the holy Liturgy

When we come to church, we must:

👉🏾Stand reverently and participate actively

👉🏾Listen attentively to the Scriptures and apply them to our livesReceive Holy Communion with faith for eternal life
The Liturgy proclaims in its opening hymn:

👉🏾“Whoever does not remain until the end of the Liturgy, who does not listen to the Scriptures, and who does not receive the Holy Communion, let him be separated from the communion of the Church.”Therefore, preparation is essential.
