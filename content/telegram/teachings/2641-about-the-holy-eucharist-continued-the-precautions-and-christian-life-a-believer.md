About the Holy Eucharist  
 
(Continued)👆

The Precautions and Christian Life a Believer Should Follow Before and After Receiving Holy Communion.

Holy Communion signifies a commitment to follow the Orthodox Church's teachings, which should inspire us to perform good works and acts of charity. It is not a reward, but a means to receive God's grace. Preparation through confession and repentance is crucial before partaking. Holy Communion symbolizes the union between the believer and Christ, affirming our commitment to His teachings.

The main reason why we should approach Holy Communion with spiritual diligence is not because the Son of God, His holy body, or His precious blood can be defiled by anything else, but rather because we are united with His flesh and blood. We must always strive to live in a manner that avoids the obstacles that Satan, who is jealous of our salvation, places in our way to tempt us and lead us into sin. In order to save ourselves from sin, we must participate in the holy ceremony of receiving the flesh and blood of Christ, which we take for our eternal salvation. We must spend our lives in faith and conduct, doing righteous works. Christian vigilance is necessary because Satan often throws subtle obstacles in our way.

If we stumble and fall into sin after receiving Holy Communion, we must repent and confess our sins to the priest. We should stop receiving Holy Communion for the time being, complete our penance, and seek advice and guidance from the priest before resuming Holy Communion. Once we begin to receive Holy Communion, we should avoid sins and offenses that would prevent us from approaching it. If we commit a sin that should not be repeated after receiving Holy Communion, we have dishonored this great sacrament and must repent and work to strengthen our spiritual purpose.

We should not take repentance lightly or assume that we can continue to repeat the same offense. We must strive to control our thoughts and emotions, live steadfastly in morals and faith, and quickly repent if we fall into sin. We must constantly strive to strengthen our spiritual purpose in order not to be separated from the Holy Communion.

In addition, there are certain rituals and beliefs that may be common in some places but are not in accordance with the Church's canon and tradition. When we encounter doubt or confusion, we should seek guidance from the Church fathers, teachers, and scholars to clarify the true teachings of the Church. We should not be led astray by beliefs or practices that are outside of the Church's system. As St. Paul advised Timothy, "Avoid godless chatter, because those who indulge in it will become more and more ungodly" (2 Timothy 2:16).

 
May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
