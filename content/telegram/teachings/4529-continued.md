4. To Teach Us Righteousness

Beyond redemption, Christ came to restore the image and likeness of God (Genesis 1:26–27). His entire life was the perfect teaching His silence, His speech, His acts, and even His sufferings were all revelations of righteousness.

He is the perfect Teacher, correcting false doctrine and revealing true wisdom. In Him we learn how to walk humbly, love purely, and live with justice. He lifted the veil, revealing God as our loving Father, not distant and wrathful, but near and merciful.

“Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest…” (Matthew 11:28–29)

Let us also heed the apostolic wisdom:

“Godliness with contentment is great gain... For the love of money is the root of all evil.” (1 Timothy 6:6–11)

Greed, pride, and vain ambition are tools of the enemy. But in contentment and holiness, we find peace.

5. To Reveal Divine Love

The crowning glory of Christ’s mission is love. Divine love is not a concept but a Person Jesus Christ, who laid down His life for us.

“For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son…” (John 3:16)

While we were yet sinners, He died for us (Romans 5:8–11). He took on flesh, bore our sins, and poured out His blood so that we might be reconciled. This love is unmerited, sacrificial, and eternal.

“Beloved, if God so loved us, we also ought to love one another.” (1 John 4:10–13)

Thus, Christian love is not mere sentiment it is sacrificial, practical, and radiant with grace. It is the mark of true discipleship and the seal of the Spirit.

Conclusion: Live As He Taught

In light of this great revelation, let us not live as those still under bondage. Let us live according to what Christ has taught us in law, in word, and in deed. Let us put away greed, malice, and pride, and instead embrace humility, love, and holiness.

Let us, as heirs of His kingdom, walk in the light He has given, that when He appears again in glory, we shall be found worthy to reign with Him.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
