Continued 👆🏾

St. Isaac the Syrian teaches, "Enter into your heart's chamber, shut its doors behind you, and seek Him out. He is nearer to you than you are to yourself."  This seeking, this turning inward to cultivate stillness and communion with God, is vital.  

Here are some practices that may help:

Guard your prayer rule diligently. Even short, heartfelt prayers throughout the day can help maintain a spirit of communion with God.

Study the Scriptures.  Immerse yourself in the Psalms, the Gospels, the wisdom of the Church Fathers. Let their words nourish your soul and guide your steps.

Practice fasting. Fasting, even once or twice a week, helps to subdue the passions and clear the mind, making us more receptive to God's grace.

Give alms.  Even small acts of generosity can help to soften our hearts and turn our focus outward.

Remember, my child, the Christian life is a journey, not a destination. We are all works in progress, constantly striving towards God. Do not be discouraged by your imperfections, but rather, let them draw you closer to the One who can heal and perfect you.

Before approaching the chalice, engage in honest self-examination. Confess your weaknesses and distractions to God, asking for His help to receive the Eucharist worthily. 

And never hesitate to seek guidance from your spiritual father.  He, as a shepherd appointed by God, can offer the specific counsel and direction you seek on this blessed path.

May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you always.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
