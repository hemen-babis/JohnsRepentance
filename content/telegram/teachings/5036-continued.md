IV. The Cosmic Restoration Through the New Eve

Behold the divine reversal!  

Where Eve: 

1. Listened to the serpent → Mary hearkened to Gabriel  

2. Plucked the fruit in disobedience → Mary offered her obedient "Fiat" 

3. Became mother of dying → Mary became mother of Living  

Saint Irenaeus proclaims: "The knot of Eve's disobedience was loosed by Mary's obedience."  

V. The Practical Devotion for Faithful Souls 

How then shall we honor her?  

1. Imitate Her Virtues

- Her humility (Luke 1:38)  

- Her silence (Luke 2:19)  

- Her endurance (John 19:25)  

2. Seek Her Intercession
Remembering Christ's first miracle at Cana (John 2:1-11) performed at her request  

3. Ponder Her Mysteries
As she "kept all these things in her heart" (Luke 2:51)  

Conclusion: The Ladder to Paradise

O Most Holy Theotokos:  

- You are our life, our sweetness and our hope 

- The dawn announcing the Sun of Righteousness  

- The bridge leading exiles back to Paradise

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
