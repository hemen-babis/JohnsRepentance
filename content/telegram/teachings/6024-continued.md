Continued 👇🏾

✍🏾Therefore, Christians, since our Lord was born of the Holy Virgin Mary and was crucified on the Cross for our salvation, we must no longer live as slaves of Satan and sin.

As the Apostle teaches us:

👉🏾“Beloved, let us love one another.”(1 John 4:7)

Let us live in true love, keep His ommandments, turn away from all sin, and repent sincerely. We do not know the hour when we will depart from this temporary life. 

Therefore, let us prepare for the Kingdom of Heaven by confessing our sins, being cleansed from sinful deeds, and receiving His holy Body and precious Blood with faith and reverence.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
