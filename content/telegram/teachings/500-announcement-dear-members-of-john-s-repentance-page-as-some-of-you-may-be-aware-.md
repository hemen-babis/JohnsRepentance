#Announcement 

Dear members of John's Repentance page, as some of you may be aware, the Ethiopian Orthodox Church is in the midst of a persecution by the Ethiopian government. Even though this has been going on for a long time, it has now reached a level where we either lose the integrity and sovereignty of the Church or we fight back their efforts.

The government's efforts to break the Church has been limitless and those of you who have been following the news will understand this better because what has been done is beyond most people's imagination of limitless efforts. 

Today is a historical day because the Holy Synod has made it's stances clear to the government and all of us. The Holy Synod has asked the government to act fast and take it's hands out from Church affairs or the Church will march all around the world to spread awareness of the persecution we are facing until the problem gets resolved. 

Dear members of John's Repentance page, these are times where we all need to do our due diligences and prove our faith. What is asked from us is to 

1) Live according to the teachings of the Church. 
2) Deliver on the Holy Synod's requests.

👉 It is meaningless for us to say we are Orthodox Christians and not walk in the path of rightousness. We must all sanctify ourselves and go at least one step further in our spiritual lives during these difficult times so God can quickly send His mercy up us. Practically speaking, if we don't have a father of repentance, we must strive and look for one now. If we are not partaking of the Eucharist, we must start today. If we do not partake from the Holy Flesh and Holy Blood of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ often enough, we must strive to do so more often. 

As st. Paul said "We wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places." (Ephesians 6:12) so we need to use all the spiritual weapons at our disposal to combat the evil because if we don't do it today, when will we?

👉 For the second point, we should keep an eye on the Holy Synod's announcements and act accordingly.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
