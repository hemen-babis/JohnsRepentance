(Continue) 👆

As we journey towards the crucifixion and resurrection of our Lord, let us keep these lessons in our hearts and strive to live a life that is faithful and obedient to God's will. May we seek His forgiveness and mercy, and may His love and grace guide us always.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
