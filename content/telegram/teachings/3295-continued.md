(Continued) ☝️

These six commandments, brethren, are not mere rules but pathways to transformation. They call us from the old self of sin and death into the new life of righteousness and peace in Christ. As we contemplate these divine precepts, let us strive to internalize them, allowing them to permeate our actions and renew our minds, that we might live not under the law but under grace, exemplifying the love of God in every thought, word, and deed. Let us go forth in peace, fortified by the wisdom of our Lord Jesus Christ, to love and serve the Lord in gladness and singleness of heart. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
