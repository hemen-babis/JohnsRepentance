Continued👆🏽

Conclusion

As we enter this sacred season, let us not see it as a burden but as a divine opportunity. Just as Christ fasted and overcame temptation, so too can we emerge victorious through prayer, humility, and steadfast faith. May this Great Fast elevate our souls and bring us closer to our Creator.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
