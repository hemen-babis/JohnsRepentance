(Continued)👆🏾

In summary, God is one; God is three - an unfathomable mystery and a profound truth. The unity and trinity of God are essential to understanding His divine nature, His creation, and His salvific action in the world. This understanding requires God's self-revelation, which is manifest in the incarnation of the Son, the teachings of the church, and the guidance of the Holy Spirit. Only through this revelation can we begin to comprehend the depths of the mystery of the Holy Trinity.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
