5. Preaching Christ in All Forms and Seasons

The Church, our Mother, catechizes her children through feasts, icons, chants, and books each a vessel of divine truth. The nine major and nine minor feasts of our Lord His conception, birth, baptism, crucifixion, resurrection, and more are not mere rituals but proclamations of the gospel.

Even the icons, painted in silence, speak to those who cannot read; they are Scripture in color. The beating drum, the golden cross, the faithful rosary these are the sacred instruments of the Church’s song of Christ crucified. The apocryphal texts, too, bear witness to the love of saints who bore torture for Christ’s sake, whose blood mingled with His in the cup of suffering.

6. Rejecting the False Gospels of Signs and Philosophy

Unlike the Jews who sought signs, or the Greeks who sought wisdom, the Orthodox Church seeks Christ crucified. She does not confuse Him with philosophers or prophets, nor treat Him as a mere intercessor or teacher. She rejects the modern blasphemies which attempt to reduce Christ to a voice of morality, or worse, to a tool of political agenda.

No, He is the God of nature, the King of kings, the Lord of lords, our Redeemer and our Hope. We do not follow a philosophy or a movement we follow a Person, the Living Christ, whom we call “my Lord and my God.”

7. Let Us Hold Firm to the True Gospel

Let not the blasphemies of heretics or slanderers unsettle your hearts. The Orthodox Tewahedo Church has always preached Christ, not in word only, but in sacrament, icon, feast, and martyrdom. To say otherwise is to echo the voice of the serpent who blasphemes with a forked tongue.

Therefore, beloved, let us be steadfast in the faith. Let us listen to the voice of our holy Church, rooted in the apostles, nourished by the saints, and crowned by the Cross. Let no storm shake your trust, for Christ crucified is the wisdom of God and the power of God (1 Cor. 1:24).

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
