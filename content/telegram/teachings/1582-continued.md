(Continued) 👆🏾

May the Theotokos and Ever-Virgin Mary intercede for us. May her Dormition be our hope, her life our model, her love our guide. Let us fast, let us pray, let us love as she loved, and let us hope as she hoped. Through her intercessions, may God grant us grace to complete our journey in faith, in hope, in love.

In the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
