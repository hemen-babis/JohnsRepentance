(Continued) 👆

When we approach the Holy Communion and the house of God, it is preferable to have a full garment that is used exclusively for this purpose. By doing so, we show respect and perfect preparation, and sincere devotion and fear of God within us. After using the garment, it can be washed and reused for the next time we approach the Holy Communion. However, we should not forget the poor Christians who may not have special or extra clothes. They can wash and reuse their existing clothes without needing to buy a new one. No one should be kept away from the Holy Communion because they cannot afford special clothes.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
