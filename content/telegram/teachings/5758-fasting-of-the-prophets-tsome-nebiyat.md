Dear followers of the Orthodox teaching, we greet you with the peace of God. Today, according to the topic mentioned above, we present to you the following teaching, and we entrust you to read and learn from it:

✍🏾The Fast of the Prophets

Fasting of the Prophets: This is one of the seven great fasts of the Church. It is observed every year from Hidar 15 until the eve of the Nativity.

✍🏾 The Fast of the Prophets lasts for 44 days before the Nativity. During this fast, the faithful remember the fulfillment of the prophecies spoken by the prophets and the completion of the 5500 years of human suffering.

✍🏾When God the Father willed to save the world, the prophets guided by the Holy Spirit prophesied in every generation about:

👉🏾 His taking on human flesh

👉🏾 His flight into Egypt

👉🏾 His baptism in the Jordan River

👉🏾 His enlightening the darkened world through His teaching

👉🏾 His suffering for the salvation of mankind

👉🏾 His crucifixion and death

👉🏾 His resurrection, ascension, and second coming

✍🏾 The prophets longed to see these prophecies fulfilled. They prayed and fasted, saying, “Stretch out Your strength and stretch out Your hand.” They counted days, weeks, months, and years, waiting for the moment when God would become man for the salvation of the world.

✍🏾Names and Significance of the Fast

✍🏾The Fast of the Prophets is called the “Fast of the Prophets (tsome Nebiyat)” because it commemorates the fulfillment of the prophecies and prayers of the holy prophets who awaited the birth of our Lord.

👉🏾 It is also called the “Fast of Adam(tsome adam)” because it marks the fulfillment of the promise of salvation given to Adam.

👉🏾It is called the “Fast of the Sermon” (tsome sibket )because it is a time when the gospel about the birth of Christ is widely preached.

👉🏾The fast ends on the day of the Nativity, which is why it is also known as the “Fast of the Nativity.”(tsome lidet)

👉🏾 It is called the “Fast of the Apostles”(tsome hawaryat) because the holy apostles said, “We fast on the day of the Resurrection, and what do we do on the day of the Nativity?” They observed the fast that the prophets observed before the birth of Christ.

👉🏾 It is also called “Philip’s Fast”(tsome filipos). The Apostle Saint Philip traveled throughout Africa, teaching the Gospel in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, performing miracles, and converting many to the faith. He died as a martyr among those who rejected his teachings. When his disciples were about to bury his body, they observed a week of fasting and prayer, asking God to return the body of the apostle to them. God granted their request, and his body was returned on the third day and buried in glory.

👉🏾 After finding the body of the apostle, his disciples continued the fast until the birth of our Lord. That is why the prophets also call it “Philip’s Fast.”

✍🏾Additionally, after the angel Gabriel announced to the Holy Virgin Mary that she would conceive the Son of God through the Holy Spirit, our Lady observed a fast before giving birth, saying, “What shall I do, since I have conceived a God whom heaven and earth cannot contain?” This fast is therefore also known as the “Fast of Mary.”(tsome Mariam)
