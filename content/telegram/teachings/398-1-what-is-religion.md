3./ Foundation (rock)

The knowledge found in religion is not the knowledge that a person has researched or discovered. When our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ wanted to reveal his identity to the world, he asked his disciples, 'Who do you say I am?'   And Simon Peter answered. He said, "You are the Christ, the Son of the living God." And Jesus answered and said to him "O Simon, son of Jonah, blessed are you, for flesh and blood did not reveal this to you, but my Father in heaven. And I say unto thee, Thou art Peter, and upon this rock I will build my church, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it. And I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven. Whatever you bind on earth will be bound in heaven, and whatever you loose on earth will be loosed in heaven.

Another thing we can understand from this is that if the source of religion was not God, the heavenly father, the knowledge that his disciple Simon Peter had about the identity of our Lord Jesus Christ would not have been different and better than that of other people of his time. But God revealed the truth to them, which was not seen by the physical research and search of this world of flesh and blood. This is why the apostle Saint Paul warned them: 'Let your faith not be in the wisdom of men, but in the power of God. My words and my preaching were not with persuasive words of wisdom, but with revelation of spirit and power.' Cor. 2:5

Just as St. Paul said that religion is one, he also says that there is only one foundation. "In as much as the grace of God was given to me, like a wise master carpenter I laid a foundation, and another shall build upon it." But let each one be careful how he builds up against him. For no one can lay a foundation other than that which has been laid, which is Jesus Christ.' He said (1 Cor 3:10-12)

Religion is given from God

God gave Religion. And we accepted. Man cannot paint religion, make religion, or reform it. Our Lord and Savior Jesus said, "For I have given them the promise you gave me; And they received him, and they truly knew that I came from you, and they believed that you sent me." This is what he explained. This is why John 17:8 also said, "I have revealed your name to those whom you gave me out of the world."   Also, John the Evangelist clearly stated this: "The Word was in the beginning, and the Word was God, and the Word became flesh." (John 1:1) This 'Word' is God the Son. This God we call 'Jesus' existed in the beginning; from his characteristic father, God the Father and his characteristic life with the Holy Spirit, He was a creator. So, it is God himself who has revealed his name and identity to us to the extent that we can understand it, and religion is not a discovery made by human beings through research. (John 17:6) in addition to this, it is said that 'From long ago, God spoke to our fathers through prophets in many ways and in many ways, and He spoke to us at the end of this age in His Son, who made all the heirs and created the worlds; Hebrews 1:1-2

As Jesus has said to Simon Peter "O Simon, son of Jonah, blessed are you, for flesh and blood did not reveal this to you, but my Father in heaven. And I say unto thee, Thou art Peter, and upon this rock I will build my church, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.
