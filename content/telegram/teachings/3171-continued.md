Continued 👇🏾

As we commemorate this feast, let us draw near to the courage and conviction of Saint Kirkos and his holy mother. Let their ordeal inspire us to steadfastness in our own spiritual battles, buoyed by the assurance that the angels of God encamp around those who fear Him. In our supplications and in our sufferings, may we ever call upon St. Gabriel, trusting in his powerful intercession as the guardian and protector of the faithful.

Thus, with hearts uplifted, we proclaim: Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will toward men! May the prayers of Saint Gabriel, Saint Kirkos, and Saint Ielouta guide and guard us all. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
