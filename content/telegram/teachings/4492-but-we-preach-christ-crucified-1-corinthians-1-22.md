👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾“But We #Preach #Christ #Crucified” (1 Corinthians 1:22)

Part 1

The Glory of the Cross and the Apostolic Gospel Preserved in the Orthodox Church

Beloved in Christ,

Peace be unto you who seek the truth, and grace to all who strive for the incorruptible crown of faith. Let us now meditate upon the words of the Apostle Paul, not as words of mere memory, but as the breath of the Holy Spirit echoing from the mount of Zion into the ends of the earth:

 “But we preach Christ crucified.” 1Corinthians 1:22

This declaration though short in speech is deep in mystery, high in glory, and terrible to the proud. In these few words, the great Apostle discloses not only the content of his message, but the very nature of the divine purpose of God for the salvation of the world.

I. The Conflict of the Cross Against the World’s Wisdom

When the apostles were sent forth like lambs among wolves, they encountered not eager hearts but hardened minds and darkened spirits.

The Jews, who had crucified the Lord of Glory, were not satisfied with the preaching of Christ. They demanded signs mighty deeds like the dividing of the sea, the raining down of manna, the guiding cloud. In their hardness, they desired miracles more than truth.

The Greeks, puffed up with worldly wisdom, had adorned their minds with the philosophies of Socrates, Plato, and Aristotle. They had made reason their idol and scoffed at the idea of a suffering God. “What folly!” they said. “What madness to proclaim a God who hungered, thirsted, and was crucified like a criminal!”

The Romans, blinded by idolatry, bowed to emperors who exalted themselves as gods and molded golden images for the people to worship. Yet when they heard that this Gospel removed the distinction between slave and free, man and woman, citizen and foreigner that all were one before God they saw it as a threat to their empire and turned their cruelty upon the apostles.


But amidst this rejection, the apostles did not alter their message. They did not bend to the demands for signs, nor dress the gospel in the garments of philosophical speech, nor make peace with Caesar’s idolatry.

They preached Christ crucified a scandal to the Jews, foolishness to the Greeks, a threat to the Romans yet the wisdom of God and the power of God to those who believe.


II. The Content of Apostolic Preaching: The Whole Christ

Do not think that “Christ crucified” refers only to His death upon the wood of the Cross. The apostles preached the whole Christ:

His descent from heaven

His incarnation in the womb of the Virgin

His baptism in the Jordan

His teachings and miracles

His Passion and Crucifixion

His Resurrection on the third day

His Ascension and Lordship

His divine nature as Son of God

This Christ, the apostles proclaimed, was foretold by the prophets, foreshadowed in the law, and confirmed in the Gospel. And they commanded that all who believe be baptized in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, that they might receive forgiveness of sins, new birth, and the indwelling of the Holy Spirit.
