(Continued part) 👆🏾

In addition, we advice our questioner and to everyone else, if perhaps we are ashamed to confess all the sins we have committed to our regular penitent father, or if we lose faith in him or suspect that our secrets are not kept confidential; And if we think that these things will harm our life, if possible, we should talk to our father and build trust. If he can't do this, we should respectfully, using spiritual wisdom and humility inform them that we will continue our confession life with a different father. We should choose another father whom we trust very much. The reason why we say this is that many of the faithful are unable to openly confess to their fathers about their wrongdoings because they think their secrets will be wasted. And some of them find it hard to be fully transparent with their spiritual fathers because they are a big part of their social lives. 

To be free from all this danger, we strongly recommend to find a father whom you really trust and don't doubt. Since the things we do to inherit the Kingdom of God are not performed according to the laws of the flesh, we must be extra careful and evaluate our ways and actions

In the further content, for a better understanding, we may come back with related content in regards to the relationship and responsibilities of both The father of repentance and the children of repentance..

Additional information - Penance must be primarily in physical. However, we explain that believers who cannot find a father of repentance in their local church can contact a father of repentance at a distance, they can do penance, they can get spiritual advice and education by contacting them by phone or any other communication channel that is convenient for them.
