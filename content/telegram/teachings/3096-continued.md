(Continued) ☝️

Finally, as we celebrate their feast, let their lives and sacrifices be a lamp that lights our path and a beacon that guides our faith, encouraging us to live as they lived, in steadfast devotion to Christ. May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, which was sufficient for these holy apostles, be also our strength and stay in our Christian pilgrimage.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
