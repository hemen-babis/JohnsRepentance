👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾(Anqetse Bitsuan )#blessed are those who #mourn. For they will be #comforted.(#Matthew 5:4; #Luke 6:21)

(Part 7)

Our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ said,“Blessed are those who mourn, for they shall be comforted.” (Matthew 5:4)

In these divine words, mourning does not mean despair, but the holy sorrow that springs from repentance and love for God. The tears of a penitent heart are precious before the Lord. They wash away sin, soften the soul, and open the gate of mercy.

Throughout Scripture, we see how holy men and women wept not from hopelessness, but from a longing to be united again with God. Their tears became prayers, their sorrow turned to joy, and their repentance became a light to the generations that followed.

1. The Sinful Woman Who Wept Before Christ

Saint Mary ( baleshtowa mariyam ) stood behind our Lord at His feet, weeping, and began to wash His feet with her tears. She wiped them with her hair, kissed His feet, and anointed them with precious ointment (Luke 7:38). Through her sincere repentance, her many sins were forgiven.

As written in the Gospel of Luke, one of the Pharisees invited our Lord Jesus Christ to dine with him. While He sat at the table, a woman known in the city as a sinner entered the house. When she saw the Lord, she brought an alabaster jar of perfume and, with deep sorrow, began to wash His feet with her tears and wipe them with her hair.

The Pharisee, seeing this, judged her in his heart, thinking, “If this man were truly a prophet, He would know who this woman is.” But the Lord, knowing his thoughts, said to him, “Simon, I have something to say to you.” Then He spoke a parable:

“There was a certain creditor who had two debtors. One owed five hundred denarii and the other fifty. When they could not repay, he forgave them both. Which of them will love him more?” Simon replied, “The one who was forgiven more.” Jesus said, “You have judged rightly.”

Then He turned toward the woman and said, “Do you see this woman? I came into your house; you gave Me no water for My feet, but she has washed My feet with her tears and wiped them with her hair. You did not greet Me with a kiss, but she has not ceased to kiss My feet. You did not anoint My head with oil, but she has anointed My feet with perfume. Therefore I tell you, her sins, which are many, are forgiven, for she loved much. But to whom little is forgiven, the same loves little.”

And He said to her, “Your sins are forgiven.”
