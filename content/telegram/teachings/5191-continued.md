4. Preaching and Baptism

At the age of thirty, John emerged from the wilderness to proclaim the coming Kingdom. He cried in the wilderness:

"Prepare the way of the Lord; make His paths straight. Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand!" (Luke 3:3-6)

He baptized all Judea in the Jordan, calling them to true repentance. To the Pharisees and Sadducees, he warned:

"You brood of vipers! Produce fruit worthy of repentance. Every tree that does not bear good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire." (Matthew 3:7-10)

To the tax collectors and soldiers, he preached honesty, moderation, and compassion. He humbly declared:

"I indeed baptize you with water for repentance, but He who comes after me is greater than I; He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire." (Matthew 3:11)

Through this ministry, John fulfilled his role as the forerunner of Christ, guiding the people from darkness into the light of salvation.

Conclusion

Beloved, Saint John the Baptist is more than a prophet; he is the bridge between the law and grace, the voice crying in the wilderness, the herald of salvation. His birth, life, and ministry reveal the providence of God and the call of every soul to repentance. Let us honor him, follow his example of holiness and courage, and prepare our hearts to receive Christ with joy.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
