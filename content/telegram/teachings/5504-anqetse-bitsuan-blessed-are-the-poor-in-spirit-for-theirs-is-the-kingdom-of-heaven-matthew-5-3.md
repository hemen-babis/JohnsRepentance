👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾(Anqetse Bitsuan)#Blessed are the #poor in #spirit, for theirs is the #kingdom of #heaven.” (Matthew 5:3)

(Part 1)

Beloved in Christ,Let us open our hearts to the holy teachings on humility, for in the eyes of God, it is the foundation of all spiritual life. From the example of our Lord Jesus Christ, to the lives of the King David, and Nicodemus, we see that true greatness is found not in power, knowledge, or wealth, but in a heart that humbles itself before God.

✍🏾To be poor in spirit means to be truly humble before God to recognize that everything we have is from Him, and that we possess nothing of our own. It is not about physical poverty, but about the state of the soul. A person who is poor in spirit acknowledges his complete dependence on God and gives glory to Him alone.

The opposite of this humility is the sin of pride, which caused both man and the devil to fall.

Adam’s sin was the desire to “be like God” (Genesis 3:5).

The devil’s sin was his arrogance in saying, “I will ascend above the heights of the clouds; I will make myself like the Most High” (Isaiah 14:14).

These examples show us that humility is the spiritual power that conquers pride and restores the soul to God. True humility is the root of the spiritual life. Just as a tree without roots cannot stand, bear fruit, or survive, so a spiritual life without humility cannot grow, bear righteousness, or endure.

Heartfelt humility is not for appearance or display. It is not about speaking softly or pretending to be lowly. True humility is inward it is written in the heart, practiced in truth, and lived daily. It is a humble spirit that submits to God’s will, honors others, and accepts correction without bitterness.

The prophet Micah reminds us of what God desires from His people:

“He has shown you, O man, what is good; and what does the Lord require of you, but to do justly, to love mercy, and to walk humbly with your God?” (Micah 6:8)

The Teaching of Christ and the Apostles on Humility

Our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ revealed the greatness of humility in the Gospel when He said:

“He who humbles himself will be exalted, and he who exalts himself will be humbled.” (Luke 18:14)

Through this divine word, we understand that the way to glory is not through pride or self-importance, but through humility before God and man. The one who lowers himself in heart and spirit will be lifted up by the grace of the Almighty.

The Apostle Paul also emphasized the necessity of humility for spiritual growth and exaltation. He warned believers, saying:

“I say to everyone among you not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think, but to think soberly, as God has dealt to each one a measure of faith.” (Romans 12:3)

He further instructed:

“Be of the same mind toward one another. Do not set your mind on high things, but associate with the humble. Do not be wise in your own opinion.” (Romans 12:16)

Through these teachings, Saint Paul reminds us that humility preserves unity among believers and guards the soul from pride, which separates one from the grace of God.

Likewise, the Apostle Peter called all Christians to live in humility, saying:

“All of you, clothe yourselves with humility toward one another, for ‘God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble.’ Therefore, humble yourselves under the mighty hand of God, that He may exalt you in due time.” (1 Peter 5:5–6)
