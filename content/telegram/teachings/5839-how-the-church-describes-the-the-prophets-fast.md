✍🏾The Season of Sermon(Zemene sibket)

The period from Tahsas 7 to Tahsas 26 during the Fast of the Prophets is known as the Season of Sermon.

Why It Is Called the Season of Sermon(Zemene sibket)

It receives this name because during this time the Prophets foretold, taught, and proclaimed the coming of the Lord in the flesh.As the Church remembers their testimony and continues to proclaim the mystery of the Incarnation, this period is called the Season of Sermon, meaning a time of proclamation and divine teaching.

1. The First Sunday Called “Sermon”

The first Sunday of the Season of Sermon is itself called “Sermon.”(Sibket)

Meaning of the Day

On this Sabbath, the Church teaches that the Incarnation of Christ was revealed beforehand in the Torah of Moses, in the writings of the Prophets, and in the Psalms.

Because the prophets proclaimed His coming long in advance, this day is named “Sermon,” which signifies a message that unites humanity with God.

During this week the Church reflects on the genealogy of Christ, from the calling of Abraham to the kingdom of David, recognizing the prophetic preparation for the birth of the Savior.

2. The Sermon of the Prophets

On the Sabbath of Sermons, the Gospel reading declares that our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ is the One about whom Moses and the Prophets wrote.

Gospel Reading

(John 1:45)This part affirms that Christ is the fulfillment of the Law and the Prophets.

Teachings of the Prophets

David the Prophet and King, through the Holy Spirit, foresaw and sang about the salvation brought by Christ.(“Send forth Your hand from above and save me.”)

Isaiah the Prophet, one of the greatest among them, proclaimed the mystery of the Incarnation, saying:
“Oh, that You would rend the heavens and come down, so that the mountains might shake at Your presence!” (Isaiah 64:1)
The Church teaches this prophecy as pointing to Christ descending from heaven and being born of the Virgin Mary.

3. The Preaching of the Church

Following the apostolic teaching, the Church preaches Christ at all times.

Apostolic Witness

Saint Paul teaches:
“We preach Christ crucified.” (2 Corinthians 4:5)
The Church proclaims the hope of salvation that our Lord Jesus Christ granted and accomplished for humanity.

The Hymn of Saint Yared

As Saint Yared sings,
“We preach the Son who existed before the world, who is the Savior.”
The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church continually proclaims Christ the Son of God, who died for our sins and rose to give life to all, whether in times of abundance or in times of hardship, in every season and circumstance.
