Dear followers of the Orthodox teachings on John's Repentance website .we greet you with the peace of the Lord. Today, based on the topic mentioned above, we present to you the following important lesson. We encourage everyone to read it carefully and learn from it.

✍🏾Christianity as a Life of Imitating Christ

Our Lord Jesus Christ taught that Christian life is a life of imitating of christ. He said, “If you know these things, blessed are you if you do them” (John 13:17). Christianity is not only knowing Christ, but doing what He did.

The Apostle Saint Paul clearly explains this principle when he says:

“Imitate me, just as I also imitate Christ” (1 Corinthians 11:1).
This means that Christian life and spiritual service are fulfilled by imitating Christ in our daily living.
After our Lord washed the feet of His disciples, He gave them a clear rule for service, saying:
“I have given you an example, that you should do as I have done to you” (John 13:15).

Therefore, spiritual service, expressed briefly, is doing to others what Christ has done for us. When lived out in practice, Christian life is the imitation of Christ in humility, love, and obedience.

Loving as Christ Loved
When our Lord gave the new commandment, He said:

“A new commandment I give to you, that you love one another as I have loved you” (John 13:34).
By this commandment, Christ teaches us that Christianity means loving one another according to His example of love, not according to human standards.

Saint John the Evangelist explains this teaching in his epistle, saying:

👉🏾“He who says he abides in Him ought himself also to walk just as He walked” (1 John 2:6).
To abide in Christ is not merely to confess Him with words, but to walk in the same path He walked.

👉🏾Our Lord further confirms this when He says:

“If anyone serves Me, let him follow Me; and where I am, there My servant will be also” (John 12:26).

👉🏾True service to Christ requires following Him in life, conduct, and sacrifice.

Saint Peter, the light of the world, teaches us about the example of Christ in his epistle, saying:

“For to this you were called, because Christ also suffered for us, leaving us an example, that you should follow His steps”(1 Peter 2:21).

Saint Paul also explains the humility of Christ, saying:

“He emptied Himself, taking the form of a servant, and coming in the likeness of men. And being found in appearance as a man, He humbled Himself and became obedient to the point of death, even the death of the cross”
(Philippians 2:7–8).

✍🏾Through this teaching, Saint Paul shows that Christ is both our Redeemer and our Exemplar. He redeemed us by His sacrifice, and He gave us a model for how to live through His humility and obedience.

Therefore, if you know and believe that Christ is the Redeemer of your soul and the Exemplar of your life, and if you follow His example in humility, obedience, and love, then you are truly blessed and worthy of honor.
