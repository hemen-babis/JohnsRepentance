✍🏾Spiritual Meaning of the Week

This week reminds us that Sunday is a day of rest and reflection.
We also remember the spiritual lives of the righteous fathers, the heavenly joy they inherited, and the Christian conduct that led them to eternal life. Their example strengthens us to live in a manner worthy of the Kingdom.

As believers, we are instructed to turn away from anxious attachment to temporary earthly matters. Instead, we must persevere in Christian conduct, so that when Christ comes in judgment, we may inherit the eternal, imperishable Kingdom of Heaven.
