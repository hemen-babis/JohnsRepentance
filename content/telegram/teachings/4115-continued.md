II. The Torah (The Law of Moses)

With Moses, the divine revelation grew clearer, though still veiled in types and shadows. Through the Law, miracles, visions, and commands, God revealed His justice and holiness. Here was His glory in fire and cloud; here was His voice from Sinai, shaking the mountain and the hearts of men.

III. The Gospel

And in the final epoch, the fullness of revelation dawned not in thunder or mystery, but in the form of a man, the Son of God incarnate. He whom no man has seen, the Only-Begotten Son who is in the bosom of the Father, has declared Him (John 1:18). Through His voice, His deeds, His suffering, and His rising again, He revealed not only God’s essence but God’s face.

The First Ordinance: Baptism

In the unfolding of this divine drama, the first ordinance established by our Lord in the flesh was Holy Baptism. Let us not treat this as a ritual for children or a rite of passage it is the very death and resurrection of Christ made present to each believer.

Why was He baptized?

To fulfill what was written (Ps. 113:3–6), and so, fulfill all righteousness.

To reveal His divinity as the heavens opened and the voice of the Father declared, “This is My beloved Son” (Matt. 3:17).

To offer Himself as an example to all (Matt. 11:28–29), beckoning us to follow Him through the waters.

To cancel the debt of sin nailed to the cross (Col. 2:13–15).

To sanctify the waters, so that the creation that once drowned the world in Noah’s day now brings salvation through grace.

Why are we baptized?

To be cleansed from sin and made holy (1 Cor. 6:11).

To become children of the Trinity, born not of blood or the will of man, but of God (Matt. 28:19; John 1:12; John 3:5).

To be saved not merely from punishment, but into eternal life, as it is written: “He who believes and is baptized shall be saved” (Mark 16:16).

In Baptism, the old man dies, the new man rises, and the image of Christ is imprinted upon the soul. Through this sacrament, we are armed to fight against the devil’s works, for the very Spirit that descended on Christ now rests upon us.

Beloved, let us stand in awe of this profound mystery: that God was revealed in the flesh, and in doing so, He shattered the dominion of the devil. The works of the deceiver lies, sin, death, and alienation from God are destroyed not by sword or fire, but by the humility of God incarnate.

May this lesson kindle in us the fire of reverence, gratitude, and unshakable faith. Let us glorify the Father, who sent His Son; the Son, who was revealed to destroy the devil’s works; and the Holy Spirit, who makes all these mysteries present to us even now.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
