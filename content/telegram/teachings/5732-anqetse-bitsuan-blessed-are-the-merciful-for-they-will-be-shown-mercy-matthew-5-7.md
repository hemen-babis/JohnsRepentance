👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾 (#Anqetse #Bitsuan)“#Blessed are the #merciful, for they will be shown #mercy” (Matthew 5:7)

(Part 22)

Dear followers of the Orthodox teachings ,we greet you with the peace of God. Today, based on the verse above, we present to you the following important lesson. We entrust you all to read it with diligence and learn from it.

✍🏾God Shows Mercy to the Weak, the Despised, and the Helpless

Saint Luke shows us that God’s mercy reaches those whom society rejects. The tax collector, considered one of the despised of his time, “stood far off and would not even lift up his eyes to heaven, but beat his breast, saying, ‘God, be merciful to me, i am sinner.’” Because of his humility, he received greater mercy than the proud Pharisee and returned to his house justified (Luke 18:13–14). God lifts up those who lower themselves before Him.

✍🏾Zacchaeus the Tax Collector

When our Lord saw Zacchaeus climbing a tree to see Him, He said, “Zacchaeus, hurry and come down, for today I must stay at your house.” Zacchaeus came down quickly and welcomed Him joyfully. The crowd murmured because Jesus chose to stay with a sinner, but Zacchaeus stood before the Lord and said, “Lord, half of my possessions I will give to the poor, and if I have taken anything from anyone unjustly, I will restore it fourfold.”Jesus declared, “Today salvation has come to this house, because he too is a son of Abraham” (Luke 19:5–9). Mercy transformed his life and brought salvation to his home.

✍🏾The Helpless Man at Bethesda

At the Pool of Bethesda, a helpless man cried out, “Lord, I have no one to put me into the pool when the water is stirred; and while I am coming, another steps down before me” (John 5:7). The Lord saw his suffering and had mercy on him, commanding him to rise, take up his bed, and walk. His weakness became a moment of divine compassion and healing.

✍🏾The Power of Mercy

Saint Yohannes Afewerk teaches that mercy is profound wisdom and a strong shield for those who practice it. He explains that mercy is the companion of God, standing beside Him and blessing those whom He loves. It should never be despised, for through its purity it grants great spiritual freedom to all who live by it.

He continues by saying that mercy has great power, especially when shown to the rejected and to those who have fallen into sin. Mercy breaks chains, extinguishes burning trials, removes corruption, and takes away the gnashing of teeth. Through mercy, the gates of heaven are opened with ease. In summary, “mercy is the queen who makes a person resemble God.”
(Chrysostom, Commentary on James 2:13)

✍🏾The Main Characteristics of Mercy in the Bible

Using the above teaching as a foundation, we now consider the primary qualities of mercy as revealed in Holy Scripture.

1. Mercy Is a Virtue Through Which We Imitate God

True Christians are called to imitate Christ. Showing mercy is one of the essential virtues by which we resemble our Lord. God is merciful, kind, and forgiving.

Our Lord teaches us directly:
“Be merciful, just as your Father is merciful” (Luke 6:36).

Saint Paul also instructs us to follow Christ’s example, saying,
“Be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, just as God in Christ forgave you” (Ephesians 4:32).

✍🏾Mercy Is Given Freely by God

The mercy God has shown us is not something we earned. The apostle teaches,

“He saved us, not by works of righteousness which we have done, but according to His mercy, by the washing of regeneration and renewing of the Holy Spirit” (Titus 3:5).Therefore, when we show mercy, it must be genuine, wholehearted, and without discrimination.
