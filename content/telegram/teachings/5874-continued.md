Continued 👇🏾

The Apostle Paul explains this clearly:

“For the word of God is living and active, and sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing even to the dividing of soul and spirit, joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intentions of the heart.” (Hebrews 4:12)

Because of this truth, a division appears:

• Those who believe accept the Word and struggle spiritually with the “sword” of truth.
• Those who reject the Word oppose it and often persecute those who believe.
