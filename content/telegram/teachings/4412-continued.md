Continued 👆🏾 

May all who approach this sacred mystery do so with reverence and awe, recognizing in the humble forms of bread and wine the overwhelming love of God, poured out through Christ our Lord, who sustains us unto eternity.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
