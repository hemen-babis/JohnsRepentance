12. Making the sign of Cross as Power and Victory

We make the sign of Cross because it is the sign of Christ’s victory over death and Satan. As St. Paul says, “The word of the cross is foolishness to those who are perishing, but to us who are being saved it is the power of God” (1 Cor. 1:18). The Cross itself is not God’s power, but through it the power of God is revealed. By making the sign of Cross, we receive strength and life from Christ who conquered through it.

13. The Cross as the Terror of Demons

The devil trembles at the sight of the Cross. For it was upon the tree of Golgotha that his age-long tyranny was shattered, his schemes made void, and his power brought to nothing. Just as in the wilderness the bronze serpent lifted by Moses brought healing to the Israelites (John 3:14), so the Cross of Christ brings deliverance and strikes fear into the enemy. When we make the sign, Satan remembers his defeat and flees. The Cross is for us the weapon of light, and for him the sign of everlasting humiliation.

Conclusion

Therefore, beloved, the glory of the Cross shines in every part of our Orthodox life. It is our faith, our lesson, our proclamation, our remembrance, our love, our power, and our victory. Let us never neglect this holy seal, but make it with reverence, with understanding, and with joy. For through it we confess the Lord who died and rose again for us, and through it we are made heirs of His eternal kingdom.
