Conclusion: Emulating Mary's Virtues

In contemplating the Nativity of Mary, let us strive to emulate her virtues: her humility, her obedience, her purity, and her total dedication to God's will. As we honor her, we are called to reflect on our own calling to be bearers of Christ in our world, to bring forth the fruits of the Spirit in our lives. May the Nativity of the Theotokos inspire us to live lives worthy of the calling we have received in Christ Jesus. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
