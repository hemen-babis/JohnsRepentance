18. The Cross as Our Reproach and Pilgrimage

To follow Christ is to take up His reproach. Moses, the prophet, chose “the reproach of Christ” above the treasures of Egypt (Heb. 11:26). We, too, when we make the sign of the Cross, remember that this life is a pilgrimage: we must endure suffering with the people of God rather than chase the fleeting pleasures of sin. The Cross reminds us that we are on a journey, bearing reproach, until we meet the Lord face to face.

19. we make the sign of cross because The Cross is the Sign of His Second Coming 

Our Lord Himself declared: “Then shall appear the sign of the Son of Man in heaven” (Matt. 24:30). That sign is the Cross. At His second coming, He shall be revealed with the marks of His Passion “every eye will see Him, even those who pierced Him” (Rev. 1:7).

Thus, while still on earth, we venerate the Cross, the sign of His coming, and we pray that our resurrection may be a resurrection unto glory and not unto condemnation. The Cross, which once marked His suffering, will shine in heaven as the banner of His triumph.

Conclusion

The Cross is our blessing, our sacrament, our glory, our joy, our reproach, and our hope of the second coming. To make the sign of the Cross is to confess all these truths at once: that Christ has reconciled us, feeds us, glorifies us, and prepares us for eternal life.

Therefore, beloved, let us not be ashamed of the Cross, but let us embrace it with boldness, worshipping Him who hung upon it, that through it we may inherit His eternal kingdom.
