(Continued)👆

To sum up, to live in sin is death and slavery. Sin takes away from us peace, rest, tranquility, understanding. It also causes stress. It keeps us away from the grace and fruits of the Holy Spirit.  It makes us vulnerable to worldly thoughts, actions, and lust of the flesh. It takes away from God's sonship, from the protection of the Trinity, from life, from love, from the goodness and virtues of God. Sin will also take us to eternal death. There are ups and downs in the journey of life. The crutch that help us stand up again is repentance. Repentance is like being born again or being regenerated every time. Anyone who commits sins every now and then adds diseases to old age. The Christianity of a person who does not repent is useless and his sins will be many. A person who repents and frequently examines himself for his soul with his confession father is like a healthy and peaceful person because he always asks a doctor for help.

So our questioner, thinking about running away from repentance and fighting sin is like making a plan to widen the door for sin or extending the date for repentance, so we recommend that this is not appropriate. Instead, as our Lord Jesus Christ told us, "if your burden is heavy, come to me", because he called us to help us from our burden, we must repent and fight to be freed from sin by taking down our burden continuously. God is our power to be able to do what we think we cannot do. It is said to draw near to God and he will draw near to you (Jas 4:8).  Thus, we should have our hopes in the faithful GOD and listen to his calling for our repentance. His unwavering words say "God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble" (James 4:6), so that no one falls into the grave of sin that he commits regularly and does not fall away from being the child of God, and that GOD's grace that is constantly given to him is not lacking, everyone should have a life of repentance. 

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
