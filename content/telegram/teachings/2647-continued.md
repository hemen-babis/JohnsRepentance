(Continued) ☝️

In conclusion, the feast of Hosanna encapsulates a multifaceted theological and spiritual truth, beckoning the faithful to both celebrate and emulate the humility and sacrificial love of Jesus Christ. Let us, therefore, wave our palm fronds not merely as artifacts of ritual but as emblems of our commitment to follow Christ, our King of Peace and Savior, into the holy city of eternal life. As we chant "Hosanna," let it be with a profound longing for deliverance and a deep joy in the salvation He brings.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
