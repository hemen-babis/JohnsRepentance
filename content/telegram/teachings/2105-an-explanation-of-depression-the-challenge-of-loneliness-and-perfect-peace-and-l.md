---An explanation of #depression, the challenge of #loneliness and perfect #peace and #light

👉🏾 Question from member: I often feel depressed and lonely. Life has no meaning to me, I just want to die and live with Jesus, I've been looking for happiness all my life but I can't find it, I feel like my mind is going to explode, please pray for me, I need prayer, what do you recommend?

✍️Answer:- Why does a person choose loneliness? Why does a person hide? In essence, man is not happy without God, because there is no peace without God. Jesus Christ said in the Gospel of John, chapter 14, verse 27, "Peace I leave with you, and my peace I give you, the peace I give you is not as the world gives; do not let your hearts be troubled, do not be afraid."
 
The world kisses us today then bites tomorrow; gives today then takes away tomorrow. But God does not take away what He has given, no matter what, there is no peace without God. What is life? Life is the opposite of death, life is living. Life is God, it is written, "Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life."
 
Therefore, if we believe in the Son, we live with Jesus who is the life. It is Jesus Christ who gave life when human beings could not live according to the commandment, it is Jesus Christ who enlightened the dark world. In fact, this Christmas (birth) season is called the light. And if it is said who is the light: it is Jesus Christ.

This world was dark before God the Son became a man. The world lived in darkness. It is called light because God became a man and enlightened the world by making us members of His body. Many people's lives are in darkness. Darkness is sin, darkness is betrayal, darkness is hatred, darkness is lack of love; On the other hand, light is Jesus Christ, light is life, light is religion, light is peace, light is help, blessing and love. Because it is our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ who gave us that, there is no life without Jesus Christ.
 
As the owner himself told us, John Gospel chapter 15 verse 5 said "without me you can do nothing", so without Jesus Christ nothing can be done. How can peace be found without Jesus Christ? How can you live? Absolutely not possible. When Judas betrayed Jesus Christ for 30 dollars he died because he was separated from Jesus Christ who is life. We remember that when the devil was once the archangel, when he forgot God and said, "I am the creator," and then failed and separated from God who is life.

Also, when Adam lived peacefully in paradise for seven years and did what he was told not to do, he lost peace from God who is life, and found it difficult to live. but then we remember that he found peace when he returned to God, when he reconciled with God, so there is no peace without God.
 
Our questioner said that they want to die and live with Jesus Christ. So, those of us who do not have peace on earth, those who have fallen into sin on earth, those who have not tried to repent to find Jesus Christ, how can we find peace if we die? What qualifies us for the kingdom of heaven is the work we have done on earth to live with Jesus Christ and enable us to live in heaven. If we die on earth covered in sin, without repentance, confession, and reconciliation with God, we will not be able to live with Jesus. Therefore, it means that we cannot live with our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ before we repent.

About Holy #Eucharist and  and about our Lady #Virgin #Mary
 
Beloved and respected, all our members who follow the orthodox education that we convey on John's Repentance page; Based on the topic mentioned above, we have sent you the important lesson below, so we entrust you all to read and learn from it.

"I am the living bread that came down from heaven. If anyone eats of this bread, he will live forever, and the bread that I give for the life of the world is my body." (John 6:51)
 
This word speaks of the body of Christ in which eternal life is found. And this flesh was obtained from the Virgin Mary. The Gospel of John begins as follows: "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God... and the Word became flesh" (John 1:1 and 14). When he said, "The Word became flesh," he is actually confirming that the Son of God came down from the heavens to save the fallen Adam, took the flesh from the Virgin Mary's body, and the soul from her soul, and appeared as a man. This is God who became man. Because, in the Old Testament (before God appeared in the flesh), God asked Adam, "Where are you, Adam?" / Gen. 3:10/ After making him tell his whereabouts, He gave him a promise of salvation, "I will be born from your grandson and save you" (Klemenitus).

Later, when the time came, the angel Gabriel was sent to the Virgin Mary and said to her, "Behold, you will conceive and give birth to a son, and you will call his name Jesus, and he will be great." He is also called the son of the innocent. (Luke 1:1-3) Holding this word, when we refer back what Isaiah said in his prophecy, "...therefore the Lord himself will give you a sign, behold, a virgin will conceive and give birth to a son, and she will call his name Immanuel." He says. (Isaiah 7:14)

The Lord Jesus also taught extensively about Bread from Heaven (John 6: 22-59). His teachings of ‘Bread from Heaven’ are the basis for Mystery of Holy Eucharist. St John the Evangelist summarized this as follows: “Most assuredly, I say to you, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink His blood, you have no life in you. Whoever eats My flesh and drinks My blood has eternal life, and I will raise him up at the last day. For My flesh is food indeed, and My blood is drink indeed. He who eats My flesh and drinks My blood abides in Me, and I in him.” (John 6:53-56). This eternal word remains to be the core theology of mystery of Holy Eucharist.

Jesus Christ, when he taught about his holy body and his precious blood, which was begotten from the Virgin Mary, united and given to the world for salvation, he said, "Verily, verily, I say unto you, Except ye eat the flesh of the Son of man, and drink his blood, ye have no life in you." (John 6:53).
All those who eat and drink the flesh and blood of our Lord Jesus Christ, who was crucified on Friday to save us, will inherit the kingdom. The holy body and the noble blood given to the world for salvation is from the Virgin Mary. This is why we call her the cause of salvation (sign of salvation) in our teaching about the Virgin Mary.
 
 
This is why we say that the Virgin Mary is the origin and manifestation of the sacrament. Without having her, without believing that she is the Mother of God, without believing in her purity, her virginity, her glory and her intercession, it is not possible to receive Holy Communion, it is not possible to receive the sacrifice of the faithful.
