(Continued)☝️

As we leave the Liturgy, we carry with us the peace and grace of the heavenly realm, commissioned to be bearers of this divine mystery in the world.

Let us, therefore, approach this sacred mystery with awe and reverence, ever mindful of the holy covenant that binds us to our Creator and Redeemer. In the divine Liturgy, we find our true home, our spiritual sustenance, and our eternal calling.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
