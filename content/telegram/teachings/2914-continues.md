(Continues) ☝️

Let us then proceed with both humility and boldness, as we live out the high calling of our faith, ever mindful that in embracing the Christian way of life, we are indeed embodying the very essence of the Gospel: love enacted, faith embodied, and hope eternal.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
