Phos-φῶς (Light): A Survey of the Old and New Testaments
Phōs (Light) is a beginner’s guide to the Holy Bible written from the perspective of the Ethiopian Orthodox Täwahədo Church. It offers a comprehensive overview of both the Old and New Testaments, emphasizing the unity of Scripture and the divine light that illuminates those who truly know God and understand His Word.
The book is designed for Sunday school teachers, seminary students, campus ministry members, and young Täwahədo believers—especially those in the diaspora—who seek sound, English-language resources faithful to the teachings and traditions of the Mother Church.
