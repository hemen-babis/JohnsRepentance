8. Temples of the Holy Spirit an urgent exhortation

“Do you not know,” the apostle cries, “that you are God’s temple and that the Spirit of God dwells in you?” (1 Cor 3:16; cf. 6:19).  Therefore:

Keep the body pure from idolatry, fornication, impurity, and drunkenness.

Shun every work that would grieve the indwelling Comforter.

For if we defi le the temple, God will destroy the destroyer.

Let each of us examine himself.  Have we preserved these holy vessels?  Or have we, by vain and unclean deeds, driven away the Heavenly Guest?  If so, brethren, repent, that the grace and power of the Spirit may flood our hearts anew.

9. Final summons—He who has ears, let him hear!

The Holy Spirit still speaks: through councils, through Scripture, through the quiet testimony in every conscience.  Let us listen and obey, embracing the apostolic commandments abstaining from idols, blood, strangled meat, and fornication walking in that ancient path where the Holy Spirit rests.

may the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
