(Continued) 👆🏾

In conclusion, let it be known, to all seekers of divine truth, that the Holy Communion is not merely a symbolic gesture. It is a profound, living, and dynamic participation in the unique and eternal sacrifice of Christ. It is with this deep understanding that we, the faithful of the Orthodox Tewahedo Church, approach the Holy Eucharist with reverence, recognizing it for the divine sacrifice that it truly is.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
