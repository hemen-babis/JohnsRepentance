III. The Fruit of Apostolic Preaching: Holiness of Life

The apostles did not merely proclaim salvation they formed the Church in holiness. Those who received the Gospel were taught to walk in the likeness of Christ. Consider what they instilled in the baptized:

To grow in the likeness of saints, that the heart might reflect the image of the Savior

To flee paganism, cutting off the idols of the mind and hand

To guard the body the temple of the Holy Spirit from fornication, and to sanctify marriage in purity

To ordain righteous priests, faithful shepherds over the flock of Christ

To honor bishops, abbots, and priests, not as men, but as vessels of the divine mystery

To live by fasting and prayer, and engage in the spiritual struggle that purifies the soul

Such was the gospel preached—not just words, but a way of life. Not abstract theology, but Christ formed in the people, a living testimony of grace.

IV. The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church: A Living Apostolic Witness

As the apostles preached and labored, so too has our Holy Orthodox Tewahedo Church continued in their steps. For two thousand years, she has:

Proclaimed the word of Christ with unshaken boldness

Comforted those who were far from God

Baptized multitudes into the heavenly kingdom

Adorned the Church as the Bride of Christ, pure and radiant

Raised up faithful servants priests, scholars, and saints who have carried the apostolic light into every generation

She has not altered the gospel, nor adopted the customs of heresy, nor bent to the winds of modern corruption. Though some scoff, though others slander, saying “She does not preach Christ,” yet she preaches Christ indeed with her lips, her liturgy, her doctrine, and her martyrdom.

V. The Enemies Within and Without

Yet, beloved, a shadow has fallen in recent times. There are some who arise:

Criticizing the Church, not from love but rebellion

Seeking to change her worship, not by the Spirit, but by the dictates of flesh and blood

Desiring to hand the flock of God to the beast, by sowing lies and confusing the faithful

They are blinded by materialism, dazzled by the world’s treasures, and cry out in arrogance, “Renew the Church! Change Orthodoxy!” But in their cry is no love for truth, only weeds sown among wheat.

Let such ones beware. For the Church is the pillar and ground of the truth, not subject to innovation, not built on human invention, but rooted in the unchanging gospel of Christ crucified.

VI. What Does It Mean to Preach Christ?

To preach Christ is to reveal His character:

His humility and obedience

His compassion and longsuffering

His purpose in taking flesh not to rule by force, but to save by love

His divine will that none should perish, but all should come to repentance

Thus the Church preaches Christ not only in words, but in sacrament, in life, in tradition, and in holiness.

Conclusion: Remain Faithful to Christ Crucified

Let us, therefore, not be ashamed of the Cross, nor deceived by vain speech, nor swayed by worldly arguments. Let us proclaim with holy boldness:

 “We preach Christ crucified” the hope of the hopeless, the strength of the weak, the salvation of the lost, the glory of the Church.

Let us honor our Holy Tewahedo Church, remain within her maternal care, and walk in the ancient paths of the apostles and saints.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
