About the Holy family's return to the Holy land. 

Beloved members of John's Repentance spiritual service, today we celebrate the Holy family's return from Egypt to Israel. We call this feast 'Debre Kuskwam' or Mount of Kuskwam. Let us learn about this Holy day and partake from it's blessings. 

The Departure from Egypt: A Symbol of Spiritual Liberation

The journey of the Holy Family from Egypt to Israel mirrors the exodus of the Israelites from the land of bondage. Just as Moses led God's chosen people out of slavery into the Promised Land, so too did Joseph guide the Holy Family from the land of Egypt, symbolizing a departure from the spiritual bondage of sin and idolatry. This journey represents the Christian's path from the bondage of sin to the freedom of grace.

The Suffering and Endurance: A Lesson in Humility and Trust

The trials and tribulations faced by the Holy Family during their exile in Egypt and their journey back to Israel serve as a testament to their humility and unwavering trust in God's providence. Their hardships, akin to the tribulations faced by every Christian soul on its journey towards salvation, are a reminder of the suffering Christ Himself would endure for the redemption of mankind.

The Reception and Hospitality in Ethiopia: Embracing the Universal Church

The warm welcome and hospitality the Holy Family received in Ethiopia is emblematic of the universality of Christ's message. Ethiopia, often regarded as a symbol of the Gentile nations in early Christian literature, represents the outreach of the Church to all nations and peoples. The Holy Family's sojourn in Ethiopia and the blessings they bestowed upon the land prefigure the spread of Christianity and the blessings it brings to all corners of the earth.

The Return to Israel: Fulfillment of Divine Will

The return of the Holy Family to Israel signifies the fulfillment of God's will and the completion of a divine journey that began with the Incarnation. This return was not just a physical journey but a spiritual one, marking a full circle from prophecy to fulfillment, from the promise of the Messiah to His living presence among His chosen people.

The Crossing of the River: A Miraculous Testament

The miraculous crossing of the river, facilitated by the divine intervention of Christ, is reminiscent of the crossing of the Red Sea by the Israelites. It signifies the triumph of faith over seemingly insurmountable obstacles and the power of God to make a way where there seems to be no way.

Conclusion: The Everlasting Impact

The journey of the Holy Family from Egypt to Israel is not merely a historical event but a spiritual odyssey that encapsulates the essence of the Christian journey. Each step of this journey, marked by faith, hardship, divine intervention, and fulfillment of God's will, offers profound lessons for the faithful. It serves as a microcosm of the Church's pilgrimage through history, a journey from the shadows of sin into the glorious light of Christ's redemption.

In the manner of John Chrysostom, let us reflect deeply on these lessons, drawing from them strength and inspiration for our own spiritual journeys, continually seeking the grace and guidance of the Holy Family in our quest for salvation.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.
