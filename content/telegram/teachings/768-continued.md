(Continued)👇

In conclusion, the mind of Christ is a way of thinking that is oriented towards God and his will. It is a way of thinking that is characterized by humility, love, and selflessness. To cultivate the mind of Christ within us, we must be willing to embrace a life of prayer, fasting, and obedience to God's will. May we all strive to have the mind of Christ and to live lives that reflect His love and grace.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
