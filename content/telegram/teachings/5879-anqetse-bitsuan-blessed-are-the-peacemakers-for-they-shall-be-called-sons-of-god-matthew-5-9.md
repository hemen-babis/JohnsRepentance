Dear followers of the Orthodox teachings on John's Repentance website .we greet you with the peace of the Lord. Today, based on the topic mentioned above, we present to you the following important lesson. We encourage everyone to read it carefully and learn from it.

✍🏾Making Peace Among People

Our Lord God and Savior Jesus Christ established peace among people. In His time, He especially reconciled the Jews with the Gentiles, and also the Jews with the Samaritans.

✍🏾Rather than those who claimed to be children of the kingdom, Christ praised the faith of the Gentiles. When the centurion said, “Only say the word, and my servant will be healed,” the Lord responded, “Truly I tell you, I have not found such great faith, not even in Israel” (Matthew 8:10; Luke 7:9). In this way He revealed the depth of the Gentile’s faith and opened the preaching of the Gospel to all peoples.

✍🏾He preached the Gospel in Samaria and commanded His apostles to preach there and to the ends of the world
(Acts 1:8; Mark 16:15; Matthew 28:19).Even Herod and Pilate, who had been enemies, temporarily reconciled with one another during the judgment of Christ
(Luke 23:11–12).

✍🏾The Apostle Paul teaches, “At that time you were alienated from the commonwealth of Israel and strangers to the covenants of promise, having no hope and being without God in the world. But now in Christ Jesus, you who were once far off have been brought near by the blood of Christ… So then you are no longer strangers and foreigners, but fellow citizens with the saints and members of the household of God” (Ephesians 2:12–13, 19). Through this, he declares that the Gentiles who believe in the Gospel are made one with the faithful of Israel in Christ.

✍🏾Our Lord taught us to live in peace with all people. He said, “You have heard that it was said, ‘An eye for an eye and a tooth for a tooth.’ But I say to you, do not resist an evil person. Whoever slaps you on your right cheek, turn to him the other also. Whoever wants to sue you and take your cloak, give him your garment as well. If anyone compels you to go one mile, go with him two. Give to the one who asks you, and do not turn away from the one who wants to borrow from you.”He continued, “You have heard, ‘Love your neighbor and hate your enemy.’ But I say to you, love your enemies, bless those who curse you, do good to those who hate you, and pray for those who persecute you, so that you may be children of your Father in heaven. For He makes His sun rise on the evil and on the good, and sends rain on the righteous and the unrighteous.”(Matthew 5:38–45)
Through this, He teaches us that true peace requires sacrifice and a heart shaped by mercy.

✍🏾The Apostle Paul likewise says, “Live in harmony with one another. Do not be proud, but associate with the humble. Do not be wise in your own opinion. Do not repay anyone evil for evil. Consider what is good in the sight of all people. If it is possible, as much as depends on you, live at peace with everyone. Beloved, do not avenge yourselves, but leave room for the wrath of God; for it is written, ‘Vengeance is mine; I will repay,’ says the Lord. If your enemy is hungry, feed him; if he is thirsty, give him something to drink; for in doing this you will heap coals of fire on his head. Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.”
(Romans 12:16–21)

👉🏾He taught that peace must be pursued with all people, and he worked to reconcile believers such as Onesimus and Philemon, making them co-workers in Christ (Philippians 1:16–17).

✍🏾Saint Paul, the Lamp of the World, taught extensively about peace throughout his epistles. To the Corinthians he wrote, “God has called us to peace” (1 Corinthians 7:15), 

He exhorted the Romans, “Let us follow the things which make for peace and for the edification of one another” (Romans 14:19), urging believers to actively seek what builds up the community and strengthens unity.
