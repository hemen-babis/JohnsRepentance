(continued part)👆👇

In the morning, when Nebuchadnezzar looked from his balcony, he saw four of them walking in the fire. Yesterday we tied three men and threw them out of the fiery furnace, but today I see four of them released  and walking in the fire. And immediately he went out of the oven and said to them, come out. They ran out. Not even an ash was found on their clothes. Nebuchadnezzar saw this and said, "Praise be to God, the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, who sent his angel and saved the poor." 'King, do you think it's true? It was because of their magic' said the Babylonians. And the king said 'If by magic, save yourselves by your magic for there is none greater than you, and then added the Babylonians to the fire and burned them.

And the king returned the decree with a decree saying that there is no one who can save except the God of Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego. (Dan chapter 3)

👉🏾 In addition to the above, here are some of the main works of intercession and mediation that the angel Saint Gabriel is known for, which he performed by the grace given to him from the Creator:-

👉 1st/ He told the prophet Daniel in a vision about what was going to happen. 15:16)

👉 2. Also, in the vision that the prophet Daniel saw again, he said this about the angel, "While I was still praying, the angel I had seen in the vision before, Gabriel, came flying... He taught me and told me... Daniel, I have come now to give you wisdom and understanding" (Dan 9: 21-23)

👉 3rd / Saint Gabriel is the angel who saved Daniel from the lion's mouth.

👉 4th /  Saint Gabriel saved the three men named Ananya, Azaria, and Misael from the furnace fire. 

👉 5th/ He announced to the priest Zacharias and Saint Elizabeth that they would give birth to John the Baptist in their old age.

👉 6. He announced to the Virgin Mary that the physical word Jesus Christ, who is the cure of the world, would be born from the her.

👉 7th/ He saved  St. Jeletan and the child Kirkos from a boiling fire in an iron tank. 

👉 8th/ He led the wise men to the city of Bethlehem where the Lord was born, leading them with the sign of a star.

👉 9th/ Old Joseph was told in a dream to take his mother and baby and flee to Egypt.

👉 10/ In addition, Saint Gabriel announced the conception of Samson; He brought forth water for Agar in the desert. He who interpreted the mystery to Daniel; He is an angel appointed over serpents and scorpions.

In general, we find this angel's intercession and guardianship in many readings of the holy books; God has helped us to write to you on the occasion of the monthly holiday that we celebrate today, March 28th, so that we may all receive the intercession and blessings of the angel Saint Gabriel. 

Therefore, if the Lord God had not sent his angels to protect us night and morning, day and night, on the road, when we sleep at home and when we get up, Satan's army would have prevent us from having life in this world and the next .

God's prophet, Saint David, said, "No calamity will enter your house. He will raise up angels for you to guard you on your way. They will lift you up with their hands so that your feet will not stumble over a stone..." Telling us about the guardianship and protection of angels.

In another paragraph, he said, "God's angel will live around those who fear him, and will save them." He said that angels will save people who believe in the guardianship and intercession of angels, and that they will save us from physical and spiritual suffering. (Ps 33:7, 90/91:10')

Since angels are appointed to have authority over all creatures, the prophet Enoch told us about the power of intercessor and guardianship of Saint Gabriel saying that he is one of the noble angels appointed over the serpents, the righteous in heaven, and the cherubim. (Enoch 6:7)

May the prayers and supplications of this great angel of light rest upon us
