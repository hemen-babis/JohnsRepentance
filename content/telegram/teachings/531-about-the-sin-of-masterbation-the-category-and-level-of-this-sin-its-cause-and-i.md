About the #Sin of #Masterbation

The category and level of this sin; its Cause; and its Impact and the Solution

Category and level of Masterbation sin. 

Masturbation is one of the highest level of sin that is outside the law and order of nature. This type of sin, which Satan has devised as a new philosophy of fornication, is called Masterbation. It is a shameful act of sex with oneself in which a man spills his seed hiding behind a closed door and burning with adultery desire, for he failed to control his tempted emotion for a second. The main method by which Satan makes people easily subject to this sin is through a secret way of individiual training so that men and women assured themselves as if they did not committed adultery as long as they didn't sleep with or get near to anyone. However, this is even graver failure than those who commit adultery between a man and a woman because it is out of natural law and order. In fact, the idea of homosexuality starts from this and it is connected to the spirit of homosexuality. It can be understood from the stories in the Bible that it is a type of sin that will destroy the world if people refuse to repent by cooprating in this sin.

In this age, it is a type of sin that children and adults are suffering from, and many of them have been trained secretly by Satan since their childhood. What's worse is that the sin is private and hidden, and apart from being hard to control, it is difficult to get rid of the habit that one has been accustomed to since childhood. Many people knowingly or unknowingly started it as easy and simple, but it becomes hard to talk openly about their problem or to repent ; as they feel ashamed; so most did not find a solution.  But when we say all these, we are not shaming or pressing their wounds, but it is intended mainly to give lesson to show that it is the path of destruction that Satan has opened for people. So that they need to decide to get rid of this sin once and for all and repent.

On the other hand, for those who have not committed this sin, it is said in the Holy Book to "flee from fornication" (1 Cor 6:18), so that God may protect them from this evil and they should understand the severity of the sin of fornication in advance and refrain from this danger. Therefore, it is necessary for you to understand that , the reason  we talk about this subject again and again is that the youth of our time are lost due to lack of mentors and teachers. As it is said, "My people are lost for lack of knowledge" (Hosea 4:6).

In general, it is important to understand that the main function of adultery is to replace God's law with the devil's law, which means replacing our eternal life with eternal death. That is why our enemy, Satan the devil, is using this type of adultery as his main weapon to separate us from the Creator and is fighting to bring all mankind to the same path of destruction. When everyone joins in sin, the destruction it brings not only to the individual, but also to the country and the earth. As you know, the main cause of the plagues that happened to the people of Noah's day was the sin of adultery.
