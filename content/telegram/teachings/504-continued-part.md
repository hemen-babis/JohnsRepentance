Therefore, no matter how weak a husband is, you should not despise him. If you want to correct his weakness, as St. Peter advised us, "Wives, if some of your husbands are disobedient to teaching, submit to them so that they may find instruction in the lives of their wives, watching your chaste lives with fear" (1 Peter 3:1).

The fathers say, "May God throw all the anger, the quarrels, the sickness, the spirit of hatred into the sea and into the cliff." We should learn from the Bible, from history and from other people we know or heared that there is no  justification for a fight, no justification for divorce. In general, marriage is martyrdom, and our ancestors achieved martyrdom through many hardships, but our martyrdom is mainly to live patiently with the marriage we started.

Therefore, our dear member, we have briefly explained the spirituality of marriage above in this lesson, we believe that if you understand this properly, you will have learned the wisdom of caring instead of fighting, being one instead of being separated, loving instead of revenge and resentment, loving instead of hating, being justified instead of being lost.
