(Continued) 👆

Thus, those who trust in the guardianship and intercession of the angels will be saved from both physical and spiritual harm. These radiant beings, placed in authority over all creation, have the power of intercession and protection, as revealed by the Prophet Enoch (Enoch 6:7).

In our era, we often face trials and tribulations akin to those of Saints Kirkus and Yelutan. Let us, therefore, implore the intercession and mediation of the great Archangel Gabriel, praying that he may reconcile us with our Creator.

May the prayers and intercession of this great Angel of Light be upon us all.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.
