👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾#Anqetse #Bitsuan (“Blessed are those who #hunger and #thirst for #righteousness, for they shall be filled.”)
#Matthew 5:6; #Luke 6:21

(Part 18)

Dear beloved in Christ, followers of the Orthodox faith, we greet you in the name of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. Continuing our lesson on the holy Beatitude, “Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they shall be filled,” we learn that the true satisfaction of the soul comes only from God, who rewards those who seek Him with faith and sincerity.

✍🏾Saint Simeon the Righteous

The Holy Evangelist Luke tells of a righteous and devout man in Jerusalem named Simeon, who was waiting for the consolation of Israel. The Holy Spirit had revealed to him that he would not die before seeing the Lord’s Anointed.

Moved by the Spirit, he entered the temple. When the holy parents brought the Child Jesus to fulfill the requirements of the Law, Simeon took Him into his arms and blessed God, saying:

“Lord, now You are letting Your servant depart in peace, according to Your word;for my eyes have seen Your salvation,which You have prepared before all peoples a light for revelation to the Gentiles,and the glory of Your people Israel.” (Luke 2:29–32)

Joseph and the Virgin Mary were amazed at what was spoken about Him. Simeon also blessed them and said to Mary:

 "This Child is appointed for the fall and rising of many in Israel,
and for a sign to be opposed,
so that the thoughts of many hearts may be revealed.
And a sword shall pierce your own soul also.” (Luke 2:34–35)

Simeon stands as an example of patience, devotion, and the reward granted to those who wait faithfully upon God.

✍🏾The Prophetess Anna

The Prophetess Anna was a holy woman who dedicated her entire life to righteousness through fasting, prayer, and unwavering devotion. Because of her faithfulness, she was counted worthy to encounter Our Lord Jesus Christ in the temple and to give thanks to God at His appearing.

The Holy Evangelist Saint Luke writes:

“There was a prophetess, Anna, the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Asher.
She had lived with her husband seven years from her virginity.
She was a widow eighty-four years old,
who did not depart from the temple,
but worshiped with fasting and prayer night and day.”
(Luke 2:36–37)

At the very hour when the Child Jesus was brought into the temple, Anna came forward, gave thanks to God, and proclaimed Him to all who were waiting for the redemption of Jerusalem (Luke 2:38).

Through her perseverance, purity, and devotion, she became an example for all holy women and for every believer who seeks righteousness before God.

✍🏾Cornelius the Righteous

Another example of true righteousness is Cornelius the Centurion, whose life teaches modern Christians how to walk in faith, charity, and reverence.

Scripture describes him as:

a devout man,

one who feared God with all his household,

generous in almsgiving,

constant in prayer.

Saint Luke narrates:

“About the ninth hour of the day, he clearly saw in a vision an angel of God coming to him and saying, ‘Cornelius.’
He stared at him in fear and said, ‘What is it, Lord?’
The angel said, ‘Your prayers and your alms have ascended as a memorial before God.
Now send men to Joppa and summon Simon, who is called Peter.’”
(Acts 10:1–5)

When Cornelius’ messengers reached Peter, they described him as:

“A righteous man who fears God,
respected by the whole nation of the Jews,
instructed by a holy angel to send for you
and to hear a message from you.”
(Acts 10:22)

When Peter entered Cornelius’ house, Cornelius fell at his feet in reverence (Acts 10:25), showing his deep respect for God’s servant and the word of salvation.

Cornelius stands as a model of prayer, humility, generosity, and obedience to God. His story shows that righteousness is lived through action, devotion, and a heart open to the will of the Lord.
