Continued ☝️

Through this faithful stewardship, we not only preserve the treasures of our faith but also enrich the spiritual lives of those who will follow in the paths we have trodden.

Thus, through the diligent observance and transmission of tradition, the Church continues to thrive as a beacon of truth and grace in the world, guiding the faithful through the teachings of the past while illuminating the path to eternal salvation.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
