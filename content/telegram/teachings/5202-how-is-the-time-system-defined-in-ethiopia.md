Beloved in Christ, peace be multiplied unto you. Today we shall open our minds to behold the wisdom of God, as it is revealed through the order of time in our Holy Church. For just as the heavens declare the glory of God, so too the reckoning of days and seasons in Ethiopia bears witness to the divine harmony that governs the universe. Let us, then, walk carefully through the mystery of the appointed fasts and feasts, that we may discern the providence of God working through them.

The Fast of Nineveh (Tsome Nenewe)

In the year 2018, the remembrance of Nineveh’s fast began on the 18th of Metikeʾ. This fast, unlike others, has no Tewsak (fixed cycle), but begins with the Mebaja Hamer, the proclamation day. Thus, when Metikeʾ falls in Meskerem, the fast of Nineveh(tsome nenewe) is set in the month of Tir 25. 

The Great Fast (Abiy Tsom)

To discover the beginning of the Great Fast, one counts the days from Nineveh’s fast( Tsome nenewe) until abiy tsom. In 2018, this count was fourteen. Thus, the Tewsak of Abiy Tsom is 14, 

The beginning of (Abiy tsom) on 2018 = 2018 Mebaja Hamer + abiy tsom tewsak Mebaja hamer 

abiy tsom tewsak =14

so the beginning of the great fast(abiy tsom) is on monday Yekatit 9

Debre Zeyt

To discover the beginning of the Debre zeyt , one counts the days from "Nenewe" fast until abiy tsom is 41 days then ,dividing  this number by 30 then.The number of days for Debre Zeit was reckoned at 11. Thus:

Debre Zeyt= Mebaja Hamer + Debre Zeyt Tewsak  

25+11=36

36 - 30= 6

Therefore, the feast of Debre Zeit was kept on Megabit 6, which fell upon a Sunday. 

Hosae'na

From Nineveh’s fast(tsome nenewe) to Hosae'na, there are 62 days. Dividing, we find a remainder of 2.

Hosaena= 2018 Mebaja Hamer + Hosaena Tewsak

25 + 2 = 27 → Megabit 27

Thus 2018 Hosanna celebrate on Sunday, Megabit 27. 

(Siklet)

From Nineveh’s fast( tsome nenewe) to the day of siklet are 67 days. Dividing by 30, the remainder is 7.

Siklet = 2018 Mebaja Hamer + siklet of tewsak

25 + 7 = 32 → remainder 2 → Miyazya 2

Thus the Siklet, the remembrance of our Lord’s Passion, fell on Friday, Miyazya 2.

Thus the clergy’s procession was kept on Wednesday, Miyazya 28.

From "Tsome nenewe " to the Apostles’ fast are 119 days. then Dividing by 30, the remainder is 29.
