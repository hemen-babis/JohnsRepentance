An explanation of #depression, the challenge of #loneliness and perfect #peace and #light

(continued part)👆👇

Our dear member told us that their mind is going to explode. It is God who gives us peace of mind. 
The mind wants shelter, it wants peace but the conscience blames it. For example, a person knows that stealing is bad before learning to steal, knows that killing is bad before learning to kill. Our consciousness tells us what is good and bad because that is the way it was created; Man is a creature created to distinguish good from evil, evil from good, and what is useful from what is not. 

Therefore, we must all seek life, and if we are willing to find life, we can repent of our sins and find God; God is near, God is love. "You return and I will return," God is not a cruel father who tells you to stay where you left. What He told us is  those of you who have fallen come back to me, . God is a compassionate and merciful father.
 
First He created us in His image, we humans failed to live, we fell, we were the kings of creation but because of our sin we fell from our glory; He came back in search of us, born of the Holy Virgin Mary as a man. After that, he paid many prices for us, to bring back our exiled ones, he was exiled for us, then He was whipped with 6666 lashes, crucified in the square of Calvary, to bring us back what we lost and even more. He gave us his holy body and his precious blood. Eat my flesh, drink my blood, that he may bring us back with food that we have fallen by eating, there is no greater life than this.

Even after He has conquered death,  He told us that  if you fall into sin, even if you make a mistake, repent and come to Me. Therefore, what is needed to live with God is to persevere in his house until the end, it is necessary to continuously take part of his holy body and his precious blood, the Eucharist. 
 
As we have said so far, life means living with God, and what is needed to live with God is to first repent. Therefore, may God help us all who have fallen into sin to repent, stay in the house until the end of our lives, receive His holy body and his precious blood, and live with God in his house.
 
Praise be to God, the Virgin Mother and the cross.
