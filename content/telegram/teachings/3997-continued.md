The Call to Holiness

Though we cannot attain the holiness of character that belongs to God alone, we are invited to partake in the holiness of grace. This is the sanctification granted to us through faith, obedience, and participation in the life of the Church.

Let us, therefore, seek holiness—not as a mere moral obligation, but as the path to union with God. Let us purify our hearts, walk in righteousness, and embrace the grace offered to us, that we may be counted among those chosen to dwell eternally in His presence


May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
