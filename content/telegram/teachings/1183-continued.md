(Continued) 👆🏾

Let us turn our attention to the remarkable story of Saint Sosna—a woman falsely accused of adultery and sentenced to death by stoning. In the face of imminent peril, she fearlessly declared, "It is better for me to be sentenced to death than to be punished by God for committing this sin." Her steadfastness and unwavering faith invoked divine intervention, as God sent Daniel and His archangel, Saint Michael, to save her from her accusers.

In closing, our beloved members, we beseech you to cast off the garment of fear that impedes your journey towards God. Embrace the call to wholehearted devotion and unwavering trust. Remember that fear cannot conquer the human soul, for the soul's ultimate fate rests solely in the hands of the Divine.

Let your faith be a testament to the power and love of our Lord, Jesus Christ. For it is He who proclaims, "Be of good cheer, I have overcome the world" (John 16:33). In this knowledge, we find the strength to conquer fear, to embrace our divine calling, and to inherit the eternal blessings that await us.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
