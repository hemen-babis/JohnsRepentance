1. Meaning of the Divine Liturgy

The Divine Liturgy is a communal prayer offered in unity, according to the order of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church. It is the highest and most complete form of prayer, because in it the faithful unite with heaven, the angels, and the saints.

During the Divine Liturgy, five sacrifices are offered. Every Christian who participates offers these sacrifices to God.

The Five Sacrifices Offered During the Liturgy

1. The Eucharistic Sacrifice

During the Divine Liturgy, the bread is transformed into the Body of Christ, and the wine is transformed into His Blood by the power of the Holy Spirit.
This holy sacrifice is offered only during the Liturgy. Outside the Liturgy, there is no Eucharistic sacrifice.

The holy sacrifice prepared in Bethlehem and is offered in the church, which represents Calvary. Our Lord was born in Bethlehem, where the sacrifice was prepared, and through His crucifixion on Calvary the sacrifice was completed for the salvation of the whole world. Therefore, the holy sacrifice is continually offered to the faithful in the church, which is spiritually called Calvary.

2. The Sacrifice of the Lips

This is the sacrifice of thanksgiving, praise, and prayer, offered by the priest, the deacon, and the people together.
As Scripture says, it is the fruit of our lips, offered in unity to our Creator.

3. The Sacrifice of the Lamp

The lamps lit during the Liturgy are an offering to God.

They symbolize Our Lord Jesus Christ, who came as the Light of the world, illuminating those who sit in darkness.

4. The Sacrifice of Incense

Incense is offered only by the priest. He incenses the altar, the sanctuary, and the congregation.

This symbolizes the prayers of the faithful ascending to the throne of the Holy Trinity.

Just as the angels in heaven offer incense before God while proclaiming, “Holy, Holy, Holy,” so the priest on earth represents the heavenly ministry and offers incense to God.

5. The Bodily Sacrifice

During the Liturgy, the faithful stand for most of the service.

At specific moments, they bow down and humble themselves before God.

Standing, bowing, and physical reverence are all forms of bodily sacrifice, offered in obedience and humility to the Creator.

✍🏾 The Three Main Parts of the Divine Liturgy

The Divine Liturgy is divided into three major sections:

1.The Preparation

2.The Reading and Teaching

3.fre kidase (main holy liturgy )

The Holy Liturgy here vary according to the feast day, and there are fourteen in total. These are:

1.The Holy Liturgy

2.Kidase of the Apostles

3.Kidase of Our Lord

4.Kidase of the Virgin Mary

5.Kidase of Yohannes Welde Negodgwad

6.Kidase of the Three Saints

7.Kidase of Saint Athanasius

8.Kidase of Saint Basil

9.Kidase of Saint Gregory

10.Kidase of Saint Epiphanius

11.Kidase of Saint John Afewerk

12.Kidase of Saint Cyril (Zekerlos / Qerellos)

13.Kidase of Saint Jacob the Apostle

14.Kidase of Saint Dioscorus

Each holy liturgy is chosen according to the feast, season, or saint commemorated.

👉🏾 If it is the feast of Our Lord, the Holy Liturgy is Kidase Egzi

👉🏾 During the Advent of Our Lord and if pagume is on sunday, the Holy Liturgy of Saint Athanasius is celebrated.

👉🏾If it is the feast of Our Lady, the Holy Liturgy of the Virgin Mary is celebrated
(Gosa Labye Qale Senaye).

👉🏾 If it is the feast of the angels or the apostles, the Holy Liturgy of Saint Jacob of Serugh (Jacob Zesrug) is celebrated.

👉🏾 From the Feast of the Nativity until the Feast of the Circumcision, the Holy Liturgy of Saint Gregory the Theologian is celebrated.
