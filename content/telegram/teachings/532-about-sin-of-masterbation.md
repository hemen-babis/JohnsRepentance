The main causes of Masterbation

The following are considered to be the root cause of homosexuality and masturbation. 

1. Natural feeling and free will

Of course, since it is natural to be attracted to the other sexe, it is impossible to say why those feelings arose. But since it is possible to sanctify the emotions, it is possible to sanctify them by running away from evil and doing good. If the horse is headed for a cliff, the rider does not only tighten the bridle; He guides the horse in the right way. David says, "And do good" (Ps 33:14), avoiding evil and doing good are connected to each other. Therefore, when such feelings come to us, apart from avoiding them, it is also important to do spiritual work. Among these are fasting, prayer, prostration, reading spiritual books, prostrating, listening to hymns, listening to sermons, attending congregations, consecrating mass; Receiving spiritual advice while calling the repentant father. When we strive to do this, the spirit burns away from us because it cannot stay with us.

2. The battle of the evil spirit

We must always understand that the root cause of evil spirits is our weak thoughts, attitudes, or free will and desires. If Satan finds the smallest hole of will, he will lick it wide like a snake. And he will take us away from our God. Satan will drive us to the edge of sin if we don't think about it. He praises us until we fall, but after we fall, He praises us more than He praised us before, or He hardens our hearts and appoints us as the next role of the executor. The solution to this is to fall and get up, fight somehow and come back with repentance.

3. By not distancing yourself from sin

We tempt ourselves by not keeping ourselves away from sin. So, Stop talking about sensual nonsense in person or on the phone, Stop watching pornography, Stop watching and reading social media related to fornication, Stop watching erotic music videos and erotic images posted on social media for business use; also avoid bad advice and being influence by friends or others.
