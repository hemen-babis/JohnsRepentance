IV. The Breaking of Bread and the Burning of Hearts

As they neared Emmaus, the Lord made as if to go further. But they, stirred by the burning in their hearts, begged Him to stay. “Remain with us, for the day is far spent.” O blessed hospitality! O hearts yearning for more than earthly company! He who had once said, “Abide in Me,” now entered their home and sat at their table.

And then O wonder! when He took bread, blessed it, and broke it, their eyes were opened. Not by His face, not by His voice, but by the breaking of bread did they know Him. For it is in the Divine Mystery, in the Holy Eucharist, that the Lord makes Himself known to His people. Then He vanished, not because He had left them, but because their faith had now been made sight.

V. Return to Jerusalem

They said to one another, “Were not our hearts burning within us while He talked with us on the road?” Immediately, though the hour was late, though the road was long, they arose and returned to Jerusalem. For once the truth is known, delay is a sin. They went to join the apostles and the Church, to share the good news and become partakers in the joy of the Resurrection.

VI. Lessons for Us Today

Let us now draw out the lessons, as a master draws water from a deep well:

Think on God Always: Whether in sorrow or joy, in labor or leisure, let your minds be fixed on God. Let us turn our thoughts from gossip and vanity to the remembrance of Christ.

Confess Your Faith Boldly: Do not hide your faith. Do not hesitate to speak of Christ in your homes, your workplaces, and among strangers. Like the Emmaus travelers, testify to His truth without fear.

Let Your Heart Burn with the Word: Open the Scriptures daily. Sit under the teachings of the Church. Let the rebuke of Christ warm your heart and correct your thoughts.

Welcome the Lord into Your Home: Invite Him to abide with you, especially in the evening hours, when temptation and weariness draw near. Pray, read, and meditate, that He may break the bread of life before your eyes.

Return to the Church: The disciples left Jerusalem in despair but returned in faith. If you have wandered far from the Church, come back. Let the love of God burn away your doubts and the joy of the Gospel lead you home.

Conclusion

Beloved children of the Resurrection, the journey to Emmaus is not far from you. It is the path of your own life. Christ walks beside you, even when you do not recognize Him. He teaches you through Scripture, reveals Himself in the breaking of bread, and calls you back to Jerusalem the place of peace, the heart of the Church.

May our hearts burn within us as we walk with Him, may our eyes be opened in the Eucharist, and may we run with haste to the holy fellowship of the saints.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
