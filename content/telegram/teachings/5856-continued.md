3. Fasting and Spiritual Protection

During this holy fast, the prophets and apostles fasted and received blessings from God. The Holy Fathers of the Church established this fast for us, so that we too may share in their blessings.

We are called to fast and pray with determination, calling upon:

The God of the prophets

The God of the apostles

The God of the righteous ones and martyrs

We ask Him, through the intercessions of our Lady, the Holy Virgin Mary, and all the saints, to bless us, protect us, and grant us His heavenly Kingdom.

Part 8 Continues ...

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
