8. The Ladder of Jacob

She is the golden ladder Jacob saw, set between earth and heaven, with the King enthroned above it and angels ascending and descending (Genesis 28:12). Through her, the Eternal descended into time, and through her, mankind is raised to heaven.

9. The Pure Chalice and the Living Temple

She is the chalice from which the world drinks the glory of the Trinity, the temple more glorious than any built by human hands. As Ephrem says, her glory surpasses even Solomon’s temple, for she is not adorned with gold but with the indwelling of the living God Himself.

10. Her Intercession

Again and again, the Fathers plead: “Pray for us, O Lady, that the grace and glory of your Son may not be taken away from us.” For if the Lord was pleased to dwell in her, how much more will He hear the prayers she offers for His people?

Conclusion

Therefore, beloved, on this Saturday, let our praise of Mary not be with lips alone but with purified hearts. She is the ladder, the temple, the tabernacle, the heaven, and the mother yet in all these titles she points not to herself, but to the Son she bore. To honor her is to glorify Him.

Let us then cry with the angels and the saints:

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
