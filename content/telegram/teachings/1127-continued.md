(Continued) 👆

The son of Tolik named Saminas, who was impressed by Ajgu, went from Edok to his uncle's house and passed away. While Hana was walking around holding the pillow of the bed, according to the custom of their country, Saminas quickly got up and said to her, 'Say to the God who created the heavens and the earth, and peace be with you, Hana.' The Jews who were there were frightened and asked, "What did you see, Saminas? What are you saying?"

He said, 'I heard all the angels praising her, saying that the child born from Hannah's womb will give birth to the God who created the heavens and the earth. And she is the one who raised me." Instead of thanking God, when they heard this, they were filled with jealousy and malice. They thought that if Hana saved the fetus in her womb, she would use it against them. Therefore, they urged to stone Hana and Yaakim to death before she gave birth.


At that time, the great archangel Gabriel appeared to Joachim and told him to take Hannah to a mountain called Lebanon. When he first revealed her to the angels on his wings, it was to give her the right of guardianship, as he said through the emissary who was entrusted to protect her from the time of mercy.

Because she is not of this world, she left her home accompanied by her spiritual relatives and angels so that the people who were in the house would not enjoy worldly pleasures because of her birth. As the mother of the Lord who was seeking Adam, who had left his home and lost God, she left the house and traveled to Mount Lebanon, a great place where her ancestors and prophets had seen her in a vision. For she is the Bride of Lebanon who was prophesied to be born in a den of lions under the shade of the mountains of Lebanon.

The wise King Solomon was visited by God and revealed to him that our Lady would be born on Mount Lebanon: "Come with me from Lebanon, my spouse, With me from Lebanon. Look from the top of Amana, From the top of Senir and Hermon, From the lions’ dens, From the mountains of the leopards." (Song of Solomon 4:8). And so, the Holy Virgin Mary was born on Mount Lebanon (Mah 4:10).

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
