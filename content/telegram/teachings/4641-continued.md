IV. Canon and Custom: Wisdom in Simplicity

The Church, in her wisdom, does not bind the faithful to golden boxes and acacia wood alone. For it is written, “God saw all that He had made, and behold, it was very good” (Gen. 1:31). Therefore, the Ark of the New Covenant may be fashioned from other materials, depending on the ability of the local church.

If gold is available, it may be overlaid.

If acacia wood is not present, another wood may be used.

If precious stones or marble can be carved for the tablets, let it be done; if not, wood suffices.

If two tablets cannot be placed in one Ark, let one be used.

And if even the Ark cannot be made, the tablets themselves may be reverently used as the Ark. This is not a compromise, but a canon a sacred provision given by the Church.

V. Flesh and Blood: The Mercy Seat of the New Covenant

“He who eats My flesh and drinks My blood has eternal life” (John 6:54). This is not poetry. This is not metaphor. It is the very foundation of the New Covenant. The altar where His Precious Blood is poured is the Ark of Mercy. The priest who approaches it must do so with trembling, and the faithful must receive with awe.

Yet know this also: he who walks in righteousness shall be hated. Not only the wicked are criticized even the good are slandered. But let him not shrink back. As it is said, “A man who does not speak his heart is like one who is ill.” Speak the truth. Bear the reproach of righteousness. Guard the Ark.

VI. The Ethiopian Church: Faithful to Moses and Christ

Let none dare say the Ethiopian Church pits Moses against Christ. She holds both in honor.

In Moses and in Christ,

In the Old and the New,

In parable and in faith,


She explains the unity of the covenant to all who inquire. As the History of the Ethiopian Church (p. 93) attests, she is not ignorant nor naive. She is ancient, apostolic, and full of wisdom.

And so, when we ask, “Why do we call the altar where His Precious Blood is poured an Ark?” the answer is simple: because it is. It is the throne of the Lamb. It is the new mercy seat. It is the holy place where God meets man.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
