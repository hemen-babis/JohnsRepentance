III. Jacob’s Ladder

Finally, let us ascend with Jacob’s vision. He saw a ladder reaching from earth to heaven, upon which angels ascended and descended, and the Lord stood above it (Gen. 28:12–13).

This ladder is the Virgin Mary, by whom heaven was united with earth.

The stone upon which Jacob rested signifies the foundation of the prophets, who foretold her.

The ladder itself reveals the Incarnation: the descent of God to man, the ascent of man to God.

The King upon the ladder is Christ, who reigned nine months in her womb as upon a throne, and who reigns eternally in the heavens without end.

The angels ascending and descending proclaim the mystery: that by her, the Word took flesh; 

On the other hand, the angels ascending and descending upon the ladder clearly reveal that the human flesh, once humbled from its original glory and weakened in nature, was exalted and glorified by the Word of God, and is now united with Him in the one divine nature of the Godhead.

Thus Mary is revealed as the “Mother of God,” the golden ladder joining heaven and earth, the living gate through which the King of glory entered.

Conclusion

Brethren, behold the wonder: from Genesis to Revelation, in prophecy, in parable, in vision, the Virgin is ever present. She is the Woman who conquers the serpent, the Ark that saves from wrath, the Ladder uniting heaven and earth.

Therefore let us not neglect so great a gift, but with reverence and love seek refuge in her intercessions. For through her, the Word became flesh; through her, the curse was lifted; through her, we are invited to life eternal.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
