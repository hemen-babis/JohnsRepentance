(Continued) 👆🏾

As we venerate this sacred mystery of our redemption, let us live out the implications of the Incarnation in our lives. Let our worship be incarnational, touching the very flesh of our existence, transforming our actions, suffusing our relationships, and transfiguring the cosmos itself with the light of Christ.

And thus, may we walk in the way of the Lord, adorned with the virtues, anointed with the chrism of the Spirit, until we come to the full stature of Christ, to dwell in the New Jerusalem, where tears are wiped away, where death is no more, and where the Lamb is the light thereof. Amen.

Let us, therefore, celebrate this feast with joy and with a spirit of profound gratitude, as we marvel at the love of God made manifest in Christ Jesus. For in Him, we have redemption through His blood, the forgiveness of our trespasses, according to the riches of His grace which He lavished upon us.

Let us pray that the light of Christ, the Incarnate Word, may ever shine in our hearts, guiding us to the eternal embrace of the Father, in the fellowship of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
