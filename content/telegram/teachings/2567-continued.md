(Continued)☝️

Thus, as we traverse the solemn path of Lent, let our gaze be fixed not only on the tribulations of the present but on the promise of the new Jerusalem, descending from heaven, adorned as a bride for her husband. In this divine vision lies our hope, our inspiration, and our ultimate destination.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
