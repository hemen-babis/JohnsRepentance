Conclusion: Begin in Prayer, End in Glory

Therefore, beloved, let us begin every day in prayer. Let our first breath be a whisper of praise. Let our waking thoughts ascend to the throne of mercy. For the man who prays in the morning walks with God throughout the day.

Glorify Him in all things. In pain, glorify Him. In joy, glorify Him. And whether the path is smooth or rugged, give thanks, for He is your Shepherd, your Physician, and your Father.

Let us rejoice, then, with the angels. Let us lift up our eyes with the Virgin Mother, and embrace the cross with boldness, knowing that by it, we are made partakers of divine glory.

may the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
