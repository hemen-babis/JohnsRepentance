About Holy Eucharist and  and about our Lady Virgin Mary
 
(Part 2)

The words of Christ himself, "If ye eat not the flesh of the Son of man," confirm us that he was revealed as a perfect man. This is a word that states that the Son of God is called the Son of Man, because he was born of the Virgin Mary, who is of the human race.
 
For this reason, Our Lady was praised by our fathers as the source of the sacrament. Among them, Father Hriakos said in the liturgical prayer of St. Mary, "O Mary, you have brought us the true food and the true drink, so we glorify you and praise you" (v.9); We also call our Lady our mother, our intercessor, the reason for our salvation, etc., because she gave birth to Jesus Christ, who gives us eternal life, who gave us the true food of body and soul. As our Lord Jesus Christ also told us that, "This is that bread which came down from heaven: not as your fathers did eat bread, and are dead: he that eateth of this bread shall live for ever." (John 6:52 and 58)
 
Therefore, we Christians believe in this way, we honor the source of the Lamb, the cause of our salvation, in all its glory, and we say to the mother of our Savior, 'Rejoice, O Virgin full of grace'. As the Virgin Mary said, " for, behold, from henceforth all generations shall call me blessed." (Luke 1:48), we Orthodox Christians thank her, and all our generations to come will thank her.
 
Additional point -  Before incarnation, there were two natures and two persons. Divinity and Humanity. The Divine nature, God the Son, is one nature with the Father and the Holy Spirit. He is also one of the three persons of God. He is present everywhere, is eternal, knows everything, and can do everything. On the other aspect was Humanity, the Human nature. Humanity is a combination of Flesh/body (Soil, Air, Fire, Water) and rational Soul (Think, Speak, Immortal). The human nature has limited knowledge and power. We believe that God the Son perfectly united with body and soul from St Virgin Mary. A prominent church scholar, St Cyril (Kyrillos) explained this biblical fact saying “starting from incarnation, there is only one person Jesus Christ. Whatever belongs to Divinity, is an attribute of His humanity and whatever belongs to humanity is an attribute of His Divinity.”
 
Based on Orthodox teachings, incarnation is from two Persons to one Person; and from two natures to one united nature. From two Persons to one Person means Divine Person and Human united in to one hypostasis, Jesus Christ – He is both perfectly Divine and perfectly Human. Similarly, from two natures to one nature means Divine nature and Human nature perfectly united in to one united nature. We believe that this incarnation is without alteration/change, without reversion, without mingling/mixing, without dwelling, without superimposing, without separation, and without detachment.
 
May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
