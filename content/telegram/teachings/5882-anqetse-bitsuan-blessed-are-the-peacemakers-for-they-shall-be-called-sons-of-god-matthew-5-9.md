Dear followers of the Orthodox teachings on John's Repentance website .we greet you with the peace of the Lord. Today, based on the topic mentioned above, we present to you the following important lesson. We encourage everyone to read it carefully and learn from it.

✍🏾Making Peace Between the Holy Scriptures and Their Interpretation

👉🏾Saint Cyril of Alexandria teaches that the words of our Lord, “Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called children of God,” also apply to those who bring peace within the understanding of the Holy Scriptures. A peacemaker is not only someone who reconciles people, but also someone who brings harmony to the interpretation of God’s word.

✍🏾Those who labor to unite the teachings of the Law and the Prophets, the Old Testament and the New Testament, the four Holy Gospels, and the Epistles are blessed. These scholars act as mediators, revealing the mystery that binds all the Scriptures together. By showing how each part completes the other, they build peace within the Church’s understanding and guard the unity of faith.

✍🏾The holy fathers who gathered at the Councils of Nicaea, Constantinople, and Ephesus are also true peacemakers. They reconciled differing interpretations, defended the Orthodox faith, and guided the Church to a unified confession. Through their spiritual labor, they protected the faithful from division and preserved the right understanding of the Scriptures.

✍🏾Blessed also are those who bring together believers who differ in reading or emphasis, helping them discover the deeper unity found in the mystery of the Scriptures. By guiding them toward agreement on what is shared and charity in what differs, they allow the Church to live in harmony.

They are called children of God because, in their work, they imitate the life of the Son of God, Jesus Christ, who reconciled all things to Himself and brought peace through truth.

✍🏾For They Are Called Children of God

To be called a child of God is a great privilege. Just as a son is an heir, so too are the children of God heirs of His promises (Romans 8:17).

👉🏾The Evangelist Saint John says:
"But as many as received Him, to them He gave power to become children of God, even to those who believe in His name; who were born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God" (John 1:12-13).

Thus, the children of God are those who believe in His name and are born of God. These children of God love one another, for Saint John continues:
"Everyone who believes that Jesus is the Christ is born of God; and everyone who loves the Father loves Him who is born of Him. By this we know that we love the children of God: when we love God and keep His commandments" (1 John 5:1-2).

✍🏾The promise that God has given to His children is that they will be set free from the bondage of corruption and will attain the glorious freedom of the children of God (Romans 8:21).

✍🏾The children of God are those who are led by the Holy Spirit. They are not afraid of suffering for Christ’s sake, and they are heirs of glory. Saint Paul explains in his letter to the Romans:

👉🏾"For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, these are the children of God. For you have received the Spirit of adoption as sons, whereby we cry, ‘Abba, Father.’ The Spirit Himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God. If children, then heirs; heirs of God; if indeed we suffer with Him, then we are joint heirs with Christ, so that we may also be glorified together" (Romans 8:14-17).
