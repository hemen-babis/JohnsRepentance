(Part 41)

Dear followers of the Orthodox teachings on John's Repentance website .we greet you with the peace of the Lord. Today, based on the topic mentioned above, we present to you the following important lesson. We encourage everyone to read it carefully and learn from it.

✍🏾Peace and the Church:

👉🏾Making peace is the primary task of the Church. The Church is the house of God, where humanity is reconciled with God through repentance, and where people live with God in love. It is a spiritual institution that teaches love to reign among people and preaches forgiveness. In this way, it contributes to peace among people. It also promotes inner peace by teaching a person to submit the body to the soul.

✍🏾 In the liturgy, the Covenant, and all other prayers, the Church always prays for peace. It also has a prayer called “Mestebekue Be-ente Meskel .” This is what it has learned from the Creator, the holy prophets, the holy apostles, and the holy scholars. For example, the following prayer sections for peace are briefly presented.

The Church testifies that peace is found in the Resurrection of Christ, proclaiming:

“Christ rose from the dead with great power and authority; He bound Satan; He set Adam free; peace is found.”Through His Resurrection, Christ defeated the devil, freed humanity from bondage, and restored peace and joy.

✍🏾 During the Holy Liturgy , the priest prays for peace for all, saying:“On this Holy day , let us ask God, our God, who keeps all things in perfect peace, to protect us throughout our lives.”

✍🏾 After His Resurrection, our Lord greeted His disciples with the words, “Peace be with you” (John 20:21). Following this tradition, the Church prays for the faithful by proclaiming, “Peace be with you all,” twelve times during the liturgy .

✍🏾 The faithful respond, “With your spirit,” asking that God’s true peace may dwell within them. This shows the importance of peace in the Christian life. Likewise, when our Lord healed the sick and forgave sins, He said, “My child, go in peace” (Mark 5:34; Luke 7:50).

✍🏾The Holy Church, following this example, instructs the faithful to “go in peace” (enter your homes in peace) at the end of the Liturgy.

✍🏾 The Church continually prays for peace during the Liturgy, according to the words of the Prophet Jeremiah: “Seek peace and pray to God for it” (Jer. 29:7).

✍🏾 In the Liturgy, the priest prays for the peace of the Church, saying:“Remember, O Lord, the peace of the Holy Church, which is one, which the Apostles gathered together.”

✍🏾 The deacon also proclaims:
“Pray for the peace of the Church, which is one, which the Apostles gathered together.”
When the deacon says, “Pray in the holy Church and in the assembly,” the people respond, “Bless our assembly and keep it in peace.”

✍🏾 In addition, during the Liturgy, the Church glorifies God with the angels, saying:“Glory to God in heaven, and on earth peace to people of good will.”

✍🏾When the deacon proclaims, “Pray for peace and love, for they are perfect, and greet one another with a holy greeting,” priests greet priests, deacons greet deacons, men greet men, and women greet women.

✍🏾 In the Liturgy prayer for peace( Be ente kidsat), the Church says:

“We pray for peace, that God, whose Body and Blood make us one, may unite us through His forgiveness.”When praying for peace in the country, it also says:
“We pray for our country, Ethiopia, that God may grant it perfect peace throughout its history.”

✍🏾 At the conclusion of this prayer, the Church humbly asks God, saying:“May He protect all of us who pray and intercede in peace and unity of spirit; grant us love, enlighten our eyes and hearts, and let us approach to God and ask Him to accept our prayers according to His will.
