✍🏾 There are 14 feasts of Saint Michael in a year. They are:

✍🏾 There are 14 feasts of Saint Michael celebrated throughout the year. Each commemorates a specific act of protection, mercy, or deliverance that God performed through His archangel.

1. Hidar 12
The day Saint Michael was appointed to lead the people of Israel, where he defeated the devil.

2. Hidar 13
The day when thousands of angels gathered and celebrated the feast together. It is known as The Day of the Thousands of Angels.

3. Tahisas 12
The day he helped Duratwas and his wife Theobstan.

4. Tir 12
The day he helped the poor boy Telafinos and protected Jacob from his brother Esau.

5. Yekatit 12
The day he appeared in human form to a man who had nothing to eat, drink, or wear, became a guarantor for him when he borrowed money, helped Samson, and destroyed the nations.

6. Megabit 12
The day he spoke to Balaam’s donkey and saved a man named Matthew and his wife and children.

7. Miyaziya 12
The day he brought Adam into Paradise and released the prophet Jeremiah from prison.

8. Ginbot 12
The day he lifted the prophet Habakkuk by a strand of his hair, carried him from Jerusalem to Babylon where Daniel was imprisoned, and fed Daniel in the lions’ den.”

9. Sene 12
The day he rescued Bahran from the sea and saved Afomiya from Satan.

10. Hamle 12
The day he rescued the poor boy Telhasa from the sea, defeated King Sennacherib and the army of the Assyrians, and saved the righteous King Hezekiah.

11. Nehase 12
The day he saved Sosna from the rebenat and helped Emperor Constantine defeat Maximian.

12. Pagume 3
The day Saint Raphael offered the annual incense of prayer before God.

13. Meskerem 12
The day he helped the martyr Fasilides, and when the prophet Isaiah visited and advised King Hezekiah.

14. Tikimt 12
The day the prophet Samuel anointed David as king, and Saint Michael stood with him. On this feast he presents 14 petitions before the Creator.

✍🏾Saint yared teaches that the petitions of Saint Michael are fourteen. These are the supplications he offers to the Creator on each of his fourteen feasts, asking for mercy and forgiveness for humankind.

✍🏾Therefore, we should always remember the feasts of Saint Michael, which are celebrated every month of the year.
