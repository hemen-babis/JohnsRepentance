Dear followers of the Orthodox teachings .we greet you with the peace of the Lord. Today, based on the topic mentioned above, we present to you the following important lesson. We encourage everyone to read it carefully and learn from it.

Blessed Are the Pure in Heart

👉🏾Our Lord taught in the sixth Beatitude, “Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God” (Matthew 5:8). In the spiritual life, the heart holds a central place. Scripture uses the word heart to describe the whole inner life. It includes the mind, desire, emotion, and will.

✍🏾When the wise Solomon said, “My son, give me your heart, and let your eyes observe my ways” (Proverbs 23:26), he was calling us to walk in the will of God. He also taught, “Keep your heart with all diligence, for from it flow the issues of life” (Proverbs 4:23). This means we must guard our inner spiritual life with care.

✍🏾Our Lord explained, “Out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaks. A good person out of the good treasure of the heart brings forth good, and an evil person out of the evil treasure brings forth evil” (Luke 6:45). A person speaks and acts from what fills the heart. Words reveal the life within.

✍🏾He also warned, “This people honors me with their lips, but their heart is far from me” (Matthew 15:8). In other words, there are those who speak religious words, yet their actual life and actions do not reflect closeness to God.


👉🏾Saint James teaches:

“Draw near to God, and He will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners; and purify your hearts, you double-minded.” (James 4:8)

✍🏾This instructs us to cleanse our inner life, the heart, so that God may draw near to us. Purity begins within. When the heart is purified, the whole life becomes aligned with God.

✍🏾A pure heart is a life free from the stains of sin, surrendered to God in love, lived with sincerity and faithfulness. It is a heart that reflects the presence of God.

The perfect example of such purity among all humanity is the Holy Virgin Mary, our Redeemer’s Mother. She is pure in spirit, pure in speech, and pure in deed.

For this reason the Church rightfully calls her:

“The purest of the pure.”

Her body was pure, her soul was pure, and her heart was pure. Because of this purity, she became the dwelling place of the Most High. The divine nature, which no creature can behold, took flesh from her and became man for our salvation.

Our holy fathers praised her, saying:

“She was pure and holy like the Ark on Sinai. She dwelt in the Temple as the Ark once dwelt in the sanctuary. Her food was heavenly, and her drink was the drink of peace.”

👉🏾Saint Dioscorus also proclaimed her purity in the Divine Liturgy, saying:

“By the will of the Father, He came down and was incarnate of the Virgin Mary, the pure one. He was born of her, He dwelt among us, He was held in the arms of His Mother, and He was nourished as a child.”

✍🏾The Main Manifestations of Purity of Heart

1. Purifying the Heart Through Repentance

Saint Paul teaches:

“For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.” (Romans 3:23)

This means no one begins with a pure heart. Purity is restored through repentance. This is why Saint David prayed:

“Create in me a clean heart, O Lord, and renew a right spirit within me.” (Psalm 51:10)

Through true repentance, God grants a renewed, purified heart.
