Continued 👆🏽

Their hope? The Resurrection of Christ. His rising from the dead opened paradise. He descended to the depths and shattered the gates of hell, liberating the righteous. Now they wait not in gloom, but in glory.

Signs and Shadows of Resurrection

From the beginning, God gave us symbols:

The sun rises and sets, daily proclaiming resurrection.

Sleep and waking mirror death and life.

Abel’s blood cried out from the ground (Gen. 4:10)—a dead man bearing witness.

Enoch was taken and did not taste death (Gen. 5:24) a foreshadowing of eternal life.

Even dry bones in Ezekiel’s vision became a mighty army when filled with the breath of God (Ezek. 37). God spoke through Daniel: some shall awake to everlasting life, others to shame (Dan. 12:2). Isaiah prophesied, “Your dead shall live, their bodies shall rise” (Isa. 26:19).

Christ confirmed it: “All who are in the graves will hear His voice” (John 5:29).

The Resurrection Body: Glorious and Incorruptible

Saint Paul teaches with holy wisdom: What is sown in weakness shall be raised in power; what is sown a natural body shall rise a spiritual body (1 Cor. 15:35-49). Just as we bore the image of the earthy man, so too shall we bear the image of the heavenly man—Christ.

He who rose first will raise us. He shall transform our lowly bodies to be like His glorious body (Phil. 3:21). For we shall be like Him, says St. John, for we shall see Him as He is (1 John 3:2).

This is our hope, our faith, our inheritance: not to return to mere life, but to rise incorruptible, radiant, and holy.

The Grace of the Resurrection

O death, where is your sting? O hell, where is your victory? (1 Cor. 15:55; Hosea 13:14) The Resurrection of Christ is our victory, our inheritance, and our transformation. It is the moment when we pass from darkness to light, from death to life, from earthly to heavenly, from perishable to eternal.

He rose not only for Himself, but for us to make us partakers of the divine nature, to seat us with Him in the heavens, and to fill us with the hope of glory.

Let us fall down and worship. Let us live as those who will rise. Let us speak and think and pray with eyes fixed on the risen Christ, and say with trembling joy:

"Christ is risen from the dead, trampling down death by death, and upon those in the tombs bestowing life!"

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
