👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾The Holy #Cross: The Disciples and Your #Mother (#John 19:26–27)

(Part 11)

Indeed, my beloved brethren in Christ, let us turn our minds and hearts to the profound mystery of the Holy Cross, that venerable standard of our salvation, upon which our Lord Jesus Christ, the King of Glory, wrought our redemption. Let us delve into the sacred narrative, drawing forth lessons from the very words presented to us, much as a bee gathers nectar from the most fragrant blossoms.

Christ’s Care from the Cross

When our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ saw His beloved disciples and His Most Holy Mother standing at the foot of the cross, His heart was not merely concerned with His own suffering, but with the care and comfort of those He loved.

He said to His Mother:

 "Behold your son, your helper, your comforter."

And to His beloved disciple John, He said:

"Behold your Mother, your comforter."

By this sacred act, He gave us all to her, making her the Mother of the faithful. This moment teaches us that the cross, though an instrument of suffering, is also a vessel of divine care, uniting all believers as a family under the protection of the Mother of God.

The Meaning of the Feast of the Cross

Beyond the beauty and joy that mark the Feast of the Cross, it is important to understand its deeper meaning where it comes from and the blessings it brings through the Holy Word. Like Saint John, whose heart burned with love for the Cross, and Saint Eleni, who was depicted as sharing in its suffering, we are called to look upon the Cross with reverence and gratitude. As the women of Jerusalem stood with the Virgin Mary and heard the words, “Behold, your mother,” so too did Eleni rise up, three centuries later, to bring the Cross out of hiding and share its blessing with the world.

Saint Eleni, who truly understood the mystery and power of the Cross, succeeded in her holy mission. She uncovered the Cross from its burial place, and through her faith, it became a light to the whole world. For whoever glories in the Cross is never ashamed.

The Apostle Paul declared, “But far be it from me to boast except in the cross of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, by whom the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world” (Galatians 6:14). While Saint Eleni was still reflecting deeply on this truth, her son, Emperor Constantine, saw a sign in the sky a radiant Cross bearing the words, “By this sign, you will conquer.”

Throughout her life, Eleni had been grieved that the Cross had been hidden through envy and deceit. Yet she prayed with steadfast faith and longing that it might be revealed once more. God answered her prayer, and through the power of the Cross, the good news of victory and salvation was made known. This triumph became a symbol of hope and glory for all Christians throughout the world.
