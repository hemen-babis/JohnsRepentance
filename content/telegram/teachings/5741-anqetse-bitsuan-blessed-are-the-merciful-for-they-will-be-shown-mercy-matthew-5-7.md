2. Showing Mercy as an Expression of Love

Showing mercy is one of the clearest signs that love truly lives in the heart. God the Son became man so that humanity could receive divine mercy, and He calls us to reflect this same mercy toward one another. Love is not proven by repeating the words “I love you,” but by showing compassion, kindness, and forgiveness from the heart.

Our Lord teaches that this love must extend even beyond those who care for us. It includes those who oppose us, misunderstand us, or treat us with hostility.

Matt. 5:45-47 teaches that God “makes His sun rise on the evil and the good.” Just as Christ showed mercy even to those who crucified Him, we are called to imitate Him by showing mercy to all human beings without discrimination. As it is written, “Love works kindness” (1 Cor. 13:4).

Our Lord also taught us the meaning of true love:

👉🏾“Do to others as you would have them do to you. For if you love those who love you, what credit is that to you? Even sinners love those who love them. And if you do good to those who do good to you, what credit is that to you? Even sinners do the same. And if you lend to those from whom you hope to receive, what credit is that to you? Even sinners lend to sinners, expecting to receive back. But love your enemies, and do good, hoping for nothing in return, and your reward will be great, and you will be children of the Most High. For He is kind to the ungrateful and the evil.”
(Luke 6:31-35)

This teaching shows that mercy is not just an action; it is the outward expression of genuine love. True Christian love reaches beyond those who love us and embraces even our enemies, just as God shows kindness to all, including the ungrateful and the wicked.
