5. Jesus is perfect God (Lord) /Amlak

(continued part)👆👇

The word God (Amlak) is Geez word and its meaning  is ruler, creator, helper, shepherd, the one who created and rules the world, who has no other ruler than him.

As we have seen above, the Apostle St. John said in his Gospel, "All things came into being through Him, and nothing came into being without Him." (John 1:3) We have already seen that this means that the heavens and the earth were created by him. In his first message, St. John said, "He is the true God and eternal life, and he has explained to us the unity of divine power and character of Jesus Christ with the Father and the Holy Spirit." (1 John 5:21) Therefore, to know the divinity of Jesus Christ, it is enough to know that Jesus is God and the Creator. (Ps 17:31) For more evidence, we will quote from the prophecies of the prophets, the gospel of Christ and the testimony of the apostles as follows:

Prophet Isaiah is the first among the prophets who spoke a very clear and open   prophecy about the descent and birth of Jesus Christ. First, he said, "The Lord himself will give you a sign. Behold, a virgin will conceive and give birth to a son, and she will call his name Immanuel." (Isaiah 7:14) Then he said, "For unto us a child is born, unto us a son is given, and the leadership shall be upon his shoulder, and his name shall be called the mighty God, the everlasting father, the prince of peace." (Isa 9:6) It is not debatable that it was said about Jesus. Even if there are those who argue that it is not about Jesus, the evangelist Saint Matthew wrote us the answer. "By the prophet from the Lord, behold, a virgin will conceive and give birth to a son, and they will call his name Immanuel. All this has happened so that what is said will be fulfilled. The interpretation is that God (God) has been with us." (Matthew 1:22-23)

The apostle Saint John said in one of his messages, "We know that the Son of God has come, and that he has given us the understanding to know what is true." ... His son is Jesus Christ, who is the true God and eternal life." Isaiah saw and testified to the truth of what he prophesied, and clearly spoke to us of his Sonship of God, his divinity and his divinity. (1st John 5:20-21) When the prophet Jesus said that there is no God and Creator other than God, he said, "Do not be afraid and do not be alarmed, did I not tell you from the beginning?" Or did I not show you? You are my witnesses. Is there any god besides me? There is no shelter, I don't know anyone," he wrote to us. (Isaiah 44:8)
As we have seen above, Jesus is God. And God is the creator. (Genesis 1:1, 1:26) If God is God (Amlak), He is God, for He said, "There is no other God besides Me." (Deuteronomy 20:3) God who created the heavens and made the earth and established it... thus says I am God and there is no other besides me.
