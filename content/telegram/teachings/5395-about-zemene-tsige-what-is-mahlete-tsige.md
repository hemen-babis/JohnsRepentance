👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾About #Zemene #Tsige (#What is #Mahlete #Tsige?)

(Part 5)

Beloved faithful in the Orthodox Church, peace and grace be multiplied unto you. We greet you in the name of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.

Today, through the blessings of God, we share with you a series on the mystery of Zemene Tsige. this lesson, drawn from the lives of the saints and the wisdom of our Fathers,may this lesson inspire repentance, humility, and love for God in every heart that reads it.

2.2.The poor man Aulōgis and Abba Daniel’s remorse and the cry for intercession

✍🏾Aulōgis was a poor man who lived on the small income he earned by grinding millstones and selling them. After spending a little on himself, he would use the rest to help those who were in greater need than he.

✍🏾One day, while doing this, he saw a monk named Abba Daniel. This monk said within himself, “If this man can help the poor even in his poverty, how many people would he help and how many good deeds would he do if he had great wealth?” So Abba Daniel prayed to God to give this man wealth.

✍🏾Although God told him that riches would not be good for Aulōgis, Abba Daniel, following his own will, continued to beg God to grant him wealth. After this, Abba Daniel, as was his custom, while digging stone, found gold buried in a pot. He took the gold and gave it to the king. When the king saw his honesty, he said, “How loyal is this man who brought the treasure to me rather than keeping it for himself?” The king then appointed him to serve in his palace.

✍🏾After Aulōgis was appointed, his honor and wealth increased, and he left his previous work. However, he became a judge who did not judge rightly or defend the oppressed.

✍🏾At this time, Abba Daniel saw God’s anger in a dream God judging Aulōgis for his sins. Realizing that his own prayer had brought this upon Aulōgis, Abba Daniel fell at the feet of the Virgin Mary icon and begged her to intercede for mercy and forgiveness

Abba Daniel was deeply troubled by the vision he saw in his dream. He feared that perhaps Aulōgis had acted unjustly toward the poor and had violated the law of God. Because of this, he chose to speak to him 

"As you pray to your Son who died out of love for mankind, have mercy also on me to the son of tsige, that I too may receive forgiveness."

This teaches us that the Virgin Mary, through her intercession, seeks mercy even for those who are unaware of the punishment awaiting them so that all may find forgiveness through repentance and the compassion of God.

2.3.The miracles recorded by Saint Deksios and the sobriety of sacred signs

✍🏾 The miracles of our Lady were gathered and written by Saint Deksios. Our Lady also gave him a blue chair that no one else could sit on, and a robe that no one else could wear.

✍🏾 After the death of Saint Deksios, the bishop who succeeded him wanted to sit on this chair and wear the episcopal robe. But when he tried, they said to him, “This was given only to Deksios,” but out of pride he sat down, saying, “I am a bishop like him why should I not take what was his?” He immediately died.

The above account shows the holiness and divine favor granted to Saint Deksios.

✍🏾“O Mother of God, helper of the weak and defender of the needy You who have no need of help from others just as You received the man who sat upon his throne  of Your faithful servant Deksios, so now arise with the power of Your Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, to drive away my enemies, both demons and sins.”

👉🏾This prayer, drawn from the story, teaches us to call upon our Holy Mother to deliver us from all spiritual enemies. Through her intercession and the strength of her Son, we find protection, mercy, and victory over the powers of darkness.
