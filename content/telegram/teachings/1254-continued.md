(Continued) 👆🏾

Beloved members, as we celebrate the Feast of the Paraclete, let us remember the immeasurable gift bestowed upon the apostles and the birth of the Church. Let us seek the indwelling of the Holy Spirit in our lives, for it is through His presence that our hearts are set ablaze with divine love, our minds are enlightened, and our souls are sanctified. Just as the apostles, let us go forth and proclaim the Gospel in the language of those around us, bearing witness to the saving grace of our Lord Jesus Christ.

May the Feast of the Paraclete serve as a constant reminder of the power and guidance of the Holy Spirit in our lives. Let us fervently pray, "Come, Holy Spirit, fill the hearts of Your faithful and kindle in them the fire of Your love. Send forth Your Spirit, and they shall be created, and You shall renew the face of the earth."

Through the intercessions of the Holy Apostles, may we be blessed abundantly with the fullness of the Holy Spirit. Amen.
