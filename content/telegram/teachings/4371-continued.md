IV. The Purpose of the Apostles’ Fast

Why then do we fast this fast?
We fast:

To imitate the apostles who overcame the world through fasting and prayer.

To humble the flesh that we may be raised in the spirit.

To seek the intercession of the Holy Apostles, that their courage may become our courage, and their victories our victories.

To draw near to God, that we may be filled anew with the grace that launched the Church into all the earth.

This fast is not merely a tradition. It is a weapon of war, a shield of faith, a school of humility. And yet, O how many have neglected it! Some, in error and ignorance, call it a "priestly fast" as if fasting were for clergy alone and the laity exempt.

Let it be known and proclaimed without ambiguity: Fasting is for all Christians. There is no clerical fast and lay fast. There is only the fast of the Church, the fast of the apostles, the fast of the faithful. For the Lord did not say, “The priests will fast,” but “they will fast” meaning all His disciples, all who follow Him.

V. The Danger of Neglect and the Reward of Obedience

Let us not cast away this mighty weapon! Let us not shrink back like cowards when our forefathers stood as soldiers! The world today, with its temptations, confusion, and pride, can only be overcome by those who fast as the apostles fasted, who pray as the apostles prayed, who love Christ as the apostles loved Him.

Scripture has warned us:

“Whoever listens to Me will dwell in safety and will escape from the fear of trouble.” (Proverbs 1:33)

Do you desire safety? Do you long for peace? Then listen, obey, fast, and pray.

VI. Final Admonition and Blessing

Let us, therefore, beloved brothers and sisters, return to this holy fast with zeal. Let no one make excuses. Let no one delay. Let every household renew this sacred tradition, fathers instructing children, mothers praying with daughters, youth encouraged by elders.

As the apostles fasted, so let us fast. As they triumphed, so let us triumph. As they were filled, so let us be filled.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
