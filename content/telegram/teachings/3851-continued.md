Continued👆🏽

Thus, my brethren, the Fasting of Nineveh stands as a testament to the boundless mercy of our Lord. It is a call to every soul burdened by sin to repent, to seek the Lord while He may be found, and to humble oneself before His mighty hand. Let us not be as those who trust in their strength, nor as those who boast in their wisdom, for the Lord brings the mighty low and exalts the humble.

To Him be the glory, to the Virgin be honor, and to the Cross be eternal praise!

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
