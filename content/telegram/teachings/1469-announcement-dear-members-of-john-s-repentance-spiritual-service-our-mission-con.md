#Announcement 📣

Dear members of John's Repentance spiritual service, our mission continues to grow, and as we look forward to further strengthening our services, we humbly request your involvement and support. Please reach out to us if you're interested in this opportunity. (@yohannesneseha)

We believe that with increased manpower, our reach can be extended even further, ensuring that our offerings are accessible to the global community. The areas in which we currently need assistance are as follows:

1) Question and Answer Support for Amharic page: We require help in managing previously answered questions. Instead of burdening our spiritual leaders with repeating responses, we need individuals who can provide links to past answers and share the knowledge responsibly, only commenting when they are certain of the correct response.

2) Translation: As we aim to make our resources available to a broader audience, we are seeking volunteers who can translate text lessons from Amharic to English for our English version of John's Repentance website.

4) Moderation for Amharic page: To maintain the sanctity of our community, we need moderators who can manage inappropriate messages, advertisements, links, and phone numbers posted on our page. If such posts appear, writing a polite correction or contacting the admin for removal will be necessary.

5) YouTube videos for Amharic page: We need assistance on uploading videos to YouTube 

6) Outreach: We need your help on sharing our service to many more people.  

7) Social media management: We need assistance managing our social media platforms like Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. This is much easier than it sounds. It's usually a copy paste. 

In essence, your support would contribute to enhancing our services, helping our spiritual leaders, and saving the time of all families who use the John's Repentance website.

Please note, this volunteer opportunity can be fulfilled using your mobile phone and can be carried out at a time that suits your schedule. This is an unpaid, voluntary service offering, filled with the opportunity for spiritual blessings.

We thank you in advance for your generous cooperation and encourage you to reach out to us if you're willing to be a part of this spiritual mission. Your contribution will be greatly appreciated, and we are excited about the potential to work together in the service of God and our community.
