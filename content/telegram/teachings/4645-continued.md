IV. Why Do We Bow Before the Ark?

We bow not to marble, nor to wood. We bow to Him who sits at the right hand of Majesty (Heb. 8:1), the One through whom all things exist (1 Cor. 8:6), who wears the title King of kings and Lord of lords (Rev. 19:16). He is the One whom angels veil their faces before, whom the cherubim serve, and the seraphim praise.

Thus we kneel before the altar because it holds Him who was nailed to the Cross on Good Friday, Him who said at the Last Supper: “This is my body… this is my blood.” The Ark now is not a relic of memory, but the place of the sacrifice of Calvary, re-presented in every Divine Liturgy.

When the Ark is lifted during baptism, no one bows to the mere church building. All worship is directed to Christ, the faithful and true Ark, who is both the High Priest and the Offering.

V. “At the Name of Jesus Every Knee Shall Bow”

It is written (Phil. 2:10): “At the name of Jesus every knee should bow, in heaven and on earth and under the earth.” This is not mere figure. It is our instruction. The Ark, inscribed with His name, is like a silent preacher, crying out to us:
“What do you wait for, O man? Bow your knee!”

And we, with all things in heaven, on earth, and beneath the earth, bow to Him who is enthroned at the right hand of God. The saints in glory, the angels in light, and the faithful on earth join in reverent worship before this mystery.

Conclusion

O faithful soul, do not gaze upon the altar with casual eyes. There stands the Ark of the New Covenant, wherein is not the Law, but the Lawgiver; not tablets of stone, but the Bread of Life. Let us then approach with awe and trembling, with reverence and love, for the One who gave Himself once upon the Cross, now gives Himself again upon the altar.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
