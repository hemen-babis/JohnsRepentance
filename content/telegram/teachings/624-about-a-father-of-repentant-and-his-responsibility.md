About a Father of Repentant and his Responsibility 

(Part 2)

A penitent father should teach each of his children separately according to their religious knowledge and spiritual strength, and prepare them all for Holy Communion.
 
A penitent father is one who maintains perfect closeness among his repentant children. It gives the children of repentance the opportunity to serve as part of the solution to many problems. Starting from helping those in need, it is the role of the father of penitence to teach them by being an example to each other. This means that the penitent children of a priest should be as close as brothers and sisters born of the same father and mother, and even more so.
 
Repentance precedes Holy Communion. Since there is no time when mankind is free from sin, he should tell the sin he has committed to the penitent father every day and receive the canons and do what he is ordered. This great responsibility   is placed on the penitent teacher because a penitent father is a trustee. 

Our Lord said in the Gospel, "Who has a hundred sheep and if one of them is lost, he leaves the ninety-nine in the desert and does not go looking for the lost one until he finds it?" Luke 15:5. Priests are supposed to protect and serve each of us individually without forgetting a single person that they have been entrusted by God to protect us. 
 
A repentant father's personal relationship with his repentant children should be beyond  family's relationship or responsibility. Because  he is the guardian of our souls, he gives us guidance in spiritual and our social lives. But it is seen that many priests come to the houses of the believers only to offer services  of sprinkling holy water and blessing with  the cross. However, priests should give priority to teaching, advising, accepting confessions, comforting and disciplining as the church system has set for them. Since it is their goal to treat each of their penitent children with care and prepare them for the Holy Communion, their relationship should go beyond the spirit of family solidarity and focus on this main purpose of their priesthood. This is the greatest responsibility of the priest.
 
A penitent father must know the number of family members of his penitent children and each one of them.
 
A penitent father should remember his penitent children and their families and ask about them regularly.
 
A penitent father should practice prayer by praying with his children. Showing the true love of father and son. Worrying not only about their spiritual but also their physical life; Praying regularly for their work to be blessed, generally keeping a close eye on the children. This is the first and last task.
 
All in all; The Father of Repentance (Father of the Soul): He is in charge of our soul, which is invisible and intangible. Also, they are empowered by God to inherit the heavenly kingdom that we will inherit in the later days, which cannot be seen with the flesh and cannot be reached by research and knowledge. Therefore, the fathers we treat as penitent fathers should be those who have been proven by their knowledge, morals, fasting, prayers, religion, and conduct, and who should always take responsibility for their repentant children with diligence and vigilance. Because we need a dedicated priest who can stand as a witness to all the spiritual work we do before God, just as the holy angels stand as our guardians in subtle and mysterious ways. In this real world and in the life we live as a Christian, our priests and fathers have been appointed by God to be shepherds of our souls. Therefore, repenting is not a human command, but a Creator's instruction.
