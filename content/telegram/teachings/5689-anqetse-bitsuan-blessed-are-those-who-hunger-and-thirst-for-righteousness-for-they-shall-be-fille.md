👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾#Anqetse #Bitsuan (“Blessed are those who #hunger and #thirst for #righteousness, for they shall be filled.”)
#Matthew 5:6; #Luke 6:21

(Part 15)

Beloved in Christ, just as our bodies long for food and water, our souls long for God, His Word, and His salvation. We invite you to read, reflect, and learn how to hunger and thirst for righteousness, 

3. Hungering and Thirsting for His Holy Flesh and Precious Blood

Hungering and thirsting for righteousness means hungering and thirsting for the holy flesh and precious blood of our Lord Jesus Christ, where true righteousness is found. When we say “hungering and thirsting for righteousness,” our Lord teaches in His Gospel,

“Whoever drinks of the water that I shall give him shall never thirst; but the water that I shall give him shall become in him a well of water springing up into everlasting life.”
(John 4:14)

He also says,

“I am the bread of life; whoever comes to me shall never hunger, and whoever believes in me shall never thirst.”(John 6:35)

Here, the Lord refers to the spiritual hunger the deep longing of the soul for the bread of life, which is His own body.

This truth was foreshadowed in the Old Testament when the Israelites hungered and the Lord fed them with heavenly manna, and when they thirsted and drank water that flowed from the rock. The manna and the water symbolized the Body and Blood of Christ, the true food and drink that give eternal life.

Our Lord Himself declared this clearly, saying:

“I am the bread of life; whoever comes to me will never hunger, and whoever believes in me will never thirst.

I am the living bread that came down from heaven. If anyone eats of this bread, he will live forever; and the bread that I will give for the life of the world is my flesh.
Truly, truly, I say to you, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink His blood, you have no life in you.

Whoever eats My flesh and drinks My blood has eternal life, and I will raise him up on the last day.
For My flesh is true food, and My blood is true drink.
Whoever eats My flesh and drinks My blood abides in Me, and I in him.”(John 6:33–56)

Saint John the Apostle also reveals this mystery in the Book of Revelation:

“And he said to me, ‘Write, Blessed are those who are called to the marriage supper of the Lamb.’ And he said to me, ‘These are the true words of God.’”
(Revelation 19:9)

Therefore, to hunger and thirst for righteousness is to desire the Holy Communion the life-giving flesh and precious blood of Christ through which the faithful are united with Him and receive eternal life.

4. Hungering and Thirsting for the House of God

Hungering and thirsting for righteousness also means longing for heavenly life the spiritual life lived in the house of God, through prayer and worship. The righteous soul finds joy and peace in the dwelling of the Lord.

King David expressed this divine longing, saying,

“I rejoiced when they said to me, ‘Let us go into the house of the Lord.’”(Psalm 122:1)

He also said,

“Blessed are those who dwell in Your house; they will be ever praising You.”(Psalm 84:4)

To live in the house of the Lord according to His will is the greatest blessing. David further declared,

“Better is one day in Your courts than a thousand elsewhere; I would rather be a guest in the house of my God than dwell in the tents of the wicked.” (Psalm 84:10)

He prayed with deep desire, saying,

“One thing have I desired of the Lord, that will I seek after: that I may dwell in the house of the Lord all the days of my life, to behold the beauty of the Lord, and to inquire in His temple.”(Psalm 27:4)

Saint Paul also taught his spiritual son Timothy about the holiness of God’s house, saying,

“I am writing to you so that you may know how one ought to behave in the house of God, which is the Church of the living God, the pillar and foundation of the truth.”
(1 Timothy 3:15)

Thus, to hunger and thirst for righteousness means to desire to live continually in the presence of God, to pray without ceasing, and to dwell spiritually in His holy Church.
