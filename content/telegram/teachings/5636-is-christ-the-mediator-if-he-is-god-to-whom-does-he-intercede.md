👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾Is #Christ the #Mediator? If He is God, to #Whom Does #He #Intercede? 

(Part 19)

Dear faithful followers of the Orthodox teachings , we greet you in the peace and grace of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. Today, by the help of God, we present this important teaching for your reflection and spiritual growth.

✍🏾1.The Intercession of Christ in Prayer

When Jesus Christ was incarnate as Lord, He appeared in the flesh and showed humanity how a righteous man should obey his Lord. When He revealed Himself as God, He taught humanity how to worship Him rightly (Matt. 11:28; John 13:13).

✍🏾In this, He revealed that He would clothe humanity with the eternal garment of salvation. Through this act, He disclosed His plan to bring eternal life, to destroy the works of the devil, and to proclaim the good news of salvation to mankind (Gen. 3:15; Isa. 61:10). Christ also promised that He would perform this intercession Himself, without the help of anyone else, for it is His divine work that no one can share or interfere with.

👉🏾The teachings of the Old Testament are a shadow of the salvation that God accomplishes in the New Testament. “These are a shadow of the things to come, but the substance is of Christ” (Colossians 2:17).

✍🏾The message of Genesis 3:8-21 reveals that reconciliation true intercession can only be accomplished by God. As Jeremiah 50:34 states: “Their Redeemer is strong; the Lord of hosts is His name; He will plead their cause thoroughly, to give rest to the earth, and to discomfit the inhabitants of Babylon.” Likewise, the Advocate of our souls, mentioned in 1 John 2:1, is none other than the Lord God Himself.

✍🏾Since God redeems humanity from all sin, and He redeems according to His own will, He is fully for Himself and through Himself. As Scripture says: “For of Him and through Him and to Him are all things. To Him be glory forever!” (Romans 11:36). All intercession and salvation belong to God alone.

The Word of God in Creation and Salvation

As we read in Genesis, God, who created all things by His Word alone, used the same Word to seek and save humanity, who had fallen into the debt of eternal death (John 1:3). As it is written in Psalm 107:20, “He sent His Word and healed them, and saved them from their destruction” (see also Isa. 55:11-12).

✍🏾 “To save that which was lost” (Matt. 18:11) – In the New Testament, this saving Word was made flesh (John 1:14). When the Word became flesh, He was called the Son of God (Luke 1:35; Rev. 19:13).

👉🏾However, this incarnation was not simply to demonstrate that the Word could become flesh. Rather, as Scripture explains in Hebrews 9:22, “without the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness.” Therefore, He was called the Lamb of God, destined to be the perfect sacrifice for the forgiveness of sins for all humanity.

God Himself became the intercessor by appearing in the flesh while remaining fully God (1 Tim. 3:16).

✍🏾 As the Apostle Paul clearly wrote:

"God was in Christ reconciling the world to Himself, not counting their trespasses against them, and has committed to us the word of reconciliation" (2 Cor. 5:19-20).

While it is common for sinners to be reconciled through a human intercessor, God reconciled the world to Himself by His own authority. Therefore, the only intercessor confirmed by Scripture is Jesus Christ.

✍🏾Christ’s Intercession

"It is Christ Jesus who died, rather, who is also raised from the dead, who is at the right hand of God, who also intercedes for us" (Rom. 8:34).

✍🏾 The Holy Word literally describes that He intercedes. It is not a human interpretation or metaphor. Therefore, to believe or say that “He is not an intercessor” or “Christ does not intercede” is to depart from the truth of God’s Word.
