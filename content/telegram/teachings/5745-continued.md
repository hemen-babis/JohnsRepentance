✍🏾Saint Michael: Protector and Intercessor of God’s People

Saint Michael was a very humble and caring angel, appointed over both the angels and the people of God. His appointment was not to rule over them like a king, but to guide the angels, protect us from the attacks of Satan, and assist us through his prayers.

✍🏾Our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ taught that the holy angels protect and help us, standing before Him on our behalf, saying, “See that you do not despise one of these little ones; for their angels always see the face of my Father in heaven.” (Matthew 18:10)

✍🏾 If even the angels assigned to protect us care so deeply and are so zealous for our salvation, how much more does Saint Michael, exalted in holiness as the chief of the angels, intercede for us before God? (Luke 13:6-9)

✍🏾Psalm 33:2 reminds us of his role: He “stands up for the children of Your people.” This shows clearly that Saint Michael helps us live righteous lives through his prayers and intercession, using the authority and power given to him by God.

May the prayers and blessings of the Archangel Saint Michael be upon us forever!

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
