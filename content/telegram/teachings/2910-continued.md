(Continued) ☝️

As St. John Chrysostom might remind us, our membership in the Church is our pathway to communion with Christ and through Him, to eternal life. This is the essence of church membership: it is a sacred calling to embody the love of Christ, to nurture the faith within ourselves and among our brethren, and to advance steadfastly towards the heavenly kingdom.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
