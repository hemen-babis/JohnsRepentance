(Continues) 👆🏾

Conclusion

To destroy the unity of the Church is to assail the very heart of God's plan for humanity. The punishment for such transgression is severe, for it not only undermines the foundation of our faith but also rejects the sacrifice of Christ on the cross. Let us, therefore, heed the wisdom of our forefathers, embracing the oneness of the faithful with a fervor that befits our calling.

In doing so, we become true disciples of Christ, united in love, faith, and purpose, for the glory of God and the sanctification of the world.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
