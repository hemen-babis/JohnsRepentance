Continued 👇🏾

Conclusion: Our Sacred Proclamation

Thus we preach: Christ crucified not a mere concept, but our living Lord: Creator and Redeemer, teacher of humility, conqueror of death.

Beloved, let the Cross not be a relic of history, but the center of your life:

Confess His divinity,

Embrace His humility,

Endure suffering with purpose,

Look for your glorification.

May the crucified Christ transform us: “Not I, but Christ lives in me.” (Gal. 2:20) May our lives bear the stamp of the Cross, and may our hearts echo the words of Chrysostom: the Cross is our armor, our identity, our eternal victory .

Amen. May we stand firm in this proclamation, that neither Jew nor Greek, sinner nor saint, but all may see in us the power of Christ crucified.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
