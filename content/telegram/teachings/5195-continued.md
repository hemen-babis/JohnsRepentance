5. The Fast of Nineveh"Tsome Nenewey"

This fast is unique, for it has no Tewsak of its own but depends entirely upon the Mebaja Hamer. 

1. If the Beale Metike'(በዓለ መጥቅዕ) (reference point) falls in Meskerem, the Fast of Nineveh is kept in Tir.

2. If Beale Metike'(በዓለ መጥቅዕ)  falls in Tikimt, the Fast is kept in Yekatit.

3. If Beale Metike'(በዓለ መጥቅዕ) is greater than 14, and when added to the day’s Tewsak exceeds 30, then also the Fast is in Yekatit.

Thus by the wisdom of the fathers, the time of this fast is never in doubt.

Conclusion

Beloved, see how nothing in the Church is by chance. Even the counting of days is sanctified. God, who set the stars in their courses and divided the times and seasons from the beginning, has also ordered the calendar of His Church for the salvation of His people.

Therefore let us keep the fasts and the feasts not as burdens, but as blessings; not as mere reckonings of time, but as holy seasons appointed for repentance, thanksgiving, and joy. For through the discipline of the calendar, the soul is trained in order, obedience, and remembrance of the works of God.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
