4.2. The Lamb and the Tree of Life

The Virgin Mary, the Mother who gave birth to Christ, is prefigured in the Old Testament. When Abraham offered Isaac, a ram was found caught in a thicket.

“Abraham lifted up his eyes and saw a ram caught by its horns in a thicket.
He took the ram and offered it in place of his son.” (Genesis 22:13)

✍🏾Isaac represents the world,
the ram represents Christ,
and the tree of life where the ram was caught symbolizes the Virgin Mary

Just as the ram was bound in the tree and became a ransom for Isaac, so Christ took flesh from our Lady, was bound upon the Cross, and became the ransom for the sins of the world.

✍🏾Thus, the parable of Isaac and the ram is fulfilled in Christ and His Mother. The Virgin Mary is the living Tree of Life, bearing the fruit that brought salvation to all.

✍🏾The prophet Balaam foresaw this mystery and said:

“A star shall rise from Jacob,
and a scepter shall spring from Israel,
and He shall remove sin and unrighteousness.”
(Numbers 24:17; Isaiah 27:9; Romans 11:26)

This “star” is Christ, born from the “East of Jacob,” the radiant Virgin who gave light to the world.

4.3. The Sealed Garden

“The prophets who saw the heavens declared,
‘A garden enclosed is my sister, my spouse;
a spring shut up, a fountain sealed.’” (Song of Solomon 4:12)

This prophecy speaks of the Virgin Mary, the sealed and holy dwelling in which the Word of God took flesh. She is the enclosed garden of purity, the living fountain from which the waters of salvation flow to all who believe.
