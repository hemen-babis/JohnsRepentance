👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾#Anqetse #Bitsuan (“Blessed are those who #hunger and #thirst for #righteousness, for they shall be filled.”)
#Matthew 5:6; #Luke 6:21

(Part 16)

Dear beloved in Christ, followers of the Orthodox faith , we greet you in the name of our Lord and Savior. Today, we continue our lesson on the divine words: “Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they shall be filled.” Let us read with attention, reflect deeply, and learn from this holy teaching.

6. Hungering and Thirsting for Truth and Justice

Righteousness is truth and justice.
When we say hungering and thirsting for righteousness, it means to sincerely seek truth and justice, to pray earnestly for them, and to live according to them in all our deeds. The true Christian life is a life dedicated to truth and justice.

Christ Himself is the Truth, as He declared:

 “I am the Way, the Truth, and the Life.” (John 14:6)

And in Deuteronomy it is written:

 “He is the Rock, His work is perfect; for all His ways are justice. A God of truth and without iniquity, just and upright is He.” (Deut. 32:4)

✍🏾God is a God of truth and justice. Therefore, those who worship Him must also live in truth and righteousness.

✍🏾The Righteousness of God in Scripture

The heart of God was revealed through the words of His servant David:

“The word of the Lord is right, and all His works are done in faithfulness. He loves righteousness and justice; the earth is full of the goodness of the Lord. By the word of the Lord the heavens were made, and all their host by the breath of His mouth.” (Psalm 33:4–6)

And again,

 “Righteousness and justice are the foundation of Your throne; mercy and truth go before Your face.” (Psalm 89:14)

✍🏾Because God is a God of truth and justice, all who believe in Him must strive to walk in the same way doing what is right and fair before Him.

The prophets also bear witness:

“Do justice and righteousness.” (Jeremiah 22:3)
“Let justice roll down like waters, and righteousness like a mighty stream.” (Amos 5:24)

Thus, we are called to hunger and thirst for truth, justice, and righteousness in our lives, in our hearts, and in our dealings with others.

7.Hungering and Thirsting for the Kingdom of Heaven

To hunger and thirst for righteousness is also to long for the Kingdom of Heaven, which is the ultimate goal of every believer.
Our Lord taught:

“Seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness.” (Matthew 6:33)

And again:

 “For what does it profit a man if he gains the whole world, yet loses his own soul?” (Mark 8:36)

Therefore, our greatest desire must be the salvation of our soul to live in unity with Christ and to inherit His kingdom.

✍🏾Abiding in Christ, the Source of Righteousness

Our Lord said:

 “Abide in Me, and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit of itself unless it abides in the vine, neither can you, unless you abide in Me. I am the vine; you are the branches. Without Me you can do nothing.” (John 15:4–5)

✍🏾Only when we remain in Christ can we bear the fruits of righteousness. Apart from Him, we are lifeless branches that wither and perish.

Those who abide in Him and keep His words alive in their hearts will receive what they ask, for their will is joined with His divine will.
