(continued)👆👇

It was not the tree of knowledge that caused Adam's death, but because he broke the commandment. Therefore, the secret of the rebellion is breaking God's word to Adam that he should not eat, because God's word is true. And that tree is God's creation, and God's word will come true so it brought death, not life, it brought shame not honor; It means that he was stripped from his spiritual grace, the grace of the Holy Spirit. That is what we should know.
 
May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
