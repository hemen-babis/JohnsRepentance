2.The Revelation of the Lord’s Birth to the Shepherds

✍🏾In the quiet of the night, while the shepherds were keeping watch over their flocks in the fields, a light descended from heaven and dispelled the darkness. This divine light revealed the glory and majesty of the Lord. Then the angel of the Lord appeared to them and became both a messenger and a preacher, announcing great joy that would come to all people.

✍🏾The angel declared to them that Christ the Lord, who had come for their salvation, was born in the city of David. He gave them a sign so that they might recognize Him, saying:

👉🏾“You will find a baby wrapped in swaddling cloths and lying in a manger.”

Suddenly, a multitude of angels appeared, beholding at the same time the majesty and humility of the Lord. 

They saw Him in heaven, enthroned with the Father and the Holy Spirit, and they also beheld Him on earth, lying humbly in a manger.

Therefore, they glorified God, saying:

“Glory to God in the highest.”

And as they beheld Him on earth in humility, they offered fitting thanksgiving and praise, proclaiming:

“Peace on earth, good will toward men.”

After witnessing these great wonders, the shepherds did not remain silent. They hurried to Bethlehem according to the sign given to them, as it is written:

👉🏾“They found Mary and Joseph, and the baby lying in the manger. When they had seen Him, they made known what had been told them concerning this Child. And the shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all the things that they had heard and seen, just as it had been told them.”
(Luke 2:16–20)

The shepherds were the first believers to whom the good news was preached by the angel of the Lord. They became witnesses to the birth of Christ by proclaiming to others all that they had seen and heard.

Likewise, we too must go to Bethlehem, that is, to the Church, to seek the Lord who performed this great mystery in flesh and blood for the salvation of humanity. Let us come to know Him by receiving His holy Body and precious Blood, by believing in Him, and by honoring Him.

Therefore, let us continually give thanks in the Holy Liturgy, proclaiming:

“Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace to men of good will.”

3.The Magi and the Star

When our Lord was born, the miracle of His Nativity was revealed not only on earth but also in heaven. A new star appeared, visible to the whole world. Just as the angel announced the Lord’s birth to the shepherds, so the star alone proclaimed His birth to all nations.

Guided by this star, the wise men from the East, called the Magi, came to Jerusalem. King Herod and all the city were troubled by the news they heard, asking where the King of the Jews had been born.

According to the prophecy, the Magi proceeded to Bethlehem. The star they had seen went before them and stood over the place where the Child was. As the Gospel says:

“When they had come into the house, they saw the young Child with Mary His mother, and fell down and worshiped Him. And when they had opened their treasures, they presented gifts to Him: gold, frankincense, and myrrh.”
(Matthew 2:11)

The holy Fathers of the Church teach us the meaning of these gifts:

👉🏾Gold signifies the kingship of our Lord

👉🏾Frankincense signifies His priesthood

👉🏾Myrrh signifies His saving death

Through these gifts, the Magi confessed Christ as King, Priest, and Savior.
