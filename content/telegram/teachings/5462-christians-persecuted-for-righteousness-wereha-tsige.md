👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾#Christians #Persecuted #For #Righteousness (#wereha #Tsige)

(Blessed are those who are persecuted for #righteousness, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven, #Matthew 5:10)

(Part 4)

Beloved children in Christ,Grace and peace be multiplied to you from our Lord Jesus Christ. Today we turn our hearts to the teaching of our Savior, who said, “Blessed are those who are persecuted for righteousness’ sake, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven” (Matthew 5:10). Let us carefully consider what this Beatitude reveals to us about suffering, exile, and the ways in which the faithful are tested for the sake of holiness. 

This Beatitude is Also Interpreted as Exile

✍🏾Although this eighth Beatitude primarily speaks about suffering for righteousness (persecution), some Church scholars also understand it in relation to exile. Yet, they emphasize that persecution itself is a form of suffering a kind of martyrdom that may appear in different ways.

Persecution, therefore, can be divided into two main types:

👉🏾 Physical persecution

👉🏾 Spiritual persecution

✍🏾 Physical persecution refers to being forced to flee or move from one’s physical place of residence to another. It involves a literal change of location from one home, region, or even country to another due to faith, danger, or other pressures.

✍🏾Spiritual persecution refers to an inner or mental displacement a movement from one’s former beliefs, goals, or way of life to another. This occurs without any physical relocation but involves a deep transformation or struggle within the soul.

In some cases, persecution can be both physical and spiritual, where the believer suffers both outwardly in body and inwardly in spirit for the sake of righteousness.

The Fourf Persecution from the Perspective of Righteousness

From the viewpoint of righteousness, persecution can be understood in four distinct categories. Each reveals a mystery of the Christian journey how the soul moves between the world and God, between obedience and rebellion, between faith and apostasy.

1. The First Persecution: For Righteousness – The Spiritual Migration from the World to God

This is the persecution for righteousness, which takes place in the spirit. It is the inward struggle of a soul that turns away from the world and sets its course toward God. It is not fought with swords or prisons, but within the heart the battle between the flesh and the spirit, between pride and humility, between self-love and divine love.
This persecution is the foundation of Christianity.

2. The Second Persecution: Because of Righteousness Suffering for Living in the Truth

The second persecution is because of righteousness the suffering that comes upon those who follow the path of truth. It is the persecution endured by the apostles and early Christians, who believed in Christ and proclaimed His Gospel to the nations.They were reviled, imprisoned, beaten, and slain because they refused to deny the Lord. Throughout the history of the Church, countless believers have shared this same cross, enduring scorn, exile, and death for the sake of their faith.
