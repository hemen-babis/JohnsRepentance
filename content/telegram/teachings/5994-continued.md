Continued 👇🏾

👉🏾Concerning His birth from a virgin womb: and His name being called Emmanuel:
(Isaiah 7:14; Ezekiel 44:2; Matthew 1:22–23)

👉🏾About His birth in Bethlehem:(Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:1–6; Luke 2:4–6)

👉🏾About His birth from the family of Abraham:(Genesis 12:3; Genesis 22:18; Matthew 1:1; Romans 9:5)

👉🏾About His birth from the tribe of Judah:(Genesis 49:10; Luke 3:33; Hebrews 7:14)

✍🏾Therefore, according to the prophecies and the appointed time that was counted, God the Word, who was announced by the angel Gabriel, was born in Bethlehem at the completion of nine months and five days, of the Holy Virgin Mary, with her virginity fully preserved, for the salvation of the world.

✍🏾Emmanuel, who was born of the Holy Virgin Mary, is the Creator of the angels, and therefore the angels praised Him.

✍🏾Because He is the True Shepherd, the shepherds glorified God together with the angels, saying:

👉🏾“Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will toward men.”(Luke 2:14)

✍🏾Because He is the Creator of all creatures, the ox and the donkey recognized Him and warmed Him with their breath.(Isaiah 1:3)

✍🏾Because He is the King of kings, the Magi offered Him gifts of gold, frankincense, and myrrh.
Thanks be to our God, Emmanuel, who was born of the Virgin Mary and, by His birth, granted us life and salvation.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
