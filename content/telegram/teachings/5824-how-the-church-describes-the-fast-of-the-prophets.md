✍🏾The Division of Time during the Fast of the Prophets

According to the tradition of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church:

👉🏾Hidar 6 – Tahsas 7 or 13:
Zemene Astemhhro (Season of Instruction)

👉🏾Hidar 13 – Hidar 19:
Kidist (Holy Week)

👉🏾Hidar 20 – Hidar 26:
Mikurab (Synagogue Week)

👉🏾Hidar 27 – Tahsas 3:
Metsagu

👉🏾Tahsas 4 – Tahsas 6:
Debre Zeyt (three days only)

👉🏾Tahsas 7 – Tahsas 13:
Zemene Sibket

👉🏾Tahsas 14 – Tahsas 20:
Zemene Birhan (Season of Light)

👉🏾Tahsas 21 – Tahsas 27:
Zemene Nolawi (Season of the Shepherds)

👉🏾Tahsas 28 – Tahsas 29:
Lidet (Nativity Days)

✍🏾Meaning of Zemene Astemhro

It is a season set apart to instruct the faithful about the goodness and mercy of God, who forgives all our sins. During this time, believers are reminded that, just as God shows compassion toward us, we are called to forgive one another.

✍🏾Purpose of the Season of Astemhro

During this period:

The Church continually asks God for forgiveness on behalf of her children.

Believers repent and seek forgiveness for their sins.

Teaching is given abundantly, guiding the faithful toward spiritual renewal.

✍🏾The Sabbaths of the Season of Instruction

According to the writings of St. Yared, the Sundays of this season are:

1. Astemhro (E-tezekiro)

2. Astemhrot (Lotu Sbhat)

3. Mkurab (Synagogue – “God is perfect in His existence”)

4. Metsagu (Yibelu Israel)

5. Debre Zeyt (The Sabbath given for the rest of humankind)

These divisions were established by St. Yared in Dǝgwa Ze-Astemhro.The names of the four Sundays, except the first Astemhro , are the same as the Sundays of the Great Lent.

✍🏾Are the Sundays the Same as the Sundays of Lent(abiy tsom)?

The names of the Sundays match those of the Lenten season.
However, the content is different.

During the nights and services of these weeks:

the Psalms chanted,

the readings appointed for the Divine Liturgy, and

the teachings delivered

all differ in focus and mystery.

✍🏾Meaning of “Different in Mystery”

When we say that the teachings “differ in mystery,” we mean that their spiritual emphasis matches the appointed time.
However, the Church always teaches one unchanging foundation:

The eternal Word of God is proclaimed in every season.
