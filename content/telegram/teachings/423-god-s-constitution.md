A/ The ten words of the old testament 

The ten words of the old testament are the ten words that God made a covenant with mankind.

We believe that God has given His true word that by keeping these laws, we will receive grace and blessings from God, and that if we break them, we will suffer calamities. They are as follows.

1. I am God your God, you shall worship no other god before me (Exodus 22:7)

2. Do not take the name of God your God in vain; For God will surely punish him who makes his name worthless. (Exodus 20:7).

3. Consider the Sabbath day, that you may sanctify it. (Exodus 20:1-11)

4. Honor your father and mother; In the land that God will give you so that your life will be long. (Zech 20:12)

5. Do not kill a soul (Zech 20:13).

6. Do not commit adultery (Exodus 20:14)

7. Do not steal; (Exodus 20:15)

8. Do not bear false witness against your neighbor (Exodus 20:16).

9. Do not covet everything that belongs to your neighbor (Exodus 20:17).

10. Love your neighbor as yourself (Leviticus 19:18)

Explanation

God is the supreme king, the God of gods. He is the creator and ruler of the visible and invisible worlds. He has set forth a constitution that specifically governs human beings. Therefore, it is the duty of all to preserve what he has done, as no one can undo or improve it. These laws are divided into 10 articles, and 3 of them are related to God. They are:

1/ I am God your God. You shall not worship any other god before me.

2/ Don't take God's name, your God's name in vain; For God will surely punish him who makes his name worthless.

3/ Think of the Sabbath day, that you may sanctify it.

This revelation warns us that we should not give God's worship to idols or to fortune-telling. He is the only one who should be worshipped, because worship belongs to his nature and personality.

He alone was, and He alone is, and He alone is God who lives forever.

Therefore, we should not give the honor and worship that is due to him to a foreigner because there is no one who can compete with him.

He also warns us not to invoke his name in vain. God's name is only invoked in praise and in prayer;

The 3rd paragraph urges us to celebrate God's Sabbath, which God made as a day of rest for the glory of mankind, to make it a day of rest.

On this day, he warns us not to do any other physical activities than doing charity, listening and preaching the holy word.

But the other seven clauses are what everyone who is a human being should do for himself, human beings and the like, should do and fulfill. And they are: 

1/ Honor your father and mother.

That means, think about the expenses that parents spent on your life, food, health; It means that you have to know what you owe them and be greatful for what they did for you. 

His human wisdom in honoring his parents; It is revealed that he is a higher creation. It means that all other creatures raise their children by God's natural duty, but later they never help their children, and in fact they don't even know each other. Just as parents raised children with great care, if children helped their parents well, they respected their nature and revealed their dignity.
