This act of kindness underscores the promise of God to those who trust in Him and honor His servants. The archangel’s words call us to live lives adorned with good deeds, sacrificial love, and almsgiving, for such acts prepare us for the eternal kingdom.

The Legacy and Countless Miracles of St. Michael

The miracles of St. Michael are countless, a testament to his role as the helper of the faithful and a reminder of the power of God working through His heavenly servants. His intercessions are a source of strength for the Church, his guardianship a shield against the forces of darkness, and his example a call to humility and steadfast devotion to the Almighty.

A Call to Honor St. Michael

As we celebrate the feast of this glorious archangel, let us remember the lessons of his life and deeds. Like Durataos and Tewablet, let us honor St. Michael with faith, even in times of trial, trusting in his intercession and the providence of God. Let us strive to emulate his unwavering loyalty to God, his humility, and his zeal for righteousness.

May the prayers of St. Michael the Archangel fortify us against all adversities, guide us on the path of salvation, and lead us to the eternal joy of God’s kingdom. To Him be glory, now and forevermore. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
