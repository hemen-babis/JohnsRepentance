(Continued)☝️

This, dear faithful, is the heart of our confession: that God became man so that man might partake in the divine life. It is a mystery not merely to be understood but to be lived, as it transforms all who embrace it through faith, leading us into the embrace of the ineffable, the eternal, and the holy. Thus, let us approach this mystery not as mere spectators but as participants in the divine nature, walking in the light of Christ’s truth and love.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
