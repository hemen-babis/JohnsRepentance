4. Jesus Christ is the creator

(continued part)👆👇

✍The Apostle St. Paul said that the worlds were created with the word "let it be". The Word of the Trinity is the Son, so we must realize that the Word of the Son is not only for the Son himself, but also for the Father and the Holy Spirit. St. Paul also said, "He is the type of the invisible God.

✍The visible and the invisible, whether they are thrones or lordships or principalities or powers in heaven and on earth, everything was created by him before all creation. All things were created by him and for him." trhough this, he explained to us that Jesus Christ is the creator. (Colossians 1:15-16)

✍In addition, when St. Paul reinforces his thoughts, he mentions what was written by the prophet David and pointed out that what David wrote was about Jesus, he said about his son, "You laid the foundations of the earth from the beginning, and the heavens are the work of your hands. They will perish, but you will remain steadfast." They all age like clothes, you wrap them up like a cloak and they change, but you are you and your years never end," he wrote to us. (Hebrews 1:10-12, Psalm 101:25-27)

✍As the prophet Isaiah said, "Thus says the Lord who made you from the womb and redeems you: I am the one who stretched out the heavens by myself and established the earth. Who was with me?" It says" (Isaiah 44:24) It is God who said that I created the world alone. And in the name God there is a trinity of names: Father, Son, Holy Spirit. However, their trinity is in body, in name, in task; but they are one in will and power in creating  and passing the world.  So It has been said 'I created alone' . We can understand from this word that when God created the worlds, there was no worker or collaborator, and that the worlds were created by one Will and Power as three names, tasks, and bodies. And the name God expresses Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, which usually speaks of Unity.

👉Therefore, Jesus Christ who is called God together with the Father and the Holy Spirit, and who existed before ages, is now, and will live through ages, is not a creature, but a creator. As it is said "I am the Alpha and the Omega, the Lord God who is and who was and who is to come." (Revelation 1:8)
