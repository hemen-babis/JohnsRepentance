(Continued) 👆

Morality is the practical interpretation of what we believe. Believers and demons, as they are creatures of God, have testified that Christ is the Son of God. As written in the Holy Gospel, "And the unclean spirits, whenever they saw Him, fell down before Him and cried out, saying, “You are the Son of God.'" (Mark 3:11) "And when He had looked around at them with anger, being grieved by the hardness of their hearts, He said to the man, “Stretch out your hand.” And he stretched it out, and his hand was restored as whole as the other. Then the Pharisees went out and immediately plotted with the Herodians against Him, how they might destroy Him. But Jesus withdrew with His disciples to the sea. And a great multitude from Galilee followed Him, and from Judea and Jerusalem and Idumea and beyond the Jordan; and those from Tyre and Sidon, a great multitude, when they heard how many things He was doing, came to Him" (Mark 5:5-8).
 
In his message, the apostle Saint James said, "You believe that there is one God. You do well. Even the demons believe—and tremble!" (James 2:19). He who keeps God's law and order will be saved from eternal judgment.

The Christian, who is the seed of Christ, should be the solution to the war of separation of evil in our world today, with his good orthodox morals. "Flee from evil and do good and you will live forever" (Psalm 37:27)

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
