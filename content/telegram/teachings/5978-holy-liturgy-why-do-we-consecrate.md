👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾(#Holy #Liturgy )Why Do We #Consecrate?

(Part 6)

Dear followers of the Orthodox teachings on John's Repentance website .we greet you with the peace of the Lord.Today,based on the topic mentioned above, we present to you the following important lesson.We encourage everyone to read it carefully and learn from it

✍🏾We celebrate the Holy Liturgy because our Lord Jesus Christ said:

👉🏾“Where two or three are gathered together in My name, there I am in the midst of them.”
(Matthew 18:20)

✍🏾Our gathering together,founded on the eternal promise of our Lord, becomes the means through which we receive His grace and divine help.

✍🏾Just as the Holy Liturgy is the true image of the one Heavenly Church, the gathering of believers is the first and essential foundation of the Holy Liturgy.

✍🏾The liturgical order that is fulfilled in the Holy Liturgy first existed in the world of the holy angels.On earth, this same heavenly order is fulfilled when the faithful gather together in the temple of God.(Isaiah 6:1)

✍🏾We now reflect on the deep reverence and spiritual understanding with which the ancient Church,in the time of the apostles, regarded the gathering of believers.Through faithful obedience to the word of the Lord, the Church received its order of worship from the heavenly hosts, and this sacred order of thanksgiving was handed down through Holy Tradition, becoming the foundation of the life of the Church.

✍🏾The Ancient Tradition of Gathering Believers in the Holy Liturgy

✍🏾The practice of gathering together to glorify God and offer sacrifice is already found in the Old Testament. However, the true foundation of the Christian Holy Liturgy was established when our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ celebrated the Passover with His disciples on the night before His suffering.(Luke 22:7)

✍🏾Just as God chose the sons of Jacob as the foundation of the congregation of Israel, Christ gathered the apostles and made them the foundation of the Church. He seated them at the table of the Kingdom of Heaven, forming the new assembly of believers, the true Israel.
(Luke 22:29–30)

✍🏾Through this act, the unity of the Church was established in and through the apostles. Having received understanding of the divine mysteries through the grace of the Holy Spirit, the apostles gathered the faithful and celebrated the Holy Liturgy together as one body.(Acts 20:7)

✍🏾This practice was firmly rooted in the apostolic Church.The Apostle Paul clearly speaks of believers coming together as a necessary condition for worship, saying:

👉🏾“When you come together as a church…”(1Corinthians 11:18)
He further explains that this order was not human invention, but divine tradition:

👉🏾“For I received from the Lord that which I also delivered to you.”
(1Corinthians 11:23)

✍🏾This reveals the mystery of the gathering of believers in the Holy Liturgy, a sacred practice that has been preserved and handed down through Holy Tradition as an essential part of the life of the Church.

✍🏾The Fathers of the Church, who were disciples and successors of the apostles, faithfully received this apostolic tradition and handed it down directly to the generations that followed. They confirmed its truth by teaching that the faith of the Church and the Holy Liturgy are inseparable, for the teaching of the Church is expressed in the Liturgy, and the Liturgy, in turn, proclaims and confirms the teaching of the Church.

✍🏾During the time of these apostolic Fathers, all believers took part in the Holy Liturgy and approached the Holy Mysteries. Even those who were sick were not excluded, but were brought so that they might receive and share in the grace of the Liturgy.

✍🏾Anyone who deliberately missed the Holy Liturgy without illness or serious reason was regarded as having separated himself from the life of the Church.For this reason, church buildings were constructed to accommodate the full celebration of the Holy Mysteries and the participation of the entire community. This ancient understanding of church architecture continues to be clearly seen in our Church today.
