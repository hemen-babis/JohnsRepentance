👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾The Fast of #Nineveh
Who Is #Nineveh? 

(Part 2)

Dear followers of the Orthodox teachings on John's Repentance website .we greet you with the peace of the Lord.Today , based on the topic mentioned above, we present to you the following important lesson.We encourage everyone to read it carefully and learn from it.

✍🏾The Fast of Nineveh was a fast of repentance observed by the people of the city of Nineveh. By the time of the holy Prophet Jonah, the wickedness and evil deeds of Nineveh had risen up to heaven before God.

✍🏾 Because of this, God sent the Prophet Jonah to Nineveh to warn its people, calling them to repentance so that they might be saved from the coming wrath.

✍🏾 Nineveh was located about 800 kilometers from Gath-Hepher, the place where the Prophet Jonah was born and raised. Although Jonah first withdrew from his mission, he later obeyed the command of God. As the Scripture says:

👉🏾“Jonah began to enter the city a day’s journey, and he cried out and said, ‘Yet three days, and Nineveh shall be overthrown.’”(Jonah 3:4)
According to this verse, the Prophet Jonah went through the city of Nineveh, proclaiming the word of God.

✍🏾 The response of the people is written clearly in the Holy Scripture:“So the people of Nineveh believed God, proclaimed a fast, and put on sackcloth, from the greatest of them to the least of them.”(Jonah 3:5)

👉🏾Through repentance, fasting, humility, and turning back to God, the people of Nineveh were reconciled with Him and were saved from destruction and divine wrath.For this reason, the Church remembers this repentance and calls it the Fast of Nineveh.

👉🏾 What Was the Sin of the People of Nineveh?

The sin of the people of Nineveh is not listed one by one in the prophecy of Jonah. Instead, it is described in a general but very serious way. God says:

“Arise, go to Nineveh, that great city, and cry against it; for their wickedness has come up before Me.”(Jonah 1:2)

✍🏾 The Scripture does not specify their sins in detail in the Book of Jonah. However, the words “their wickedness has come up before Me” clearly reveal the greatness and severity of their sin. A sin that reaches heaven is not small, hidden, or accidental.

✍🏾 Although their sins are not listed in Jonah, they are described more clearly in other prophetic books.

👉🏾 The Witness of the Prophet Nahum

✍🏾 The Prophet Nahum speaks openly about the sins of Nineveh. He begins his prophecy by saying:

“The burden against Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.”(Nahum 1:1)

One of the major sins of Nineveh described by Nahum is conspiring against God.

👉🏾“There is one who plots evil against the Lord, a wicked counselor.”(Nahum 1:11)

✍🏾Conspiring Against God

The kings of Nineveh during the time of Prophet Jonah humbled themselves, repented, and saved their city from destruction through fasting and prayer. But the rulers who lived before and after that generation became known for cruelty, arrogance, and rebellion against God.

✍🏾They trusted in idols, glorified violence, and opposed the will of the Most High. In their pride, they behaved as though God could be challenged, ignored, or replaced.
This is what it means to plot against God. Not only with words, but with actions, systems, and attitudes that deny His authority.

✍🏾A Warning for All Generations

This lesson is not written for Nineveh alone.
Those who oppose God through injustice, pride, corruption, cruelty, or rebellion should take warning. Whether in leadership or among the people, no one escapes accountability before God.

✍🏾 Do not miss the opportunity to repent and be reconciled with God. The leaders of Nineveh became an example by humbling themselves first and leading their people in repentance. Through this, they saved both their city and their nation from the wrath of God.

✍🏾 No matter how great the sin, do not allow it to push you away from repentance. God does not hate the sinner, but the sin. What destroys us is not falling, but refusing to return.
