3. Kotiba and Her Family The Sin of Judging the Innocent

Finally, we come to Kotiba and her family, whose story stands as a warning to every soul that judges without understanding. When our Lady, weary and hungry, sought food and water during her exile, they looked upon her and mocked her, saying:

“You are fair in appearance, but if your heart were good, you would not wander in exile you would dwell in palaces among kings.”

In their blindness, They despised the humble Virgin who carried the Lord of Glory in her arms. And behold, divine justice answered their cruelty: they were transformed into monkeys, their beauty turned to shame, and even the dogs of their own house chased them away.

VI. Conclusion: The Glory of the Persecuted

Beloved, in this month of Tsige, let us honor the Virgin Mary, the Redeemer, and all the saints who bore persecution for the sake of righteousness. 

Persecution for righteousness is the crown of the faithful. It purifies the soul, strengthens virtue, and prepares the believer for eternal communion with God. Let us, therefore, persevere in truth, reject sin, and remember the promises of Christ, who blessed the persecuted and established their reward in heaven.
