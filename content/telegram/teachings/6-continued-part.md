3.  When God willed to create Eve, who will be his helper for Adam: “And the LORD God said, it is not good that the man should be alone; Let's create a suitable assistant for him” (Genesis 2:18). Here also, "God said" refers to oneness, while "Let’s create" refers to more than one.

4.  Another one, when the sons of Noah rebelled against God and started building a tower in the field of Sanatsor: "God said, Come, let us go down, as if they had lost their language there so that one would not hear the words of the other". Here, the word 'God said' indicates unity and the word 'let's go. down' indicates that there is more than one.

So, the verses we mentioned above are saying that God is one in character, divinity, creating and sustaining this world, and not one in body. However, they do not clearly explain that it is three, except that it is not physically one. However, when we continue reading the bible through, it is known that God is three in body. For example:-

5.  When God revealed himself to Abraham, "At noon, while he was sitting at the door of his tent, God appeared to him through the tree of Meri. He lifted up his eyes and saw three men standing before him. When he saw them, he ran from the door of the tent to meet them. He bowed to the earth. He said, "Lord, if I have found favor in your sight, I beg you not to let your servant pass over me." "And he lifted up his eyes and behold, he saw three men standing before him," and the saying that God is three in person (Trinity). And in the speech of Abraham, "O Lord, if I have found favor in your sight, I beseech you not to let your servant pass away from me." This indicates that God is one in character, will and power.

6.  In addition; When the prophet Isaiah explained the trinity, "I saw God sitting on a long and high throne, and the area of his garment filled the temple. ..And the seraphim were standing above him. And they were shouting to each other: "Holy, Holy, Holy, the Lord of hosts, the whole earth is full of his glory." The word refers to the unity and trinity of the Trinity. (Isaiah 6:3)

The New Testament

1.  The New Testament is an age of revelation. And it is God Himself who is revealed. The revelation that has been revealed in this age is very unique and clear compared to the revelation that was revealed to others in different ways previously. St. Paul explained this as follows: "Since ancient times, God has spoken to our fathers through prophets in many ways, and He has spoken to us at the end of this age through His Son, whom He made the heir of all things, and through whom He created the worlds." (Hebrews 1:1)

The physical word Jesus Christ taught in different ways that God is three in body, task, name, and unity, and one in character, will, and power, creating and sustaining the world. Occasionally, the trinity of God is revealed in an audible and visible body. For example, at the time of the baptism, on Matthew 3:16-17, he made it clear that there are three bodies: the Father who made a sound from heaven, the Holy Spirit who came down in the form of a dove, and the Son who stood in the middle of the Jordan in human flesh, and the three bodies have three names: Father, Son, Holy Spirit. And to see how the names were distinguished-  In heaven, "This is my beloved son, in whom I am well pleased." His fatherhood is known and called Father. And he said that Jesus is my son, so he is called son. It is clearly written that the one who came down in the form of a dove is the Holy Spirit as the Spirit of God, so we call the Trinity three by name. 

2.  Also, "Go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you" is a powerful word that expresses unity and trinity indicating their name. (Matthew 28:19)
