👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾What #Should We #Pray?

(part 7)

Beloved brethren in Christ, children of the Holy Church, peace and grace be with you. Today we turn our hearts to a most important matter: What should we pray? For prayer is the breath of the soul, the ladder to heaven, and the key by which the faithful unlock the treasures of God’s mercy.

The Fathers of the Church, guided by the Holy Spirit, have preserved for us the prayers that sanctify each day, prayers that nourish both monks in their solitude and the faithful in their homes. Let us, then, consider these treasures of prayer one by one.

1. The Psalms of David

The Psalms are the school of prayer. From the monasteries of the desert to the homes of the faithful, the Psalms are repeated daily. The Fathers ordered that the Psalms be divided according to the days of the week, so that all 150 must be prayed regularly:

The Psalms of David, like all prayers, contain a prayer appointed for each day.Let us now take a closer look at them:

Monday → Psalms 1–30

Tuesday → Psalms 31–60

Wednesday → Psalms 61–80

Thursday → Psalms 81–110

Friday → Psalms 111–130

Saturday → Psalms 131–150

Sunday → The Prayer of the Prophets and the Song of Solomon

If one cannot recite all, the Fathers permitted the recitation of “One King”—that is, ten psalms per day.

For example, Psalms 1–10 on Monday, Psalms 31–40 on Tuesday, and so forth.Those who are unable to recite everything instead choose certain hymns and offer them each day.

2,Praises of Mary (Wudase Maryam)

No day should pass without the praise of the Holy Virgin. If possible, the Praises of Mary should be recited all the seven days praises ; if not, it is fitting to recite them daily. After completing the regular daily prayers, it is proper to recite the praises of mary for the day and thus partake in the blessings of Our Lady.”

The monks of Waldeba, the teachers and students of the Church, and all faithful souls lift up the “praises of  Mary” daily. The Virgin is the Covenant of Mercy, and through her intercession we are strengthened. Let us, therefore, make it our habit to greet her in prayer, whether at night before sleep or in the morning when we rise.

3. The Gospel of John

The daily reading of the Holy Gospel, especially the Gospel of John, Dividing it into chapters and reading it every day is counted as prayer itself. For in these words we hear the voice of Christ.The Fathers pray this prayer diligently each day.

4. Other Prayers

There are also other prayers such as the Praise of God, the remembrance of Golgotha, the appearances of Christ and His Mother, the prayers of the Book of Prayer, the Psalms, and the miracles of God. Each one prays according to their ability. But at the very least, every Christian should pray the Psalms and the Praises of Mary.
