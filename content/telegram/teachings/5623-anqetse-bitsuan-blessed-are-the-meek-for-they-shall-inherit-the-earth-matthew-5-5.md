👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾(#Anqetse #bitsuan) #Blessed are the #meek,for they shall #inherit the #earth" (Matthew 5:5 )

( Part 9)

Dear followers of Orthodox teachings . we greet you in the name of God. Today, we share an important lesson on the blessedness of the meek, and we encourage you to read, reflect, and learn from it.

✍🏾What Is Meekness?

Who are the meek, and why are they called blessed? Many people misunderstand meekness, thinking it is a sign of weakness, ignorance, or passivity. This is entirely mistaken. In truth, the meek are wise, strong, and full of spiritual understanding.

Meekness is a sign of strength, maturity, and self-control. It manifests in two ways: spiritual meekness and personal meekness.

Spiritual meekness is rooted in the fear of God and the wisdom that comes from Him.

Personal meekness is related to moral competence, emotional maturity, social conduct, and knowledge. It includes patience with others, self-control in difficult times, humility in leadership, listening to others, and accepting when someone has a better idea.

While people who do not believe in God may exhibit personal meekness, our focus here is on spiritual meekness, the meekness that is blessed in the eyes of God.

✍🏾The Meek Are Wise

Our Lord said, “Behold, I send you out as sheep in the midst of wolves; therefore be wise as serpents and harmless as doves” (Matthew 10:16). Spiritual meekness combines wisdom and humility. The Apostle James also teaches, “Who is wise and understanding among you? Let him show by his good conduct his works in the meekness of wisdom” (James 3:13).

He further describes heavenly wisdom: “But the wisdom from above is first pure, then gentle, easy to be entreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without hypocrisy” (James 3:17). Thus, true meekness is a treasure, full of wisdom and spiritual insight.

✍🏾Characteristics of the Meek

1. The Meek Put God First:

The meek put God first in all things. They do this out of deep love for God and complete trust in Him. As the wise Solomon said, “In all your ways acknowledge Him, and He will direct your paths” (Proverbs 3:6), the meek place God first in every aspect of their lives. Our Lord also taught, “But seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things will be added to you” (Matthew 6:33). The meek prioritize the kingdom of God, trusting that everything else they need will be provided. In this perfect faith, they rely fully on God for all their needs. Jesus Christ said, “Blessed is everyone who does not stumble in Me” (Matthew 11:6; Luke 7:23), showing that His salvation is for those who believe meekly and wholeheartedly in Him.
