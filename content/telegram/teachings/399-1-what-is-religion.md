1.  What is Religion?

(continued part, part 3)

Religion is given once by God, because it is said that "religion was given once and for all to the saints." (Jude 3) The food varies from time to time, but the content is the same. For example, a baby is given milk and soft food when he is a baby, and solid food when he grows up, but his body nature remains the same and does not change.
As we received religion from God, people's role is only to accept it and use it to inherit eternal life. This means that no individual or entity in this world has the authority to add or subtract from the truth (religion) given by God. Even synodoses do not add or subtract from religion, but make important decisions to strengthen religion and reduce canons. This is what happened at the councils of Nicaea, Constantinople, and Ephesus. By identifying (condemning) the heretics who tried to change the religion of the church, they confirmed the true religion and did not make a new religious decision. The 318 fathers who gathered at the Council of Nicaea made a decision regarding the day on which the Easter is celebrated because it was not a matter of religion, but of the celebration of the day of the celebration of religion. As for religion, they confirmed what they had accepted and did not make a new decision.

Therefore, religion in the time of the Father, in the time of the Torah, in the time of the Gospel, the condition of its nutrition varies according to the ability and understanding of people of each time, but the content (theme) is the same. "Since ancient times, God has spoken to our fathers in many ways through the prophets, and He has spoken to us at the end of this age in His Son, who has made all the heirs and created the ages." Heb. 1:1-2 The religion of the holy angels, the father, Adam, Noah, Abraham, Moses, David, the prophets, and later the apostles and martyrs, the righteous and the scholars; is one.

Therefore religion is given once; It means that it does not need any reformation or change because it has always been (without reduction or remainder). They accept the religion, protect it, and inherit it, not renew it or improve it. The religion given to her by God, which our forefathers accepted from Him and lived in, is perfect and has been given to us once, so she does not age.

As we received religion from God, people's role is only to accept it and use it to inherit eternal life. This means that no individual or body in this world has the authority to add or subtract from the truth (religion) given by God. Religion is one

The word that religion is one is a truth that does not raise the question of how to understand that there is one creator. Religion means accepting your Creator and trusting in His laws. And the creator is one in nature. Therefore, belief in the existence of one creator and the performance of worship worthy of his nature should be in one system. This is what we mean by religion. In short, religion means accepting the existence of a creator and being faithful to the accepted laws of the creator. So if it is said to be religion to accept that there is a creator and leader of the worlds, and if it is believed that the creator is one, then accepting that one creator and being subject to the law can be only in one system (religion),  not in many systems or religion.

When the Apostle St. Paul said that religion is one as the creator is one, he said, "There is one Lord, one religion, one baptism, who is above all, who works in all, who lives in all, and who is the Father of all." (Eph 4:5)
