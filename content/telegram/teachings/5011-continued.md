VII. The Temple of the Living God

Unlike the golden lampstand of the Old Covenant, which was extinguished at dawn, the Virgin bore the eternal Light that never fades (Ex. 25:31–40). Unlike Solomon’s temple, which could be destroyed, she became the true temple of the living God, indestructible, incorruptible, and filled with grace.

St. Ephrem testifies: she is “the temple of the living God, a temple that cannot be destroyed.”

VIII. Preserved by the Holy Spirit

From her mother’s womb, the Virgin was preserved by the Holy Spirit for her holy calling. She is the chalice of the whole world, the vessel from which we drink the sweetness of salvation, the glory that flows from the Holy Trinity.

IX. Our Intercession Through Her

Therefore, we cry out:

O Virgin, pray that we may not be deprived of the grace and glory of Your Son.

O Lady, intercede for the forgiveness of our sins.

O Most Pure One, guard us under the light of Your Child, who was born for the salvation of the world.

For if He dwelt in you and came forth for our salvation, surely your prayers are a strong fortress for us.

Conclusion

Let us then praise the Virgin not for herself alone, but because she became the vessel of our salvation, the mother of our Lord Jesus Christ, the temple of the living God, the second heaven, and the joy of all creation.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
