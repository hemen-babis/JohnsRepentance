👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾The Glorious #Ministry of the #Holy #Angels

(Part 8)

Beloved children in Christ,Let us open our hearts to understand the ministry of the holy angels The holy angels are pure and faithful servants of God, glorifying Him without ceasing and carrying out His will among humankind. From the time of creation through both the Old and New Testaments, they have revealed God’s love, guided His people, and protected those who walk in His ways.

The foremost ministry of the holy angels is to glorify and praise God, their Creator. Their unceasing worship reveals both their purity and their perfect obedience to the will of the Almighty.

When they praise, they proclaim the holiness and majesty of the Lord:

“Holy, holy, holy are You, Lord of spirits; the whole earth is full of Your glory.”(1 Enoch 9:39; Isaiah 6:2–4)

As the Book of Revelation also declares:

“You are worthy, our Lord and God, to receive glory and honor and power, for You created all things; by Your will they exist and were created.”(Revelation 4:11)

Saint John, in his vision, describes the multitude of angels surrounding the heavenly throne, joining in praise of the Lamb who was slain:

“Then I looked, and I heard the voice of many angels around the throne and the living creatures and the elders; and the number of them was ten thousand times ten thousand. They said with a loud voice, ‘Worthy is the Lamb who was slain to receive power and riches and wisdom and strength and honor and glory and blessing!’ And every creature in heaven, on earth, under the earth, and in the sea said, ‘Blessing and honor and glory and power be to Him who sits on the throne and to the Lamb forever and ever.’”(Revelation 5:11–14)

✍🏾 In addition to their ministry of praise, the holy angels reveal the power, goodness, and mercy of their Creator to His creation. This divine service is especially shown in their relationship with Adam and his descendants, for through them, humanity witnesses the care and presence of God among His people.

The Ministry of the Angels in the Old Covenant

When our father Adam was expelled from Paradise because of his sin, the holy angels helped him to repent and comforted him with the hope of salvation.

👉🏾The Testimony of Enoch:
Enoch spoke about the ministry of the holy angels, saying,

“There my eyes saw the angels dwelling with them, and their resting place with the saints, praying, interceding, and praying for the children of men.”
(Enoch 9:22–23)

👉🏾 The Time of Noah:When the world was about to be destroyed, the holy angels assisted our father Noah in the work of building the ark.

👉🏾The Time of Abraham:Our father Abraham, the father of many nations, was always under angelic protection after leaving Haran. When he sent his servant, “Ibor,” to Syria, to the house of Bethuel, Abraham said,

“The Lord, the God of heaven, will send His angel before you.”
(Genesis 24:7)

👉🏾The Time of Jacob:
Jacob said:-“The Lord, who has fed me all my life long and who has redeemed me from all evil, bless the lads.”(Genesis 48:16)God sent His angel to save and protect Jacob throughout his life.

👉🏾 The Time of Moses and Israel:
God revealed to Moses that Israel was led by His angel to the Promised Land:

“Behold, I send an angel before you to guard you on your way and to bring you into the place I have prepared. Pay attention to him and obey his voice, for My name is in him.”(Exodus 23:20–21)
