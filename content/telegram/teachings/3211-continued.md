(Continued)☝️

In embracing these teachings and practices during the Fast of Assumption, we not only honor the Virgin Mary but also seek to emulate her purity, obedience, and faithfulness. Let this season be a time of spiritual introspection, divine encounter, and sanctification, as we aspire to the holiness exemplified by our Most Holy Lady, the Theotokos.

May the blessings of our Lady, the Holy Virgin Mary, encompass you all, guiding us towards the eternal joy and the everlasting peace of her Divine Son. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
