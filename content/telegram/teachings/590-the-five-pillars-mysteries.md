The Five Pillars Mysteries

(Part 12)

5. Mystery of Resurrection of (from) the dead (Con'd)

On Friday, while our Lord was being crucified, the thief on the right, Fyatawi Zeyman, saw our Lord performing 3 miracles in heaven and 4 on earth, and believed that He was truly God. And he said to our Lord, Lord, remember me when you come into your kingdom; Luke 23:42. Our Lord said, "Verily I say unto thee, To day thou shalt be with me in paradise." Luke 23:43 The gate of heaven, which was closed for five thousand five hundred years (5500), was opened for the mortal Zeyman. Therefore, this is a proof that it is heaven where we rest in the soul.

What makes our fathers' life special  is that they examined themselves without wasting the time of salvation that was given to them, repented and became the heirs of God's kingdom. The benefits of mystical penance are measured from this point of view because the grace of the Holy Spirit, which is enough to fulfill all the mysteries, is obtained through repentance. Another  example is the story of the lost son in Luke 15, who was able to return to his father's house after he repented, is an example of this. Before it is too late for us, may His holy will allow us to repent of our sins and make us heirs to His kingdom. "Remember now thy Creator in the days of thy youth" Ecclesiastes 12:1

May God's mother, the virgin, and the cross remain glorious, Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
