About #Paradise and the #tree of #knowledge:

👉Question: - Where is/was the garden or Eden? What is the tree/fruit that they ate?

Dear and respected members who follow the orthodox education and spiritual service that we convey on the John's Repentance website; Based on the question mentioned in above, we have sent you the following reply lesson, so we entrust you to read and learn from it.

✍Answer: Paradise, as we understand from its name, means a place that is unique, pleasant and satisfying to the spirit, restful, special, and naturally blessed with unique natural grace. This is what we call Paradise, and  it was given to Adam to inherit.
 
Today, when we see a place where plants and birds abound, where the whole earth is full of flowers,  we are amazed and we call the place a "Paradise". 
 
When God gave Paradise to our father Adam and our mother Eve, there was no suffering, no pain, no hunger and thirst for water, no going up and down, no tiredness in work; They lived happily for a thousand years, and if they were lucky, after a thousand years, God renewed their nature, and they would have lived for a thousand years.
 
In the midst of that, after God appointed them to rule over everything, to lead, to enjoy everything, without depriving them of anything except from the tree of knowledge which was in the middle of Paradise. So, when we think about heaven or the tree of knowledge, what we ask should not be  research questions like what kind of wood is it? Where did it grow? or What does it look like? Our dear member, Paradise is an independent, holy land or world. Apart from this, it is not Religion to think about how far Paradise is from this earth by the measure of holy angels, or how far it is from this world we live in.

In Noah's time, the ark was given to Noah until the flood passes or dries up, so that Noah would not be destroyed by the flood. The ship being on Mount Ararat, there was something that the scholars or the fathers fathers have researched about how close the ship's direction indicator was to heaven. 
 
Because it is the Holy Spirit who explains everything to them, our fathers have documented it's direction and distance from this world. If you, our inquirer, have in-depth knowledge of religion or holy books and have asked for research, contact us at our address and we will discuss it in detail.
 
But with such a question, since not everyone has the same spiritual knowledge, it is better to explain it as a generalization  that in paradise  our father Adam and our mother Eve glorified it as a child, and God's grace was abundant for them. Today, it is known how much pain and suffering we go through to get what we need for food, but it was not like that for them. In that paradise, God made Adam the king and made all the animals to live in subjection to him, that is what we call paradise. But then the grace of childhood that Adam received from God was stripped/taken away from him due to sin he committed, he was expelled from Paradise and its door was closed to him.
 
God ordered him to inform them that God is the creator and Adam is a creature, that there is a ruler and a subject, and that he is a creature that lives in law and order. He said, "Don't eat this, you will die." This is a law, an order. It was forbidden to eat from that tree because its nature brought death to Adam.
