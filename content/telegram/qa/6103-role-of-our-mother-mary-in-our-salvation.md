👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾role of our #mother #Mary in our #salvation

✍🏾Question :  What is the role of our mother Mary in our salvation?

✍🏾Answer : My beloved child, may God’s grace and the boundless mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ ever dwell within your heart. Your question regarding the role of our Most Holy Mother Mary in our salvation is of profound spiritual importance, touching upon one of the most cherished doctrines of our Orthodox Faith. It is a question that requires deep reverence and careful elucidation.

As we journey together in understanding the mysteries of our faith, let us turn our gaze to the Mother of God, the Theotokos, for her role is central and indispensable in God's divine plan for humanity's salvation, though always understood in relation to her Son, our Lord Jesus Christ.

1. The Theotokos: The Ark of the New Covenant and the Gate of Incarnation

Firstly and most fundamentally, the Most Holy Theotokos is the Mother of God (Theotokos in Greek, which means "God-bearer" or "she who gave birth to God"). This is her most exalted title, affirmed by the Third Ecumenical Council in Ephesus (431 A.D.). She is not merely the mother of Christ's human nature, but the mother of the Person who is both fully God and fully Man.

Her role in our salvation begins with her "Fiat" (Let it be done), her humble and free acceptance of God's will at the Annunciation: "Behold the handmaid of the Lord; be it unto me according to thy word." (Luke 1:38, Septuagint) Without her "yes," without her pure and obedient vessel, the Incarnation of God the Word would not have occurred in the manner God ordained. She became the living Ark of the New Covenant, carrying within her womb the very Creator of the Universe, who took flesh from her pure blood.

2. The New Eve: Undoing Disobedience

The Holy Fathers often refer to the Theotokos as the New Eve. Just as the first Eve, through disobedience, brought sin and death into the world, leading to our condemnation, so too did the New Eve, through her perfect obedience and purity, become instrumental in bringing forth the New Adam, Christ, who would conquer sin and death. Her purity of heart and mind, her absolute devotion to God, made her worthy to become the vessel for salvation. We believe that she was chosen and preserved by divine grace, and that through the indwelling of the Holy Spirit at the Annunciation, she was made perfectly pure and sinless, a fitting dwelling place for God.

3. Her Participation in the Economy of Salvation

Our Mother Mary did not merely give birth to Christ and then disappear. She actively participated in the economy of salvation:

👉🏾At the Nativity: She brought God into the world.

👉🏾At the Presentation in the Temple: She offered Him to God and heard the prophecy of Simeon concerning the sword that would pierce her soul (Luke 2:35), foreshadowing her suffering at the foot of the Cross.

👉🏾At the Wedding at Cana: She interceded on behalf of others, prompting Christ's first public miracle: "Whatever He says to you, do it." (John 2:5) This is a profound lesson for us on obedience to Christ.

👉🏾At the Foot of the Cross: She stood steadfast, enduring unimaginable pain, truly sharing in the suffering of her Son for our salvation (John 19:25-27). This co-suffering, not co-redemption, further highlights her intimate participation.

4. Our Intercessor and Mother

Having completed her earthly course, our Mother Mary was assumed into heaven, body and soul (her Dormition). From her exalted position, she continues her maternal care for all humanity, especially those who call upon her Son. She is a most powerful intercessor before the throne of God. We seek her prayers, not because she grants salvation, but because her prayers, as the Mother of God, are especially potent and efficacious. She is our advocate, our solace, and a fount of mercy, always pointing us towards Christ, her Son and our God.
