👉 About guidance from #spiritual #father 

👉🏾 Question፦ Also, my previous priest would tell me it was a sin to break the post Eucharist cultural traditions (such as touching water) and was something I needed to confess. If our spiritual father mistakes cultural traditions as doctrines or dogmas, is that perhaps a sign to get another spiritual father that is perhaps more knowledgeable about Orthodoxy?

👉 Answer፦ Dear Nathan,

Your concerns about the boundaries between cultural traditions and ecclesiastical doctrine are very valid and reflect a desire for a pure and undiluted practice of Orthodoxy. The role of a spiritual father is indeed pivotal, providing guidance and interpretation of the Church's teachings and traditions. It is crucial that this guidance be deeply rooted in the authentic teaching and spiritual wisdom of the Church.

If you find that certain practices, particularly those that are culturally rather than doctrinally based, are being enforced as if they were dogmatic or doctrinal requirements, it is important to seek clarification. The Church, in its wisdom, differentiates between tradition (which can be culturally specific) and dogma (which is universally applicable and doctrinally sound). When cultural traditions are mistakenly presented as dogma, it can lead to confusion and spiritual burden.

Approaching your spiritual father for a dialogue is a recommended first step. Express your concerns respectfully and ask for clarification on the teachings. If after such discussions, you continue to feel that the guidance you are receiving does not align with the broader, universal teachings of Orthodoxy, or it leads to spiritual distress rather than growth, it may be appropriate to seek additional perspectives. Consulting with another knowledgeable and experienced clergy member can provide a broader understanding and help affirm whether your spiritual concerns are being adequately addressed.

The relationship with a spiritual father should be one that fosters spiritual growth, understanding, and a deeper connection with the teachings of the Church. If you consistently feel that your spiritual needs and quest for Orthodox truth are not being met, it may indeed be prudent to consider finding a spiritual guide who can better support your journey.
