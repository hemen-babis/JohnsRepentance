Question፦ Do you have any suggestions for someone struggling with anger issues?

Answer፦ Dear seeker of peace, 

In the luminous words of the Holy Apostle James, "Let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath" (James 1:19). My beloved, anger is an age-old ailment of the soul, one that not only ancient desert fathers grappled with, but also many in our fast-paced modern age. For the heart inflamed with anger is like a turbulent sea, never finding its rest.

Firstly, immerse yourself in regular prayer and fasting. As the Holy Father St. John Cassian once said, "Anger is the first passion to be born of our attachment to pleasures and is the first to die when we cut off such attachment." By fasting and prayer, we weaken the passions and fortify our soul.

Secondly, the practice of stillness (hesychasm) can be a balm to an agitated soul. Taking moments in your day to sit in quiet reflection, focusing on the Jesus Prayer ("Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner"), can redirect the energies of your heart towards the Divine and away from the passions.

Lastly, seek the guidance of a spiritual father/partner. In the Orthodox tradition, we believe in the power of confession and spiritual guidance. Through the sacrament of confession, one can obtain not just forgiveness, but also direction and healing. We can assign you a spiritual friend who can guide and support you walkbthe right path. Which state do you live in? 

How often do you find moments of silence in your day? What are the triggers that provoke your anger, and how might you address them in light of Christ's love and patience? 

Walk in the footsteps of Christ, who endured all things with patience and humility, and let His peace be your guiding star. 🙏🏼

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
