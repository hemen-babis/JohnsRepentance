👉Question:- I had a question and I need your sincere help: I have committed many sins in my life, especially the sin of adultery, I have committed adultery with many women, with animals, with a married person and with a relative. I want to confess my sins to a spiritual father but how can I say these things in front of him?

✍️Answer:- It would have been better if we hadn't made a mistake and fallen into sin in the first place, but after we fall, repenting and coming back is a must. If a person falls into sin, how can it be difficult for him to repent? We should never feel overwhelmed or afraid. Basically, if we don't get embarrassed when we do it, how can it be difficult for us to say it? When we sin, we are dying, but when we repent, we are being saved. Therefore, the one and only choice choice we have not to live a life hunted by sin nor to receive the wages of our sins is to repent.

When a person is ashamed to talk about their sins, they run away from repentance, pile sin on top of sin, and keep the door wide open for more sin.

The rightous deeds or sin that a person does every day is written in God's record. Therefore, a person should watch and control their deeds, humbly continue the good, and confess the bad every day. Otherwise, since we don't know the day we will be called, we need to be ready every day, lest we hear the words, 'Depart from me' on the day of judgment.

When a person commits a sin, they usually pretend that they have benefited and are happy, but later the results are not beneficial. In factz it is harmful, it does not give peace, it creates chaos and lack of peace. When they do it, they do it knowingly, but when they are about to speak and repent, it seems to them a shame. This is a hindrance to repentance which is the method that is most commonly used by the devil to prolong the time for people to repent and to keep them in sin and to prevent them from revealing the secrets of sin that he practices secretly and the methods he used to make them captives of sin.

Among the ways of salvation that Satan fights against, there is nothing greater than repentance. Repentance destroys all the buildings of sin that the enemy, the devil, has built to destroy the lives of Christians. Repentance wipes out all sins - This is a great mystery that is performed by the grace of God. 

We are humiliated when we first commit the sin, and we are glorified when we say and confess the sin we have committed. By sinning, we were dirty, damaged, defiled, but when we repent, we are being sanctified, freed and glorified. 

The idea that our dear member raised is one which many people do either without knowing, without thinking it is bad, or knowingly, influenced by peers or by satanic entertainment industries. Later, they find it difficult to get out as easily as they entered it. Whichever it may be, by sinning, we lose God, by sinning, by sinning we live in darkness, and darkness is Sheol, eternal fire.
