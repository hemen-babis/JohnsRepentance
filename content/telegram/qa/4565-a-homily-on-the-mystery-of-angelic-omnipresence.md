👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾A Homily on the Mystery of #Angelic #Omnipresence

Beloved brethren, children of the light and of the day, heirs of the apostolic faith,Let us raise our minds from the dust of the earth and turn the eyes of our souls toward the heavenlies, where mysteries abound and the invisible realm resounds with the praises of the Most High. Today, we are summoned to consider a question which has stirred the thoughts of many:

How can an angel be present everywhere at the same time?

Let us not, my brothers and sisters, measure spiritual realities with the crooked reed of human logic, nor weigh celestial mysteries on the scale of carnal understanding. This question, though asked in curiosity by some, is often laced with subtle disbelief, even daring to challenge the power and wisdom of God. But hear me, O faithful soul, for this is not merely a philosophical inquiry it is a matter of reverence, faith, and divine truth.

On the Nature of Angelic Presence

It is sheer folly yes, madness to reduce the mystery of angels to the laws of physicality. Shall the pot say to the potter, “Why have you formed me thus?” The spiritual realm operates not by the mechanics of flesh and bone, nor by the logic of gravity and time. Angels are ministering spirits, fiery and swift, beings not bound by material limitations.

Have you not read in the Holy Scriptures how the angel Gabriel stood before Zechariah and said, “I am Gabriel, who stands in the presence of God” (Luke 1:19)? Mark well, O Christian, the gravity of this declaration. It is not mere poetic speech it is theological gold. When Zechariah doubted the word concerning the conception of his son, the angel did not reason with him as men do. He answered with heavenly authority:
“I stand before God.”

To stand before God is no idle posture. In Scripture, standing is often associated with intercession. The Psalmist declares,

“If Moses, His chosen one, had not stood before Him in the breach, to turn away His wrath...” (Psalm 106:23).

Therefore, Gabriel's statement reveals his priestly role as intercessor and servant before the throne of the Almighty. It affirms not merely proximity, but function, authority, and holy presence.

Where God Is, His Angels Are

Where then is the presence of God? Is He not everywhere, filling heaven and earth? Does not the prophet cry,

“Do I not fill heaven and earth?” says the Lord (Jeremiah 23:24).

So then, wherever the omnipresent God is, there also are His holy angels. Not because they are gods, far be it from us to suggest so, but because they share in His divine mission by grace, not by nature. The angels move with spiritual subtlety, granted authority by the One who sends them.

The Psalmist again assures us,

“The angel of the Lord encamps around those who fear Him, and delivers them” (Psalm 34:7 [LXX 33:7]).

And if he encamps around the faithful, then he is with them, though not in bodily form, but by presence and power.

On the Speed and Vision of Angels

Fools stumble when they take spiritual symbols literally. When the Scriptures speak of angels having many eyes and many wings, they do not suggest that angels are grotesque with feathers and eyeballs! Rather, the many eyes signify their all-seeing wisdom, and the many wings denote their swift movement. An angel reaches the ends of the earth in the blink of an eye not because he flies with wings like a bird, but because his nature is not hindered by material resistance.

Think, O man, if a soul can travel faster than the wind, and an angel faster than the soul, how much more the Holy Trinity, who made them both? What foolishness it is to measure such divine things with our sluggish reasoning!
