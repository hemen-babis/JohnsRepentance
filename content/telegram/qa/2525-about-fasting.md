👉 About #fasting 

👉🏾 Question፦ Hello I have a question 

Why do we fast until 3pm? Is there a specific reason?

Answer፦ Beloved in Christ, the practice of fasting until 3pm is deeply rooted in the tradition of the Orthodox Church, reflecting our journey of self-denial, repentance, and communion with God. This specific time recalls the hour at which our Lord Jesus Christ gave up His spirit on the Cross for the salvation of the world, as noted in the Gospel of Matthew: "And about the ninth hour Jesus cried out with a loud voice, saying, 'Eli, Eli, lema sabachthani?' that is, 'My God, My God, why have you forsaken me?'" (Matthew 27:46). By aligning our fast with this momentous hour, we unite ourselves more closely with the sufferings of Christ, remembering His ultimate sacrifice for us.

Fasting until 3pm allows us to exercise discipline over our physical desires, turning our focus towards spiritual nourishment and prayer. It is a tangible expression of our faith, reminding us to rely not on bread alone, "but on every word that comes from the mouth of God" (Matthew 4:4).

As you embark on this journey of fasting, consider how this practice might deepen your relationship with Christ. Reflect on the sacrifices He made for us and how we can offer our own small sacrifices as a way of drawing closer to Him. How might this period of fasting until 3pm enhance your spiritual life and lead you closer to the heart of God?
