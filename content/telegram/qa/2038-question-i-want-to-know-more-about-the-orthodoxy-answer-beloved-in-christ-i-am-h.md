Question፦ I want to know more about the #Orthodoxy. 

Answer ፦ Beloved in Christ,

I am heartened by your eagerness to learn about the Orthodox faith, a treasure of spiritual depth and ancient wisdom. In Orthodoxy, we embrace a journey of transformation, rooted in the life and teachings of Jesus Christ, and enriched by the traditions and practices developed over centuries.

One of the core tenets of Orthodoxy is the concept of *theosis*, the process of becoming more like God in our thoughts, actions, and being. This is beautifully encapsulated in 2 Peter 1:4, where it is said, "Through these he has given us his very great and precious promises, so that through them you may participate in the divine nature." This participation in the divine nature is at the heart of our spiritual path.

In the Orthodox tradition, we emphasize the importance of liturgical worship, prayer, fasting, and almsgiving, alongside a life of repentance and humility. Our liturgy is a tapestry of Scripture, prayer, and sacrament, deeply symbolic and reverent. It is in the liturgy that we encounter the living Christ and are nourished by the Holy Eucharist.

As you seek to know more about Orthodoxy, I encourage you to connect with a spiritual father or guide who can lead you in this journey. Our community offers spiritual partners who are ready to walk with you, sharing insights, answering questions, and offering the support you need.

Remember, the journey into Orthodoxy is not just about learning a set of doctrines, but about transforming your life to reflect the love and humility of Christ. 

How might you begin to explore this ancient path and open your heart to the transformative journey that awaits?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of The Virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
