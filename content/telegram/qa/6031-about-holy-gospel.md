👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾About #Holy #Gospel 

Question :-Melkam Gena ✝️ 

I was wondering during liturgy when we kiss the Bible I noticed at last night’s liturgy some people have the Bible placed on their back- and made a cross and also a circular motion. What is happening? And what do they do or how do they ask for that?

✍🏾 Answer :- During the Holy Liturgy, when we kiss the Gospel, we are showing reverence for the Holy Scriptures. As outlined in the Book of Gtsawe the liturgical guide used in our Church every part of the liturgy is done according to a set order, including which Gospel reading, Epistles, and chapters to follow.

✍🏾The priest reads the Gospel during the service, and we are instructed to kiss it, bow, and blessed by it. a sign of respect. While the liturgical book does not specify exactly how to perform these actions, they are meant to express reverence for God’s Word.

✍🏾The specific gesture you noticed, where some people place the Gospel on their back and make the sign of the Cross with a circular motion, is a form of personal or traditional devotion. the form of these actions may vary, but their purpose is the same: to show our faith and belief in the Gospel and the Cross, as these are the means of our salvation.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
