What should I do if I still love my Muslim ex-boyfriend even after confessing to a Bishop and trying to follow religious teachings?

👉 Question: Hi, I wanted to ask you questions. I am a college student and when I was in campus I fell in love wish a muslim guy. First it was just talking so I didn't take it seriously but slowly it turned into physical things and we start having sex. I know it was wrong but I couldn't help my self. We dated almost for 5 months and all those times we use to have sex, go out clubimg, drink and alot of things. Then he starts taking about marriage add if that was possible and the only thing I was scared of was my family. I don't know I was just blind by love but I know I can never marry him. A few months later, I left the city and I started thinking about everything and I got really scared and started going to church. I met a Bishop and I told them everything and he said to do 'kédër' and get a father of pennance to confess my sins. I said okay and I stop the relationship with my him. But I couldn't do every the Bishop told me because I still love him and think about him. I am scared because even if I do all but my heartache for him it will be a sin. I don't know what to do please tell me what to do and pray for me.

Answer፦ There are things that we do knowingly and unknowingly. When our dear member listed and explained everything to us, they themselves admitted that they knew it was bad when they did it. Those who knowingly engage in bad behavior should repent and seek God's protection, council of Church fathers and restraint themselves from such actions because this is the right thing to do. 

Many of us sin, knowing that it is wrong, but we don't want to correct our mistakes. While our dear member's coming to repentance is commendable, it is a weakness that they do not return and correct their mistakes.

Just acknowledging what sin is does not mean anything if we do not truly understand it. For example, do we understand that premarital sex and having sex with non-religious individuals are sins? If so, why do we engage in such acts? 

Would a sane person knowingly drink poison that they know will kill them? Just as a healthy person would not do such a thing, we must understand that sin is worse than that because it results in losing God, peace, paradise, and eternal life.

If we are willing to repent and return to God, then everything we have done wrong will be forgiven. The holy father who spoke to our dear member gave her a good advice and she should be grateful for this blessing because bishops are the main fathers of the church and it is not easy to find time with them. 

As the bishop said, you must repent, confess and do the baptism of Keder. So you must not look back, understand that this is something that separated you from God, your creator and avoid it at all costs. Do we not believe in eternal life? Do we not believe in the God that became one of us to share his nature with us? Do we not know that we are members of the body of Christ? Do we not know that the Holy Spirit is to reside within us? Do we not know that it is Holy blood of Christ that is united with our and flows through our vains? Do we not know that it is the Holy Flesh of our Lord and savior Jesus Christ which has been made one with ours? Do we not understand the love of God? And if we do, is this what we are willing to trade for misery and pain on earth, and eternal fire thereafter? Because these are the only fruits of such a relationship, it doesn't even bring earltly joy we just choose to think it does.
