👉👉👉 About #Love for God

👉🏾 Question፦ Hello beloved Families in Christ, I had a question 

Can we love God on our own or do we need God to love God?

Answer፦ Peace and blessings upon you, child. Your question delves into the very heart of our relationship with the Divine. 

The truth is, we are utterly incapable of loving God on our own.  Why? Because true love, agape in Greek, is not merely a human emotion but a divine gift, a fruit of the Holy Spirit working within us (Galatians 5:22).

Think of a sun-parched field. Can it yield a harvest on its own? No, it needs the life-giving water of rain, a gift from above.  Similarly, our hearts, withered by sin, are incapable of genuine love for God without His grace.  

As St. John Chrysostom beautifully wrote,  "It is not our love to God that should come first, but His love to us." For it is He who first loved us (1 John 4:19) while we were yet sinners (Romans 5:8). 

Therefore, we need God to love God.  We need His grace, poured out through the Holy Spirit, to soften our hearts, open our spiritual eyes, and ignite within us the flame of true love for Him. This love is not passive; it compels us to seek Him in prayer, study His word, partake in the sacraments, and strive to live according to His commandments.  

So, child, do not despair if you feel your love for God is weak or imperfect.  Instead, draw near to Him with humility and sincerity.  Ask for His grace, and He, in His boundless mercy, will surely grant it to you.  For He desires nothing more than our love, a love that is His own gift to us, returned to Him with joy and thanksgiving. 

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
