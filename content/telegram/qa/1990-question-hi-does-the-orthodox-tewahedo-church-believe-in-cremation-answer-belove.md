Question፦ Hi does the Orthodox tewahedo church believe in #cremation?

Answer፦ Beloved in Christ,

Grace and peace to you. In the Orthodox Tewahedo Church, as in the broader Orthodox Christian tradition, we uphold the sanctity and respect of the human body. This belief stems from the understanding that the body is a temple of the Holy Spirit (1 Corinthians 6:19), an integral part of the human person, created in the image and likeness of God.

Therefore, the Church does not endorse cremation. We believe in the resurrection of the body, as professed in the Nicene Creed, and thus maintain the practice of burial, honoring the body as it awaits the resurrection. This is deeply rooted in our theology and liturgical practices, reflecting our reverence for the body as an essential component of human personhood.

In this regard, the Orthodox Church encourages burial as a way of expressing hope in the resurrection and a deep respect for the gift of the body, which God has bestowed upon us. It is a practice that aligns with our understanding of life, death, and the hope of eternal life in Christ.

As you contemplate this teaching, may I ask you to reflect on how you view the body in relation to your spiritual journey? How does this understanding influence your respect for the physical aspect of our human existence, as you walk in faith towards the Kingdom of God?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of The Virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
