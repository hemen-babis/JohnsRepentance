👉 Question፦ My following question is related to love. 

Ive asked many questions like “where is my Godly spouse?” “When will he come?” “Why is God making me wait?” “How can I learn to be more patient through this process?” “Why am I so pressed or obsessed with the idea of having a man in my life?” “What’s the best way to vet out if a person or an offer is from God or the enemy?” 

I was hoping you could help answer some of these questions.

Answer፦ Beloved one, your heartfelt inquiries about finding a godly spouse are shared by many, and it's beautiful to see your desire to align this aspect of your life with God's will. Your experiences, including the anticipation and the encounters with potential partners, are all part of the journey God is using to shape you, preparing you for the future He has planned.

In addressing your questions and concerns:

1. God's Timing and Purpose: While waiting can be challenging, it's important to trust in God's timing. He knows the desires of your heart and is working all things for your good (Romans 8:28). This period of waiting is not passive but an active time of growth, self-discovery, and deepening your relationship with God.

2. Patience in the Process: Developing patience can be cultivated through continued trust in God's promises and faithfulness. Reflect on past instances where God has been faithful to you; this can bolster your trust and patience. Remember, "But they that wait upon the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint" (Isaiah 40:31).

3. Self-Reflection: Your question about being "pressed or obsessed" with finding a partner is insightful. It's beneficial to explore your feelings and desires in prayer and perhaps with a trusted spiritual counselor. Sometimes, understanding deeper motivations can bring clarity and peace, helping you to focus on God's presence and love in the present.

4. Discerning God's Will in Relationships: To discern whether someone is a potential spouse sent by God or a distraction, seek alignment with Scriptural principles and godly character traits. Prayer, time, and godly counsel are crucial. Observe their fruits of the Spirit (Galatians 5:22-23), how they treat others, and their commitment to their faith and personal growth.

5. Preparation for Marriage: Use this time to prepare yourself to be the godly spouse you desire to have. Grow in virtues like kindness, patience, and love. Engage in activities that build your character and deepen your faith.

6. Being Proactive in a Godly Way: While waiting on God, it's also okay to be part of Christian groups or activities where you can meet someone with shared values. However, let these interactions be natural and spirit-led rather than forced.

Your journey to finding a godly spouse is also a journey of self-discovery and spiritual growth. Cherish this time with God, knowing that your relationship with Him is the foundation upon which all other relationships are built.

Would you consider taking time to reflect more deeply on what God might be teaching you in this season of waiting and how you might grow through it?
