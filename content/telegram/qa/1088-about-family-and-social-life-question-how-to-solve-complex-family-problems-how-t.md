About #family and #social life

Question: How to solve complex family problems? How to build a loving and Christ-centered family?
 
✍️Answer:- The first question we should ask ourselves is how did complexity arise? What is the source of this complexity, and answer these questions from the different stakeholders involved. When did it start, and how long did it last? Understanding these issues is crucial to finding a solution. As the saying goes, "knowing the problem is half the journey to the solution." Therefore, the first step in solving a problem is to understand it.

If there is complex problems in a family, this is often a sign of lack of Love in the family. Love is the foundation of everything, and if it is present in our families, everything else follows suit. As the book says, "Where there is love, there is no mistrust, hatred, or possession. Love is the root of all good."

Therefore, the complexity of our lives is due to the lack of love in our homes, neighborhoods, and surroundings. Saint Peter said, "With love, anything is possible, and with love, there is patience and meekness." The absence of love creates complex problems in our homes and elsewhere.

The solution is to cultivate love in our homes. Love is God, and if Christ is in our homes, there will be no problems. When Christ is in our homes, love is present. Therefore, we are all called to live in love and in Christ. We should never have a life or family outside of Christ. Christ loved us and gave us His holy body to eat and His precious blood to drink. This is what Christianity is all about. It is when we understand this that we understand what Love is.

To build a family that is free of complexity, we must rely on love, hold on to God, make the Virgin Mary our mother, seek the intercession of holy angels, righteous martyrs, virgin monks, and look to the way of life of our ancestors for guidance. This is possible through the grace of God, and may our God help us live in love.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
