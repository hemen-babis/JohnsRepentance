---About the #sin of #adultery

👉 Question፦ Hello father, what can I do to stop my adultery with women?

Answer፦ Dear member if John's Repentance service, It is indeed a matter of great consequence that you bring before us, for the act of adultery is a sin that profoundly afflicts both individual souls and societies at large. A sin not only of the flesh, but one of dissatisfaction and desire for novelty, it is a significant challenge that must be grappled with, not just for the individuals involved, but for the community as a whole. As we know from the teachings of our Lord Jesus Christ in Matthew 12:39, he condemned the adulterous and wayward generation, hinting at a spiritual restlessness and discontent that undergirds the act of adultery itself.

We must understand that adultery has many faces. It is not confined merely to the illicit relations between a man and a woman. Indeed, it stretches to a fundamental restlessness and desire for constant change. It is a yearning for more and for new, a persistent dissatisfaction with the present moment and circumstances. This adulterous attitude stretches beyond mere romantic relationships and seeps into every aspect of life.

Adultery, then, is the act of desiring more than what is given to us by God. It is the refusal to accept and be content with what we have, to honour and cherish what God has bestowed upon us. It is a revolt against the divine will and order, which dictates that a man is meant for one woman and vice versa.

Moreover, the act of infidelity extends beyond the marital bed. It extends to all relations outside the institution of holy matrimony - it is the indulgence in sexual acts before marriage, self-gratification, homosexuality, and even worshipping false gods. The sin of Israel against their idols, for example, is a form of spiritual adultery. To stray from the path that our God has illuminated for us and to follow another is to commit spiritual adultery.

What, then, is the remedy for such a condition? It lies in combating the very tendencies that give rise to adultery. If we are prone to seeking the new and untried, we must strive to remain content with what we have, adhering to the teachings of God. Recognizing the harm and destructiveness of adultery is the first step towards redemption. This harm can manifest itself in various ways, whether it is through consuming inappropriate media, participating in illicit activities, or allowing our thoughts to wander into immoral territories.

The antidote lies in understanding the gravity of the sin and recognizing its consequences. As we see in Genesis 19, those who indulged in debauchery and adultery in the time of Lot were consumed by divine fire, while those who stayed true to God's commandments were saved. Understanding the potential for destruction that adultery harbors, we can then arm ourselves with the strength and resolve to resist such temptations.

Fasting, prostrating, praying, repenting, participating actively in the Church, and engaging with the Word of God are key practices that can fortify us against this sin. These acts of devotion help foster a spiritual mindset that is resistant to the allure of adultery. Furthermore, we can find solace and strength in the lives and stories of those who remained chaste and virtuous, for these stories bear testament to the possibility of a virtuous life.

Ultimately, the path to redemption from this sin lies in following in the footsteps of our spiritual ancestors, who triumphed over such temptations and remained true to their faith. We must strive to cultivate a steadfastness of spirit, resisting the allure of novelty and change and finding contentment in the divine order that God has set before us.

Thus, may God, our loving Father, imbue us with the strength and fortitude of our spiritual forefathers to resist the destructive lure of adultery. For it is in His power and mercy that we find our salvation.
