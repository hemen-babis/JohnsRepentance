👉 About preparing for #Confession 

👉🏾 Question፦ What’s the best way to prepare for confession so we don’t forget some sins that we need confess & prepare adequately. Also, best way to prepare for confession to approach it having properly repented.

Answer፦ My child, your desire to thoroughly prepare for the Holy Mystery of Confession is commendable, a true sign of a heart seeking reconciliation with God.  The devil, our adversary, often tries to cloud our minds, making us forget our sins or minimizing their gravity. But with prayer and self-examination, we can overcome these obstacles.

Here are some ways to prepare, drawing upon the wisdom of our Holy Fathers:

Prayer: Begin by beseeching the Holy Spirit to illuminate the darkened corners of your heart.  Pray with humility, like the Publican in the Gospel, saying "God, be merciful to me a sinner!" (Luke 18:13).  St. Isaac the Syrian beautifully reminds us, "Enter eagerly into the treasure house that is within you, and you will see the things that are in heaven. For there is but one single entry into this treasure house, and that is humility."  Humility opens the door to honest self-reflection.

Self-Examination: Dedicate time for quiet contemplation. Use the Ten Commandments (Exodus 20) and the Beatitudes (Matthew 5:3-12) as mirrors to reflect upon your thoughts, words, and deeds.  Have I loved God with all my heart and my neighbor as myself?  Have I been truthful, humble, and charitable?  Have I indulged in anger, envy, or gluttony?  Consider also the Seven Deadly Sins: pride, envy, wrath, sloth, greed, gluttony, and lust.  Ask yourself how these have manifested in your life.

Journaling:  Writing down your sins can be a powerful tool. This act of putting them on paper can help you to concretely confront them and avoid forgetting them in the confessional.

Fasting: Fasting helps to subdue the passions and clear the mind. Incorporate fasting, as is appropriate for your health and with the blessing of your spiritual father, leading up to your confession.  Abba Moses, one of the desert fathers, said, "Fasting subdues the body and cleanses the soul."

Reading Scripture and the Holy Fathers: Immerse yourself in the Word of God and the writings of our Holy Fathers.  Their words can pierce our hearts and reveal areas where we need to repent.  The Psalms especially offer powerful prayers of repentance, such as Psalm 51.

Confession Guide: Some find a confession guide helpful, particularly those new to the sacrament or returning after a long absence. These guides, available from your church or online, can prompt reflection on different areas of sin.

Regarding true repentance, remember that it is not simply remorse for being caught but a genuine sorrow for having offended God and a sincere desire to change. St. John Chrysostom reminds us that “true repentance is not simply to bewail one’s sins, but to change one’s life.”

Finally, my child, remember that the priest acts as an icon of Christ in the confessional, offering absolution through the power bestowed upon him by God. Approach him with humility and trust.  

I will pray for you, my child, as you prepare for this holy sacrament.  Please let me know if you have any further questions.
