---About our #baptismal #names

Question፦ Hello,

I was wondering if we are supposed to keep our christina name private or anyone can know it. Thank you! 

Sincerely,

Answer ፦ Beloved in Christ,

Your inquiry touches upon a beautiful aspect of our Christian life – the baptismal name, a symbol of our new birth in Christ and entrance into the holy family of the Church. In the tradition of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church, as well as in the wider Orthodox Christian world, the baptismal name is given with profound significance. It serves not only as a marker of one's Christian identity but also as a constant reminder of the saint whose name we bear, encouraging us to emulate their virtues and piety.

The sharing of your baptismal name is not a matter of strict confidentiality but rather of personal discretion and spiritual context. It is a name to be lived by, prayed by, and, if led by the Spirit, shared in the context of building up one another in faith. "Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father in heaven." (Matthew 5:16) Your baptismal name is part of the light you carry, a testament to your rebirth in Christ and your journey towards Theosis—becoming partakers of the Divine nature (2 Peter 1:4).

Therefore, whether you choose to share your baptismal name openly or keep it as a personal treasure is up to you. What is essential is to live by the meaning and example of the saint or holy figure you are named after, allowing their life in Christ to inspire and guide your own.

As you ponder this, consider how your baptismal name shapes your spiritual journey. How does it inspire you to live a life of faith, hope, and love in the footsteps of Christ and the saints?

In Christ’s love.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
