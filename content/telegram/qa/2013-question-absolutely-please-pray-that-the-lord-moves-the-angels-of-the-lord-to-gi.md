---Question፦ Absolutely 💯 please #pray that The Lord moves the Angels of The Lord to give me direction and guidance for my destiny 🙏 Thank you so much 💓

Answer፦ Beloved in Christ,

Your request for divine guidance and direction in your life, especially in the context of your son's challenges, is a testament to your faith and reliance on the Lord. In the book of Psalms, King David proclaims, “The steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord, and He delights in his way” (Psalm 37:23). Trust that the Lord is indeed guiding your steps, even in times of uncertainty and difficulty.

Prayer is a powerful tool in seeking God's direction. In your daily life, set aside time for prayer, not just for requests, but also for listening to God's voice. The Holy Scriptures and the lives of the Saints are rich sources for understanding God's will and finding solace in the midst of trials.

Regarding your son, remember that each person is a unique creation of God, loved and valued. Your patience, love, and understanding towards him reflect Christ's love for us. St. John Chrysostom once said, "The family is a little Church." In your interactions with your son, you are demonstrating the virtues of patience, compassion, and unconditional love, which are at the heart of our Christian journey.

I will certainly pray for you and your family, asking the Lord to grant you wisdom, patience, and strength. Remember that you are not alone in this journey; the Church is here to support and guide you.To connect with a spiritual father, I recommend you find one who can accompany you on this journey from your local Orthodox parish, Oriental or Eastern. To learn more about the rich tradition of Orthodoxy, I encourage you to engage with our social media platforms: https://linktr.ee/johnsrepentance

Now, dear child of God, how will you incorporate prayer and reflection into your daily routine to seek and discern God's guidance in your life?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of The Virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
