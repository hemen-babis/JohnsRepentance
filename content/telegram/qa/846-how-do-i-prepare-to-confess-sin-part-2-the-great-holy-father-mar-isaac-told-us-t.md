---How do I prepare to confess sin?

(Part 2)

The great holy father, Mar Isaac, told us the great spiritual truth, "If the human being is not the enemy of sin first, he cannot take down sin from his head." Abhorrence is the greatest level of hatred, so it is necessary not only to abhor the practice of sin, but also to abhor the thought of that sin.

To repent of sin is to be ashamed of sin, to hate sin, and to turn back. A person who is ready to repent without a thorough understanding of these is not free from the slavery of sin, so he will not hesitate to return to his previous duty easily. Therefore, the first and most important thing that helps us start away from sin is to admit that we have sinned and confess to ourselves, and to hate sin. Hating sin is the greatest force that helps us separate from sin and leave it forever. We cannot truly repent of sinfulness while in love with sin, nor can we live with a problem that can be corrected and not be corrected without separating from it.
 
- Bringing a change in thinking: To solve a problem in any area of our life, we must have a different perspective and need from the previous one. Glory cannot be found in a lazy mind, problems cannot be solved by a needy (problem-creating) mind, it is impossible to love with a hateful mind, and it is difficult to hate with a loving heart. Nor will he come to repentance with a sinful mind. Therefore, any change requires a change of thinking first. For this, our greatest powers are faith, good vision, and hope. In this way, a sinner must first stop his sin and change from loving sin to hating his sin in order to repent spiritually. Because in a life of sin, we cannot understand the importance of righteousness.

- Not to worry, but to repent: A sinner who worries and a sinner who repents are completely different. Anxiety is a disorder of terror caused by the abundance and evil of our sinfulness, and this kind of temporary regret can occur even in a conscience that has not awakened from sin. But spiritual repentance comes next to knowing sinfulness, hating sin, and being ashamed of sin, and it is the first step of repentance. Saint Paul said in his second letter, "I am very glad that you are sorry for the sake of God, so that not even one of you may perish." Grief for God is repentance that gives eternal life" (2 Cor 7:9) "Return to me with all your heart, but do not tear your clothes, but your heart" (Job 2:13, Ps 50:17, Job 2:1)
 
- Using our free will for good deeds: Sin's greatest strength and confidence (guaranty) in us is our love for her, not her power. The devil has no other confidence without our consent. If we deny our will, if we overcome it, we will be rewarded. But if we give our consent to the work he offers us, we will be defeated and humiliated. If we remain losers, our defeat will not be inevitable for the time being. Because if Satan finds the small hole of will, he will lick it like a snake and widen it, and he will lose us from our God. When our evil will screams, it repeatedly whips us with the whip of sin and drives us down to the valley of death, hell, to the world of damnation, and store us there forever.
 
God predestined all human beings for life and salvation. But the decision is made when our free will is added to it. So whoever wants life can be saved. Salvation is in our hands. Our God always stretches out His hand and calls us to repentance so that we can use our free will to adjust and return to freedom. He who repents will be saved. He who refuses and ignores the call will perish.
