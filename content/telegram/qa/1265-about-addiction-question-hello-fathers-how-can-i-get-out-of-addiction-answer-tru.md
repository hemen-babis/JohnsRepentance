About #Addiction 

Question: Hello fathers, how can I get out of addiction? 

Answer፦ Truly, addiction is one of the most grievous trials of our age. It has touched our society, our church, and even our very families, testing the strength of our faith and causing profound suffering. Addiction is not merely a mundane issue but a spiritual struggle against forces that seek to diminish our inherent dignity and to tarnish the image of God within us.

Many forms of addiction exist, from substances like tobacco and drugs, to behavioural issues such as gluttony, promiscuity, and deceit. Regardless of its form, addiction invariably results in a loss of self-control and a corruption of the soul, as one is led away from virtuous conduct and into behaviours that do not befit a child of God.

Recognize, dear member, that addiction is not just a struggle against mere flesh and blood, but against the spiritual forces of darkness. As our Lord taught, it is the devil who comes to steal, kill and destroy, and addiction is one of the weapons he uses in his relentless war against the human soul.

The holy fathers of the church, drawing from the wellspring of apostolic wisdom and the holy scriptures, have instructed us on how to handle such trials. We do not fight addiction with the weapons of this world, but with the spiritual armour of God. It is by fortifying our souls with faith, prayer, fasting, repentance, and the sacraments that we stand firm against this onslaught.

Yet, in the face of addiction, many people despair, believing that once ensnared in the grip of addiction, escape is impossible. This is a lie from the evil one himself. Do not forget the words of the apostle Paul, "No temptation has overtaken you except what is common to mankind. And God is faithful; he will not let you be tempted beyond what you can bear. But when you are tempted, he will also provide a way out so that you can endure it."

The journey to recovery from addiction begins with an honest admission of one's struggles and a fervent desire for transformation. It requires the courage to confront one's sins and weaknesses and the humility to seek help. Remember, the journey might be tough, but with God, all things are possible.

Seek spiritual counsel from the fathers of the church or a trusted spiritual advisor who can guide you through this process. Their wisdom, coupled with the grace of God, can set you on the path to recovery. Remain steadfast in prayer, for prayer is the bridge that connects us to God and the armour that shields us from the assaults of the evil one.

Consider seeking help through confession and spiritual counseling, as these practices can provide an avenue for healing. In the sacrament of confession, we confront our sins and express our desire for repentance, and in doing so, we receive the Lord's forgiveness and mercy.

In this struggle, you are not alone. The church is your family and your support, providing you with the necessary spiritual resources and the fellowship of the faithful. We encourage you to turn to your fellow believers for support and to include them in your journey towards healing.

Rest assured, dear member, that there is hope. Addiction is not an unbeatable foe. With the help of God and the support of the Church, you can overcome this affliction. Through the intercessions of the Mother of God and all the saints, may you find strength, courage, and the determination to break free from the chains of addiction.

In closing, we lift you up in prayer, entrusting you to the mercy and grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, who, through His death and resurrection, has triumphed over sin and death. May His love be your strength and His peace your comfort, today and always. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
