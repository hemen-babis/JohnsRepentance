Question : So I have a question involving Qidase. Could someone please explain the priest walking around the Church with the incense. I understand in the Coptic Church the priest circle the Church to show how St. Paul went around the world preaching the Gospel. Also the Priest only circles halfway around the Church to symbolize the 12 who preached locally at the beginning of their mission. Can someone point me to when or if this is done in our Liturgy and point me to when it is happening so I can look for it. A bonus will be the actual words that are being said by the Church. Thank yall and Enkwan Abero Aderechu.

Answer :- I’m happy you are asking such insightful questions about the Qidase and the role of incense during the service.

👉🏾During the Qidase, the priest burns incense inside a censer. The incense is a symbol of divinity, representing the prayers of the faithful rising to God. The censer is covered with a lid, symbolizing the Virgin Mary, who gave birth to Christ while remaining a virgin. The rod or chain that holds the censer also represents the Holy Spirit, who guides and sanctifies all things.

✍🏾As the priest walks around with the incense, he does so in imitation of Saint Paul, who traveled the world preaching the Gospel. The priest’s movement around the Church symbolizes how the Gospel spread throughout the world through the apostles’ travels and teachings.
The Qidase is divided into three parts:

1.The Preparation Part: 

2.The Reading and Teaching Part: Here, the Scriptures are read and the priest teaches the faithful.

3.The Fruit of the Holy Liturgy: This is the moment when the bread and wine are consecrated and become the Body and Blood of Christ.

Although it would be difficult to describe in exact numbers when the priest leaves the sanctuary to burn incense, but it happens when the Qidase begins,priest starts by saying the prayers of blessing (such as "Serawite" and invoking the name of God, "Egzio"). First, the main priest performs the ritual, followed by the assistant priest. The incense is burned while the priest moves around, and the names of the faithful are mentioned as prayers are offered.

✍🏾In general, the walking around with the incense is symbolic of the apostolic journeys. It also serves to purify the space and invite the presence of the Holy Spirit, as the priest walks and offers prayers for the faithful. This act represents the spread of the Gospel and the sanctification of the gathered community.

✍🏾I hope this helps you understand the deeper meaning behind the incense procession during the Qidase. It is a powerful moment that connects us to the apostles and the presence of God.
