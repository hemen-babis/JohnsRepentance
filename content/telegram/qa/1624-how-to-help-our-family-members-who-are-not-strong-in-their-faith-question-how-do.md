How to help our #family members who are not strong in their #faith

👉🏾 Question፦ How do I convince my brothers  to get married and go to church  they dont think it is a big deal..... they both have partners and children 

👉 Answer፦ Beloved in Christ, your brothers, having been baptized into the faith, have been sealed with the promise of salvation. Yet, as you have rightly observed, they have wandered, like sheep without a shepherd, into the wilderness of the secular world, away from the embrace of the Church and the nourishing Word of God.

It is a lamentable sight, indeed, to witness those who have been initiated into the mysteries of our faith living outside the protective walls of the Church, especially when they are entrusted with the sacred duty of raising children. For in the words of our Lord, "Let the little children come to me, and do not hinder them, for the kingdom of God belongs to such as these."

How then, dear member, do we guide these souls back to the fold? It is a task that weighs heavily upon the hearts of all who serve the Church, and it is a reflection of the trials and tribulations that the Body of Christ faces in these times.

To guide them back, we must first approach them with love, humility, and patience. Remember the parable of the prodigal son. The father did not chastise or rebuke his wayward child but waited with open arms for his return. Similarly, you must be that beacon of hope and love for your brothers, showing them the beauty and depth of our faith through your own life and actions.

Engage them in gentle conversation, not with the intent to rebuke, but to understand. Find out what has distanced them from the Church and address those concerns with compassion. Invite them to partake in the life of the Church, not just in the liturgical services but in its charitable works, its community, and its teachings.

Moreover, pray fervently for their return. The power of prayer is immeasurable, and the intercessions of the saints can work wonders in the hearts of those who have strayed.

Lastly, trust in the divine providence of our Lord. He, who seeks out every lost sheep, will not abandon your brothers. In His time and in His way, He will draw them back to Himself. You may also call us at our phone number (512) 537-1651 for further guidance. 

Let us all, as members of the one, holy, catholic, and apostolic Church, take up this sacred duty of guiding our brethren back to the fold. Share this counsel with your family and community, for in unity, our efforts will bear fruit.

May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God the Father, and the communion of the Holy Spirit be with you and your brothers. And may the prayers of all the saints intercede for us. Amen.
