Question :-Hello family,
Hope this message finds you all well.
Will someone please confirm The Fast of the Apostles is starting on June 1st and ends July 12th?

✍🏾Answer : - May the peace and blessings of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you my child. Regarding your question about the Fast of the Apostles, you are correct:

The Fast of the Apostles begins on Ginbot 24 (Monday, June 1) and continues for 40 days through Hamle 4 (July 11). We conclude the fast and celebrate the feast on Hamle 5 (July 12), the day of Saints Peter and Paul.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
