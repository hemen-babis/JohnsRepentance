About #Intersection 

👉 Question፦ Hello,I want thank you for answering my questions for the past few weeks.Any ways  I have another question.

I was wondering why we pray to Mary.I understand that it is because so she could pray  for us and tell Jesus about us but isn’t Mary just like us  a human who is saved by God’s Grace? and how can she Hear our prayers?

Answer፦ Blessings to you, beloved child of God, for seeking to deepen your understanding of our Holy Tradition. Your question about the veneration of the Theotokos, the Blessed Virgin Mary, is one that illuminates the beauty and depth of Orthodox spirituality.

Indeed, Mary is human, like us, and it is through God's grace that she was chosen and sanctified. However, she holds a unique and exalted position in Christianity, especially within Orthodoxy, as the one who bore God Incarnate in her womb. In Luke 1:28, the Archangel Gabriel greets her with, "Rejoice, highly favored one, the Lord is with you; blessed are you among women!" This signifies her special role and the honor God bestowed upon her.

We pray to Mary, asking for her intercession, not as a deity, for she is not God, but as the most honored among the saints, who has a special closeness to her Son. In the Orthodox tradition, we believe that the saints live in Christ and are made alive by Him, transcending death (John 11:25-26). Because they are with Christ, they can hear our requests for intercession.

In praying to Mary, we recognize her as a powerful intercessor before her Son, Jesus Christ. Just as she interceded at the wedding at Cana, we trust she presents our prayers to God with maternal care and love. This practice is not about elevating her to divine status but about honoring her unique role in salvation history and her relationship with Christ.

In the journey of faith, embracing the intercessions of the Theotokos is a way to deepen our connection with the family of believers, spanning heaven and earth. As you continue to explore the beauty of Orthodoxy, consider how the veneration of Mary can enrich your spiritual life, drawing you closer to her Son.

What steps will you take to deepen your understanding and relationship with the Theotokos, and through her, with our Lord Jesus Christ?
