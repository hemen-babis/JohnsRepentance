Our dear member stated, “I am ashamed to express the wrongs I have done to a confession father.”

But yet, repentance is to regret what we have done; it is saying that "I have done things that aren’t expected of a believer of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ." It is to say that we should accuse/judge ourselves before being accused by other people. It starts when a person accuses themselves, when they reveal their secrets to themselves, and when they are ashamed of what they have done in the past. It is followed by confessing our sins with heartfelt regrets before God. We should not be ashamed or embarrassed when we confess our sins as we were not ashamed or embarrassed when we were doing it because when we were doing the wrongdoings, we were dying but with confession, we are making reconciliation with God, and we are being saved. We are coming from death to life and coming out of darkness to light. It is a way that we are starting to live together with God. 

When we go to a confession father to repent for what we have done, we are admitting our hate for the sins and deciding that we would not do them again. St. Paul wrote, in 1 Corinthians 11: 31, “For if we would judge ourselves, we should not be judges.” Repentance is when we, the sinners, judge ourselves and our sins. Therefore, what our questioner mentioned to us will be an answer for all of us, to say that we should be ashamed of our sins, repent, and decide not to do it again, but we should not be afraid to confess our sins to our fathers and repent.

Acts 3:19, St Paul wrote, “Repent therefore and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out, so that times of refreshing may come from the presence of the Lord.” 
Matthew 9:13 also mentions, “...For I did not come to call the righteous, but sinners, ]to repentance.”  Therefore, it is the sinner who needs repentance. Our brothers and sisters, you must understand that no matter how serious our sins are, we must come to God in repentance, believing that we will be forgiven and that we will be cleansed. We have the opportunity to choose a confession father or a spiritual father who is a true guardian, mentor, and teacher. You are expected to state your repentance in detail without being ashamed or embarrassed by your sins to your spiritual father. As said in the book, the priest means the grave, because the grave does not reveal everything, and the priest does not speak about anyone's sin, he should be a secret keeper.

Just as water washes the cleanest and the dirtiest, without discrimination, so should the Priest wash away the sins of the child indiscriminately, and not speak of the sins he hears. Perhaps, if there is a Priest who speaks, he is responsible to God, not us. But since we are told to go and examine ourselves to the priest, it means that we should go and confess our sins to our repentance father. It is God who commanded us to confess our sins. It is written in Matthew chapter 8:4 "And Jesus said to him, “See that you tell no one; but go your way, show yourself to the priest, and offer the gift that Moses commanded, as a testimony to them.” So, we advise our fellow members to do as it is told. If in case you don't have a father of repentance, no Christian should spend a day without a father of repentance. Having a father of repentance and repenting is a spiritual obligation. And so we advise you to look for a spiritual father, talk to the head priest of your parish, and consult with friends and family who have a father of repentance. You may also reach out to us and we will assist you in this regard if you can't find a sheppard for your soul nearby.
