👉Question :- Beloved of the Lord I have two questions

What is the meaning of #Psalm 68:31

✍Answer👇

"Princes shall come out of Egypt; Ethiopia shall soon stretch out her hands unto God". Psalm 68:31
 
This is a word used to refer to what the Egyptians did and tribute for. First of all, when we know Egypt and Ethiopia both in the history of the world and in the history of the Bible, they are two sides of the same coin. This has gone beyond history and we have reached it in our time, where by we can even simply understand about Ethiopia and Egypt  by simply seeing and listening current happenings. That is why they are inseparable sides of the same coin; Both positively and negatively. Their stories are not different whether they are being treated like a caste or they are living close to each other in friendship. The history of the Bible and the history of our church actually tell us that in the past, Ethiopia was called Ethiopia, including Egypt, and that there were also many countries that Ethiopia includes. 

Therefore, it cannot be denied that the Egyptians paid a lot of money to make Ethiopia a friend. Basically, Ethiopia is the first country in terms of history of religion and history of greatness. We are saying this not only because we live in it or because we in the Orthodox religion and want the honor and glory of Ethiopian Orthodox history, but because it is true. 

Princes, princes, dignitaries, rulers come from Egypt, or the mediators who are said to bring gifts of honor to Ethiopia, come to Ethiopia saying that we will pay and offer gifts. We now know who Egypt and Ethiopia are in the history of the church in the Old Testament and the New Testament. When Ethiopia's name is mentioned in the Holy Bible, Ethiopia is always with God. It is known that she is with God and is not separated from worship. Even when one look at the Old Testament and the Torah system and may say that it is Jerusalem, but if we tell the truth, Ethiopia is the first in performing the correct system and following the correct worship of God. While Israel, when she was the center of God's prophets, all those prophecies were spoken to her, all those hopes were given to her, all those parables were explained to her, and above all, while she was the one to whom God's great work was revealed, but how many times did she sin against God? But in the history of Ethiopia, the era that was clearly and boldly stated is that it is a country that worshiped God in the era of 1000 years. In fact, the scholars said that it was even before that. Ethiopia's luck is God and she has no other worship. Even in this era, no history has been written that says Ethiopians have  left a single day of true worship and worshiped idols, grieved God, and suffered plagues and wrath. Let alone in our history, there is no such history written about the other nations who even do not believe.
 
Not only the Bible but many stories have recorded how much Israel has disappointed God, and committed adultery by worshiping idols. At all, they now say we do not accept the true God, the God who is a man, the God they hoped for, the God to whom so many prophecies have been made; It means that they are forbidden from receiving and seeing.   They were the ones who were waiting for him, more than we did; they were hoping that He would be born from them. But when he was born, they did not accept him, and even hanged him to death. Therefore, since Ethiopia is great in all of this, as she has never stopped worshiping God, no matter how many hardships it has.
 
As for the Egyptians, when we speak the truth, we can easily see in history that the words spoken by David in Psalm 68:31 (the word that our member asked us to explain), actually the Egyptians paid tribute to Ethiopia, and presented a gift (Amaha) to Ethiopia. Even in our country's history, Emperor Zara David gave Ethiopia the  cross (Gamade Meskelu), which is more than a gift of gifts of honor (Amha). Leaving aside the rest, this alone is proof enough.
