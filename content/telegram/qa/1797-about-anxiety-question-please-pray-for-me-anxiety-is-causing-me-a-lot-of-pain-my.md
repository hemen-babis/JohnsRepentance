About #anxiety  

👉 Question፦ Please pray for me - anxiety is causing me a lot of pain. My pain is so high that there doesn't even seem to be a God - please help me! 

Answer፦ 

Dearest Seeker, In the vast richness of human existence, where threads of joy and sorrow intertwine, there are moments of profound despair, akin to what you are experiencing. The weight of such anguish can indeed make one question the very fabric of existence and the presence of a Divine Creator.

The Orthodox tradition, as you rightly pointed out, holds the suffering of Christ as the pinnacle of all human suffering. When we juxtapose our pain against His, it provides a perspective that can be both humbling and enlightening. Christ's suffering was not just physical but also spiritual, a torment that encompassed the entirety of human experience.

Anxiety, as you described, is a multifaceted beast. It arises from both tangible and intangible sources, from fears both seen and unseen. It is a testament to the complex nature of our minds that we can be tormented by things both real and imagined. But remember, as the ancient fathers taught, the devil is the architect of all evil, the one who sows the seeds of doubt, fear, and despair. Yet, God does not abandon us in these trials. As St. Paul and St. Peter professed, we must find joy even in our suffering, for through it we are refined and brought closer to the Divine.

To navigate this labyrinth of anxiety, one must first discern its origin. Is it borne out of personal insecurities, external pressures, or perhaps spiritual warfare? Identifying the root cause is the first step towards healing.

Once identified, the Orthodox tradition offers a plethora of spiritual tools to combat this ailment:

1. Confession to the Church Fathers: Pour out your heart, detailing the onset and nature of your anxiety. This act of vulnerability can be cathartic and provide clarity.
2. Unceasing Prayer: Retreat, if possible, to a monastery and immerse yourself in prayer, allowing the fathers to intercede on your behalf.
3. Resilience: The devil seeks to dishearten us. By holding onto hope, we deny him victory.
4. Spiritual Practices: Engage in fasting, prayer, prostration, and reading of the holy scriptures. These acts fortify the spirit against the onslaught of anxiety.
5. Repentance: Sin distances us from God. By repenting, we bridge that gap and find peace in His embrace.
6. Engage in Spiritual Music and Literature: Let the divine words and melodies cleanse and rejuvenate your soul.
7. Unwavering Faith: Cast all your burdens onto God, trusting in His infinite wisdom and mercy.

Our Savior, Jesus Christ, beckons us with open arms, offering solace and peace. He reminds us not to be burdened by the worries of tomorrow but to seek His kingdom and righteousness above all.

In your moments of doubt, remember that God's grace is ever-present. Anxiety, in its essence, is a manifestation of disbelief. By nurturing our faith, we shield ourselves from its clutches.

Lastly, dear seeker, know that you are not alone in your struggle. The community, the church, and all believers stand with you. We shall keep you in our prayers, imploring the Almighty to grant you peace and strength.

May the cross of our Lord shine brightly, and may the Virgin Mother of God intercede on your behalf. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
