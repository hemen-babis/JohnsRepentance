About the type of #cross we should wear

Question፦ Hello, I was wondering if it's okay to wear other kinds of cross necklaces (gold, silverm etc) or does it have to be the type that is shaped in wood? If so, I was wondering what the meaning is. Thank you!

Answer፦ The cross is a special symbol for the Orthodox Church members and indicates our unity in faith. 

As our Lord, God, and Savior Jesus Christ said, "If anyone loves me, he will carry the cross and follow me." The cross represents the martyrdom of suffering in the Christian life, the spiritual activities we do, and the good deeds that we do voluntarily. It reminds us that there are many trials we must face.

Thirdly, the cross is our power, symbolizing the source of all life. It is the symbol from which we overcome all demons and rise to victory over all our subtle spiritual enemies.

Therefore, it is important to consider which type of cross wear. We know that the cross on which our Lord Jesus Christ was crucified was made of wood. The Ethiopian Orthodox Church, uses wood, bronze and gold and other metals to make a cross which is prepared according to our canon.

Although gold, silver, and bronze are valuable, it would be impossible to make crosses out of these minerals for everyone to use because they are too expensive. The grace given by God through the cross is not valued by money. The cross has a subtle grace and power from God that cannot be bought. Therefore, we should bind the cross to be a symbol of our religion, whatever it may be made from, so that we can be protected from the power of demons.

The bronze cross is similar to the altar tool used in the Old Testament sacrifices, while the silver and gold crosses are symbols of God's grace. The gold cross represents the purity, novelty and previousness of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. 

In summary, the Orthodox Church uses wooden, bronze, silver, and gold crosses in service. Each material has its own significance, and we can understand their meaning through comparison with the prophecy in Daniel chapter 2. The great image that King Nebuchadnezzar saw had a head of fine gold, chest and arms of silver, belly and loins of bronze, and thighs of iron.

Just as the stone that struck the image became a great mountain and filled the earth completely, the cross is a powerful symbol that reminds us of God's grace, protection, and victory over our enemies. We should therefore cherish and respect the cross as a manifestation of good things and as a protection against evil.

All of these elements have their own secrets of manifestation.

Nebuchadnezzar saw the vision because God intended to reveal something to him but he couldn't understand it. The prophet Daniel, a man of God, was able to interpret this mystery and reveal it. This was in contrast to the sorcerers and soothsayers who were far from God's help.

Thus, when God revealed the vision to Nebuchadnezzar, there was a wide commentary on what each element represented, such as gold, silver, clay, bronze, and iron. Since we are allowed to use them all for good, it means that we can use the cross made of gold, bronze, silver, and wood, among other materials.
