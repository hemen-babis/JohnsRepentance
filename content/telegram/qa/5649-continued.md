✍🏾Elihu Son of Barachel Meek in Wisdom and Patience

Elihu, the son of Barachel the Buzite, is another great example of meekness revealed through patience, respect, and discernment. During the trial of the righteous Job, Elihu quietly listened as Job and his three older friends spoke at length. Yet when their words strayed from truth, Elihu did not remain silent.

The Scripture says:

“So these three men ceased answering Job, because he was righteous in his own eyes. Then the wrath of Elihu, the son of Barachel the Buzite, of the family of Ram, was aroused; his wrath was aroused against Job because he justified himself rather than God. Also against his three friends his wrath was aroused, because they had found no answer, and yet had condemned Job. Now because they were older than he, Elihu had waited to speak to Job.”(Job 32:1–4)

Elihu’s restraint is a mark of meekness. Though he was young, he honored the elders by waiting for them to finish before speaking. When he finally spoke, his words were humble yet filled with divine understanding:

“I am young in years, and you are very old; therefore I was afraid, and dared not declare my opinion to you.

I said, ‘Age should speak, and multitude of years should teach wisdom.’
But there is a spirit in man, and the breath of the Almighty gives him understanding.”
(Job 32:6–8)

Elihu acknowledged that true wisdom comes not merely from age, but from the Spirit of God dwelling within a person. His humility allowed him to discern rightly between human reasoning and divine truth.

He continued,

“I waited for your words, I listened to your reasonings, while you searched out what to say.
Indeed, there was no one who refuted Job, no one among you who answered his words.”
(Job 32:11–12)

✍🏾The Holy Virgin Mary – The Meek and Faithful Mother

Our Lady, the Blessed Virgin Mary, is the perfect image of meekness, humility, and faith. She is often called “the Meek Dove” because of her gentle spirit and total obedience to the will of God. When the angel Gabriel brought her the divine message, she did not question or doubt but submitted herself entirely to the Lord’s plan, saying:

“Behold, I am the servant of the Lord; let it be to me according to your word.”
(Luke 1:38)

In her humility, she accepted the mystery of the Incarnation with quiet faith. Though she became the Mother of God, she never sought honor or recognition. Instead, she treasured the works of God in silence, pondering them deeply in her heart:

“But Mary kept all these things and pondered them in her heart.”
(Luke 2:19)

At the wedding in Cana of Galilee, her meekness and trust in her Son were again revealed. Seeing the need of others, she gently said to the servants,

“Do whatever He tells you.”(John 2:5)

Her words show not only her faith but her humility in serving God’s purpose quietly and effectively.

When many abandoned Christ during His suffering, the Holy Virgin remained faithful to the end. She stood weeping at the foot of the Cross, sharing in the pain of her Son with a heart full of love and compassion. There, her meek devotion was honored, for Christ entrusted her to all humanity, saying:

“ Behold your mother, .”(John 19:26–27)
