👉🏾 About restrictions women have when they are on their #periods

👉🏾 Question፦ Hello, 

I was wondering why girls aren’t allowed to go inside the church when they are on their periods? A question was asked & I wasn’t sure what the church’s teaching on this is. 

Thank you 🙏🏾

👉 Answer፦ Peace and grace be multiplied unto you, beloved in Christ.

You ask well, for to seek understanding is itself a fragrant offering before God. Many stumble here, imagining that the Church despises womanhood, or that the natural courses of the body are sin. Far be it! He who fashioned Eve from Adam's side and took flesh from the womb of the Virgin would never call His own handiwork defiled. "All that God created is very good," says the Scripture (Genesis 1:31).

Why then this ancient restraint? The fathers teach us that it is not a sentence of guilt, but a hedge of reverence. From of old, the spilling of human blood - whether from a wound or the hidden chamber of nature - has been kept apart from the holy place. For within the sanctuary is poured only the Blood of the Lamb, which alone takes away the sin of the world. The law of Moses foreshadowed this mystery; Christ fulfilled it. Yet the Church, like a wise mother, preserves the sign so that our hearts may never grow careless before the dread and life-giving Mysteries.

Mark this well: the woman in her days is not exiled from God. Let her pray in her chamber, let her lift her heart like incense, let her read the divine Scriptures and call on the Name above every name. Only let her refrain, for a little while, from crossing the threshold where the Blood of Christ speaks more loudly than all earthly blood. This is no reproach but a schooling in awe, that all of us - men and women, whole and wounded - may learn that the House of God is heaven upon earth.

Therefore, my child, receive this ordinance not as a yoke of shame but as a reminder of the majesty of Him who sanctifies both body and soul. When the appointed days are past, enter again with joy, and know that the Lord who fashioned you is never far from those who seek Him in purity of heart.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
