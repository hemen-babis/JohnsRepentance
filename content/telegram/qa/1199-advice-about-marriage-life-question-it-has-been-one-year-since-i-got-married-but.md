Advice about #marriage life

Question: It has been one year since I got married but I couldn't have physical intimacy with my wife, because she has no feelings at all, she feels great pain, we tried different solutions, advice, treatment, prayers, but we couldn't find a solution, what solution do you give us?

Answer: In facing such a challenging situation, we turn to the teachings of the Church and seek solace in our faith. What does the Church offer to the needy? Prayer. Our questioner has mentioned that they have already tried prayer, along with various solutions, advice, treatment, and yet they have not found a solution. As Christians, we believe in the power of prayer and in God's ability to heal, but we must also recognize that God's timing and plan may be different from our own.

When we approach prayer, it is important not to limit it to a fixed timeframe. God knows what we may not think, and He knows what we do not know. There may be something that will ultimately benefit us in ways we cannot yet comprehend. Our approach to prayer should not be focused solely on a predetermined date or outcome. It is essential to remember that everything is in God's hands.

If we set a specific date by which we expect to be saved or find a solution, and if that date passes without the desired outcome, it is not right for us to abandon our faith or lose hope. Instead, we should continue to seek God's guidance and intervention. By practicing the faith to the fullest, actively partaking from the Eucharist, We beseech God to make all things right. We have fulfilled our part, and if we grow weary and do not find an immediate solution, we must trust that we have done what we can.

It is crucial to understand that not all prayers are answered according to our desires or timeline. We must recognize that there are aspects beyond our control, and we have our part to play while God has His. Yet, even in situations where we do not understand the reasons behind our circumstances, we should accept God's will with joy. Ultimately, the solution lies in God Himself, and we should not lose sight of this truth.

During such challenges in life, it is not uncommon for people to offer help and solutions outside the teachings of the Church. However, we must remember that this is ultimately God's work. It is God's will for a husband and wife to be united and for them to be helpers to each other. It is not appropriate for anyone to intervene and suggest alternative solutions based on their own judgment. We must have faith in God's plan and trust that He will guide us through our difficulties.

Patience is a virtue we learn through our relationship with God. It is His will for us to come together in marriage, and beyond that, it is His will for us to remain united as one and potentially have children. We must not do anything that interferes with God's plan. We should trust that our actions will not harm our souls or bodies. We can draw inspiration from the story of Hannah and Joachim, who endured years of childlessness but remained steadfast in their faith. In their patience, God blessed them with a child who became the salvation of the entire world. Likewise, God worked a miraculous birth through the Virgin Mary, and He granted Elizabeth, who was old, a child who would baptize the Lord.

Therefore, dear member, your story is unique, and God has something in store for you that we may not yet comprehend. Perhaps it is a path towards holiness and sanctification. Have you considered this possibility? Remember the example of Demetrius and Princess Wine, who maintained their purity and holiness for forty-eight years, sleeping in the same bed, wearing the same clothes, and laying the same carpet. They trusted in God's plan and purpose. Who knows what God has prepared for you? We must contemplate this thought.
