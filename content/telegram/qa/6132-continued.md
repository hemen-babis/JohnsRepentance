7.The Night Prayer (6:00 PM)

This is the hour when we remember the expected coming of our Lord.

It is also the hour when sleep and distraction are strongest, making prayer more difficult and more valuable.

At this time, we may pray as we are able:

👉🏾wudase maryam

👉🏾Bartos Prayer

👉🏾Arganon

👉🏾Sene Golgotha 

👉🏾Prayers to the Seven Archangels

What Should We Pray During the Seven Times?

The Church does not imprison us with one fixed prayer per hour. She gives us life, not chains.
At each of the seven times, we may pray:

👉🏾Wudase Maryam and Tselote Maryam

👉🏾Abune Zebesemayat (አቡነ ዘበሰማያት)

👉🏾The Psalms of David (መዝሙረ ዳዊት, Mezmure Dawit), divided through the day

👉🏾Gospel prayers, short supplications, praises, and Gedlat

✍🏾These are the prayers by which saints repented, wept, endured, and were sanctified. When we pray them, we do not stand alone. We stand inside the same river of prayer that carried the righteous before us.

✍🏾The Heart of the Teaching

God does not count our prayers. He receives our hearts.The value of prayer is not in length, but in attention.Not in quantity, but in humility.Not in perfection, but in repentance and faith.Even a short prayer, offered with a watchful heart, is precious before God.
