(Continued part)👆👇.
 
In general, the sacrament does not depend on the state of marriage; After all, the Holy Church has never commanded us to stay away from the Holy Communion for a single day until marriage. This is an attitude that some people have brought by habit, or even counselors and teachers have been absorbed in the people's system, not God's system; and we need to understand that it is not the correct teaching of the Church. Besides, it is not an honor for us to continue to be a father of repentance for people living far from the Holy Communion, but a great shame. Why? The first and foremost task of a penitent father is to teach his penitent children, equip them for penance and Holy Communion, and watch over them to live steadfastly in this spiritual life. But as we can see what is happening in practice, does everyone who is a Christian receive the Holy flesh and the precious blood of Christ?  Not really.
 
On the other hand although it is not comparable with our faith, when we see other faith institutions around the world who claim to be in Christianity, even those who do not believe in partaking fekm His holy flesh and precious blood do not leave without receiving 'in rememberence' each time they come to their church, never. Our church, however, does not consider the Holy Communion as a memorial, and not do not take this mystery simply or boldly. Rather, we receive it believing that it is the real holy flesh and his noble blood that our Creator shed for us through all that suffering on Friday; and we accept it in the fear of God . This is Christian ethics. As Christian ethics, we correct our wrongdoing and accept it through a life of repentance. So, the only thing that may keeps us far from the Holy Communion before and during marriage is our own sin.

So the answer to the question is that if there is a time when we are different from Holy Communion, the biggest thing is our own reason. Thi may be, either we didn't get real lesson of the Orthodox Church; Or that we are not willing to repent for what we committed a sin; Or that we have not decided to stop that sin. But, in general, there is no time to deprive us the Holy Communion except in this way.
