The division of religion or the difference of faith began on the day the angels were created. The division was based on the betrayal of the devil and the belief of the angels. Among mankind, the division of religion began with the sons of Adam, Abel and Cain. It is remembered that when Abel, the son of Adam, offered a pure lamb as a sacrifice, saying that God loves something pure, but Cael offered it with weeds, believing that God would not eat it, what would he do to it? This indicates that there was a difference of faith between Abel and Cael. Next, Isaac's sons, who started pushing while still in his mother's womb, Jacob, who is called Israel, and Esau, who gave up his birthright and sold it in lentil stew, became the reason for the division of religion. (Genesis 25:21-23)

But after these events, faith is seen to be an unstoppable issue that is increasing every day as the number of human beings increases. A large number of individuals have come to the conclusion that there is no creator and no religion due to the lack of answers to the question of what is the truth. That is why we say that not all faith can be called religion, but religion can be called faith.
