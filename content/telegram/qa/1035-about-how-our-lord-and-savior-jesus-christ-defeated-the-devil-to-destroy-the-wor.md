---About how our Lord and Savior #Jesus #Christ defeated the devil. 

"To destroy the works of the devil, the Son of God has been revealed" (1 John 3:8).

Dear children of God, we appreciate you very much when we see that you are following the teachings and spiritual advice that we always convey on John's Repentance page. As God has entrusted us to serve we entrust you to read and friends and family. 

Something new and wonderful happened in earth 5500 years after creation. It was a miracle that has never happened before, an abstract secret that is impossible for a creature but possible for a creator: the secret of Christ's birth from his mother, the Holy Virgin Mary, who gave Him flesh and became a man. Even though it was prophesied that He would come down from heaven and be born of Mary, it was a marvel that He was born from her body, her soul, and her flesh.

Evidence:

The apostle St. Paul wrote, "In the past God spoke to our ancestors through the prophets at many times and in various ways, but in these last days he has spoken to us by his Son, whom he appointed heir of all things, and through whom also he made the universe" (Hebrews 1:1-2).

The apostle St. Paul said, "When the end of the age came, God sent his Son, born of a woman, born under the law" (Galatians 4:4).

Christ's main purpose and reason for coming to earth in the flesh was to live forever in glory as the first physical word of His character. He experienced hunger, thirst, weariness, death, illness, and everything that human beings go through. This was necessary to make Him compatible with Adam's nature (1 John 3:8) so that He could destroy the works of the devil.

What is the work of the devil? The devil's work is to commit sin. Betrayal, arrogance, and falsehood are some examples of sins that the devil committed.

1st/ Betrayal

The devil, who had the position of leadership in the world of angels, arose an evil desire and sought the authority of the Creator, denying the Creatorship of God, even denying himself, and sowing the weeds of betrayal. The devil, filled with the rebellion of apostasy in his evil mind, sowed the weeds of apostasy on the hearts of Adam's children. (Mathew 13:25) But Christ removed Adam and his children from the sin of apostasy that the devil brought into the world, destroyed the tares that the devil had sown in the wheat field, and removed them from the hearts of mankind. (Ezekiel 36:25-32)

"However, false prophets were also among the people, and they will be false teachers among you, and they will deny even the Lord who redeemed them, and many will follow them in their own ways, and the path of truth will be insulted by them." (2 Peter 1:1:2) 

"For certain individuals whose condemnation was written about long ago have secretly slipped in among you. They are ungodly people, who pervert the grace of our God into a license for immorality and deny Jesus Christ our only Sovereign and Lord." (Jude 1:4) 

"He says, 'They deny religion, they are numb, as if they are burning in their own conscience'" (1 Timothy 4:2) which describes the devil's work of treason and explains his evil deeds.
