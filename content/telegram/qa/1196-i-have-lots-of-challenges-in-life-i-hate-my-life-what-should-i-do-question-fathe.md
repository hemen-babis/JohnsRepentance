---I have lots of challenges in #life. I hate my life, what should I do?

Question፦ Fathers, I am suffering a lot on this earth, what should I do? I pray every day so lack of prayer is not the reason.  3 days ago, I tried to commit suicide but couldn't. And even today, I'm not happy. The problem is:

1) I am divorced
2) I work a lot but have nothing
3) I don't get along with my family. Nobody likes me. I live in my mother's house with my kids.

Answer፦ Beloved child of God, we mpathize deeply with the suffering and pain you have endured on this earthly journey. It is in times like these that we must turn to our faith and seek solace in the wisdom and guidance of our Lord. Know that your struggles are not in vain, for God often uses trials to refine us and elevate us to greater heights of nobility and honor.

As human beings, we often fail to recognize the abundance of blessings that God bestows upon us. We become complacent and fail to work diligently to cultivate and nurture the gifts that have been entrusted to us. However, when faced with small challenges, we easily become disheartened and feel as if we are being consumed by fire, pierced by a knife, or plagued by misfortune. These hardships weigh heavily on our spirits, testing our inner strength in ways that seem unbearable.

Yet, remember the words of Scripture: "O Lord, deliver me from this snare, free me from the net in which I am entangled, and rescue me from the temptations that surround me." To shield us from these trials, we have been instructed to pray, "Lord, lead us not into temptation."

Though today may appear bleak and life may seem like an arduous journey, take heart, for we are alive in Christ forever. Though weariness may overwhelm us, our inner strength is far greater. Though we may be poor in material possessions, God, by His grace and divine will, will make us abundantly rich. He is the one who bestows honor upon us in countless ways, showering us with blessings beyond measure. This test that seems insurmountable is but a stepping stone to a future of joy and happiness.

Remember, God works in ways that surpass our understanding and imagination. His gifts are immeasurable and boundless, bestowed upon us generously. He may appear to be a judge, yet if He believes in us and loves us, He tests us with challenges that may seem insurmountable, but He knows we can overcome. 

To you, dear questioner, I beseech you, believe that this trial you are facing is a foundation and a bridge to a brighter tomorrow. Trust that God's plan for you encompasses things you cannot fathom or comprehend at this moment. Embrace the gift of faith and hold steadfast in the knowledge that God, in His infinite wisdom, is working in your life.

Regarding your sense of being unloved and rejected, do not perceive yourself as unlovable or despised by others. Love is a complex matter, and sometimes the reasons behind others' actions or emotions remain concealed. Do not hastily conclude that you are hated or cursed, for such thoughts only serve to empower Satan's influence in your life. Instead, seek the truth that lies beyond appearances and pray for understanding and reconciliation.

In your current circumstances, living in your mother's house with your children, it may be challenging to find peace and harmony within your family. However, I implore you, do not lose hope. Trust in God's providence and believe that He can heal and restore relationships. Many have experienced the mending of their lives through God's intervention, and I firmly believe the same can happen for you.

In your darkest hours and moments of sadness, turn to the Mother of God, the Blessed Virgin Mary. Entrust your troubles to her, for she is a source of comfort and strength. I have faith that she will intercede on your behalf and guide you through this storm. Please reach out to us at our address, for we are here to support you and walk alongside you on this journey of healing and restoration.
