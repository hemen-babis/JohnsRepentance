6. If Jesus is a judgmental God, how did he receive the death penalty?

(continued part)

Among the questions that many believers and heretics raise, why did Jesus die? takes precedence. The tendency of those who question this is to say that Jesus is not almighty God but a creature. It is possible to answer the question with a question by saying: - if he is a being, how did he conquer death and get up?  For there is no one among creatures who has died of his own will and has conquered death by his power. When we look at it broadly, why did Jesus Christ come? suddenly or on purpose? If it came with a purpose, what was the purpose? It is imperative to clarify the question.

Jesus Christ came to this earth with a plan and purpose. Its aims are exemplary and redemptive. Redemption means that Jesus shed his blood and broke his body to free Adam from the slavery of the devil and the citizenship of hell. Without being born of the Virgin Mary, without being baptized, without fasting, without being crucified, and without suffering all that, he could have done everything by his word alone. But the human being learns good and bad things from the human being himself, in order to teach human beings the way of life not by words but by deeds, he came to this earth by being born as a human being and doing everything that a human does except sin, and teaching us not to sin but to do righteousness (to be an example).

For example, after Jesus Christ was baptized by his servant John in the Jordan River, he made a law saying that whoever believed and was baptized would be saved, he entered the monastery of Corinth and after fasting for forty days and forty nights, he taught that when you fast, you should not be seen as hypocrites. After teaching by day and praying by night at Debrezeit, he warned us not to enter into temptation and pray. Finally, he washed the feet of the apostles and taught humility by saying, "Do to others as I have done to you." "For I have given you an example that you should do as I have done for you." (John 13:15)

For those who ask us why Christ died, another answer to their question will be why Christ was born. Adam was created and we Adamites are born from Adam to live forever. But Christ was born to die. What we should note about this is that birth and creation are very different things. For there is creation in our birth. But it should not be created at the birth of Jesus Christ. For He is eternal without beginning and without end. "I am the Alpha and the Omega, the first and the last, the beginning and the end." (Revelation 22:13)

So God brought us from non-existence to existence so that we might praise his name and inherit his glory and live forever. But when we, who were created to live, chose death instead of life, shame instead of honor, sorrow instead of happiness, and when we were expelled from heaven and descended to hell, God took our debt and sin upon himself, accepted the death sentence which we deserved, and returned us to our former glory. He died in order to achieve this goal, not by accident or by others' coercion. The Lord said that his death was by his will and his resurrection was under his authority. (Joh 10:15-18) The mystery of his crucifixion is to give us life. "Just as Moses revealed the serpent in the wilderness, so the Son of Man deserves to be crucified so that everyone who believes in him will not perish but have eternal life." (John 3:14-15)
