About #peace and equality in #society 

👉 Question፦ Why is it difficult for people to live together in peace and see each other as equals? 

Answer፦ In Orthodox Christianity, the belief is that every person is created in the image and likeness of God. This fundamental teaching highlights the inherent value and dignity of every individual, emphasizing the importance of living together in peace and treating each other as equals. It raises the question: If we are all created as holy beings in the image of God, why is it so difficult for us to coexist harmoniously?

One of the underlying challenges lies in the deviation from our natural identity. In today's era, where various ideologies and beliefs abound, there are conflicting views on the origin and nature of humanity. While Orthodox Christianity affirms that humans are God's handiwork, some other perspectives propose alternative explanations, such as humans descending from apes. These divergent beliefs create a disconnect and contribute to conflicts among individuals, as they fail to recognize the shared foundation of our creation.

When we distance ourselves from our natural identity and disregard the fact that we are made in the image of God, we deviate from the correct understanding of our purpose and nature. God has taught us to love, respect, and refrain from harming one another. However, when individuals forget their divine origin, their behavior and mindset become distorted, leading to actions that contradict the principles of love and unity.

When we fail to acknowledge the inherent value of others and view them as equals, it stems from a lack of trust. This lack of trust is often fueled by the desire for honor, seeking personal recognition and avoiding any perception of inferiority. However, a person who is created in the image of God should not harbor such thoughts or engage in such behavior. It is important to remember that the purpose of our creation is not to exalt ourselves and seek superiority, but rather to glorify God's name and inherit His Kingdom.

The absence of trust gives rise to a pursuit of glory, vengeance, fame, and pride. It causes people to lose sight of their inherent equality and disrupts the bond between individuals. Love, which should be the foundation of our relationships, becomes scarce, and people find it difficult to trust one another equally. In the Orthodox Christian perspective, God's love transcends boundaries of race and language. As believers, we are called to adopt a Christian worldview that recognizes the equality of all people, for Christ died for each and every one of us.

The solution lies in embracing the Christian faith and cultivating a Christian mindset. To see everyone as equals, we need to realize that we all belong to God and that we are united before Him. By adopting an Orthodox perspective, which encompasses the teachings of Christ, we can overcome the barriers of distrust and perceive one another with the eyes of love and equality. This includes loving ourselves, loving our enemies, blessing those who curse us, doing good to those who harm us, and loving those who hate us, as Christ Himself taught.

Therefore, the key to living together in peace and seeing each other as equals is to follow Christ and embody His teachings. By recognizing the sacrificial love of Christ, who offered His holy body and precious blood for all humanity, we come to understand that His love extends to everyone. As Orthodox Christians, we strive to emulate His example and perceive all individuals as one, irrespective of their differences. May the grace of the Good Healer guide us in obeying His commandments and working towards our salvation.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
