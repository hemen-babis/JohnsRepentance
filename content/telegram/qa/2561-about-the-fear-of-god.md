👉 About the #fear of God 

Question፦ Psalm 2:11 “Serve the Lord with fear, and rejoice with trembling” 

What does it mean to fear the Lord ? Are we supposed to be scared or is it about having respect / reverence. Or is it the type of fear where we know our lives and destiny is in His hands and how we should do good in fear so we don’t end up in hell?

Answer፦ Beloved in Christ, your inquiry touches upon a profound aspect of our spiritual journey—understanding the fear of the Lord. In the Orthodox tradition, this concept is not about terror or dread, but rather a deep reverence and awe for God, acknowledging His infinite holiness and majesty. It is the beginning of wisdom, as Solomon the Wise proclaims in Proverbs 9:10, "The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom, and knowledge of the Holy One is understanding." This fear is a form of love, a recognition of God’s omnipotence and our desire to live in harmony with His will.

It is indeed a respectful fear, one that compels us to turn away from sin and embrace a life of virtue, not out of terror of punishment, but out of love and a desire not to grieve our Heavenly Father. It’s a call to remember that our actions in this life echo into eternity, shaping our relationship with the Divine.

This reverence is nurtured through prayer, participation in the sacraments, and living a life in accordance with the teachings of Christ and His Church. It is about cultivating a heart that is sensitive to the presence of God in all aspects of our lives, leading us to act justly and love mercy.

As you contemplate this reverence for the Lord, ask yourself: How can I cultivate a deeper sense of awe and love for God in my daily life? How might this reverent fear guide me to live in closer communion with Him and His creation?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
