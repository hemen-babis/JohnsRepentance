---About #Faith 

👉 Question፦ Thanks for answering my previous question.

What does faith look like practically while we wait for the things we pray for? Things like jobs, exams, graduate school and overall success in life. How does one approach these things patiently while having strong faith in God and maintaining a positive mindset.

So in summary,

* What should the mindset of someone who has faith be like? Is faith just a mindset of or it also an action? And if so, what actions do faithful people take?

Answer፦ The practical embodiment of faith, especially in seasons of waiting and anticipation, is a vivid tapestry woven with threads of trust, patience, and active engagement in the life Christ has called us to.

To envisage faith practically, consider the words of St. James the Just, "Faith without works is dead" (James 2:26). This profound truth elucidates that faith, while deeply internal, is also vibrantly external, manifesting through our deeds, our patience, and our steadfastness. Faith is not merely an abstract sentiment or hopeful mindset; it is a dynamic force, compelling us towards love, good works, and unwavering trust in God's providence.

In times of waiting—for a job, for academic success, for any of life's myriad milestones—let your faith be active. Pray unceasingly, as St. Paul exhorts, but also engage with the world as a beacon of Christ's love. Study diligently, prepare thoroughly for your endeavors, and serve others selflessly, embodying the faith you cherish.

Moreover, recall the patience of Job, the perseverance of Abraham, the unwavering trust of the Theotokos. They did not passively await God's promises but lived each day in vibrant faith, anchoring their souls in hope, even amidst trials.

In embracing this active faith, cultivate a mindset of gratitude and trust, recognizing each day as a gift and each challenge as an opportunity to witness the steadfastness of your faith. Such a mindset transcends mere optimism, anchoring you in the profound truth of God's unchanging love and promises.

Now, dear one, as you contemplate your journey, ask yourself: How can I embody my faith today, not just as an internal conviction but as a visible, tangible expression of trust in God's plan for me?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
