About #blood #donation and use of #contraceptives 

👉 Question፦ Bless me my father and peace from Kenya... please enlighten me on the our church's view on the use of contraceptives and blood donation

Answer፦ Blessings to you, child of God from the radiant lands of Kenya!

The Orthodox Church, while rooted deeply in the traditions of the early Church, recognizes the complexities of modern life. In matters of conscience and personal ethics, the wisdom of the Church Fathers and Holy Scriptures guides us.

Concerning contraceptives: The Orthodox Church holds that the primary purpose of marriage is the union of two souls and the potential for the procreation of children. The use of contraceptives is not universally accepted. While some local churches may permit the use of non-abortive contraceptives within marriage for specific reasons, always it is with the understanding that it is not to be used for mere convenience or to live a life free from children. As Saint John Chrysostom wrote, “To reject children in marriage, to avoid the blessing of fertility is to diminish the greatness of God’s gift." Therefore, it's crucial to approach this matter with prayer, spiritual reflection, and counsel from a spiritual father.

Regarding blood donation: The act of giving one's blood, the very essence of life, to save another is a beautiful representation of Christ's sacrificial love. The Scriptures remind us, “Greater love has no one than this, that someone lay down his life for his friends.” (John 15:13). By donating blood, we share in this life-giving act and embrace our call to love our neighbors.

In both matters, dear seeker, it's essential to ground your decisions in prayer and seek guidance from a spiritual father or guide within the Orthodox tradition.
