About the #Soul After #Death

Question፦ What is the Ethiopian Orthodox Church Teaching on the Soul After Death?

Answer፦ The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church teaches that after death, the soul and body separate. The soul continues to exist in an abstract form, while the body is laid to rest in the grave. It is believed that both the wicked and the righteous will experience this separation, but their ultimate fate differs.

According to the Orthodox doctrine, those who have committed evil deeds will go to hell, while those who have done good deeds will go to heaven. The criteria for entering heaven are based on the teachings of Matthew chapter 25, where Jesus speaks of acts of kindness such as giving bread and drink to the needy. It is believed that the righteous, who have demonstrated virtuous behavior in their earthly lives, will be rewarded with eternal life in heaven.

The concept of the afterlife in the kingdom of heaven is described as a state free from hunger, thirst, fatigue, envy, and racism. It is a realm where worldly limitations and transient nature cease to exist. The Church teaches that this eternal life is obtained through the resurrection, and it emphasizes that believers will not die but will be transformed.

The example of Christ's resurrection serves as a model for believers. Jesus, after being buried and guarded by soldiers, rose without the need for someone to open the grave or release Him. Similarly, the Church teaches that believers will be resurrected by the authority of Jesus Christ. It is believed that regardless of the circumstances of death, whether through burial or non-burial, all individuals will ultimately rise again when Christ returns.

The teaching of St. Paul provides further insight into the understanding of resurrection. He used the analogy of planting seeds to illustrate that just as grains sown in various conditions will ultimately grow, so too will individuals rise in accordance with their deeds. The wicked will rise with their sinful deeds, while the righteous will rise with their righteous deeds.

In summary, the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church affirms the existence of life after death. It teaches that the soul and body separate at the time of death, and while the body is laid to rest in the grave, the soul continues to exist. The ultimate fate of individuals is determined by their actions in life, with the wicked facing hell and the righteous attaining eternal life in heaven. The Church emphasizes the importance of resurrection, drawing inspiration from the example of Christ's resurrection, and teaches that believers will be transformed and enter into a state free from worldly limitations in the kingdom of heaven.
