---About deepening our #understanding of God

👉 Question፦ I'm willing to take Literally any step to deepen my understanding of Christ and His Church, i feel as church would be one of my options, speaking to intelligent people that are living for Christ and also want to see some things that i could learn about spirituality, and when people say god does everyone mean the same god but different opinions about god, or one specific god that they believe in? Even though I am very confused and lost in this world I really do believe there was a creator of a universe which people called god and i believed it was the christian god! But as i started to hear everyone say things about Christianity and and saying religions are fake and they where showing proof might’ve been fake but it is so believable and understandable that you will be curious and don’t know what to believe anymore and it says bibles are rewrote and made by satanic elites I was so broken but i still continued with my faith, I just wish god could come talk to me or give me a sign!

Answer፦ My dear seeker of truth, your earnest inquiry touches the heart deeply. In your journey towards understanding Christ and His Church, it is vital to remember that God reaches out to each of us in unique ways, often through the quiet whispers of our hearts rather than in loud proclamations.

In the quest for spiritual depth, the Orthodox Church offers a rich tapestry of tradition, prayer, and community, deeply anchored in the life and teachings of Jesus Christ. Our faith is not merely an intellectual exercise, but a living, breathing encounter with the Divine.

Regarding your question about God, Christianity professes belief in the Holy Trinity – one God in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. This belief is distinct and different from other conceptions of God found in various religions. As St. John of Damascus eloquently stated, “I do not worship creation. I worship the Creator of creation, who became part of creation for my sake.”

The doubts and confusion you experience are not uncommon in our age, where myriad voices and opinions clamor for attention. It is important to discern the truth amidst this noise. Remember the words of Christ in John 8:32, “And you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free.” This journey to truth is not a solitary one; it is traversed in the company of those who seek and love Christ.

Regarding the Bible, it is a sacred text, divinely inspired yet penned by human hands. While translations and interpretations may vary, its core message of redemption, love, and the revelation of God in Jesus Christ remains steadfast. In Orthodoxy, the Bible is understood within the living Tradition of the Church, which safeguards its meaning and application.

You long for a sign from God, and in this longing, you are not alone. God speaks to us in various ways – through Scripture, the beauty of creation, the love of others, and the still, small voice within our own hearts. Sometimes, the sign we seek is found in the very journey of seeking itself.

In your pursuit of a deeper understanding, I encourage you to connect with a spiritual guide, someone rooted in the Orthodox faith, who can journey with you, answer your questions, and introduce you to the transformative practices of prayer, fasting, and almsgiving that are central to our faith. This can be a clergy from your local Oriental Orthodox and if not, Eastern Orthodox Church and we are always here to answer any questions you may have and are commited to support you throughout your journey towads the Kingdom of God. 

Remember, the journey to God is a journey of the heart, a journey of transformation. What step will you take today to draw nearer to the loving embrace of our Creator?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of The Virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
