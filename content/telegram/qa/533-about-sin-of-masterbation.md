About Sin of Masterbation

Generally avoid looking at these things with the idiom instead of saying "what's wrong since I can control myself"  Because if the natural will of a human being leads him to the idea of adultery, he will begin to consume the idea, and later, when he cannot control himself, the spirit of fornication will come to put the idea into practice. 

Therefore, at the beginning, we should stay away from any sensational and adulterous actions and social networks, especially not to put our hands in the area of shame, and stop not only the act of sin, but also stop thinking about it.

4. Absence of conscious awareness and not praying

Not being diligent in the spiritual life will make us pay a big price. If our lives are not guided by the mentorship of a penitent father, by fasting, prayer and worship, we will be close to Satan's temptations. Therefore, fasting, prayer and prostration are required. There should be a private prayer that you pray morning and night without ceasing. Also, as much as the spirit dwells in the body through fasting and prostration, it burns and flees from us. In addition, if we put the cross or holy books as a shield to protect us from demons in the area where we sleep, then it will burn the demons and weaken their power, so they will disappear completely.

5. Repentance

In the journey of life there is a fall and a rise in sin. The solution to our defilement is repentance. There is no stain of sin or crime that repentance does not wash away. Because of this, even though human beings should repent, they will never stop doing their best to prevent it from happening soon. One of the dangers of procrastination is changing the heart prepared for repentance and destroying the opportunity for repentance. If sin is not eradicated immediately, it becomes a deep-rooted addiction, and this can over time extinguish and harden the spark of repentance in our hearts. Therefore, it is necessary to confess that what we have committed is a serious sin of adultery, we hate this act of destruction from the heart, we are afraid and ashamed of it, and we vow to never return to it, let alone think about committing it in the future, and repent. Our father of repentance has the responsibility of constantly monitoring and teaching us until we completely get out of this sin.

6. The main thing is not to give up!!!

 Even if we fail once, we must rise again and again and continue to the end. The struggle can be hard for you, it can be overwhelming, especially when you are about to be exposed, the struggle is very strong. If you fall every day, if you stumble all week, if you fall in sin every month, falling is always there; The main thing is that there is a change for the better from time to time, so don't give up and shake off your dust and get up for a great fight.
