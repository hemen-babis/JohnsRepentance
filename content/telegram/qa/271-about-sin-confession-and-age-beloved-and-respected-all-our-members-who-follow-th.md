👉 Question: Hello father, I have a question. Do we have to confess sins we unknowingly did as a child (under the age of 18)?

Answer: The answer is yes we have to. If we remember a sin we did, we should tell it to our father of repentance, we should repent for it. There is healing and peace of mind that comes with communicating all our shortcomings with our father of repentance. 

We have to confess for all the sins we did after the age of 7 because that is the age that is set by our fathers. That is the age that a person starts differentiating good from evil. Even though a 7 years old is not required to fast like a 40 years old, they are required to start with the basics, abstaining from meat during the fasting seasons for example.

Our church starts our spiritual training from day one. Note that at the time of baptism, 40 days for boys and 80 days for girls, children abstain from their mother's breast milk until they have partaken from the holy Eucharist. This is for them to start learning to fast, start abbyding by the laws and orders of the Church, to start living Orthodox. Recall that our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ went straight to fasting in the desert of Corinth after he was baptized. And so since the Church's basis is Jesus Christ, the Church teaches her members to fast from ever since they are regenerated through water and the Holy Spirit (after the are baptized). Hence the reason why they fast after they are baptized is for them to learn how to live by the structure, order and laws of the Church. Therefore, how can we say that we don't have to repent for the sins we have commited before the age of 18 while we started our spiritual training from day 1? We should confess all sins we recall because if we recall it, then we did it knowingly which means we were conscious of what we were doing. If there is anything that we recall, we should confess it which is how we will get complete peace. What is the benefit of carrying our sins with us? Let us breathe it out, this will benefit us in many ways but won't harm us in any. 

May God help us confess all our sins so we can stay in His house until the last breathe and inherit His Kingdom.
