Is a girl who was raped required to repent for this?

👉🏾 Question: Peace be with you in Christ, dear family of the Most High God, Virgin Mary's tithes of the proud children of the cross, as well as my fathers and brothers, who tirelessly answer our questions, may God's peace be with you all.My question is If a girl is forced to rape, should she repent in this matter?

👉🏾Answer: Beloved members of John's repentance page, first we need to define what it means to confess. It means to communicate the things that our mind holds against us, the things that we feel guilty of, the things we do not feel comfortable living with to our father of repentance. Hence if our dear sister communicates what happened to her penitent father, she would be benefited but not harmed and it wouldn't be wrong to do so. She will learn how to consulate, comfort and calm herself from the spiritual and fatherly advice so communicating the situation with her spiritual father will benefit her in many ways but won't harm her in any way.

Our fathers say: "if the owner doesn't scream, the neighbors will not hear, the neighbors will not rescue" hence communicating our situation to those who are concerned will be useful but not harmful. 

A person needing medication will only get the right medication after they communicate all their issues but if they hide them, they don't have a cure. Hence since our member has been hurt and are in grief, they need spiritual and fatherly advice to comfort them. Hence it is good if she communicates the situation with her spiritual father who will determine what to do, what she needs to do and how she should continue. "You are not the only one to have faced this my daughter, come down it will be alright. You are not guilty of the sin because you were forced into it. It would have been better if this would not have happened in the first place, but after it did, all we can do is to leave everything to God and say it is for good." 

Repentance is neede for all of us because we all sin all the time. Sin can be performed in 3 different ways: in thought, in word and in action. Hence who is clean form sin of though? Who is clean form sin of word? Only the Virgin Mary is clean from all three types of sins. Hence we all need to confess, communicate our shortcomings to our spiritual father and continuously get spiritual and fatherly advices. Hence it is not only because our dear sister has faced such a terrible situation that she has to go see the priest. She should continuously seek spiritual and fatherly advice and consult with her spiritual father on what to do.

May God help us live a life or repentance so we can stay in His house until the last breathe and inherit His Kingdom.
