👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾 About #incense 

Question :What the incense in holy litury symbolises to?

Answer: My beloved child, may God bless you with abundant grace as you seek to understand the sacred symbols of our Holy Church. Your question about the meaning of incense in the Divine Liturgy is a profound one, for this blessed aroma and its rising smoke are truly an integral part of our worship, speaking to our senses and elevating our souls.

As we continue on this journey of understanding our faith, let us contemplate the rich symbolism of incense. It is not merely an aesthetic element, but a tangible representation of spiritual realities, drawing us closer to the heavenly Kingdom.

The use of incense in worship is deeply rooted in both the Old and New Testaments, flowing from a tradition that spans millennia:

1.  Prayer Ascending to God: This is perhaps the most fundamental and universally understood symbolism. When the fragrant smoke rises heavenward, it symbolizes our prayers ascending to the Most High. The Holy Scriptures eloquently capture this:

    👉🏾"Let my prayer be set forth as incense before thee; the lifting up of my hands as an evening sacrifice." (Psalm 140:2, Septuagint, which is Psalm 141 in other numbering systems)
   
👉🏾 In the Book of Revelation, Saint John the Theologian describes the heavenly liturgy: "And another angel came and stood at the altar, having a golden censer; and there was given unto him much incense, that he should offer it with the prayers of all saints upon the golden altar which was before the throne. And the smoke of the incense, which came with the prayers of the saints, ascended up before God out of the angel’s hand." (Revelation 8:3-4)

    These passages clearly link incense with the petitions and supplications of the faithful, rising as a pleasing aroma before God.

2.  The Presence and Sweetness of God's Grace: 

The sweet-smelling aroma of the incense symbolizes the presence of God's grace, the Holy Spirit, and the virtues of the saints. Just as a pleasant fragrance permeates the air, so too does God's holiness sanctify the space and the faithful. It is a reminder that we are in the presence of the Divine, and that His grace is poured out upon us

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
