👉 About the Malk of St. #Mary and the Malk of our Lord and Savior #Jesus Christ 

👉🏾Question፦ Hello. What is the significance and importance of the Gate of Light for the praises of St. Mary? What is the significance and importance of the Malk of St. Mary and the Malk of Jesus?

Answer፦ Greetings in the peace of Christ. Your inquiry touches upon profound aspects of our faith that elevate the heart to God through the veneration of His saints, notably the Theotokos, St. Mary.

The "Gate of Light" within the context of the Ethiopian Orthodox Church refers to a liturgical hymn, part of the larger corpus sung during the midnight praises in honor of St. Mary. This hymn illuminates her role as the gate through which the True Light, Christ, entered into the world. As proclaimed in the Gospel of John, "The true light that gives light to everyone was coming into the world" (John 1:9). This hymn, therefore, emphasizes the Theotokos as a pivotal figure in our salvation history through whom divine grace entered into our temporal condition.

Regarding the "Malk of St. Mary" and the "Malk of Jesus," these refer to the respective praises and services dedicated to St. Mary and Jesus. In the tradition of the Ethiopian Orthodox Church, a "Malk" is a spiritual song or service of praise. The Malk of St. Mary involves prayers, hymns, and readings that extol her virtues and her role in the mystery of the Incarnation. The Malk of Jesus, similarly, is focused on Christ Himself, praising His divine actions and His human life, from His miraculous birth to His redemptive Passion. These services deepen our connection to the divine through the veneration of Mary, who is always pointing us to her Son, and to Christ Himself as our Savior.

These practices are not just acts of devotion but are deeply transformative, guiding the faithful towards a life reflective of Christ's love and Mary's obedience. Engaging with these praises allows one to enter into a spiritual rhythm that sanctifies time and elevates the soul towards God.
