👉🏾 Question፦ I have another question. What is the spiritual harm in listening to music? Do I always have to confess every time I listen to music or is it every time I engage or indulge in music? Answer፦ Blessings to you, seeker of truth. In our esteemed Orthodox tradition, music that elevates the soul, known as "Mezmur," is highly cherished. These spiritual songs are a means to worship and glorify God, echoing the wisdom of Saint Paul who encouraged, "Speak to one another with psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs" (Ephesians 5:19).

Conversely, what we refer to as "music of the world" often carries themes and messages that may lead one away from the path of righteousness. It is this worldly music that requires careful discernment, as its influence can be contrary to spiritual growth and mindfulness. Confession is advisable when one feels that such music has drawn them into sin or away from contemplative living.

Our service, enriched by a dedicated group of fathers and scholars, warmly invites more individuals to explore the depth of Orthodoxy. Despite our capacity to guide many, we depend on our members to extend our reach. We encourage you, and all who seek spiritual nourishment, to join us and invite others who long for such guidance.

How might you contribute to bringing others into our spiritual family, sharing the peace and joy found in our teachings?
