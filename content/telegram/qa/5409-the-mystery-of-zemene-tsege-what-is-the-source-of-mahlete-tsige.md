👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾The Mystery of #Zemene #Tsege (what is the #source of #Mahlete Tsige) 

(Part 8)

Blessed be God, who in every age reveals the hidden fragrance of His mercy through His saints and His Mother. The time called Zemene Tsege.It is the period in which the Church remembers the fulfillment of ancient prophecy when the barren earth of humanity blossomed through the womb of the Virgin.

👉🏾The holy prophet Joel foretold that “the mountains and hills of Judah shall flow with sweet grass and milk richly.”

✍🏾This prophecy pointed to the time of grace that would come through the Virgin Mary and her Son, our Lord Jesus Christ.

✍🏾When the Blessed Virgin gave birth to Christ, this prophecy was fulfilled in both sweetness and fragrance . "Sweet grass and milk", symbolize the grace that flowed from her pure womb the nourishment of salvation for all creation.

✍🏾In the fullness of time, the same Christ, who took flesh and blood from His Mother, was crucified on the Cross on Good Friday. The Son of Man, who humbled Himself to share our human hunger and thirst, poured forth living water and divine nourishment from His own side. His flesh and blood became the true food and drink of the soul.

✍🏾Through this mystery, the long famine of five thousand five hundred years  the spiritual hunger of mankind came to an end. In Him, the barren earth became fruitful once again, and the sweetness of Joel’s prophecy was fulfilled forever.

4.5 The Grace and Silence of Our Lady Mary

👉🏾“Our Lady Mary, your grace is beyond praise and cannot be measured by words. Hasten to catch the little foxes that destroy our vineyard.”

✍🏾The Evangelist Saint Luke writes, “She kept all these things in her heart” (Luke 2:51), showing how wonderful our Lady truly is. She heard the angel’s message that no creature had ever heard and was blessed with a virgin conception and birth that no one had ever seen. For nine months and five days, her womb was filled with praise. She was blessed by the prayers of angels and honored by her Creator, receiving greater grace and glory than all creation, even more than the seraphim and cherubim. Yet, while she lived in this world, she never said, “I saw this” or “I heard that,” but kept everything in her heart.

✍🏾Eve lost her grace by revealing to the devil the secret given only to her and Adam. But our Lady was blessed for keeping everything within her heart.

✍🏾We learn from both Christ and His Mother the virtue of silence and humility. When Pilate and the chief priests questioned Christ, asking, “Where are you from, and what is your teaching?” He remained silent. He only answered when Pilate said, “I have authority to crucify you” (John 19:9; Matthew 27:14).

👉🏾Therefore, we too must follow the example of our Father, Christ, and our Mother, the Virgin Mary keeping peace, silence, and humility in our hearts.
