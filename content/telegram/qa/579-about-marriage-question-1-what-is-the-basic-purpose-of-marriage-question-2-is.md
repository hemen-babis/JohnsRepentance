About #marriage

👉 Question #1: What is the basic purpose of marriage?

👉 Question #2: Is there a difference between getting married in the Holy Communion and getting married in a secular marriage system?

✍Answer ፦  The Christian way of life is twofold. One is through holy matrimony and the other is living in monasticism. Both require cleanliness. This means the virginity of the body and the purity of repentance. Since this lesson is about marriage as mentioned in the question , we will focus on the direction of salvation in marriage.  Let's look at the meaning and purpose of marriage below:

Marriage: Marriage is a wonderful mystery in which the union of the bridegroom and the bride takes place. This union though marriage is celebrated by a ceremony that takes place in the church with a sacrament which marks the joining, uniting, oneness of the couple.. "A man shall leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two shall become one flesh" (Mathew 19:5). Therefore, marriage is a great mystery in which he and she will not think of anyone for love other than each other. From the day of the marriage, they will have children, share property, and live as long as God allows them.

Marriage is established mainly based on 3 purposes (pillars); These are 1. to help each other, 2. to have a childreen, 3. to be saved from adultery. The goal is to live and walk the path of righteousness as a one heart, one word, in complete unity, and togetherness.  

1. Helping each other: "The Lord God said: It is not good for man to be alone. Let us create a suitable helper for him" (Genesis 2:18). Spiritual marriage is a gift that helps us in the Christian journey of life. In general, the mainstay of marriage is the genuine affection and love between the spouses. "The two will become one flesh" (Matthew 19.5). According to this divine word, both spouses put the needs of their spouse before their own, caring for each other, and honoring God.

2. To have children (to continue generations): "God blessed them and said to them, multiply and fill the earth and subdue it..." (Genesis 1:28) God did not leave us to live without law and order; He said to multiply after He blessed Adam and Eve in Paradise. If He blessed them, it means that He liked the marriage, that is, their union and unity. This also refers to legal marriage. He was present in heaven then and blessed their marriage, and today He is present to bless our marriage in the holy church where his holy body and precious blood were poured out.

3. To be saved from adultery: "For the danger of adultery, let each man have his own wife, and let each women have her own husband" (Cor 7:2); If a person does not live bound by a holy marriage, it is inevitable that he will be tempted by lustful flesh. When we live in religion, it is important to stay away from adultery, so marriage is one way to overcome this temptation.
Based on these three main goals, the goal of marriage is to walk together in a righteous way and gain eternal life and inherit the kingdom of heaven. 

Therefore, since Orthodox married life is a life lived in a spiritual system, it means that the covenant marriage system should be started in the Christian system in the church. This is a ritual of the church that is done with a gift or sacrament.

The difference between sacramental marriage and secular marriage

There are three parts to a sacramental marriage; The couple and God! Without any of these, it is not considered a marriage. This is the mystery in which our flesh becomes flesh and our bones become bones in the Holy Eucharist. “This bone is of my bone; It is through the Holy Communion that God's statement that "the flesh is my flesh" becomes a reality. As it is written, "They will both become one flesh" during the holy marriage ceremony, the conclusion of the great secret of the two bodies becoming physical unity and spiritual unity is the Holy Body and His Precious Blood of Christ. (Mark 10:8).
