3. Zion as the Temple

✍🏾 Because the Ark of the Covenant dwells in the temple, the Ark of Zion is also called the Temple of Zion.

👉🏾 “Every place where the ark of the Lord enters is holy” (1 Kings 8:11)

👉🏾 “Praise be to You, O Lord, in Zion” (Psalm 64:11)

✍🏾 In Zion, on Mount Zion, in the temple:

👉🏾 Thanksgiving is offered

👉🏾 spiritual Songs are sung

👉🏾 The Divine Liturgy is sanctified

👉🏾 Seatat and Mahlet are offered 

👉🏾 The word of God is proclaimed

👉🏾 Praise rises to God

👉🏾 The mourning are comforted

👉🏾 Tears are wiped away

👉🏾 The distressed find peace

✍🏾 In the temple, we give thanks to God for all He has done:

👉🏾 “Thanks be to God for His indescribable gift” (2 Corinthians 9:15)

👉🏾 “I thank You for what You have done for us” (Psalm 51:9)

✍🏾 The temple of Mount Zion is the place where we speak with God, where questions are answered, sins forgiven, histories transformed, snares broken, and mysteries opened. For this reason, the Lord declares that He will not be silent concerning Zion, nor will He rest concerning Jerusalem, the land of peace.
