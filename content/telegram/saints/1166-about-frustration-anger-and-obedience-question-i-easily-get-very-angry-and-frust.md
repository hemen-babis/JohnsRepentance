---About #frustration, #anger and #obedience 

Question፦ I easily get very angry and frustrated, especially when someone gives me orders, and I really want to get rid of this behavior but I can't. What should I do?

Answer፦ May the grace and blessings of our Lord Jesus Christ be upon you as you seek guidance in overcoming anger and frustration. Your desire to eliminate these behaviors is commendable. 

To address the issue of anger, we must first understand its root causes. Often, anger stems from our own self-will, our resistance to submitting to the will of others. You mentioned that you experience anger when someone gives you orders. This suggests a lack of appreciation for the value and benefits of obedience.

In the lives of the holy monks, we find exemplary humility and obedience. These spiritual fathers would rise in the night to chop wood and fetch water without anyone commanding them to do so. They understood that in obeying, they could transcend their own desires and draw closer to God. Through obedience, sin can be avoided, for even Christ Himself came to serve and obey for our sake.

Reflect upon the commandment given in the book of Exodus: "Honor your mother and father, obey your mother and father, that your days may be long on earth." The importance of obedience is clearly emphasized here. Our Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of God, exemplified humility and obedience to an extraordinary degree. He washed the feet of His disciples, taking on the role of a servant, teaching them the significance of humility.

Indeed, if our Lord, who is God incarnate, washes our feet, then it is our duty to obey and humble ourselves before Him. When we recognize the immense value of obedience, our frustration and anger dissipate. Christ defeated the devil through humility and obedience, triumphing over disobedience and pride. Therefore, by embracing obedience and humility, we align ourselves with the victory of Christ.

If past negative experiences contribute to your frustration and anger, you can gradually overcome them. Through purposeful reflection, discern the advantages and disadvantages of your behavior. Remember that St. Paul reminds us, "I can do all things through Christ who gives me strength." With faith, nothing is impossible.

Throughout history, countless believers who made God their strength, shield, and refuge have witnessed the limitless possibilities granted by God. Our ancestors traveled on the seas, Abune Argawi rode on a dragon, Abune Samuel was carried by a lion, and the Prophet Elijah ascended to the heavens. These remarkable occurrences demonstrate that with God, all things are possible for those who truly believe.

In your pursuit of overcoming anger and frustration, seek the intercession of the saints who have mastered these virtues. Invoke the prayers of St. John Chrysostom himself, for his teachings and writings provide profound insights into the spiritual life.

Embrace prayer, repentance, and the sacramental life of the Church. Frequent reception of the Holy Eucharist and sincere confession can provide you with the strength and grace needed to transform your heart and conquer anger. Cultivate virtues such as patience, kindness, and self-control, which counteract anger and foster inner peace.

Remember, this transformative journey requires perseverance and a steadfast commitment to God's will. Trust in His guidance, for He desires your spiritual growth and will grant you the strength to overcome these challenges. Surrender your anger and frustrations to Him, and allow His divine love to heal your heart.

May the Holy Spirit grant you wisdom, strength, and peace as you embark on this transformative path. Through obedience, humility, and unwavering faith, you will discover the freedom that comes from conquering anger and embracing the virtues of Christ.

May God bless you all.
