Spiritual advice and view about #anxiety and #depressed spirit

What does the Church say about anxiety and depression treatment?

👉Question:  Hello. What are doctrines of the Church regarding anxiety and depression?  In the medical world, for example, it is mental illness that requires different types of treatment like therapy, medication etc. Does the church support this? Thank you

✍️ Answer: First, before we ask What anxiety or depression is, it is important to know how anxiety happnes, What leads people to anexity, What  will be the end result of it? Because if we know the cause of the problem and the damage, the solution will be easy. 

First when we look closely at man, we see that a man becomes anxious, distressed, despaired, depressed, and sad due to being separated from God, the Creator. For this basic principle, it is enough to remember the first man, Adam. Adam is a creature of God created in the image and example of God, in heaven, he was given everything to live in glory. When he lived in Heaven, a law is made for him and he did what he was told to do and did not do what he was told not to do. He lived without worry for seven years, enjoying peace, love, and help, giving thanks, and singing with the holy angels. By saying, God is my father, heaven is my inheritance, paradise is my inheritance and my place. All these blessings came to him because he abided by the law that was set forth for him. 

Once he transpassed God's law, he lost a Lord like God, a place like heaven. After that, he became an abode of anxiety, he became a house of anxiety and he became worried, gripped, self-reprimanded, lost confidence, swallowed by sadness, fell into sadness.

Sin means to lose, to be deprived, to be away, to be lost, to be separated. When we talk about loss, we mean peace, help, sleeplessness, lack of dignity, which conflicts with oneself, discourages, and creates anxiety. Committing sin makes you lose God because God is the owner of everything, the owner of peace, the owner of blessings, the owner of help, the owner of grace. Therefore, when we separate from God, we lose all of these. Sin is transpassinf God's commandments. Anything that is bad is called sin. Therefore, one of the symptoms of a sinful person is anxiety or lack of peace. "There is no peace for the wicked, says my God" (Isaiah 57:20-21). Therefore, when we are in this mood, what we should do is to get up and repent and be reconciled with the Creator. Saint John Chrysostom said, "He who raises himself in repentance is better than he who raises death." It is true, it is greater.

So if we say what is anxiety, it means that it is the result of sin. If we say what sin is, it means losing God. When Adam lost God, he got that kind of anxiety. If we were to say what caused this anxiety, it was his mental problem, his disturbance, his lack of peace. So when we look at what the final solution was, he found peace when he was reconciled with God, and this is possible first of all by sincerely hating sin and returning to God in repentance. This is what the Church teaches. So, it means the only way to cure anxiety and depression is through repentance and living with God. He told us, "You can do nothing without me." John 15:5

As our questioner said above, medical experts also tell us that people who have lost their peace, people who are depressed can be saved with medicine. This may be reassuring for a while. But it does not bring a permanent solution. Man can never be at peace apart from God, so the solution is to live with God in religion. People who have fallen into sin, repenting, fulfilling their canons, receiving His holy body and precious blood, listening to the voice of God regularly, fasting, praying, prostrating, obeying God's commands, doing what God says to do, and not doing what God says not to do, will bring them out of anxiety.
