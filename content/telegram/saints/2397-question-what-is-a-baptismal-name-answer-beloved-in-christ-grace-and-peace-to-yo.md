---Question፦ What is a #baptismal name?

Answer፦ Beloved in Christ, grace and peace to you. A baptismal name, in the of Orthodox tradition, is a name given to one during the sacred rite of Baptism. This is not merely a name, but a spiritual seal, a divine whisper calling one into a life of sanctity and devotion. 

This new name is a testament to one's entrance into a life of repentance and a relationship with God, embodying the virtues and the faith of the saint or biblical figure it represents. If named after a saint, It is a call to emulate their holy lives serving as a constant reminder to live in accordance with God's will. 

It connects the baptized soul to a saint, an angel or directly with God Himself.  This name is a guiding star on the journey towards theosis, the process of becoming united with God.

As Saint John Chrysostom, a luminary of our faith, once said, "He gave us the saints as a means to unite all men to Christ." In receiving a baptismal name, one is thus woven into the celestial fellowship of saints, entrusted to their intercessions and inspired by their virtuous lives. This name is a heavenly gift, a reminder that one is called to rise above earthly desires and to embody the virtues of the saint it represents, striving for holiness and a heart aflame with love for God and neighbor.

In the Orthodox Church, this name is used in all spiritual occasions, serving as a constant call to remember one's baptismal vows and to live a life in Christ, marked by repentance, prayer, and the pursuit of sanctity. It signifies the beginning of a transformative journey, a rebirth into the life of the Spirit, where one is called to “put on Christ” (Galatians 3:27) and to walk in newness of life.

Dear soul, were you baptized in the Orthodox Church? If so, how, then, dear soul, will you seek to embody the virtues of your baptismal name, allowing it to guide you closer to Christ and His Church?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
