(Part 4)

✍Christ, the Savior of the world, will appear to the saints to give them a promise, so that we may know his goodness and forgiveness:
 
To begin with, we must understand that it is for our benefit that He chose the saints from their mother's womb, honored them, approved them and made them live in struggle. God chooses and raises up the saints only because He wants us to be saved. When he raised Lazarus from the dead for four days, it was the 'Megnez' (Unwrapping the cloth he was wrapped in when he died) that caused him to be saved. Our Lord, He who created eyes on a dry forehead, made the blind man see by bathing in the water of Siloam. He also gave us His saints to be the cause of our salvation. It is we who benefit and are saved by the promise made to them, not they. God made them for a reason. Did you know that there are fathers who were given a promise even to the Gentiles? (We will see their covenant in detail later.) The fact that Christ gave a covenant to John the Baptist, to the Blessed seed (Zerea Biruk), to Christ the breath(Estenfase Chiristos), and even to the Gentiles, shows His love for all mankind. Our God is more compassionate and merciful than we think. While we should appreciate his forgiveness and mercy, we could not break away from the mindset that existed two thousand years before His birth.
 
One thing needs to be thought through. Our Lord said in the Gospel, "He that receiveth a righteous man in the name of a righteous man shall receive a righteous man's reward. And whosoever shall give to drink unto one of these little ones a cup of cold water only in the name of a disciple, verily I say unto you, he shall in no wise lose his reward." Matthew 10:38-42 Let's take this word of our Lord Jesus Christ and think like this: 40 or 50 years of fasting, prayer, and hunger; Think of the righteous people who have been fighting for many years while thinking about those suffering of thirst. Also, think of the martyrs who gave their lives to fire and sword for the name's sake, and let us come to the Holy Word. Our Lord said, "He who accepts the righteous in the name of the righteous will receive the price of the righteous." Note that this means that when our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ said, if a person gives only a cold cup of water to the little ones in the name of the righteous person, he will receive the full value of the righteous person. He didn't say will give half, or 5%, 10% , or even 40%. What He said is, "I will give him the full price of the righteous." It doesn't matter, don't sit locked up for 40 or 50 years like the righteous, and even if you don't surrender your body to the fire and the sword  like the martyrs, but help the little ones  giving "only a cold cup of water" in the name of the righteous, and take the their full value. That's what He is telling us. This only shows the mercy, forgiveness and kindness of our Lord Jesus Christ. Understanding and knowing this and not believing that the Holy Word is applicable is a failure and unluckiness.
 
When the age of the saints comes to their rest, our Lord will appear in glory and give them a promise. And their promise is for us, not them. Therefore, God has revealed his love, mercy, forgiveness, and goodness to us and to the nations as well, by appearing to the saints and giving a promise of mercy. In the beginning, he chose the saints from their mother's womb and gave them to us to be the means of salvation. We will never ask Christ why did you do this! Can anyone ask? 'After you created an eye for him on a dry forehead, why did you order him to go and bathe in the Solomon River so that he could see?' ; Or is there is someone who can say, "Why did you order Lazarus to be untied after you raised him from the dead, why did you order them to untie the cloth (Megnez)?" and ; Will there be anyone who says 'Why do you save making saints the reason'

God has made His covenant with our forefathers like Abraham in terms of conduct and religion. 'I have made my covenant with my chosen one; And I will not profane my promise, nor change what comes out of my lips," says the Holy Word.

(Ps 88:3, 34): In the latter days, Christ Himself, the Savior of the world, said, "I am with you always until the end of the age (Matthew 28:20); He who receives you receives me" (Mathew 10:40), how wonderful is this promise that he gave to the saints? Remember that he said "heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not" (Matthew 24:35); "“Do not think that I came to destroy the Law or the Prophets. I did not come to destroy but to fulfill. For assuredly, I say to you, till heaven and earth pass away, one jot or one tittle will by no means pass from the law until all is fulfilled." (Matthew 5:17); "It is easier for heaven and earth to pass, than one tittle of the law to fail." (Luke 16:17) Are we to say to the true God who said this, You are not faithful to your word?
