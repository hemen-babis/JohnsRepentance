---How do I prepare to confess sin?

(Part 3)

(Continued) 👆

Therefore, to briefly summarize the main tasks expected of a Christian who wants to repent:
 
1. First of all, he admits that he has committed a sin and repents to his conscience  and hates the sin.
 
2. Repenting about the sin or wrongdoing committed, confessing the wrongdoing to the Creator, God, in the morning and night with prayers and supplications and confessing the sin before God (Ps 50, Dan 9:5, Neh 1:6-7).

3. By forgiving all those who have wronged us, we break down the wall of enmity and win with love and live in peace with a perfect conscience for the sake of eternal life.
 
4. To talk about the wrongdoings and sins that we have done without hiding anything, to keep it in our mind in detail, and if we think that we will forget, to be prepared with a note.
 
5. Confession of sins to the priest (repentance). Since the priest is God's servant and is appointed over us for the sake of our souls, repenting by confessing our sins to the priest; It means repenting and confessing before God so that our sins can be forgiven. (Joshua 7:19 Matthew 3:6 Leviticus 5:5-6 Acts 19:18)
 
After we have repented, we must strive to continue our penitent life in the right way of righteousness:
 
- Doing righteous deeds: When the scholars of the church teach, they tell us that God does not like us to sit with our legs stretched out and say that it is always from Him to do it. He does not allow us to say that he gives us value without work. But first we should do our part and ask him to do his part for us. Therefore, it is very important to believe that our own decision has a very significant role in the security of the world and to implement it, which includes worshiping God, fasting, prayer, prostration, almsgiving and tithing. Absence of righteousness is the source of sin against us when our inner relationship with God begins to break down. At this time, sin easily rules over us. Therefore, if we know the root causes of our trials in advance, we should avoid them and protect ourselves and walk in a righteous way.
 
- Hating sin not only with knowledge, but also with will and emotion: Many people hate sin; we don't want to do it. But when our will exceeds our knowledge, we commit it again and again while knowing that it is the sin we hate. Therefore, we should hate sin not only with knowledge or fear, but also with will and emotion. Otherwise, if we hate it only because we know it is evil or because we believe in the judgment it will bring upon us, we will be hating it and obeying it because our will is with the flesh. Even if we say that we will fight for it, we will lose and fall because our fight is not from our feelings. When we are tempted by a bad habit, if the will (emotion) is not awakened to understand the extent of the sin, it is not possible to leave the sin, because it is not possible to say that I hate the sin. Saint Paul the Apostle said, "What I hate, I will also do" (Romans 7:19). Knowing all that is good, one of the problems in which we fall into sin due to the carnal will driven by passion is not applying knowledge and not aligning our knowledge with our feelings. In general, when we say that we hate sin, the hatred must be sincere and complete, that is, with will (emotion), knowledge, and with all our strength. This is possible by fulfilling God's commandment against sin word by word, "Love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your strength" (Exodus 6:3).
