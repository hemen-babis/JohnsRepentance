---The Annual Feast of Saint #Joseph the Elder: A Reflection on His Life and Legacy

Dear faithful adherents of the teachings preserved through the ages, let us gather in the spirit of humility and reverence to reflect upon the venerable Saint Joseph the Elder, whose life offers us profound lessons in piety and devotion. We mark his annual feast with a solemnity that befits his holy standing, honoring his memory on Hamle 26 (August 1, 2024), a day sanctified by the angels themselves.

 The Earthly Origins of a Heavenly Saint

Saint Joseph, a man of humble beginnings, was born in the historically significant city of Bethlehem in Galilee, Nazareth. Prophets spoke of Bethlehem as a city abundant with water sources, nestled among mountains and thriving with commerce, cultivated by the wise teachings of Jewish scholars. From an early age, Joseph was immersed in the sacred texts of the Torah, laying the foundational stones of his righteous path.

 Family Life and Bereavement

As he matured, following the righteous statutes of the Torah, Joseph embraced the covenant of marriage. From this sacred union came forth his children: Jacob, Simeon, Yosa, Judah, and three daughters. Tragedy struck when his beloved wife passed away at the age of 52, leaving him to shoulder the burdens of parenthood alone. She was laid to rest in the ancestral cemetery in Hebron, closing a chapter of familial bliss.

Divine Providence in Choosing a Guardian

At the venerable age of 92, a pivotal moment came upon Joseph. The Holy Virgin Mary, having been consecrated to the temple and nourished by celestial sustenance, needed a guardian as ordained by divine decree. In a ceremony overseen by the priest Zechariah, all the widowed elders of Israel were called to present their staffs. Among these was Joseph, who journeyed from Nazareth to Jerusalem, his heart filled with faith.

The Miraculous Selection

In the sacred confines of the temple, the priest Zechariah placed the collected staffs and prayed fervently. When the time came to distribute the staffs, a divine sign was revealed: Saint Joseph’s staff alone bloomed miraculously, bearing balsam fruit—a symbol of new life and divine selection. Further confirming his chosen role, a white dove descended upon him, and an inscription appeared, decreeing, "O Joseph, protect Mary." This triple sign confirmed his divine appointment as the guardian of the Virgin Mary.

A Life of Service and Exile

Embracing his divine mandate, Saint Joseph, alongside the Holy Virgin and Saint Salome, undertook the arduous journey to Egypt, seeking refuge from persecution. This period of exile marked a profound chapter of faith and providence, symbolizing the protective grace extended over the Holy Family. Upon their return, Joseph devoted himself to the earthly care of Jesus Christ, nurturing the Savior with a father’s love and a guardian’s diligence.

The Earthly Departure of a Righteous Soul

Saint Joseph’s earthly journey concluded at the age of 114, after he had witnessed the growth of Jesus into manhood and the inception of His divine mission. His death was not an end but a passage to eternal communion with the divine, celebrated by the heavenly hosts and marked on earth by the annual feast on August 1, 2024.

Commemorations and Celebrations

In honoring Saint Joseph, we observe two significant festivals: June 1, 2024 commemorating his arrival in Egypt as a refugee—a testament to his unwavering faith and resilience—and August 1, 2024 the day of his heavenly rest. በዚህ These days are not merely dates on a calendar but are profound opportunities for reflection on the virtues of faith, obedience, and guardianship that his life exemplifies.

May we draw inspiration from the life of Saint Joseph the Elder, embracing our roles as guardians of our faith and protectors of our spiritual heritage. May his blessings be upon us, guiding us toward righteousness and divine favor. Amen.
