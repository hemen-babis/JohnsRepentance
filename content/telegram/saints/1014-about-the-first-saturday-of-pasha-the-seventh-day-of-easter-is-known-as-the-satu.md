About the first #Saturday of #Pasha 

The seventh day of Easter is known as the Saturday of Holy #Women.

On this day, the thirty-six holy women from one hundred and twenty families are remembered, who served our Lord with their money and energy during his time in earth. They marched to the tomb in the dark with perfumes and flowers, and this day is dedicated as a memorial to the holy women who were alongside Christ until the last moment of his lives and even after. (Matthew 28:1-18, Luke 23:27, Matthew 25:1-11).

Our Savior, Jesus Christ, also gave honor to women in his sacrifice, and many women followed him to serve him with their money and energy (Luke 8:2-3).

Even though the first women, Eve, was the reason for our expulsion from Paradise, our Lady, the Holy Virgin Mary, brought us back to our inheritance. Respecting women is not a choice but a duty, for we have gained what we lost because of women.

In the Old Testament era, women did not have the right to inheritance, and were not counted in geneology. This was because of Eve, who was considered to be the reason why we lost our heavenly inheritance, a place as glorious as Paradise. Thus, it was said that women did not deserve an inheritance. But because of our Lady, the heavenly inheritance has been restored for us and we got even more, so it is not a choice but a duty to respect women.

If the gates of heaven were closed to us because of the first women, Eve, the gates of heaven have been opened to us again because of another Eve, the Virgin Mary. In his praise, St. Ephraim said, "The gate of heaven was closed because of Eve, but it was opened again by the Virgin Mary, and this is why it was revealed that we were born to eat from the fruit of life." Respecting women is not a choice but a duty.

Our Lord chose a women who had five husbands to testify to the people of Samaria that he had sent the holy apostles to be his witnesses (John 4:18).

In the feast of Paracletus, thirty-six women from the one hundred and twenty families were women. The fact that the story of Roman women who were praised for their good work was revealed in 2 John 1 indicates the high role and honor of women in Christianity.

Moreover, to show the compassion of women, the Trinity is called the Holy Trinity in the female article. (In Amahric and Ge'ez) This means that just as a woman should not be doubted by the child she has given birth to, neither should the Trinity be doubted by their creation. Just as a woman never forgets her child, neither will the Trinity forget us. Just like a women is compassionate and caring towards her children, the Trinity is also  compassionate and caring towards their creatures.

We respect women, we elevate them because we know the role they have in society. We acknowledge that they gave birth to martyrs, virgins, monks, patriarchs, bishops, archbishops, priests, deacons, kings, and monks.

For this reason, the Holy Church commemorates the holy women who spent their lives as martyrs and saints in different eras by giving them the title of sainthood equal to men, carving tablets, preparing essays expressing their gratitude, and celebrating their feasts according to the canon.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
