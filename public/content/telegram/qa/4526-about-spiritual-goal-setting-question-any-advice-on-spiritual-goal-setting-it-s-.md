About Spiritual #Goal Setting

Question: Any advice on Spiritual goal setting? It's easy to fall into decision paralysis in figuring out where to start. There's many facets from spiritual, intellectual, physical, etc that one can choose from

Answer: Peace be upon you, my child. It brings me joy to hear your desire for spiritual growth. It is indeed true that the path to salvation has many facets, like a beautifully cut diamond reflecting the infinite light of God. Yet, like a traveler overwhelmed by many paths, one can be paralyzed by choice. 

Remember the words of our Lord, “Seek ye first the Kingdom of God, and His righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.” (Matthew 6:33).  Let this be your guiding star. 

Let us then simplify, for our Lord is a God of simplicity and order. 

Start with the Foundational Stones:

1. Prayer: As the deer pants for the water brooks, so pants my soul for You, O God (Psalm 42:1).  Begin with small, heartfelt prayers throughout your day. Morning and evening prayers, even if just a few minutes, can set the tone for seeking His presence. The Jesus Prayer ("Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner") is a powerful tool for constant remembrance of God.

2. Fasting:  Fasting is not merely abstaining from food but from passions as well. Start with the fasting days prescribed by the Church. This physical discipline strengthens the spirit.

3. Study of Scripture:  Just as you nourish your body with food, so too must you nourish your soul. Begin with a Gospel, perhaps Matthew, and reflect upon a small passage each day.  

4. Almsgiving:  "Give, and it will be given to you." (Luke 6:38).  Offer what you can, be it time, talent, or treasure, to those in need.  Even a small act of charity, done with a loving heart, is pleasing to God.

Remember these key principles:

Consistency: Like a gently flowing stream, consistency, even in small acts, carves a deep riverbed of faith.

Guidance:  Seek the counsel of your spiritual father. He can offer personalized direction based on your unique path.

Patience:  Spiritual growth is a lifelong journey. Be patient with yourself, for God is patient with us.

Do not be overwhelmed, my child. Take one step at a time, with God as your guide, and He will illuminate the path before you. 

May the Lord bless you and guide you in your spiritual endeavors.  

If you wish to delve deeper into any of these practices or have further questions, do not hesitate to ask. I am here to assist you on your journey.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
