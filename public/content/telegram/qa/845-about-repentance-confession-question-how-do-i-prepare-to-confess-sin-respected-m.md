About #repentance #confession 

👉🏾 Question: How do I prepare to confess sin? 
 
Respected members who follow the Orthodox education and spiritual services on John's Repentance page, today we send an important lesson on repentance so we urge you to read and learn from it.
 
✍Answer: First of all, it is a pleasure to hear that you are preparing for repentance. This is an act of loyalty expected from a true Christian and is more sacred than anything else, so we entrust you to repent by standing firm in your aim until the end, and we declare that we are by your side with the spiritual service that we can help you in all your ways. Please note that we confirm our word by starting to send you important teaching and advice based on your questions, which explains the meaning and process of repentance below.
 
Because God is a God of love, he always waits for us to return with open arms and call him my children. When the prophet Ezekiel said that God does not like the death of a person and he likes the return of a person to repentance, ''Repent, and turn yourselves from all your transgressions; so iniquity shall not be your ruin. Cast away from you all your transgressions, whereby you have transgressed; and make you a new heart and a new spirit: for why will ye die,...For I have no pleasure in the death of him that dieth, saith the Lord GOD: wherefore turn yourselves, and live ye." (Ezekiel 18 30-32).
 
Before we come to the question that our dear member asked about how to repent, let us ask 'what is sin?' What is repentance?' Since we have to understand clearly, it is appropriate to put their meaning first as follows.
 
- Sin :- Sin is the loss of meaning, and its secret meaning is rebellion, wrongdoing, humiliation, falling, losing and stealing what we had, lack of honor, doing evil, breaking the law, falling away from the glory of heaven, doubting, submitting oneself to the work of destruction, collaborating with death, etc. Sin can make us think of all these details of failure.
 
- Repentance :- Repentance is the meaning and explanation of the word, sorrow, regret, anger, punishment, acceptance of the canon, paying compensation for the sin committed, believing in the wrongdoing and destruction. In addition, it is a final decision to not commit sins that would otherwise disobey the Creator and deprive him of heavenly eternal life.
 
The following are the important emotional and practical processes that a person who is ready to repent should acquire before repenting, i.e. the spiritual education or understanding that is called pre-repentance.
 
Repentance preparation procedures that help us understand repentance in the right way
 
- Repentance with real feelings: Before repenting, we must admit our own sinfulness and feel remorse.
This type of regret follows failure, embarrassment, shame, loss, mistakes. A person who knows his sinfulness, thinks about breaking the law, wronging God, other people, and his own soul, condemns himself with anger and sorrow, and regrets, and hates the sin he has committed. The burning of such a feeling in us is one of the signs that we are not sleeping comfortably in the bed of sin (it indicates that there is a sign of hope or regret left in us to repent). As the prophet advised us, "If you repent and cry, you will be saved" (Isaiah 30:15). Crying for the sins you have committed is a great and bright way to be saved and to find forgiveness for your sins.
 
- Hating sin: One of the behaviors that help us easily hate sin is fearing God. The wise Solomon said, "He who fears God hates sin; "He hates envy and pride, and hates evil ways" (Proverbs 8:13). He told us that if we do not hate the sin that we are often tempted by, it means that we have appointed the work of sin as a queen over ourselves.
