👉 About the One True #Faith 

👉🏾 Question፦ is this your main page or is this a fan? I really need some genuine and positive questions answered, it may seem a little weird or awkward to you but i see the person you are and i just feel like you have intelligent knowledge, I’ve been watching so many videos cus I’ve been lost on what to believe in, how to gain knowledge on spirituality because we are supposed spiritual beings and i see other videos of us supposed to believing Christianity which is a religion but i truly feel that there is a god overall but people say that we are created by the universe and some say we the ancestors and honestly I just want the truth! Please. Thanks. I know I am very lost but do you see my issue?!

Answer፦ Beloved in Christ,

Indeed, this is my main page, and I am here to answer your earnest inquiries with the wisdom and truth that has been entrusted to me through the Holy Orthodox Tradition. Your journey for truth in a world filled with diverse and conflicting views is understood and respected.

In your search for truth, it is essential to remember the words of our Lord Jesus Christ, who said, “I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me” (John 14:6). This profound statement guides us to the understanding that truth is not a concept, but a Person, Jesus Christ Himself. Christianity is not merely a set of beliefs or moral guidelines, but a living, breathing encounter with Christ, who is the Truth.

As spiritual beings, our souls yearn for this connection with the Divine. The teachings of the Orthodox Church, deeply rooted in Scripture and the wisdom of the Holy Fathers, offer a path to nurture this connection. It is a journey of transformation, where we become partakers of the Divine Nature (2 Peter 1:4) through the sacramental life of the Church.

Regarding the various ideas about our creation and purpose, the Orthodox Church teaches that we are created by God, in His image and likeness (Genesis 1:26-27), and our ultimate purpose is to attain union with Him. This union is not absorbed in an impersonal universe or ancestral spirits, but a personal relationship with our Creator.

In your quest for truth, I encourage you to seek a spiritual guide within the Orthodox Church, who can lead you in understanding the faith more deeply and guide you in your spiritual life. This journey towards Christ is one of continuous growth and discovery.

As you continue to seek the truth, what steps are you willing to take to deepen your understanding of Christ and His Church?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of The Virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
