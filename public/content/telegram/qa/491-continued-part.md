On the other hand, if we don't get rid of sin while it is still in thought, it will deprive us of the spiritual graces given to mankind, that is knowledge, holiness, humanity, fear of God, and goodness. Therefore, we should be firm in our religion and conduct so that we are not trained in the thought of sin, and above all, keep humility and love, live steadfastly in fasting, prayer, prostration, and not giving up hope. In general, it is possible to live like a righteous person if we live by doing God's law and resisting the devil, that is, if we remove grudge, anger, ignorance, envy, and gossip from our hearts.

As the apostle Saint Paul told us, "Lest Satan should get an advantage of us: for we are not ignorant of his devices."
2 Cor 2:11. Therefore, in addition to the ways of preventing the idea of sin that we have seen above, it is appropriate to know the characteristics of the devil in order to know how we can deal with sin from the beginning by distinguishing the ideas of each devil and his tools. Among the evil works that the devil uses to separate mankind from God, the main ones are:

- Infidelity
- Arrogance
- Greedy
- Materialism
- Jealousy
- Ignorance
- Adultery
- Sexuality
- Evil
- Drunkenness
- Murderer
- Idolatry
- Racism
- Discrimination
- Heresy
- Immortality
- Seduction
- Anger
- Strike
- Enmity and dispute
- Singing

God strongly hates these listed above. They are the tares that the devil sows instead of the pure wheat to separate mankind from God. In one way or another, if we know that these are the manifestations of the devil's behavior that tempt human beings with their flesh and soul, then we should avoid and separate from all these acts of destruction. Our hatred and abhorrence of sin is known not only when we stop doing, but also when we stop thinking. So even if these ideas listed above come and fight in your mind, it is possible to multiply spirituality by gradually sowing their opposite virtues instead. For sin and righteousness do not go together; On Ephesians 6:11-19 , St. Paul told us, 'Put on the armor of God, that you may be able to resist the wiles of the devil.' By doing this, we will defeat the devil through a steadfast and unceasing spiritual struggle, and the idea of sin will be replaced by the idea of righteousness. Therefore, we entrust you to understand the answer to the question that you have presented, and implement our advice.
