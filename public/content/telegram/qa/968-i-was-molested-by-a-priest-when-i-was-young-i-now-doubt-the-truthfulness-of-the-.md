---I was molested by a priest when I was young, I now doubt the truthfulness of the #Church, what do you advice me? 

👉Question: Hello, 

I was once molested by a priest when I was around 17 years old. At that time I didn't process it because it was a traumatic event. As an adult now, I look back and I have a hard time trusting people in church. I know that there is a difference between the Holy church and the people within.I know that God and his church is true and perfect and that the people that attend the church are imperfect and sinners. However, it's sad that the place that people go to for peace can have people that can be evil in it. I know that the devil is also at work here but If we can't be safe in church, where else are we supposed to be safe? 

I also sometimes wonder how people in church, especially a church leader like a priest, do such a thing. One of the doubts that I have and my question is : Is church a place that's used to manipulate people for their own benefits? Does God even exist or is the church a system that is set up for using people? I know that this is just a doubt and that God is Holy and perfect and that it's an issue of the people in the church but I do wonder about this sometimes and it creates a doubt.  

Thank you, 

Answer: The word Church has three meanings. The first is a building, which is a church, where the word of God is preached, Christ's holy body and precious blood is served, where believes participate in the different mysteries of Christianity and where they perform their worship. This is called the Church.

The second is the union of believers, the congregation, which is also called the church. This becomes very clear in Matthew chapter 18, verses 15-20, where it says: "Moreover if your brother sins against you, go and tell him his fault between you and him alone. If he hears you, you have gained your brother. But if he will not hear, take with you one or two more, that ‘by the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be established.’ And if he refuses to hear them, tell it to the church. But if he refuses even to hear the church, let him be to you like a heathen and a tax collector." When it says "to the church," it does not mean the building or the stone, but the assembly, the believers, the union who gather in the church and praise God. So, the union of believers is called the congregation church. This union includes holy angels, holy people, and Christ as the head of the church. Those who are alive on earth, those who are in heaven, those who are in the hidden world with God, and where the Virgin Mary is, are all part of the Church.

From the two definitions above, it is incorrect to say that the Church is wrong; an individual who is a member of the Church may or may not be wrong. When we say the Church, we mean the one founded by God, including those who have finished their struggle, those who have not finished, the monastic fathers who are hidden, Saint Michael, Saint Gabriel, and the Virgin Mary. The Church is a united union, and while a deacon can make a mistake, we need to recognize this.

According to Orthodox teaching, the Church does not teach that even a bishop cannot make mistakes. Until he finishes his struggle, the bishop, monk, and priest may or may not make mistakes, but the Church never makes mistakes. The individual and the Church are not the same. They are separate. Doubt arises when we link the individual and the Church together. If we all think that way, we are mistaken.

Thirdly, Every believer who is baptized and receives Christ's holy body, which is the dwelling place of Christ, is called a church. As 1 Corinthians chapter 3 verse 16 states: "Do you not know that you are the temple of God and that the Spirit of God dwells in you?" However, every believer, every individual, or Orthodox believer can be God's abode without separating from the community. If he is separated from the community, he cannot be God's abode and hence can't be called a church.
