👉 Question – If a person who has committed a sin dies suddenly without repenting, can he enter heaven or not?

Answer – "I have no pleasure in the death of the wicked, but that the wicked turn from his way and live" says God (Ez 33:11). Suppose a person has lived a righteous and Christian life by continuously repenting, for example. In that case, even if they die with a sin they forgot to confess or repent for, they can still receive the prayer of absolution at the time of their burial, which enables their souls to be with God. But for those that have lived a sinful life, a life without repentance, they should not receive the prayer of absolution upon their death. That said even after our death, Saints and Angels pray for us so we can enter the kingdom of God. This is one of the reasons why it is crucial to pray for the intercession of Angeles and Saints, especially to the Virgin Mary, as she is the mother of God.

Advise፦ it is important to understand that our life here on earth is very short. We do not know when our time will come. Which is why it being essential to always be prepared by repenting every time we sin. God is givining us one extra day, multiple times, as a chance for us to return to Him. We need to use our time wisely by worshiping God, abiding by His commandments, lerning His words, having a pure heart helping to poor and so on.
