Conclusive Answer:

✍️ In response to your specific question, the kebero (drum) was absolutely not introduced into worship in the 16th century. Its use is ancient, foundational, and continuous within the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church, originating in the Old Testament and deeply embedded in our liturgical life and theological understanding, symbolizing the very Person and Passion of Jesus Christ.
