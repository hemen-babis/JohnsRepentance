---About the #missing_years of Christ 

Question፦ I came across a text that claims to be a scroll of the missing 18 years of Jesus life when he traveled to teach in India, Africa, and Europe. 
Does any of the 81 books of the Ethiopian Bible confirms this?

Answer፦ Dearest seeker of the truth,

Blessings to you on your spiritual journey. The quest for understanding the life of our Lord Jesus Christ is a noble endeavor, for it brings us closer to His divine teachings.

In the realm of Orthodoxy, both Eastern and Oriental, the canonized Scriptures do not speak of these "missing 18 years", which is from age 12 to 30, in such a manner. The Ethiopian Bible, consisting of 81 books, does not contain explicit accounts of Christ traveling to India, Africa, or Europe during these years. We hold dear to the teachings and traditions that have been passed down to us, believing in the providence and guidance of the Holy Spirit in preserving the essential truths of Christ's life.

St. John of Damascus once said, "The whole earth is a living icon of the face of God." Instead of being drawn to extracanonical tales, may I suggest diving deeply into the rich tradition and teachings of the Orthodox Church, where the living presence of Christ and the wisdom of the Holy Fathers will guide you in understanding His true nature and mission?

Now, my dear child in Christ, what step will you take next to delve deeper into the mysteries of our faith?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
