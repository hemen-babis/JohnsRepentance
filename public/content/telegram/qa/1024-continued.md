(Continued) 👆

Only a few are granted the grace to see truth, those who seek with a sincere heart and have faith. 

Thinking that we will find a better truth, we create our own man-made beliefs, which are unnecessary and burdensome. We see the world through habit, as if it has no shepherd, leaving us adrift like a sip without an owner. 

Our questioner may have much understanding and reading in religion and talked to many people in the past. This is good. We do not pressure people to doubt themselves by asking why they said something. What matters is that we hold onto truth and believe in it as the foundation of our faith. Even if liars try to cover up the truth, nothing will stop it from being true.

In search of truth, we do not list what is said in the Bible to answer the question but Instead, we want to understand our questioner's thoughts and have a conversation in the future, whether by phone or in writing. God is our helper and owner of our ideas, and we wish to explain the reality we sincerely believe. But ultimately, it is up to one's conscience to understand the truth.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
