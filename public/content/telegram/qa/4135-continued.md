The Curse of Betrayal and the Root of Sin

Our Lord, in His righteous sorrow, cried out, “Woe to that man by whom the Son of Man is betrayed!” (Matthew 26:24). It is not only Judas who is cursed, but all who walk in his path. And the root of his destruction? Money.

It is written: “The love of money is the root of all evil” (1 Timothy 6:10). Not money itself, but the craving, the lust for it. This desire weakens Christians, blinds them, and draws them from the grace of God into pits of ruin.

Let every believer inscribe upon their hearts this question: “What does it profit a man to gain the whole world, and forfeit his soul?” (Mark 8:36). The thirty pieces of silver gained Judas nothing but despair, and the world that tempts you today may soon testify against you.

The Modern Judas

Take heed, O children of the Church, that betrayal is not limited to that ancient night. The spirit of Judas lives on in all who wear the name of Christ while denying His truth. They:

Use the cloak of religion to manipulate and enrich themselves.

Take offerings meant for the poor and squander them on luxuries.

Turn away from the cries of the needy to pursue their own desires.

Claim faith with their mouths but trade the Gospel for fleeting gain.

These are Judases in our midst. Let them repent while time yet remains!

The Call to True Christianity

True Christianity is a fragrance to God. It does not weep for gold but for the sins of the world. It does not betray but endures. It does not steal from the poor but weeps with them. Let us, then, choose the way of the woman with the perfume, not the way of the betrayer.

Let our tears not be those of guilt too late, but of repentance now.

Let our hands not reach for silver, but for the hem of His garment.

Let our hearts not sell the Lord, but enthrone Him.

A Final Prayer

May the Lord of mercy, who endured betrayal and did not call down angels to destroy His enemies, but submitted in love, grant us the grace to flee the love of money, the poison of greed, and the counsel of the wicked.

May we, with clean hearts and tearful prayers, become heirs of the Kingdom not sellers of the King.


May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
