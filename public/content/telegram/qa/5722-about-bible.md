👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾#About #bible

Question :-what is Bible 

Answer :- My Child, You ask a question as deep as the sea. To hold the Holy Bible in your hands is to hold a universe of meaning. Many in this world see it as a mere book of history, a collection of ancient laws, or a source of moral stories. But for us, the children of the Holy Church, it is something infinitely more.

Let us sit together in the stillness of our hearts and understand what this sacred treasure truly is.

It is a Love Letter from God.

Imagine you are in a distant land, far from your father's house. You are lonely, confused, and have forgotten the way home. Then, a messenger arrives, bearing a letter. It is from your Father. In it, He tells you of His boundless love for you. He reminds you who you are, where you came from, and how to find your way back to His embrace. He warns you of the dangers on the road and promises to be with you always.

This, my child, is the Holy Bible. It is God the Father's letter to His exiled children, written not with ink, but with the very breath of the Holy Spirit through the hands of His chosen prophets and apostles. As the Apostle Paul teaches, "All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness" (2 Timothy 3:16).

It is the Testimony of the Living Word.

The Holy Bible is the divinely inspired collection of writings that reveals God’s work in creation, His covenant with His people, His laws, His prophets, and ultimately His salvation given through our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.

The Bible is not a book about many things; it is a book about One Person: our Lord, God, and Savior Jesus Christ. He is the Living Word of God, and the Bible is the written Word that points to Him on every page.

In the Old Testament, He is promised, prophesied, and foreshadowed. Adam is a type of Christ who was to come. The sacrifice of Isaac points to His sacrifice. The Ark of Noah is a symbol of the salvation in His Church. The prophets spoke of His coming, His suffering, and His glory. Our Lord Himself said to those who studied the scriptures without seeing Him: "You search the scriptures, for in them ye think ye have eternal life: and these are they which testify of me" (John 5:39).

In the New Testament, He is revealed. The Gospels are the icon of His life on earth. The Epistles are the explanation of His life within us. The Revelation of St. John is the vision of His ultimate victory.

It is a Pharmacy for the Soul.

The Holy Fathers, like St. John Chrysostom, do not call the Bible a library, but a spiritual pharmacy. Are you sick with the fever of anger? There is a psalm to cool you. Are you wounded by the arrow of despair? There is a Gospel passage to be a healing balm. Are you weak from the temptations of the flesh? There is an apostolic word to give you strength.

It is food for the hungry, water for the thirsty, and a lamp for those walking in darkness. As the Prophet-King David sings, "Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path" (Psalm 118:105, LXX).
