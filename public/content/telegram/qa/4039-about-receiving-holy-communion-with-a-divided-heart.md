👉🏽About #Receiving #Holy #Communion with a #Divided #Heart

Question: I was wondering if someone has a divided heart, should they hold back on taking Holy Communion? Thank you!

Answer:

My beloved child in Christ,

Your concern is a sign of a sincere and reverent heart, which is the first step toward spiritual growth. The Holy Eucharist is the true Body and Blood of our Lord Jesus Christ, given for the forgiveness of sins and eternal life (John 6:53-56). However, as with all holy things, we must approach it with deep preparation, reverence, and a heart that is fully turned toward God.

A divided heart—a heart struggling with sin, doubt, or inner conflict—can be an obstacle to receiving Holy Communion worthily. St. Paul warns in 1 Corinthians 11:27-29 that whoever eats and drinks unworthily brings judgment upon themselves. Therefore, it is essential to examine your conscience before approaching the Holy Mysteries.

Our Lord teaches us in Matthew 5:23-24 that if we have unresolved conflicts or a lack of peace, we must first seek reconciliation before offering our gift at the altar. This means making amends with others, confessing our sins, and striving to have a heart at peace with God and our neighbor.

My advice to you as a spiritual father:

1. Examine your heart honestly. Ask yourself: What is dividing my heart? Is it doubt, sin, unforgiveness, or worldly distractions? Identifying the root cause will help you seek the right spiritual remedy.

2. Repent and confess. If your divided heart is due to sin, approach your father of confession with humility. True repentance and absolution will heal your heart and prepare you for Holy Communion.

3. Strengthen your faith through prayer. If doubt is troubling you, spend time in prayer, asking God to strengthen your faith. Read Scripture, attend church services, and seek spiritual counsel.

4. Make peace with others. If your heart is divided due to anger, grudges, or conflicts, seek reconciliation. Forgiveness is essential for receiving the grace of God fully.

5. Prepare properly. Fasting, prayer, and spiritual reflection help align our hearts with Christ before receiving the Holy Eucharist. Approach with love, reverence, and a desire to be united with God.

6. Do not fear God's mercy. If your struggle is leading you to despair, remember that God is merciful. He desires that you come to Him with a contrite heart. Do not let guilt or hesitation keep you from returning to Him.

If your heart remains troubled, it may be wise to wait and seek further spiritual guidance before receiving Holy Communion. However, never distance yourself from God—draw closer through prayer, confession, and a sincere desire for unity with Him.

May our Lord grant you peace, wisdom, and a heart fully devoted to Him. Always remember that God’s love is greater than our struggles, and He is always ready to heal and restore us.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
