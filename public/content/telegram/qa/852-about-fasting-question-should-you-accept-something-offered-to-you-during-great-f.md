About #fasting 

👉🏾 Question፦ Should you accept something offered to you during great fasting that is not permitted or at a time before you should break your fast? On work or in a family gathering with others who are not fasting?

 What happens if you eat something that was offered that contains meat... without knowing....but prayed and made the sign of the cross before eating...What should you do after finding out?

Answer፦ Whether it is at work or with people who are not fasting, if someone offers something that is not permitted during fasting before the end of the fasting period, one should not accept it. It is important to understand that fasting is mandatory for Orthodox Christians. There is no excuse for not fasting, the only exception would be health conditions or special circumstances like our sisters who live in Arab countries working as mades in a house where there is no option to fast. The Church has seven fasting periods and every Orthodox Christian is obligated to fast during these seven fasts. The start of these fasts is announced in all churches, on social media Orthodox services, and we hear about them from friends and family. Thus, one should fast after hearing all of this.

It is inconvenient to fast for those that don't live in a Christian county. If one lives in a Christian country, they must fast during the seven fasting periods. It is not right to eat anything during fasting. However, if he/she is sick and lives in an Islamic country, such as an Arab country, where food with meat and butter are common, and it is difficult to find clean bread, eating during fasting may be allowed for a specific reason like this. However one must communicate this with their father of pennance before deciding for themselves. 

If one decides to eat diary food during fasting periods, the Church cannot force him to fast but he can fast when he is willing to obey the Church's commandments and fast. For example, when Adam was told not to do something, it was prescribed because it would harm him. He knew not to eat from the tree of knowledge which is why he was able to enjoy 7 years of peace and unity with God in Heaven. Similarly, if a person is told not to eat poison, he knows that it will harm him, so he will not eat it. Therefore, eating what is said not to eat during fasting is like drinking poison. Adam lived peacefully for about seven years without eating what he was told not to eat. But after eating, the God-like Lord lost his place in heaven and became a wandering refugee. This happened because he ate what he was told not to eat and did what he was told not to do. It means that if a person does what he is told not to do, he will get hurt. We should all know this. But if there is something beyond our control, God knows everything, God is merciful and cares for us more than we do. If we can fast and choose not to do so, we will be asked for this, just like Adam was held accountable. When the devil asked him to eat, he could have said that he would not eat, but he willingly ate. 

If someone unknowingly eats something not permitted during fasting, they should tell their repentant father, and it's not something they did knowingly. They should not break or interrupt their fast. They should continue to fast and tell their father of pennance about the problem they faced. Therefore, for repentance, they should approach their father and accept the canon. If we accidentally fail while doing spiritual work, we should be careful not to fail again.
