Question፦ I have a very hard time forgiving people, what do you advice me? #forgiveness

Answer፦ Beloved in Christ,

Your struggle with forgiveness resonates deeply within the teachings of our faith, and it is a common challenge faced by many, both within and outside the folds of Orthodoxy. In the Gospel of Matthew, Jesus teaches us, "For if you forgive other people when they sin against you, your heavenly Father will also forgive you." (Matthew 6:14). This verse is a cornerstone in understanding the necessity of forgiveness in our spiritual lives.

In the journey towards letting go and letting God work in our lives, we must remember that forgiveness is not merely an act of will, but a grace-filled process that transforms our hearts. It is a path walked step by step, often with much prayer and reflection. In the Orthodox tradition, we believe that this transformation is deeply connected to our participation in the life of the Church, through prayer, fasting, and partaking in the Holy Mysteries (Sacraments).

To begin, I would encourage you to seek the counsel of a spiritual father or guide. This person can help you navigate the complexities of forgiveness, offering guidance that is rooted in the rich spiritual heritage of the Orthodox Church. Additionally, regular participation in the Divine Liturgy and the sacrament of confession can be powerful aids in softening the heart and opening it to the grace of forgiveness.

As you embark on this journey, remember that forgiveness is not a sign of weakness, but a profound expression of Christ-like love and humility. It is in forgiving that we are most closely united to Christ, who forgave even those who crucified Him.

In your daily life, I would suggest setting aside time for prayer specifically focused on this struggle. Ask God for the grace to forgive, and reflect on the instances where you have been forgiven. Contemplate also the lives of saints who exemplified forgiveness, such as St. Stephen, the first martyr, who prayed for his persecutors even as they stoned him.

Finally, remember that forgiveness is a journey, not a destination. It is a process that unfolds in the heart, day by day, with each small act of letting go and entrusting the pain to God.

As you continue on this path, what steps will you take today to open your heart to the grace of forgiveness?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of The Virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
