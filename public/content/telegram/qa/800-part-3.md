(Continued) 👆

In the books of Nehemiah and Ezra, musical instruments are mentioned. The Psalms of David, the Book of Kings, and the Book of Chronicles also mention psalms and instruments. Therefore, as our questioner mentioned, there are new musical instruments that were not mentioned in the bible. 

While we Orthodox people sing in church with the grace of God, those in the Protestant world have taken it to a worldly style of music. This means that they may sing for their own physical pleasure, but it is not possible to please God in this way. When we observe their songs, we can see that they are unable to control themselves and are doing something that looks like a dance. Mostly, it is the instrumentation that causes this. It is impossible to say that we are giving thanks to God in such a situation, and even the dress code itself is not appropriate.

Even without going into another mystery, we can understand from what we see and observe that we should not make modern musical instruments a tool for church singing. This is one of the reasons why we say that we should not use musical instruments other than those mentioned in the scriptures. Using this kind of instrument for a young person may not lead them towards their goals and may tempt them. This type of singing, the one that involves jumping and dancing, is not called praise and singing in the name of God, but rather a mockery of God.

However, it is not necessary to say that we should not use any new findings at all. For example, some of the findings we use, such as electricity, which is being used to make the service accessible to us with modern electric tools like speakers and computers. The drum, singer's voice, cymbal, and masinqoun are also amplified. This means that the sound produced by these instruments is more prominent than the sound produced by our own tongue. Since this is done to motivate people for service, we do not say, 'We will not use it at all, or it should not be done, it is a condemnation.'

But if we bring musical instruments that are not used in the service of our church, which we see on the platforms of other faiths and call them spirituality or hymns, we will turn the believer into a worldly person. In praising God, there should be no dancing, no singing with sensuality and joy.

There are songs that we often sing in Mahlet, such as "Mahlete Genbo." It is seen that some young people who are skilled in the craft of Yaredawi Zema are engaging in unwanted things, thinking that they are pleasing God and people. Our forefathers, who knew the benefits and services of singing, opposed and forbid this. When they saw this, they stopped or corrected them. Wherever there are scholars, everything is done in an orderly manner, but jumping and spinning are prohibited.

Our questioner asked a good question. It is good that we discuss and talk about everything. If the questioner is looking for the song tools we have listed above in terms of Bible verses, there are many of them, so we think that the ones we have mentioned above will give them a sufficient answer to their question. We can talk about Bible verses if necessary in the next discussion.
