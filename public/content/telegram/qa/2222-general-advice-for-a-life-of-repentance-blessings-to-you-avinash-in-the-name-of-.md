General advice for a life of #repentance 

Blessings to you, Avinash, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.

Firstly, know that the mercy of God is boundless and His love for us surpasses all our transgressions. As it is written, “If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness” (1 John 1:9). Your desire for repentance is a precious gift from God and the first step towards healing.

In the Orthodox tradition, repentance is not just a feeling of remorse, but a turning away from sin and a turning towards God. The story of the Prodigal Son (Luke 15:11-32) beautifully illustrates this journey. He recognized his sin, felt remorse, and decided to return to his father, confessing his unworthiness. The father, symbolizing God, welcomed him with open arms. This is the kind of loving, forgiving Father we have in heaven.

Your struggle with impurity, especially in this digital age, is a common battle. It is important to remember that our bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit (1 Corinthians 6:19-20), and we are called to honor God with our bodies. This is a struggle of not just the body, but the mind and soul.

As you seek deliverance, regular prayer is essential. Pray for strength, guidance, and the Holy Spirit's presence in your life. Engaging in the reading of Holy Scriptures and the writings of the Church Fathers can provide spiritual nourishment and guidance.

Moreover, finding a spiritual father is vital. In the Orthodox tradition, a spiritual father is a guide and a confessor who can provide personal and spiritual guidance, helping you navigate through your struggles. He can provide you with specific prayers, readings, and practices to help you combat these temptations.

Additionally, engaging in the life of the Church through worship, fasting, and participation in the Holy Sacraments can strengthen your spiritual life. The Sacrament of Confession is particularly important as it is a means of receiving God’s forgiveness and starting anew.

Remember, transformation does not happen overnight. It is a journey that requires patience, perseverance, and faith. God’s grace is sufficient for you, for His power is made perfect in weakness (2 Corinthians 12:9).

In closing, I will indeed pray for your deliverance and for a heart of true repentance. Remember, God’s love is greater than any sin. What will be your first step in this journey towards healing and holiness?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
