---About the #Praise of #Mary

👉 Question፦ What is the significance of the Tefisihee Mariam that the Deacons say in our church? For example: Antee Wi’itu mesobe werq qeyih ante wisteta mena biruh? I’ve said it myself, but I just don’t know what the importantance of it is in our faith?

Answer፦ Beloved in Christ, the query you have brought forth touches the heart of our liturgical tradition, reflecting the depth and beauty of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church. The Tefisihee Mariam, or the Praise of Mary, is a significant hymn chanted by deacons, lauding the Virgin Mary, the Theotokos, for her pivotal role in the Incarnation of our Lord Jesus Christ. This hymn exemplifies our veneration of the Mother of God, not as a deity, but as the chosen vessel through whom God became man for our salvation. It embodies our acknowledgment of her virtues and her unique place in our salvation history.

The verse you're referring to "አንቲ ውእቱ መሶበ ወርቅ ንጹሕ እንተ ውስቴታ መና ኅቡዕ ኅብስት ዘወረደ አም ሰምያት ወሀቤ  ሕይወት ለኩኩ ዓለም," which translates to "Thou art the pot of pure gold wherein is hidden the manna, the bread which came down from heaven giving life unto all the world," beautifully articulates the Orthodox Christian belief in Mary as the Ark of the New Covenant. Just as the pot of manna in the Old Testament was kept in the Ark of the Covenant as a testament to God's provision for the Israelites in the desert, Mary is honored as the one who bore Christ, the true Manna from heaven, who gives eternal life to the world.

This verse draws us into the deep typology found in Scripture, where Mary is seen as fulfilling and surpassing the types and symbols of the Old Testament. The pure gold of the pot signifies her purity and the divine choice, while the hidden manna, Christ Himself, is the sustenance for our souls, the Bread of Life, who came down from heaven to nourish and redeem us.

Saint Cyril of Alexandria, a luminary father of the Church, beautifully expounds on this, saying, "Hail, Mary Theotokos, venerable treasure of the whole world... through whom the Holy Trinity is glorified and adored,... through whom the precious Cross is venerated throughout the world, through whom heaven exults, through whom angels and archangels rejoice, through whom demons are put to flight,... through whom the fallen creature is raised up to heaven, through whom the whole creation, possessed by idolatry, has come to the knowledge of the truth."

Thus, the Tefisihee Mariam is not merely a hymn of praise but a profound theological affirmation of the Incarnation and the central role of the Virgin Mary in our salvation. It invites us to marvel at the mystery of God's love and providence, drawing us closer to Him through veneration of His mother.

This understanding and reverence for the Virgin Mary are not mere traditions but are deeply rooted in the Scripture and the teachings of the Church Fathers. They invite us to ponder the mystery of our salvation and the extraordinary role that Mary plays in it, encouraging us to seek her intercessions and to emulate her faith and obedience to God's will.

As you reflect on this, consider how the understanding of Mary as the vessel containing the Bread of Life enriches your faith and draws you closer to Christ. How might this deepen your appreciation for the divine services and your personal devotion to the Theotokos?
