Question: Where do thoughts of sin come from? Do all our thoughts come from our own mind or from somewhere else? How do we know the difference between inside and outside? How can we stop?

Answer: Sin and death are not created by God, but are events that happened later in the life of mankind. Therefore, God did not create sin in Adam's character. Sin is rather the result of an evil though that is contrary to the good laws cealed in his conscience. A person becomes cunning and sinful not because it is given to him by his nature, but because he loves sin by his own free will. Of course, Satan is also the enemy of mankind, so he sees our small weak side and expands it. Therefore, using our free will, we can obey God's law and live righteously. But if we choose to live in Satan's thoughts, we will bring destruction and death to ourselves and others by our own hands. Intellectual (knowledge) is a spiritual gift and wealth that protects the soul, guides and corrects it, and keeps it away from sin and clings to what is good; By this we can distinguish between good and evil. As humans, we have the ability to save ourselves, and this ability is done voluntarily. God foresaw our weakness and made laws for us to live. After people understand God's law, his love and his judgement, the choice is in their hands, so they can think and go for what they want. If they do evil, they will perish, but if they do good, they will be justified. Since we are given the mind to distinguish between good and bad thoughts, it is possible to distinguish whether it is an internal thought that is a characteristic of the soul or an evil thought that comes from someone else. The law is one of the ways in which the divine protection is manifested, and it is shown that the result of God's protection and blessing is with us when we obey God's commands and exercise our free will. This is the will of the soul, which is called the will of the character, and the will of the soul is to live in righteousness, holiness, thanksgiving, and spirituality. In addition, we have the natural ability to prevent the sin of thought from coming, even if it does come, it will not be considered a sin. Therefore, we can use our free will to avoid evil thoughts.
