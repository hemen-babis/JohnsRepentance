Question: I can't get my husband to go to church and even if he does, he wants to leave early. I have boys, what should I do?

✍️Answer: - Dear member, it would have been helpful if you listed the reasons why your husband does not go to church. A Habit is valuable when used for good. An Orthodox Christian who is accustomed to praying will not eat without praying, as their conscience will not allow it. Similarly, a Christian who is used to attending church will continue to do so, as it sanctifies them. Let us build good habits that will serve our spiritual life. 

The Church is the house of salvation. A believer who is separate from the Church is like a fish out of the sea, which has no chance of survival. An Orthodox believer cannot remain in Christ if separated from the Church. The souls of living believers, those who have completed their struggle and are in heaven such as Saint George and Saint Arsema, and the holy angels, are all united by God in the Church. 

Therefore, when we go to church, we must believe that we are going to the Holy Virgin Mary, Saint Gabriel, Saint Michael, and to God Himself. King David understood this when he said in Psalm 122:1, "I rejoiced with those who said to me, 'Let us go to the house of the Lord.'"

Secondly, the Church is not just a building but the body of believers. A Christian can be the abode of Christ when they are united with the fellowship of believers, without being separated from the Church. As Jesus said in John 15:5, "I am the vine; you are the branches. If you remain in me and I in you, you will bear much fruit; apart from me you can do nothing." Just as a branch cannot bear fruit unless it remains in the vine, a Christian cannot bear fruit unless they remain in Christ.

The third definition of the Church is the gathering of the faithful to praise God together, to listen to the Gospel, and to receive the sacraments. The holy place where these things happen is called the Church. Therefore, attending church is our Orthodox duty.

Just as we cannot live without food or shelter, the Church is our home. If we do not love the Church during our lives, we will be buried in it after death. If your husband understands and believed this, there is no reason why he would resist a church life. 

As for the question of how to raise our children, we can learn from the experience of Moses' mother. The Israelites fled from Egypt, a place where they did not know God and suffered greatly. When Moses was born, his mother placed him in the river, where Pharaoh's daughter found him and raised him. Moses' sister connected him with his mother, who raised him and taught him the right faith.

Moses chose heavenly glory over earthly rewards, and as a result, God spoke to him and used him to save Israel from the Egypt. Therefore, we should follow the example of Moses' mother and teach our children about the divinity of God, reminding them that all good things come from God.

If we raise our children in this way, it is possible to create children who say, "I want to be like Saint Kirk, like Moses, like Elias. We do not worship idols, we do not eat food offered to idols, and we deny the gods of others, for our God is the true God." It is good to teach them the word of God from a very early age. 

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
