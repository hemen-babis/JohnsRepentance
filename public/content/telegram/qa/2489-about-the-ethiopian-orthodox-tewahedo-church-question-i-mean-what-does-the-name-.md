About the #Ethiopian #Orthodox #Tewahedo Church 

Question፦ I mean what does the name Orthodox represent in our faith. Henceforth, why do we call our church the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church?

Answer፦ Beloved in Christ, the term "Orthodox" stems from the Greek words "orthos," meaning correct or true, and "doxa," meaning belief or glory. Thus, "Orthodox" signifies "correct belief" or "true worship." This term encapsulates our commitment to preserving the authentic teachings and traditions as handed down from Christ Himself to His Apostles and through them to the Church.

The use of "Orthodox" began in the early Christian era, particularly as a way to distinguish the Church that adhered faithfully to the teachings defined in the Ecumenical Councils from various heretical groups that had diverged from these established doctrines. It was crucial in times of doctrinal disputes to affirm the identity of the Church that remained faithful to the apostolic and patristic teachings. Hence, the term "Orthodox" became a definitive marker of the true Church that maintained fidelity to the Christian faith as it was universally recognized and proclaimed.

The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church embodies this commitment to orthodoxy, having received the Christian faith in the early Apostolic Age and nurtured it through centuries, steadfast amid various challenges. Our adherence to the term "Orthodox" is a testament to our dedication to uphold the true faith and practices as received from the generations of faithful who preceded us.

The word "Tewahedo" uniquely characterizes our Ethiopian Orthodox faith, signifying the unity of Christ's divine and human natures in one, without division or separation. This profound theological principle is intrinsic to our faith, shaping our worship, our spirituality, and our very identity as followers of Christ in the Ethiopian tradition.

Our Church, bearing the name Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church, thus stands as a beacon of the undiluted Christian faith, embracing the unity of Christ and the apostolic teachings preserved and cherished in our tradition.

As you contemplate the meaning behind our Church's name, I encourage you to delve deeper into the richness of the Tewahedo doctrine. How might this understanding of Christ's nature influence your relationship with Him and guide your steps on the path of salvation?
