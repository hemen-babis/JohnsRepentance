#solution to #adultry #fornication #sin

👉Question: I could not get rid of the sin of adultery, what should I do? (What is the cause and solution?)

✍Answer : Adultery is one of the main sins that God detests, hates and grieves. Adultery is sexual intercourse outside of one's husband or wife. Therefore, it is important to understand that the main function of adultery is to change the law of God with the law of the devil, which means changing our eternal life with eternal death. That is why our enemy, Satan the Devil, uses it as his main weapon to make us fall apart from the Creator and make us commit this type of sin that is most hated in the sight of God. Saitan makes us fall in this sin  by regularly tempting us in all it's different forms, and making everyone cooperate in this sin.

Causes and solutions of adultery

1. Natural emotion and free will

- When we are faced with sinful thoughts and actions that originate from inside or outside for various reasons, we often submit our free will to our temporary feelings and fall into sin. To get rid of this, knowing God, knowing the honor God has given to man, and doing spiritual work will help us to use our free will in a good way that God loves. There is no nobler creature than man; God's prophet David said, "Man was glorious, but he did not know it and became like an animal." When God gave human beings a mind to distinguish between good and evil, He set laws but with free will.

     Psalm 33:14 says, "Flee from evil, and do good." Therefore, if we keep the law and do good, we will be justified, and if we do not, we will be condemned. Therefore, it is important to know God's law and perform spiritual activities, such as fasting, prayer, prostration, reading spiritual books, prostrating, listening to hymns, listening to sermons, attending liturgy, regularly meeting with spiritual father (in person or over the phone) and regularly taking part of the Eucharist. When we strive to do these things, the spirit cannot stay in us, so it burns and moves away from us.
-
2. Battle of evil spirits -

We must always understand that the the evil spirit is able to make us sin because of our weak thoughts, attitudes, inclinations and desires. If Satan finds the small hole of the will, he will lick it like a snake and widen it, and he will lose us from our God. Therefore, even if we ourselves are partial to our own flesh and say that we have been misled to make Satan the reason for all the sins we have boldly committed, we will not escape responsibility. Therefore, it is better to be aware of Satan's methods, tools and ways he uses to tempt us, so that we will  stay away from sin. "Lest Satan should get an advantage of us: for we are not ignorant of his devices". (2 Cor 2:11)

3. Keep yourself away from the sin of sins

When we are tempted by the idea of adultery, the basic reasons are:- 

We engage ourselves in the main causes that are the eve of adultery, such as watching sensational movies, talking about silly conversations, kissing, passionately hugging and rubbing, sleeping together, etc. In general, human beings perform related activities when their free will leads them to the thought of adultery, when they practice the idea secretly and when the spirit of fornication force them, they cannot control themselves. Therefore, we must stop not only doing the sin, but also thinking about it. If we reject the action in a real way, it must also be in thought too. Otherwise, if we entertain the idea in our thoughts; the reason we did not perform the act is considered to  be due to  inconvenience of the circumstances and not because we resisted not to perform it. Therefore, we should repent for this. We should stop sinning not only with action, but also with words and thoughts.
