Question፦ Turn me #Christian

Answer፦ My dear child, becoming a Christian is not something that is done to you, but rather a journey of the heart, a turning towards God. It is a calling, a gift freely offered by our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, and one that we must freely accept.  Think of the parable of the sower in the Gospel of Matthew (13:1-23). The seed of faith must fall on fertile ground and be nurtured to grow.

It is a lifelong process of learning, growing, and deepening your relationship with Christ.  This journey involves repentance, which is a turning away from sin and towards God.  It is not simply regretting our mistakes, but actively choosing a different path, a path illuminated by the light of Christ.

Perhaps you can tell me what draws you to Christianity at this moment? What questions or longings stir within your heart?  Sharing these thoughts can help us explore this path together.

I can offer you resources to learn more about the tenets of the Orthodox Christian faith: the Holy Scriptures, especially the Gospels, which tell the story of Christ’s life, death, and resurrection; the writings of the Church Fathers, like St. John Chrysostom, whose eloquence illuminated the path to God for generations; and the rich traditions of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church, a vibrant tapestry woven with centuries of faith.

We can also discuss the importance of prayer, fasting, and almsgiving – spiritual practices that help us draw closer to God and purify our hearts.

Ultimately, my child, the decision to embrace Christ rests with you.  I can guide you, offer support, and share the wisdom of our faith, but it is through your own free will, guided by the Holy Spirit, that you will find your way.

Remember the words of St. Augustine, "Our hearts are restless until they rest in Thee, O Lord."  Perhaps that restlessness is what brings you here today. Let us explore it together in prayer and reflection.  Would you like to share what is on your heart?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
