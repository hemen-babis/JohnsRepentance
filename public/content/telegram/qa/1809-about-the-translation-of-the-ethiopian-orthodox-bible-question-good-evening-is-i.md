About the #translation of the Ethiopian Orthodox #bible

👉 Question፦ Good evening, is it correct that the first ethiopian bible was translated from greek into ge'ez by the 9 saints who came to Ethiopia around 450 a.d ??

Answer፦ Good morning beloved one,

Indeed, Ethiopia holds a cherished place in the annals of Christian history. The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church, an ancient and proud Christian community, traces its roots back to the time of the Apostles. The tale of the Nine Saints, holy men from different parts of the Byzantine Empire, is a beloved one in our tradition. While these saints are credited with expanding Christianity in the Ethiopian highlands and establishing monastic traditions, the translation of the entire Bible into Ge'ez is a complex process that occurred over centuries.

However, it is important to note that by the time the Nine Saints arrived, Christianity was already present in Ethiopia, having been introduced even earlier through the conversion of the Ethiopian eunuch mentioned in Acts 8:27-39. The Ethiopian Orthodox Church believes its biblical canon, especially the New Testament, was translated into Ge'ez around the 5th century, with the influence of these saints and other faithful.

Remember the words of St. John Chrysostomos: "The Holy Scriptures were not given to us that we should enclose them in books, but that we should engrave them upon our hearts." How might you inscribe these words of wisdom upon your own heart, and seek deeper understanding in your journey with Christ?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
