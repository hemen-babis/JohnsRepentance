The Ten Spiritual Titles (Levels)
 
👉Question: If you could explain to me about the ten spiritual levels, our father?
 
✍Answer: Title means levels of honor, power.  As the apostle Saint Paul said in 1 Cor 15:31, "one star is different from another star in glory", the glory and power of one of them is different from another according to the sacrifices and legacies of the saints who live forever as stars for their good deeds. / Dan 12:3; Rev 12:1-4/. It has been revealed in the books of monks that there are ten ranks that the saints have reached.

Accordingly, we will look through the ten ranks that the saints can achieve through struggle and inheritance. The ten spiritual pillars are the steps or ladders on which a person can grow step by step to reach any of these ten spiritual levels or titles. Ten is the number of fullness. It is a symbol of something whole; and we find this numbering in many stories in the Bible, various books, and   history dictionaries. Spirituality means living in the world of the soul, not worrying about what passes and perishes. 

Therefore, it means that when a person grows in his spirituality, everything else that passes and disappears does not bother him except for the matter of the soul.
A person grows physically, mentally, financially …jn the same way, , there is also spiritual growth which goes gradually. As a baby does not become an adult all at once, it is also not possible to grow spiritually at once or to be perfect soon. Therefore,  gradually practice in a continued manner is important to reach the life of our holy fathers. It it is said "Think of your leaders who have told you the word of God, and look at the fruit of their lives and imitate them in faith. Heb 13:7.

In the parable of the farmer and the seed taught by our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, he said, "And another seed fell on good ground, and one yielded a hundred, and one sixty, and one thirty fruits." /Mathew 13:8/ The seed is God's word, and the good soil is Christians who listen to God's word and bear fruit, religion and food. 1 Cor 3:9 It means that according to their work, they will bear 30, 60, 100/ /thirty, sixty, hundred/ fruits or they will get different honors and powers. The ranks of saints are determined by the extent of their legacy of struggle during their lifetime. In the scriptures, it is repeatedly mentioned that God will reward each person according to his work. / Psalm 51:12; Proverbs 14:12; Matthew 16:17; Romans 2:6; Rev. 10:12/.
 
The Ten Spiritual Ranks (Titles) are as follows.

1. 'Tsmawe' (it means ignorance to worldly matters) 2. 'Lbawe' (it means clear understanding) 3. 'Taame Zmare' (it means testful song) 4. 'Enbue' (tear of repentance) 5. Condemnation 6. Love 7. 'Husset' (it means existing anywhere without moving) 8. 'Seeing Angels 9. Drowned 10. 'Being fire' . They are divided into three parts of levels /honers:-  called "purity of body, purity of soul, purity of heart".
 
 
1 'ጽማዌ' (Ignorance to worldly matters)

'ጽማዌ' (Tsuwame) it means not being able to hear about the world matters due to ignorance or choosing silence The reason these saints are called 'tsum' ('not hearing') is not because their ears fail to hear because of a natural problem, but because they do not want to hear the things of the world. In this sense, wether if a person's ear can hear or not, he will not hear any sound in the world, he will only listen to the thoughts of his heart. Saints who have reached this stage do not want to listen to anything of the world while they are in the world. But with their conscience they think of God, and with their nose they smell the fragrance of paradise. When they reach this rank, they are able to distinguish the worlds that cannot be seen with their eyes, giant beasts, souls separated from their bodies, demons. They will also reach the level of seeing angels who are on mission in this world. But because they never knew the names of the angels who appeared to them, what they did, what they said, and who they were, they were called 'Tsuwame' (not able to hear).
