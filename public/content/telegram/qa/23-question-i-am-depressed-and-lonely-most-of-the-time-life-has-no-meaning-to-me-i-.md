Question: I am #depressed and #lonely most of the time. Life has no meaning to me. I just want to die and live with Jesus. I have been looking for #happiness all my life but I have never found it. I feel like my mind is about to explode. Please pray for me. I need your prayers. What do you #advice me??

Answer: I wish that God's peace stays with you. The only way that a human gets peace and happiness is to believe that Our Lord Jesus Christ, who was born in Bethlehem from our mother Saint Virgin Mary, is the Almighty God. The creator of the visible and invisible in this world. The one the prophets spoke that He came to save us from satan, evil things, and sins. The one who the disciples preached that He was crucified to give us everlasting life by His flesh and death. He is the one who rose after 3 days from the tomb and revealed himself to Madgelon Mariam/Mary and all his disciples at different times. He, the one who ascended to heaven, will come again with graciousness to judge the people (Isaiah 3:13).

What must I do?
1. Believe in Jesus Christ as Almighty God.
2. Believe in Jesus Christ as who judges on people, not who maketh the intersection for us.
3. Believe in Jesus Christ as the world's Holly Savior.
4. Believe Jesus Christ as “The everlasting Father, Prince of Peace” (Isaiah 9:6).
5. Believe that Saint Virgin Mary is our spiritual Mother who is given to us from our Lord Jesus Christ (Psalm 87:5 & John19:27)
6. Believe that saints, prophets, disciples, Archbishops, Priests...etc as they maketh intersections for us, through their prayers.
7. Pray every day to the Lord God (Mat 6:13, 26:41, Daniel 6:16). 
8. Love all human beings without a difference as Jesus Christ loved us all despite our sins, weakness, and flaws.
9. Read the Bible every day (one verse a day or a chapter a day depending on what you can) and spiritual books as they will explain the spiritual life and experiences of Orthodox scholars. Listen to Orthodox Tewahedo Hymns. 
10. Ask advice from church Fathers/ priests, and if you don’t have access to them, reach them through social media, or talk to your brothers and sisters in Christ.
11. Go to the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahido Church every day, if not as much as possible, and participate in spiritual ceremonies, liturgy, Annual celebrations...etc 

Try these and you will see changes in your spiritual and secular life. Always know that the Lord is with you! Always seek Him, and you, our sister, will find Him. Isaiah 41:10 

“Fear not, for I am with you; Be not dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you, Yes, I will help you, I will uphold you with My righteous right hand.”


No money will give us happiness, no Authority will give us happiness, no beauty will give us peace, no fame will give us everlasting Love. No knowledge of sciences nor technology will give us everlasting peace and happiness. Happiness is possible only with the Almighty God Jesus Christ. We will keep you in our prayers and please continue to reach out to us.
