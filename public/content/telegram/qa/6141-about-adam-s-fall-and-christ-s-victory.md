Through one man Adam, all humanity fell, everyone, everywhere, regardless of location, nationality, religion, belief, or morality. Death, suffering, and corruption spread through the devil’s use of one man.

Through one man, Jesus Christ, salvation was accomplished for all humanity, because He assumed human nature, healed it, and restored it.So here is the real question 

If the fall destroyed all humanity through Adam, does Christ’s redemptive power fall short of that destruction if only a few receive it through baptism, knowledge of the Gospel, or being part of a Christian denomination?

✍🏾Answer :- My Child, At the beginning of your question, you said that this question is directed to those who believe that a person must belong to a specific Christian denomination in order to be saved. Therefore, we will answer you according to the teaching of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church.

✍🏾From your question, it is clear that you are a thoughtful reader. This is a good and deep question, not asked lightly. It comes from reflection and concern for the justice and completeness of God’s salvation.

✍🏾It is true that through Adam and Eve, death entered the entire human race. This affected all people, without distinction of religion, race, place, or morality. Because of Adam’s sin, Paradise was closed, and all humanity was destined to descend to Sheol. However, we must understand this correctly. The righteous did not suffer the torment of hell, nor were they separated from God’s care. As the Scripture says, “The souls of the righteous are in the hand of God, and no torment shall touch them” (Wisdom 3:1). Yet, Paradise remained closed until Christ.

👉🏾 Just as Adam’s sin affected everyone, so Christ’s salvation also reached everyone.

✍🏾Answer 1

We Orthodox believe that through the incarnation, suffering, death, and resurrection of our Lord Jesus Christ, the entire human race was saved from the sin of Adam and Eve and from the death that came through them. This salvation from Adam’s sin is universal. Every human being, regardless of religion, is freed from the condemnation of Adam through Christ. We are not judged for Adam’s sin, because Christ has already healed human nature.

👉🏾 The Church teaches that baptism, repentance, faith, learning the Gospel, and good works are necessary not to remove Adam’s sin, but for salvation from personal sins and from hell. This must be clearly understood. Christ has already saved all humanity from Adam’s sin. As the Scripture says, “He who believes and is baptized will be saved, but he who does not believe will be condemned.”

Answer 2

We do not believe that there is anyone today who has not, in some way, encountered the Gospel. Even before modern technology, the apostles preached throughout the world. Today, the Gospel is accessible everywhere. As our Lord said in the Gospel of Matthew, the Gospel would be preached to all nations, and the Gentiles would hear it. Hearing alone is not the way of Salivation . Acceptance remains a matter of the heart.

Answer 3

Yes, the Gospel has gone out into the world, and through Christ the whole world has been saved from Adam’s sin.
