---About #thoughts, #sin, and #confession 

When does a thought become a sin? Which thoughts should we confess and should we confess all bad thoughts?

Question: Is it when we allow and choose to focus on evil thoughts that it becomes a sin or when the thought first pops into our mind? How do we know which thoughts to confess? Do we repent only for the bad thoughts that we have entertained, or also for those that we have rejected when our thoughts flashed? Even if we try to worship and think about something else, if we don't get rid of the bad thoughts that pop up in our heads, do we also confess about them? Do we confess all the bad thoughts that pop into our heads?

Answer: There are many types and methods of sin which all fall under 3 categories: thoughts, words and actions. Every evil desire that a person has brought out in his inner conscience and has not yet put into action is called the sin of thought. This sin becomes sin of speech when it is spoken by the mouth. And if the sin that is said orally is put into action, it is called the sin of action.

The foundation of sin is thought, because no sin is committed without thought. If something happens unexpectedly, it is not often questioned. But he will be punished for not being careful in his simplicity. 

Therefore, every human being must get rid of the thoughts that are the root of sin. We should immediately correct ourselves by calling on the name of God and condemning Satan as it flashes in our mind. Therefore, when the thought of sin comes to our heart, if we immediately repent in our conscience and return to our mind, that is, without entertaining the thought, without saying it in words and without putting into action, then if we go to the priest and receive his blessings, kiss his cross, our sins will be forgiven. This is through the  authority given to the priest from God; saying  "Verily I say unto you, Whatsoever ye shall bind on earth shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever ye shall loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven" Mat 18.18

Therefore, even if the thought of sin comes to our mind, if we don't think about it again and again, if we don't say it in words, if we don't put it into action, it will be forgiven by the blessings of the priest. Therefore, even though it is good to try not to forget this type of sin by saying that you will repent in the future, but in another way, keeping the sin in our mind and thinking about it again and again may create an opportunity for it to grow to the next level of sin. Therefore, the best way is to pray the priest's cross, go to church and pray, and remove this idea of sin from our hearts every day and stop thinking about it. Sin should not be something that we hoard like money, but something that we should get rid of regularly like garbage. This does not mean that we have to go and confess to our Father every day for the sin that we mentioned above as the sin of thought, but it is forgiven by the blessings of the priest's cross as explained above. Maybe if we are in an area where there is no priest and no church, we can talk to the priest by phone. And if this cannot be done, God knows our inner problems; Lord, forgive us, remove this thought from me, we pray in faith and believe that it has been done for us. 

However, it is important to understand that this kind of approach or receiving blessings from the priest does not free us from sins that we have committed by speech or by action. Also, as stated above, if we intend to do it and end up not because of inconvinient circumstances, it is considered that we have committed the sin of thought. Hence we need to repent for it. In general, if we reptedly think it in our mind, or if we speak it out, or if we get it into action, we need to  repent and confess and fulfill the canon given to us properly. At that time, all the damage we have done earlier knowingly and boldly, or unknowingly by mistake, will be forgiven all together.
