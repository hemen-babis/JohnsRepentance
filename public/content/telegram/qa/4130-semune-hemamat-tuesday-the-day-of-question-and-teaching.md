👉🏽👉🏽👉🏽 #Semune #Hemamat – #Tuesday: The Day of Question and Teaching

Grace and peace be upon you, beloved in Christ, children of the One Holy, Apostolic, and Orthodox Church. Let your ears be attentive, your hearts prepared, and your spirits be still, for today we enter into the sacred meditation on what the Church in her holy wisdom has named Semune Hemamat

Tuesday, the Day of Question and Teaching.

This day, tucked reverently within the Holy Week, does not pass us by without profound mystery and weight. It is not an idle Tuesday. It is the day upon which the Chief Cornerstone, rejected by the builders, opened His mouth in the courts of the temple and answered not with sword nor with condemnation, but with heavenly wisdom that silences all the snares of the adversary.

I. The Day Begins with Questions from the Proud

It was on this Tuesday that the Lord Christ, having overturned the tables of the money changers the day before, was met not with praise for purifying His Father’s house, but with the clamor of rebellion cloaked in religious garb.

The high priests and the elders approached Him. Not in repentance. Not in awe. But with cunning hearts and poisoned tongues they said:
“By what authority do you do these things? And who gave you this authority?”
(Matthew 21:23)

Here, O brethren, the heart of darkness reveals itself. They were not seeking the truth but a trap. Their concern was not the Kingdom of Heaven, but the approval of Caesar. They asked not to learn but to accuse. Do you see it? When light shines, the envious cover their eyes.

They were struck not by the majesty of the miracles, nor the wisdom of His teachings, but by their own wounded pride. For the Lord had dared to cleanse what they had corrupted. He had touched their commerce, their power, their influence. And so, they conspired not with swords and soldiers, but with words meant to ensnare.

But behold the wisdom of Christ! He who fashioned the mouth used their own words against them.

II. A Divine Answer in the Form of a Question

“The baptism of John, whence was it? From heaven or from men?”
(Matthew 21:25)

O depth of riches both of the wisdom and knowledge of God! He did not answer as they desired. He answered not with rebellion against Rome nor with confession of self-glory, but with the perfect question that pierced the heart.

Look and see: had they answered, “From heaven,” their hypocrisy would have been exposed. Had they said, “From men,” the people, who revered John as a prophet, would have turned on them. So, they cowered behind false humility and replied, “We do not know.”

To such hearts the Savior gives no answer.
“Neither will I tell you by what authority I do these things.”
(Matthew 21:27)

Let the faithful take heed: truth is not for the prideful to manipulate but for the humble to receive.

III. The Day of Teaching: A Rebuke and a Revelation

On this holy day, Christ did not cease to speak. Rather, He used the opportunity to teach the multitudes with parables, to reveal the hearts of men, to warn, to guide, and to awaken.

Among these teachings, one shines with unmatched clarity and weight. “Truly I tell you, the tax collectors and the prostitutes are entering the Kingdom of God ahead of you.”
(Matthew 21:28)

Behold the reversal of all earthly expectations! The high priests are cast behind; the sinners with repentant hearts go before. This is the Gospel. This is the scandal of grace. For the proud are far from salvation, but the humble find mercy even at the gate.

You see, my children, the Lord knows the schemes of men. He discerns the question that is meant to destroy and answers not with direct confrontation, but with wisdom that cuts deeper than any sword. And He teaches still He teaches us.
