👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾 About #repentance, #confession and #penitent #father 

Why confess to a Priest instead of confessing directly to God?

👉🏾Question - Why do I have to confess to a Priest? can't I, myself, confess directly to God?

Answer: Repentance is one of the requirements for any Christian. A person who confesses their sins to the priest considers the priest as God's messenger and God as the judge. The Bible says, "For the priest is the messenger of the Lord of hosts." (Malik 2:7) Repentance is a great thing that includes various actions. Among these are: admitting the wrongdoing and confessing the sin to God, to the priest and accepting the canon through the priest or penitent father.

Therefore, when a person commits a sin, they first confesses to God admiting to their sin and pouring tears of repentance before God. We confess our sins to God not to tell him what he doesn't know, but to ask for his forgiveness and mercy by trusting that we have wronged him. But this does not exclude confessing to a priest; Confession to a priest is part of confessing to God, not a substitute for that. Confession to a priest does not exclude confession to God; On the other hand, it is wrong to think that confession to God is a substitute to confession to a priest

Confession of sins to the penitent father is not only appropriate and necessary, but it is also a duty that has no alternative because it is God's command. The priestly authority that our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ gave to the holy apostles is an abstract and heavenly authority allowing them to bind and loose sin. It is also the authority given to them by the Holy Spirit to help people enter into the kingdom of God and to prevent them from the kingdom of God. This means that when God Jesus Christ, who has all power, chose and appointed the 12 apostles and 72 disciples from among the world so that when people  committed sins, they could come to the apostles repented of their sins and confessed their sins, because they were given the authority to forgive all those who have sinned, because the authority given to them will destroy all their sins. - Our Lord says to the priests in his gospel, "You are the light of the world" Matthew 5-14. Priests are the ones who lead us to the Kingdom of God through repentance, lead us away from impurity, darkness and sin, and lead us to hate sin and live a virtuous life.
 
On the day when the Holy Spirit descended, through the sermon of Saint Peter, the apostles converted 2000 people. The people then asked the apostles 'What should we do?  We don't have our own personal lives anymore and there is nothing better than this. There is blessing under you and around you.' The apostles replied to them saying 'Repent'. If we ask what the apostles mean when they say this, it means 'come tell us your repentance' (confess your sins)

When we look at the story of Lazarus, when a man in hell said to Abraham, please let me come to you, and let me rest for a moment. Abrham said to him there is a great gulf between you and me, you cannot come to me. Then he said to Abrham, "I beg you father, send Lazarus to my family, 28 for I have five brothers. Let him warn them, so that they will not also come to this place of torment.." And Abraham said, "They have Moses and the Prophets; let them listen to them."
