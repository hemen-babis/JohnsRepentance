👉 About the #Precautions we should take before partaking the #Holy #Communion 

👉🏾 Question፦ What is the compressive list of reasons for when not to take Holy Communion that’s listed in the kidase? 

They usually say if we haven’t confessed not to take it. They also mention how we need to not have hate in our hearts among other things. 

What is a full list of things we should work on or think about  before taking Holy Communion so we can work on those things and do it in a way that’s correct and not take Holy Communion when we are not ready or in situations where it’s not appropriate according to the church and what’s mentioned in the Kidase. 

Thank you!

Answer፦ Peace be upon you, child of God.  It warms my heart that you seek to approach the Holy Mysteries with reverence and preparation.  This desire for purity of heart is pleasing to God.

The Kidase, as you rightly note, outlines several conditions that call for abstaining from Holy Communion. While not an exhaustive list, it touches upon the core principles of preparation. The specific wording can vary slightly depending on the particular Kidase used, but generally includes admonitions against partaking if one:

* Has not confessed recent sins: This is paramount.  Unconfessed sin creates a barrier between us and God, hindering the grace of the sacrament.  Confession cleanses the soul and prepares us to receive the Body and Blood of Christ worthily.  As the Psalmist says, "Who shall ascend the hill of the Lord?  Or who shall stand in his holy place? He that hath clean hands and a pure heart" (Psalm 24:3-4).

* Holds hatred or unforgiveness in one's heart:  Harboring ill will towards others mirrors the betrayal of Judas and grieves the Holy Spirit. Christ commanded us to love our enemies and pray for those who persecute us (Matthew 5:44).  Reconciliation and forgiveness are essential before approaching the Chalice.

* Is engaged in open, unrepentant sin:  This encompasses a range of behaviors contrary to the teachings of Christ and His Church, such as adultery, theft, or bearing false witness.  Repentance, a sincere turning away from sin and towards God, is a prerequisite for Communion.

* Is experiencing marital disharmony: Traditionally, the Kidase advises against Communion for those who have quarreled with their spouse, emphasizing the importance of peace and unity within the marriage bond.  Reconciliation is urged before receiving the Holy Mysteries.

* Is under ecclesiastical discipline:  If one is under penance imposed by the Church, participation in Communion might be restricted until the penance is fulfilled.  This emphasizes the importance of obedience and accountability within the community of faith.

Beyond the Kidase, the Church Fathers offer further insights into preparing for Holy Communion:

* Fasting:  Abstaining from food and drink for a period before receiving Communion is a traditional practice that helps cultivate humility and focus on the spiritual.

* Prayer: Regular prayer, both private and liturgical, strengthens our connection with God and prepares us to receive His grace.

* Self-examination: Reflecting on our thoughts, words, and deeds helps us identify areas where we need to repent and grow in holiness.

* Reading Scripture:  Immersing ourselves in the word of God nourishes our souls and deepens our understanding of His love and mercy.

* Almsgiving:  Acts of charity and compassion towards others demonstrate our love for God and purify our hearts.

Remember, child of God, the Holy Eucharist is not a reward for perfection but a medicine for healing.  Approach it with humility and a contrite heart, seeking God's mercy and grace.  It is always wise to consult with your spiritual father for personalized guidance in preparing for this sacred mystery.  He can help you discern your readiness and offer counsel specific to your circumstances.

May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.  May the prayers of St. Mary and all the saints intercede for you.  And may the Holy Spirit guide you on your path to salvation.
