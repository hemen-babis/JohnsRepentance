---About how to overcome #anxiety
 
Question: How can I overcome my anxiety? How can I find peace in the midst of life's trials?

Answer: Are we, as Orthodox Christians, meant to worry? Our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ, told us not to worry, so why are we worried? When a person is separated from God, sin becomes their dwelling place. Sin and lack of understanding deprives us from peace and makes us anxious.

A person without God has no peace or sleep. In general, a person who is dwelling in sin loses everything, and is filled with anxiety. Therefore, we are told not to worry and to "Cast all your worries on God, for He cares for you" (1 Peter 5:7).

In the sixth chapter of the Gospel of Matthew, it is written, "And why do you worry about clothes? See how the flowers of the field grow. They do not labor or spin. Yet I tell you that not even Solomon in all his splendor was dressed like one of these. If that is how God clothes the grass of the field, which is here today and tomorrow is thrown into the fire, will He not much more clothe you—you of little faith? So do not worry, saying, ‘What shall we eat?’ or ‘What shall we drink?’ or ‘What shall we wear?’ For the pagans run after all these things, and your heavenly Father knows that you need them. But seek first His kingdom and His righteousness, and all these things will be given to you as well" (Matthew 6:28-33).

This is why Orthodox Christians say "Our Father" in prayer, asking God to "have mercy on us as a father has compassion on his children" (Psalm 103:13). In addition, in Psalm 102, verse 1, it is written, "Praise the Lord, my soul; all my inmost being, praise His holy name. Praise the Lord, my soul, and forget not all His benefits—who forgives all your sins and heals all your diseases, who redeems your life from the pit and crowns you with love and compassion, who satisfies your desires with good things so that your youth is renewed like the eagle’s."

If God has mercy on us and can fulfill our desires, why should we worry? Worrying and thinking are different. We can think, dream, and plan without worrying. Worry is the result of despair, but we are people who have hope.

If we have everything we need with God, why should we give up? Our purpose is to do what we can with God and never give up hope. If we fall into sin, we repent, for "the one who conceals their sins does not prosper, but the one who confesses and renounces them finds mercy" (Proverbs 28:13). If we are reconciled to God through repentance, what we lost will be returned to us. Therefore, we should not give up but rather think it through.

Let us give all our worries to God, focus on what is expected of us, and leave the rest to Him. As the apostles said, "Cast all your anxiety on Him because He cares for you" (1 Peter 5:7). We need to understand that we are required to do this.

Knowing that anxiety is the result of sin and a sign of separation from God, the solution is to live with God, repent of our sins, listen to God, and let go of things that worry us.

Peace can be found in the midst of life's trials when we live with God. Jesus Christ is the Prince of Peace, and in John 14:27, He said, "Peace I leave with you". 

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.
