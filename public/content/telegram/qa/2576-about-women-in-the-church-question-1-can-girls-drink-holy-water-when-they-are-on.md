---About #Women in the Church 

👉🏾 Question፦ 1) Can girls drink Holy water when they are on their period? & why are they not allowed to go inside the church? 

2) Is labor pain related to the curse after the fall?

Answer፦ Greetings in the love of our Lord,

Your questions touch on significant aspects of our faith and tradition, which illuminate the profound wisdom of the Orthodox Church.

1) In Orthodox tradition, all faithful, regardless of their physical condition, can partake of Holy Water. Holy Water, blessed by the grace of God, is for the healing of soul and body and does not discriminate based on natural bodily processes. As for attending the Church, traditional customs vary, but the essence lies in the preparation and state of one’s heart. In some traditions, women refrain from receiving Communion during their menstrual period, a practice rooted more in historical cultural contexts than in doctrinal necessity. The important thing is the attitude of prayer and reverence towards the sacred mysteries.

St. John Chrysostom once said, "It is not the place that adorns the person, but the person that adorns the place." (Homilies on the Statues, Homily XX) The focus should always be on our inner disposition and our relationship with God.

2) Concerning labor pains, indeed, they are related to the narrative of the fall in Genesis. The Scriptures tell us, "I will surely multiply your pain in childbearing; in pain you shall bring forth children" (Genesis 3:16). This passage reflects the consequences of the original sin affecting humanity, including the introduction of pain in childbirth. Yet, in every suffering, there is a potential for spiritual growth and deeper reliance on God's grace. Pain, when united with Christ’s suffering, can become a source of spiritual strength and renewal.

As you continue to reflect on these aspects of our faith, consider how the practices and teachings of the Orthodox Church can guide you to a deeper relationship with Christ. What steps will you take to deepen your understanding and experience of our faith?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
