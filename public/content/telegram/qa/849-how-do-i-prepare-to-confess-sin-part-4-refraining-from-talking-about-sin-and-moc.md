---How do I prepare to confess sin?

(Part 4)

- Refraining from talking about sin and mocking it: Also, if we pretend to hate sin, but make fun of it, it means we don't hate it. "In the words of a sinful person, sin accumulates" (Sira 27:4) and "there is much sin in him, so do not let anything that is impure come out of your tongue" (Sira 23:13).

Therefore, even if we meet her (sin) again, ready and prepared as much as she wants, without arguing with ourselves, if our heart's will and our mind's knowledge work together as one and understand her futility, then it is known that our rejection of sin is perfect. It will be confirmed that we have identified her as our enemy. It is only a dog that returns to its vomit.
 
- Regularly read from spiritual things, and consult spiritual fathers: To avoid falling back into sin, regularly consult a penitent father or religious teacher, pray as much as possible, go to church and listen to the word of God, read spiritual books even at home, listen to spiritual teachings and hymns. It is important.

To sum up, we will now summarise to you what a Christian should do after washing his sinful body, since his whole life is clean and the way is straight for any spiritual service:

1. Above all, be careful not to return to sin again and defile your sacred life.

2 / Receiving Holy Communion, fasting, praying, and prostrating as much as you can without stopping and without tiring.

3/ Contributing what is due to the poor as uou can; and also to God (tithe) 10% from what you earn.

4/ Regularly receiving advice and obedience from fathers, coming to church in the daily/weekly program, learning the word of God, sanctifying.

5/. Always read spiritual books that serve as a close teacher and mentor, and more than that, do your part to live in peace and love with patience and wisdom in your life, workplace, social affairs with all mankind.

Since it is possible to do all these and other spiritual things as a whole when a person's life is renewed through repentance, may God help you to put into practice the spiritual advice given to you above, believing that God will keep your life clean and holy.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
