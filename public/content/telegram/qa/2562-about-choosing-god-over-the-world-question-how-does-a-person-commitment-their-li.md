About #choosing God over the world

Question፦ How does a person commitment their life to God & choose Him over the world? 

What does a person who has submitted His life to God look like?

Answer፦ Beloved in Christ,

Your inquiry touches the very essence of our journey towards the Kingdom of Heaven. To commit one's life to God and choose Him over the world is to embark on a path of transformation, guided by faith, prayer, and the Sacraments of the Church.

The Scripture illuminates this path in Matthew 6:33, "But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and His righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you." This verse encapsulates the heart of commitment to God: prioritizing His will above all earthly concerns, trusting that He will provide for your needs.

A person who has submitted his life to God is often recognized by the fruits of the Spirit manifest in their life, as outlined in Galatians 5:22-23 — love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control. These qualities emerge not from human effort alone but through cooperation with the grace of God, allowing His Spirit to transform us from within.

Engaging in regular prayer, fasting, partaking of the Holy Eucharist, and confession are not mere rituals but means of grace, helping us to draw nearer to God and detach from worldly desires. Through these practices, guided by a spiritual father or mother, we learn to discern God's voice and His will for our lives.

Remember, dear one, that this journey is not a solitary endeavor. The Church offers us a community of faith, a spiritual family, to support and uplift us as we walk this path together.

Now, I ask you, what step will you take today to draw closer to God and seek His kingdom above all else?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
