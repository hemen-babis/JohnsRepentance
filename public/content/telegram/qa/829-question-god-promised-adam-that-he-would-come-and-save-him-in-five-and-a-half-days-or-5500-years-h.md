👉Question: God promised Adam that He would come and save him in five and a half days or 5500 years. How do we count the days of the week?
 
✍️Answer:- The Church has always taught that in order to save Adam and his descendants, God promised to be born as a man and save them. This promise was given to Adam when he fell into sin and asked for forgiveness. God promised him that He would be born of his son, be crucified, die, rise and save him. The period from the time Adam fell or when the promise was given until the day of our Lord's birth is known as "five and a half days", according to the Lord's words and revealed to the Scholars by the Holy Spirit.

The scholars, enlightened by the spirit of God, understood that "five and a half days in God's will is five thousand and five hundred years". This means that God's five days are equivalent to five thousand years and five years and six months in man's time. It is important to note that the apostle Saint Peter explained that one day is with the Lord as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day.

Therefore, the difference between God's calendar and our calendar is clear. What is counted as one day or one year by God is counted as a thousand to us. Thus, the seven days of one week period should be understood with the grace and spiritual permission given to us. We should consider what God's thoughts and calculations look like and understand that the time calculation in the Bible, as given by our ancestors, is five thousand and five hundred years, which is called "five and a half days" by the Lord.

Saint Paul has explained that the day of the Lord or the coming of the Lord is sudden and unexpected. Therefore, we must always be ready and live our lives in accordance with God's will.

This is a powerful message that was given on Mount Olivet, as the Church teaches about the last days and the second coming, which is the foundation of this concept.

So, how are these seven days calculated? We use the grace and the gift of the Holy Spirit to reflect on what we have and count the seven days and the years according to our beliefs. As a religion, we calculate time differently from the world, and we are now entering the eighth millennium based on our calculations.

Although we have the Bible, there is a difference between our calculation and God's formula, as His calculation is counted in thousands. According to our calendar, since the creation of the world, it has been 8,800 years until the birth of the Lord, which was 5,500 years, and after His birth, according to our calendar, it has been around 2,000 years.

We acknowledge that our calculation is different from God's kindness and calculation of time, as His idea of time is different from ours. We should accept it with grace and without interfering in God's plans or doubting His wisdom, even if our time seems long.
