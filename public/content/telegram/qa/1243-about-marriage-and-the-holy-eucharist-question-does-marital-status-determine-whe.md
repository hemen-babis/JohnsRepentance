---About #marriage and the holy #Eucharist 

👉Question: Does marital status determine whether to receive Holy Communion? For example, if we are single, married, what if our partner is not willing? Or what should I do if my fiance refuses to marry me in the sacrament?

✍Answer: One of the sacrements of the New Testament is Christ's holy flesh and His precious blood. We have said that the special gift of the Holy Spirit that we have been blessed with since the birth of a boy on the 40th day and the birth of a girl on the 80th day is the food of eternal life. To separate from this is to die although we seem alive simply because we breath. Our body: If we lose  food for a few days or even for few hours for some people, it is definitely not possible to stay alive. This is one of the common weaknesses of the body where we lose our fleshly existence. Likewise, if the soul loses Christ's holy flesh and precious blood, it dies and has no life. It means that we have killed it. Therefore, in order to eat this eternal life, to drink that gives eternal life, we should learn what kind of system and what kind of spiritual preparation we should follow. We are commanded by God to live a Christian life, of which the Holy Eucharist is the foundation. Any teaching that keeps us away by threatening and exaggerating for other reasons is false teaching and should be avoided. 

Apostle St. Paul said that if we eat and drink the ultimate food of the soul without preparation and understanding, it will be a debt to us. This means that it is necessary to always have a life prepared for repentance and to accept those who are counselors in the affairs of our souls, the fathers of the church whom God has appointed as our servants and shepherds. In other words, we are commanded to always receive with repentance, so that we do not make this mistake that St. Paul wrote about (so that we do not receive without deserving or understanding this), but there is no occasion where we are commanded to depart from this mystery, not even for a single day.

Therefore, for those who are said to be before marriage, as we mentioned above, we should realize that we should continue to accept what we received in our childhood, that started from the time of Christianity,  until the end of our lives. But we should do this without committing any shame or sin as that is the only thing which keeps us away from the Holy Communion. Everyone knows what sin or shame is that keeps us away from the Holy Communion, even a child knows. There is no one who does not know the type of sin from the smallest to the highest level of sin. Murder, adultery, adultery, all these seeming sins can keep you away from the sacrament. But through repentant and reprimanded, you can continue the communion by repenting and being mentored and taught by their penitent father. 

Back to the question, it is to say that we should do this throughout our life, and it is not a matter of martial status. Both the male and female start participating in this sacrement at the time of their baptism and actively participate alone until they reach maturity age. After that, the man chooses someone who resembles him in his spiritual life and the woman chooses someone who resembles her in her spiritual life and they get married and continue with the Holy Communion together. Even those who are not married in the Holy Communion, without having this opportunity, come to repentance and they, too, can continue their holy purpose that they have abandoned. Because the Church has made a system for us, it has reconciliation for all from her creator.
