Answer፦ Beloved in Christ,

Your inquiry touches the heart of our faith, the resurrection and life in Jesus Christ, and the mystery of intercession, which are profound yet comforting doctrines of our Holy Church.

Indeed, when our Lord Jesus Christ proclaims, "Those who believe in me, even though they die, will live, and everyone who lives and believes in me will never die" (John 11:25-26), He speaks of a spiritual resurrection that precedes the physical one. This spiritual resurrection is our passage from death, the death brought by sin, to life, the eternal communion with God. By believing in Christ, we participate now in this new life, being reborn in spirit, which is a foretaste of the resurrection to come.

Intercession, beloved, is deeply rooted in our relationship with Christ and our communion with the saints. It reflects the unity of the Body of Christ, the Church, where the life and holiness of one member benefits all, and where we are called to bear one another's burdens (Galatians 6:2). In the Orthodox tradition, intercession acknowledges that we are not isolated individuals before God but are part of a holy fellowship, the living and the departed, who are united in Christ.

Our intercession for others and the saints' intercession for us do not negate the direct relationship each person is called to have with God. Rather, they express and deepen this relationship, reflecting the unity and love that should characterize the entire body of believers. As Saint Paul teaches us, we are to pray for one another that we may be healed (James 5:16), indicating that our prayers contribute to the spiritual well-being and salvation of others.

When we approach God with confidence, as advised in Hebrews 4:15, we do so not only on our own behalf but also bringing before Him the needs and sorrows of our brothers and sisters, trusting in His mercy and grace. This act of drawing near to God is both personal and communal, inviting His presence into our lives and our world.

As we reflect on these truths, may we ask ourselves how we are embodying this new life in Christ and participating in the communion of saints through prayer and intercession. Are we willing to deepen our faith by living in accordance with the commandments and actively participating in the life of the Church, thereby drawing nearer to the throne of grace?

May the peace of Christ guide you in your spiritual journey. What will be your next step to live more fully in the resurrected life that Christ offers?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
