👉 About our Lord and Savior #Jesus Christ 

👉🏾 Question፦ Was Jesus born in Ethiopia?

Answer፦ Beloved in Christ, grace to you and peace. Your question touches on the incarnate mystery of our Lord, Jesus Christ, who was born in Bethlehem, as foretold by the prophets of old. The Prophet Micah proclaimed, "But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, though you are small among the clans of Judah, out of you will come for me one who will be ruler over Israel, whose origins are from of old, from ancient times" (Micah 5:2). This prophecy reveals the humility and divine plan of our Lord's nativity, not in Ethiopia, but in the land of Judea.

Ethiopia, however, holds a cherished place in Christian history, being one of the first nations to embrace the faith. The Ethiopian eunuch's baptism by Philip the Evangelist (Acts 8:26-40) signifies the spreading of the Gospel beyond the Jewish community, embracing all nations under the grace of Christ. This story is a testament to Ethiopia's early acceptance and devotion to the teachings of Christ, making it a beacon of faith from the earliest times.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
