Therefore, as the creator is one, religion can also be only one. Religion is given once by God, as it is said, "Religion was given once for all to the saints" (Jude 3). God gave religion. And we accepted. Human beings can only accept religion, but cannot work or improve religion. Religion is not a material thing, but a life lived by believing in the revelation of the Holy Spirit. Basically, the word that religion is one is a truth that does not raise any questions for a person who understands the unity of the creator. Religion means accepting your creator and trusting in His laws. We belive in one God and the creator is one in nature.

Therefore, believing in the existence of one Creator and performing worship worthy of His nature cannot be in many, but in one unified system. This is the great truth. When the Apostle St. Paul said that religion is one as the creator is one, he said, "There is one Lord, one religion, one baptism, who is above all, who works in all, who lives in all, and who is the Father of all." (Eph 4:5) 

It is said that God made a law for the first man, Adam, with dos and don'ts. They made a covenant that if Adam kept the law he would live forever and if he broke it he would be punished with death. The first religion in the history of mankind is called this covenant relationship between Adam and Adam's creator. But Adam was deceived by the devil's evil advice and broke the law. He broke his promise. As a result, he lost a God-like father and was expelled from Paradise. But because God is good merciful , according to his promise to bring Adam back to his glory, he appeared as a man wearing Adam's body to fulfill his promise. When he appeared as a man, His name was called Christ. Those who believe that Christ is the natural son of God are called Christians. This means a disciple of Christ's side. And the faith (religion) is called Christianity. 


Therefore, as we have received religion from God, people's role is only to  belive in it and use it to inherit eternal life. This means that no individual or entity in this world has the authority to add or subtract from the truth (religion) given by God. Although, we see more number of denominations called Christianity which are founded by human philosophy; and some ask which is the right one being confused. Our answer is that the right Christianity is the one which was founded by Christ himself. Infact  it is clear that except for one, the rest were invented many years after Christ's ascension, and this has become an increasing and unstoppable matter like the number of human beings. However, the knowledge found in religion is given or revealed by God himself, not the knowledge or discovery that man has attained through research. This is what our Lord Jesus Christ explained by saying, "I gave them the promise you gave me, and they accepted it." (John 17:6) 
 
Therefore, religion is the way that God has revealed Himself and given us to bring us to Him. This is built on the foundation of St. Peter's belief that "You are the Christ, the Son of the living God" (Matthew 16:16). When our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ revealed himself to the world, when Philip arrived from a place called Caesarea, he asked his disciples, "Who do people say the Son of Man is?" After asking, he asked his disciples, "Who do you say that I am?" they have. And Simon Peter answered and said, Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God. And Jesus answered and said unto him. Son of Jonah, Son of Jonah, blessed are you, for flesh and blood did not reveal this to you, but my Father in heaven. And I say to you, you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church, and the gates of hell will not prevail against it." (Mathew 15:15-17)
