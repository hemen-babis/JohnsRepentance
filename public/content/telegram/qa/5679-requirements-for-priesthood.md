👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾#Requirements for #priesthood 

👉🏾Question :-What are the requirements to be a priest

👉🏾Answer :-My beloved child in Christ, you have asked a good and holy question: What are the requirements to be a priest?

The priesthood is not an earthly honor to be sought after, but a heavenly responsibility to be carried with fear and trembling before God. Among the faithful men, women, and clergy, the priest bears a spiritual duty higher than all others, for his service is not of this world but of heaven.

When His Holiness Abune Shenouda was asked about the priesthood, he said:

“The priesthood I am giving you is not an appointment, but a responsibility.”

Appointment is light and fills the stomach, but responsibility brings sleepless nights, deep concern for souls, and unceasing prayer for forgiveness and mercy.

A true priest never ceases to examine his own life and the lives of his spiritual children. Through fasting, prayer, and supplication, he intercedes before God, following the Lord’s command:

👉🏾“Feed my lambs, feed my sheep.” (John 21:16)

Before a priest ordained, the Holy Scriptures teach that he must be able to instruct the faithful, lead his household in godliness, be the husband of one wife, and faithfully guide his family with discipline and love. He must maintain a good reputation among people and live in peace with all.

✍🏾The Apostle also warns against unworthy habits: a priest must not be given to much wine, not double-tongued, not greedy for money, and not quick-tempered. Instead, he should be humble, gentle, forgiving, patient, and peaceful  a true example for the flock of Christ.

✍🏾A priest sits on the throne of the Apostles and receives from God the authority to bind and loose sins. As Saint John Afework wrote:

“The Lord confirms in heaven what priests have done on earth, and accepts the thoughts of His servants.”

✍🏾Thus, the mysteries performed by priests on earth are sanctified by heaven itself. Because of this great responsibility, bishops must exercise great caution before ordaining anyone to the priesthood. The faithful may also express their testimony about the candidate’s character and life before the bishop confers ordination.

The Essential Qualities of a Priest

1. Morality:
The priest must develop a life that leads to holiness. He must strive daily to live in purity of heart and thought, keeping himself away from every form of sin. His words and actions must reflect the holiness of God whom he serves.

2. Education that Leads to Holiness:
A priest must be well instructed in the faith and grounded in the teachings of the Holy Church. He should dedicate himself to reading and learning the Word of God and the writings of the Fathers, so that if the faithful ask him tomorrow, he will   answer the questions , can instruct, guide, and defend the truth with wisdom.

3. Self-Control:
The priest must rule over his desires and emotions with discipline. He should be slow to anger, measured in speech, and temperate in all things. Without self-control, the priest cannot shepherd the souls of others, for he who is overcome by his passions cannot lead others to freedom in Christ.
