About #marriage

👉 Question #1: I have a lot of problems in my marriage. My marriage was done in church, in the Holy Communion! What do you recommend??

👉 Question #2:- I could not be a good wife. When I fight with my husband, I insult him, and want to strangle him very bad. What do you advise me?

✍Answer: Our dear member, basically, our church teaches that any Christian should not be separated from the Holy Body and the Precious Blood from the time they receive the sonship of God through Baptism and are sealed with the Holy Communion on the 40th or 80th day of their life until the end. Along with this, the Church emphasizes that it is a Christian obligation to marry in the Holy Communion. Because what makes our marriage real is if we are married in the sacrament. As it is written, "the two shall become one flesh" during the sacred marriage ceremony, the great mystery of which the two bodies are transformed into physical unity and spiritual unity though Christ's Holy Body and His Precious Blood. (Mark 10:8). Apart from this, if it is a worldly marriage, this mystery will not be fulfilled in which the two become one. In short, this pair cannot be called two but one.

Of course, when we think the fact that many believers are getting married in the secular system like the Gentiles, we appreicate your  appropriate Christian behavior to decide to perform your marriage in the Holy Communion; grace and blessings will be abundant for you. No matter how many lies and illegalities abound, those have never replaced a single truth; God's law never changes. Therefore, we recommend that people who are married in a worldly system without knowing this truth and God's law, should repent and be united in the sacrament so that their marriage can be called a true marriage. The reason we want to explain all this in our introduction is to create awareness for other members as well  based on the idea that our questioner's  raised  about getting married in Holy Communion.

Dear member, in your question, it seems that you have lost hope and already concluded the problem your marriage encountered cannot be solved at all. But you didn't specificaly mention to us what the problem is. Identifying the problem correctly is getting half of the solution so it would have been very useful if you specified. 

Hence, in response to your question, even if you have not mentioned the challenges you faced, we hereby explained to you a general way and perspective of how to manage your marriage with spiritual wisdom, so we entrust you to implement our advice:

There are two things that make a spiritual marriage strong. One is to be united in religion (live in religion) and the other is to be united in purity (righteousness). The lack thereof is what destroys marriages.  In other words, marriage is a partnership in which the spouses agree that there is nothing that can separate them apart from death, adultery, and changing their religion. Therefore, if marriage is a place where people enter and do not leave, then we should be careful and aware enough to enter into it not blindly but with knowledge.

Therefore, it is important to learn about the nature of marriage before marriage. A house can support its roof only when its foundation is strong, and marriage requires preparation not only for temporary feelings and happiness, but also for challenges that may be faced in the long run. Indeed, some marriages bring blessings to husband and wife, children, and others. And some marriages don't come with this. What is the difference? The main difference between these marriages is the purpose of the marriage. If they get married for material things, or to satisfy their lust, or any other similar worldly view, the marriage will not bring blessings. Those who get married with the goal of righteousness and to travel with the spiritual goal of attaining salvation and eternal life, their marriage brings great happiness and blessing not only to the couple, but also to their children and other people.
