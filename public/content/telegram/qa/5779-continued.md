✍🏾The Ministry of Saint Welete Petros in Waldeba and Tselemt

After Saint Welete Petros entered the holy monastery of Waldeba and completed her week of spiritual preparation, she began teaching the fathers and mothers of the monastery, urging them to remain steadfast in the Tewahedo faith. Her words strengthened hearts, restored courage, and rekindled devotion among the monastics.

✍🏾When Emperor Susenyos learned how boldly she continued to resist the foreign teachings and how she strengthened the monks and the faithful to stand firm in Orthodoxy, he grew deeply troubled. Her influence was turning many back from their apostasy, and this angered him. He repeatedly sent soldiers to seize her, but while she remained in Waldeba they could not capture her. Seeing their persistence, she departed from Waldeba and journeyed to Tselemt.

✍🏾In Tselemt her teachings spread quickly, like fire racing through dry grass. The believers welcomed her with love and accepted her guidance. Many prepared themselves for martyrdom rather than abandon the faith of their fathers. Hearing this, Emperor Susenyos declared, “If I leave her alone, she will never leave me,” and commanded Balambaras Filatawos, the governor of Tselemt, to arrest her and bring her before him.

✍🏾The governor seized Saint Welete Petros and brought her to the emperor. They attempted to persuade her to accept the new religion, but she stood firm, unwavering in her confession of the true Tewahedo faith.

✍🏾Three Catholic priests came forward and challenged her with theological arguments. Wolete Petros responded with wisdom and clarity, exposing their errors and embarrassing them. Day after day they returned to pressure her, but she stood anchored on the rock of unity. Nothing could shake her.

✍🏾At last, one of the priests admitted to the king, “Trying to convert her is like pouring water on a rock.” Suseniyos, furious, decided to kill her. But his advisors warned him: “If you kill her now, the entire nation will take her death as a sacrifice for the faith. And if the people perish, whom will you rule? Let her be imprisoned instead.” The king listened and chose exile over execution.

✍🏾Melkea Christos, a prominent Catholic official, approached Suseniyos and said, “O king, if it pleases you, give her into my custody until winter ends. I will counsel her, and Alphonsus, the teacher of the Friars, will read to her. If she still refuses, then send her into exile.” The king agreed.

✍🏾Wolete Petros was taken to Gondar, where the Catholic bishop resided. She remained there with her two companions, Wolete Christos and Ehte kristos.Welete Paulos has been staying in Zege. Alphonsus Mendes visited her constantly, attempting to teach her the doctrine of Leon. The king also questioned her repeatedly about converting to Catholicism, but her answer remained unchanged: “Absolutely not.”

👉🏾Keremt passed, and Hidar arrived. Neither pressure, nor counsel, nor intimidation shook her devotion to the oneness of God as preserved in the Orthodox faith. Seeing her unbreakable resolve, Suseniyos finally ordered that she be sent into exile at Gerbel Bereha under military escort.
