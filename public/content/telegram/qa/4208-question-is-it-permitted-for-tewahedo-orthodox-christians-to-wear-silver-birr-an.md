Question፦ Is it permitted for Tewahedo Orthodox Christians to wear silver(birr)?

Answer፦ Peace be upon you, my child.  Your question regarding the wearing of silver, or ብር (birr), is a good one, demonstrating a thoughtful approach to your faith.  There is no explicit prohibition against wearing silver within the Tewahedo Orthodox tradition.  The Scriptures speak of silver being used for adornment, even in sacred contexts.  Think, for example, of the silver used in the construction of the Tabernacle (Exodus chapters 25-27).

However, the Church Fathers, in their wisdom, have always cautioned against excessive attachment to material possessions, regardless of their composition.  Saint John Chrysostom, whose eloquence illuminated the Church, reminds us, "Do not adorn yourself outwardly by braiding your hair, and by wearing gold ornaments or fine clothing; rather, clothe yourselves with the beauty that comes from within, the unfading beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit, which is so precious to God." (1 Peter 3:3-4).  This echoes the teachings of our Lord Himself, who warned against storing up treasures on earth where moth and rust destroy (Matthew 6:19).

So, while wearing silver itself is not forbidden, we must examine our hearts and ensure that our adornment does not become a source of vanity or pride.  Are we wearing silver for the sake of outward appearance and social status, or is it simply a matter of cultural tradition?  Do we prioritize the adornment of our inner selves, cultivating virtues such as humility, compassion, and prayerfulness?

Remember, my child, true beauty lies in the purity of our hearts and our devotion to God.  Fasting, almsgiving, and fervent prayer are far more precious adornments than any silver or gold.

Perhaps reflecting on these words from the Book of Sirach would be beneficial:  "The beauty of a woman cheers her husband, and surpasses every desire.  Graciousness of speech increases her charm; there is no equal to her in beauty.  The grace of a modest woman is above gold, and the beauty of her countenance surpasses precious stones." (Sirach 26:13-15)

Do not hesitate to reach out to your priest or spiritual father for further guidance on this matter.  He can offer personalized counsel based on your specific circumstances.  I will check in with you again soon.  May God bless you and guide you on your spiritual journey.

Peace be with you.
