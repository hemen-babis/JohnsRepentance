IV. A Warning to the Righteous in Every Generation

Let us now turn the mirror upon ourselves. For this Tuesday was not for the Pharisees alone. It is for us. The righteous will always face questions. The doers of good will always face resistance.

Do not be surprised, O soul, when your acts of love are met with suspicion. When you build, others will ask what right you have. When you walk in righteousness, others will question your motives. It has ever been so. For the world loves its own, and the children of light are not of this world.

Even today, the faithful are met with traps of clever words. “By what authority do you do this?” is the question of every generation to those who dare walk in obedience to Christ. Let not your heart be troubled. The Lord Himself was questioned first.

V. The Lesson for the Devout

On this Tuesday, we are called not only to remember but to imitate. Set aside worldly thoughts. Approach the temple of the heart with reverence. Study the Lord’s teachings. Be wise as serpents and gentle as doves.

Let your answer to the world’s wicked questioning not be one of rage but of wisdom. Let your deeds be so pure that no trap can ensnare you. And when in doubt, turn your eyes to Him who answered perfectly not to vindicate Himself, but to glorify His Father in heaven.

May the mercy of the Lord Jesus Christ, the intercession of the Most Holy Theotokos, and the life-giving power of the Cross shield us from the schemes of the enemy. Let us walk boldly into the temple of our hearts, cleansing it as He did, and teaching all who will hear, not with words alone, but with lives full of holy authority.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
