About #Repentance 

Question፦ What preparation is needed for repentance? What do we say when we repent?? What should we do after we repent?

Answer፦ The concept of repentance in the Orthodox Church is a fundamental question that requires careful consideration. Before embarking on the process of repentance, one must first ask oneself why they wish to repent. The reasons for repentance must be thoroughly understood, including an awareness of one's sinful nature, the need for repentance, and what one has lost by sinning. Repentance is necessary because we are all sinners, and only those who are sick need to be healed, not the healthy.

Therefore, it is important to understand the meaning of sin before repentance. Sin signifies a loss, separation, and avoidance. We lose blessings, peace, help, love, and the ability to live with God. Sin also causes deprivation and separation from holy places, prayer, blessings, and all good things.

To illustrate this point, we can examine the example of the devil, who was not initially created as an evil being. He was created as the chief of the holy angels and was close to God. However, he attempted to use his creative talents for his own benefit and was consequently cast out from the presence of God. He lost his sanctity, just as Adam was separated from God and lost his holiness, peace, and blessings. Therefore, before repenting, one must understand the severity and essence of sin and repent to regain what was lost.

When we repent, the first step is to acknowledge that we are sinners. This involves listing all of our sins, from actions to thoughts, without concealing anything. It is crucial to recognize that hiding our sins is akin to being a secret friend of the evil spirit. On the other hand, confessing our sins is living with God. We must confess to both our repentant father and to God himself. It is vital to understand that the choice to live or die is ours.

Finally, we must have the willingness to repent. Living in sin is not living. It is essential to realize that it is not right and to repent of our sinful ways. It is not challenging to turn back when we understand that the path we are on is wrong. We must hate and abhor sin and understand that it is more poisonous than poison itself. Only then can we avoid it and genuinely repent.

Moreover, repentance is a vital process that requires understanding and willingness. By acknowledging our sins, we can regain what we have lost and live with God.
