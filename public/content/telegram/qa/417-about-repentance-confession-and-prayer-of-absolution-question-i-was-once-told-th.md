---About #repentance, #confession and prayer of  #absolution 

👉Question፦ I was once told that the prayer of absolution at the beginning of the Divine Liturgy Service can be considered as forgiveness of sin before partaking in the Sacrament of Holy Communion is this Orthodox practice? Can this be considered a substitute for confession of you didn't have time to meet with your priest before?

👉Answer: If we understand the purpose of the question correctly: In the Liturgy, after the prayer, the priest blesses the people with a cross saying, 'May God forgive', does this system of atonement for sins replace the system where we will personally go to the Father of repentance and confess our sins and receive forgiveness of sins? Also, if we approach any priest in person (without confessing our sins) and say 'God forgive you' and bless us with a cross, will all our sins be forgiven? Before we answer these questions, it is appropriate to look at the types of sin.  

Types of sin: Although there are many types and methods of sin, they can be divided into 3 categories. These are of thought, speech, and action. The basis of sin is thought. Because no one commits any sin unintentionally. If something happens unexpectedly, it is not often questioned; But he will be punished for not being careful in his simplicity. Therefore, every human being must get rid of the thoughts that are the root of sin. When it is thought of, he must immediately correct himself by calling upon God's name and condemning Satan.

Constantly thinking about something wrong reveals a willingness to do it. He will become addicted to it. This will count as done. In other words, it is a matter of time, place, and situation that he was not able to do it, because if it is convenient for him, he wouldn't  hesitate to do it. In fact, if after he has spent a lot effort, he allows himself to commit his sin but faces an obstacle that is beyond his control and returns, it is considered that he has already committed , it and committed a complete wrongdoing.

Also, a person may see, hear, or speak evil things while walking, or wherever he is. But if he realizes that this is an offense and immediately calls upon the name of God and condemns Satan and abandons him, and does not follow his thoughts, it is not considered an offense. But if he is still eager to watch, hear, and talk about that thing, and if he is motivated to make that thing happen, then he will be considered to have put all this into action, committed wrongdoing, and committed a sin. On the other hand, there is a reason why a sin committed instantly without thinking about it is not considered a major sin, and we have explained this above, but there will be a canon that finds it as it is.

When the priests pray that God will forgive us, which of these types of sins (thoughts, words, deeds) will be forgiven?👇

As we have seen above, every evil desire that a person has taken out and brought down in his inner conscience and has not yet put into action is called a sin of thought. The sin that is thought in the heart is called the sin of speech when it is spoken by the mouth. If the sin that is said orally is put into action, it is called the sin of action.
