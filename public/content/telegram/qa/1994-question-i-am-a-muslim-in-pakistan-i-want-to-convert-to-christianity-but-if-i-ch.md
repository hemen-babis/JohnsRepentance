Question፦ I am a #muslim in Pakistan. I want to convert to Christianity but if I change my religion, the muslims here will kill me. What should I do?

Answer፦ My dear child in faith, your situation is indeed a challenging and heart-wrenching one. The courage you show in seeking the truth is a testament to the stirring of the Holy Spirit within you. Remember the words of our Lord Jesus Christ in the Gospel of Matthew, "Blessed are those who are persecuted for righteousness' sake, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven." (Matthew 5:10). This is a profound truth to hold close to your heart.

In the history of our Orthodox faith, many have faced persecution for their belief. The early Christians, including the holy Apostles, often practiced their faith in secret due to the threat of persecution. They gathered in catacombs and private homes, away from the prying eyes of those who sought to harm them. In this, there is a lesson for us: our faith can be kept alive even in the most trying of circumstances.

You are in a delicate situation, and your safety is of utmost importance. Embrace the Christian faith in your heart, and let your journey with Christ begin internally. Read the Scriptures, pray in the privacy of your heart, and seek the Lord's guidance. You might not be able to express your faith openly, but God knows your heart and your earnest desire to follow Him.

As you grow in your faith, seek discreet ways to connect with a spiritual father or guide, perhaps even through online resources or confidential correspondence. There are many avenues in our digital age to learn about Orthodoxy and grow spiritually without putting yourself in harm's way. You can always reach out to us at anytime for any questions you may have. We also recomend that you join our Telegram or Facebook pages for more comphrehensive lessons: https://linktr.ee/johnsrepentance

Above all, pray for guidance and protection. The Lord is a refuge for all who call on Him in truth. Your safety and well-being are paramount, and your journey towards Christ is one that can be taken with cautious, yet hopeful steps.

Now, consider this: How can you start nurturing your newfound faith in Christ within the confines of your current environment, keeping safety in mind?
