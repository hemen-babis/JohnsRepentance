---About responsibilities of a #spiritual #father 

👉 Question፦ My confession father does not answer my calls or my text anymore. They have left us in a country without any priest. They expect us to establish a church without any guidance or help. I am tired and frustrated. 

Answer፦ It is vital, beloved members, to perceive circumstances from your spiritual father's perspective. For if he is truly in the service of the Church, he shall not abandon his flock willingly. The heart of a true priest resonates with the love of a father - a love that parallels the love our Heavenly Father has for us. A true spiritual father protects and nurtures not just the body but the soul, fostering an intimate bond between us and the Divine.

Therefore, let us not hasten to judge or yield to despair. We must remind ourselves that we are all frail beings, subjected to earthly trials and tribulations. There could be myriad reasons, known only to God, for your spiritual father's absence.

Our Savior, in the Gospel of John 10:11, stated: "I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep." God, in His divine wisdom, will assuredly pave the way forward, offering us His sacred guidance. Let us then turn our hearts and prayers towards Him.

The Prophet Saint David, in Psalm 23:1-2, comfortingly reassured us: "The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want. He makes me lie down in green pastures." It is God who watches over us, who steadies our course amidst the storms of life. He extends His merciful gaze upon us, proffering His support and shielding us from those who seek our downfall.

Dear member, do not allow weariness to veil your hope. God's divine power is our refuge, our strength when we are weary. When you feel your spirit flagging, remember the words of the Psalmist, in Psalm 121:1-2, "I lift up my eyes to the mountains—where does my help come from? My help comes from the Lord, the Maker of heaven and earth."

Rest assured that God's grace is omnipresent. Let us strive to understand the circumstances of your spiritual father. We are always available to extend our help, should you need it. We have answered your question as much as we understood it but it was not very clear what you're looking to do so please reach out to us with more detail so we can assist further. Let us cast our burdens onto the Lord, for He is our peace, our solace.

May we continually glorify the God of our ancestors, our Father, magnifying His holy name in all we do. Peace be upon us all.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
