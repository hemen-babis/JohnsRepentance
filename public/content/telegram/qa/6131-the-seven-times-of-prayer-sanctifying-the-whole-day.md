👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾The #Seven #Times of #Prayer: #Sanctifying the Whole Day.

Question:Can someone explain what we should pray about during the seven prayers each day?

Answer:My child, the Seven Times of Prayer were not given to burden the body, but to heal the soul and gather the scattered hours of our day back to God. The Church, like a wise and patient mother, divided time itself and placed prayer inside it, so that no hour would remain empty of remembrance.

Each prayer hour is not random. Each one is rooted in creation, salvation, suffering, love, repentance, hope, and rest. When we pray them, we walk through the life of Christ and the history of humanity in one single day.

1.Yengeh (11:00–12:00) – Why We Pray at This Hour

We remember our Savior Jesus Christ, who loved us to the point of death, standing silently before Pilate without judgment. At this hour, we ask the God who grants rest to also grant rest to our souls.
This is the time when our father Adam was created, reminding us of our own beginning and God’s original purpose for humanity.

Morning Prayers 

Especially if we did not pray during the night, it is good to pray in the morning. These may include:

👉🏾The Psalms of David

👉🏾Wudase Maryam

👉🏾Arganon

👉🏾Bartos Prayer

👉🏾Seife Selassie

👉🏾Seife Melekot

Since morning is a time of renewal and alertness, and the hours are open to us, we should pray according to our strength and love for prayer.

2.The Third Hour Prayer (3:00 AM) – Why We Pray

At this hour:

👉🏾Our mother Eve was created.

👉🏾The Prophet Daniel prayed and was saved from the mouth of the serpent.

👉🏾Our Lord Jesus Christ was flogged without sin before Pilate.

👉🏾The Holy Apostles received the Holy Spirit on Mount Zion, and the 72 languages were revealed.

👉🏾The Virgin Mary received the Good News from the Archangel Gabriel.

Because of this, we pray especially to the Virgin Mary and the Archangel Gabriel at this hour, asking her Son to intercede for us.
These prayers should not exceed 30 minutes. If time or strength is limited, it is sufficient to pray:

👉🏾Selam Eleki three times

👉🏾One Abune Zebesemayat

3.The Sixth Hour Prayer (6:00 AM)

This is the hour when we remember:

👉🏾Christ was stripped to clothe us with the robe of glory.

👉🏾He was crowned for our sake.

👉🏾He accepted bitter suffering and death.

👉🏾The Virgin Mary endured deep sorrow.

At this time, it is good to pray:

👉🏾Sene gologota

👉🏾seyfe melekot

As the Psalmist says:
“Evening and morning and at noon I will pray, and He shall hear my voice.” (Psalm 54:17)

This hour is also dangerous spiritually, as demons are active, so we must strengthen ourselves through prayer.

4.The Ninth Hour Prayer

This is the hour when our Lord revealed His love for humanity by dying on the Cross.It is also the hour when the holy angels carry human prayers to the Creator.

Because it is the hour of Christ’s death, we pray with repentance, remembering our own death, asking forgiveness for our sins, and glorifying God.

5.The Eleventh Hour Prayer

This hour reflects rest after labor. Just as people receive their daily bread, the faithful await their eternal reward at the Second Coming of Christ.For this reason, the prayer of this hour reminds us of the Second Coming.

At this time, it is appropriate to pray:

👉🏾 wudase Maryam

👉🏾mihla tselot (ምህላ)

6.The Evening Prayer

This is the hour when God established the order of prayer through the saints and among the people.

It is a prayer to the God of peace, asking Him to grant us peace of soul and body.

Recommended prayers include:

👉🏾wudase maryam

👉🏾Anqetse birhan

👉🏾ywedswa

If one was unable to pray in the morning, this hour is especially suitable. It is also good to pray the Arganon used by our forefathers.
