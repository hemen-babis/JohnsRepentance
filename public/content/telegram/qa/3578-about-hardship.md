👉 About #hardship 

👉🏾 Question፦ A follow up question, 

For the answer of people struggling because we are in the fallen world. The ones that aren’t struggling as much as the other ones living in struggle, does God care more about them & their kids?

Answer፦ Peace be upon you, child. It is a natural human inclination to question the nature of God's love and how it manifests in a world seemingly filled with disparity.  The Holy Scripture reminds us that God's ways are higher than our ways, and His thoughts higher than our thoughts (Isaiah 55:8-9). 

The truth is, my child, God's love is not a finite resource that He dispenses more generously to some than others.  His love is an infinite wellspring, freely flowing to all His children.  

Remember the parable of the lost sheep?  The shepherd leaves his flock of ninety-nine to seek the one that has strayed (Matthew 18:12-14, Luke 15:4-7).  Does this mean he loves the lost sheep more?  No, it speaks to the boundless compassion and relentless pursuit He has for each soul. 

The struggles we face in this fallen world, though they may differ in form and intensity, are all trials that offer us opportunities for spiritual growth and drawing closer to God.  He is near to the brokenhearted (Psalm 34:18) and offers His strength to those who call upon Him (Isaiah 40:29).

Instead of comparing our lots and questioning His love, let us focus on cultivating gratitude for the blessings in our lives and offering a helping hand to our brothers and sisters facing hardship.  Remember the words of St. John Chrysostom, "If you cannot find Christ in the beggar at the church door, you will not find Him in the chalice." 

Do not lose heart, child. God sees your struggles, He hears your prayers, and He loves you unconditionally. Let us continue to seek His will in all things, for His ways lead to life everlasting.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
