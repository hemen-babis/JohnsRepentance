About #free_will, #good_will and #angels

👉 Question፦ My friend asked me this question that he said no one was able to answer for him. He was wondering if angels have a perfect will, how is it that lucifer was able to rebel against God?

Answer፦ Beloved in Christ, thank you for reaching out with such a profound and thoughtful question that probes into the mystery of divine creation and free will.

In the wisdom of the Church, we understand that God, in His infinite goodness and love, created all beings with freedom, including the angels. This freedom reflects God's own freedom and is essential for love, for without freedom, there can be no true love. While angels are spiritual and holy beings, they were created with free will, not as automatons, but as beings capable of choice.

Lucifer, originally an angel of light, was endowed with this same free will and chose to turn away from God, driven by pride and a desire to be independent of his Creator. This mystery of iniquity is rooted in the misuse of the free will granted by God. Just as humans, angels were created good but had the potential to choose otherwise. Lucifer's choice was not a reflection of imperfection in God's creation but an exercise of the very freedom that is a prerequisite for the capacity to love and be in communion.

As we reflect on this, let us remember the words of the Holy Scripture: "Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil walks about like a roaring lion, seeking whom he may devour" (1 Peter 5:8). This reminds us that our spiritual journey is one of constant vigilance and prayer, seeking to align our will with God's will.

Now, dear one, as you ponder this mystery, how might you deepen your own commitment to align your will with the loving and perfect will of God?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
