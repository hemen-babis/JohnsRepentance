👉 Question፦ When we pray to ask God for something. Are we supposed to pray about it once and understand he knows what we are asking for and stop there. Or do we keep praying about it continuously? I know that people do subye and dedicate a certain time period where they pray about something. After that, are they supposed to keep praying about the same thing untill they get what they prayed for / understand Gods will about what they prayed about —-  or are we supposed  to stop after praying about it once / a certain period, knowing that we have already communicated it to God and instead focus on other prayers like praising God and etc? 

Thank you🙏🏾

Answer፦ Beloved in Christ,

Your query touches the heart of a profound spiritual practice: persistence in prayer. The Holy Scriptures and the teachings of our Church Fathers illuminate this path with clarity and depth.

Consider the words of our Lord Jesus Christ: "And I tell you, ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you" (Luke 11:9). Here, Christ encourages not a single act of asking but a continuous, persistent knocking. The verb forms in the original Greek text imply ongoing action—keep on asking, keep on seeking, keep on knocking.

In the Orthodox tradition, persistence in prayer is not a vain repetition nor a simple reiteration of our desires. It is a deepening dialogue with God, a means through which we align our will with His and immerse ourselves in His divine presence. Saint John Chrysostom, a beacon of our faith, elucidates this by teaching that prayer is the light of the soul, the true knowledge of God and of oneself.

When we dedicate a period to pray specifically for something, like in the practice of Subye, we are not merely informing God of our needs—as He is omniscient—but rather, we are shaping our own hearts, learning to trust in His timing, and deepening our communion with Him. After such a period, it is not that we should cease to pray for that matter, but we should maintain a balance: while continuing to place our trust and request before God, we should also cultivate a heart of gratitude, praise, and worship for His eternal goodness.

Therefore, while you persist in your petition, let your prayer life also blossom in thanksgiving and adoration, for every moment is an opportunity to encounter God anew. As you journey through this balanced path of prayer, ask yourself: How is my relationship with God deepened through both my petitions and my praise?

In Christ's love,

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever, today and always.
