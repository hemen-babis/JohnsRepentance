---Explanation about the #Cross

👉 Question: Can we wear any gold cross necklace that is sold at the shop work or does it need to be bought at the church? 

✍️Answer:- When purchasing a cross, it is preferable to buy it from an Orthodox Christian shop or from the church itself. It is important to be mindful that a cross purchased for the sake of fashion or marketing is devoid of spiritual meaning. After purchasing a cross or any other holy item, it is best practice to have it blessed by the priest before put in use.

It is also important to buy any holy goods, seals, and holy books from an Orthodox Christian shop or the church to show respect for these sacred items. One should avoid buying them from those who do not understand their significance.

When we wear a cross, we must also remember the suffering of Christ and remember that wearing it is not enough and that we should "carry the cross" and live a Christian life.
