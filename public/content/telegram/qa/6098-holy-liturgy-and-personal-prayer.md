Question : why don`t we recite other prayers in holy lituargy

👉🏾Answer : My child, the Divine Liturgy is not an ordinary time of prayer. It is the highest and most perfect prayer of the Church, handed down to us by the Apostles and sealed by the Holy Spirit. When we stand in the Holy Liturgy, we are not standing alone as individuals, but together with the Church on earth and the Church in heaven.

For this reason, we do not recite personal or separate prayers during the Holy Liturgy. To do so would be to divide the heart and the attention. 

The Liturgy itself is a complete prayer: thanksgiving, repentance, supplication, praise, and sacrifice. In it, the Body and Blood of our Lord Jesus Christ are offered and received. There is no prayer higher than this on earth.
The fathers teach us that mixing other prayers into the Liturgy is like lighting a candle under the full sun. When the sun is shining, the candle adds nothing. The Holy Liturgy is that sun. If we participate in it with fear of God, faith, and love, our souls receive life itself.

This does not mean that personal prayer is unimportant. On the contrary, prayer is as necessary to our life as food and water. But every prayer has its proper time and order. Personal prayers belong before or after the Liturgy, at home, in the night, or in times of need. During the Liturgy, our prayer must be one with the prayer of the Church.

If there is a serious and compelling reason that prevents a person from offering private prayer on a certain day, this does not mean that God abandons them because they did not pray. God is merciful and knows our weakness. Nevertheless, we must never become careless, for prayer is the breath of the soul and without it the soul grows weak.

Therefore, my child, when you stand in the Holy Liturgy, stand fully present. Listen, respond, bow, repent, and receive grace. Do not add to it, and do not take from it. What the Church gives you there is more than enough for life and salvation.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
