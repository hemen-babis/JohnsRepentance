👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾#Surrendering the #Heart to #God

👉🏾Question :- How does one submit their heart to God practically.

👉🏾Answer :-My beloved child in Christ,Your desire to submit your heart to God is precious, for it shows a soul yearning for holiness and closeness to our Lord. To submit one’s heart to God is not only a matter of words, but a life lived in obedience, humility, and love for Him. Let us consider practical ways to do this, according to the teachings of our Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church.

1. Prayer 

Prayer is the most direct way to submit your heart. It is both petition and surrender.Begin each day with prayer, Pray with attention, not just words.offering your thoughts, desires, and struggles to God. Speak to Him as a child speaks to a loving father, and listen in silence. Attending the Church’s daily services, such as the 'Kidase' and Mezmur, helps the heart remain centered on God.

2. Repentance and Confession
A heart submitted to God recognizes its weakness and sins. Regular confession to your spiritual father, combined with true repentance, opens your heart to God’s grace and mercy. Humility before God and His priest helps the soul grow in purity.

3. Obedience to God’s Commandments

Submission is shown in action. Keeping God’s commandments honoring parents, loving your neighbor, fasting, and observing Church laws demonstrates that your heart belongs to God, not to worldly desires.

4.Fasting 

The body and the heart are connected. Denying your desires strengthens your heart’s devotion.

Observe the Church’s fasts carefully, offering your sacrifice to God with prayer.

Do not fast only outwardly; the inner heart must also humble itself. Avoid judgment of others while fasting.

Every moment of self-control is a moment of the heart turning fully to God.

5. Reading and Meditating on the Holy Scriptures

Feed your heart with the Word of God daily. Meditate not only on the stories but on their meaning for your life. Let the words of the Psalms, Proverbs, and the Gospels shape your thoughts, speech, and actions.

6. Humility and Service
Serving others with a pure heart, without seeking praise, is one of the clearest ways to submit to God. When we place the needs of others above our own, we imitate Christ and surrender our pride.

7. Thanksgiving and Trust
Offer everything your joys, sorrows, and daily work to God with gratitude. Trust that His wisdom guides your life, even when circumstances are difficult.

Daily Practical Steps to Submit Your Heart

1. Begin each day with a prayer of surrender.

2. Examine your conscience morning and night.

3. Follow the Church’s fasting and prayer schedule faithfully.

4. Read a passage of Scripture or a saint’s life daily.

5. Perform at least one act of charity or forgiveness each day.

6. Speak kindly and truthfully in all circumstances.

7. End your day thanking God and entrusting your heart to Him

Dear child, remember that submitting your heart to God is a lifelong journey. Be patient with yourself, keep your eyes on Christ, and allow the Holy Spirit to gently guide you. Each small step taken with sincerity pleases God more than grand gestures without love.
