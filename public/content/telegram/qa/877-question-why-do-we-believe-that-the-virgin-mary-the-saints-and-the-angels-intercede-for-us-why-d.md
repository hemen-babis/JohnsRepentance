👉Question: Why do we believe that the Virgin #Mary, the #Saints, and the #angels #intercede for us? Why don't we just ask God straight? Does God tell us to ask him through them in the bible? God is merciful so he will listen to us when we ask him, so there is no need to ask the saints and angels. When we pray to saints and angels, we are not giving enough time to God. Jesus Christ is the one who died for us, therefore we should only ask Him and give all our prayer time to Him.

✍Answer: This is a frequently asked question. We can cite many verses about the intercession of the Virgin Mary and the saints. What we call Saints: Holy Virgin Mary, Holy Angels, Holy Prophets, Holy Apostles and others who have served God, whom God has chosen, who came to be chosen by God, the Church calls them Saints.
 
We say that the saints intercede, not because we came up with our own ideas or agreed with this common point of view. Infact, nowhere in the Bible do we find the word that says saints do not intercede. Maybe we will hear the non-believers say it. Our questioner, if you tell us there is, it would be good if you could cite the source and explain it to us. Who can belittle what God has honored? Is it God's will for the saints to intercede? We see that as follows:
 
Basically, it is God who told us that saints are intercessors. Accordingly, to do what God commanded us to do,   we Orthodox  trust in the intercession of the saints. When we say the intercession of the saints, it means 'beg', 'morning', 'intercede'. 'Amalaj' means the prayer and supplication that one makes for the other. It is to God  they present the prayer, and it is the saints who intercede for us. It is not an offense if one prays for the other as an intercessor, for it is God whom they beg to.
 
There are three elements that should be included in this task that we should never forget:-

The First one is: 'Intercessor'. Intercession is for creatures. If they say why don't we pray to Jesus Christ directly, it will be like saying that Jesus Christ is a creature. Holy men intercede for sinners, that sinners may be forgiven. For example, let's look at Moses the man of God, Torah Exodus chapter 32 verse 32 "And Moses returned unto the LORD, and said, Oh, these people have sinned a great sin, and have made them gods of gold. Yet now, if you want to forgive their sin --; and if not, blot me, I pray thee, out of thy book which thou hast written." Saying this, we can see that he begged to the point of sacrificing. When Israel was wronged and quarreled with God, this is the prayer that he said, forgive them, save them.
 
Lest people say this is from the old world, Saint Paul to the Romans chapter 9 verse 1-3 "I say the truth in Christ, I lie not, my conscience also bearing me witness in the Holy Ghost, That I have great heaviness and continual sorrow in my heart. For I could wish that myself were accursed from Christ for my brethren, my kinsmen according to the flesh." He says. He said, "If my brothers are saved, I will be cursed." this is love. The gospel says that you should love one another. In the light of the world, we see Saint Paul praying for his people, and we see God's prophet Moses praying for people who need to be saved. Therefore, they are saints when we call them intercessors.
 
The second, "Receiver of prayer means (temalaj) the recipient of intercessory prayers and supplications." In religious education, the destination of all prayers is God. There is only one God to whom intercession, prayer and supplication are offered. "When we say one God, we mean the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. They are three persons who are united in one lordship, one authority, one will, and one divine glory. They accept the requests of the creatures in heaven and on earth and do the work of their will and goodness. When the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit are the Trinity, their name in unity is "God." So there is an intercessor, there is an Receiver of prayer means (temalaj) . It means that intercessors are saints, and Receiver of prayer means (temalaj) is God.
