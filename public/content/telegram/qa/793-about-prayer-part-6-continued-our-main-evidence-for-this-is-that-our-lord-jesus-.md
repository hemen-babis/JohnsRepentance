---About Prayer (Part 6)

(Continued) 👆

Our main evidence for this is that our Lord Jesus Christ prayed with His eyes to the sky, according to the Evangelist John. (John 11:4)

1.6. When we pray, we should pray with absolute faith. We should pray with a full heart and full faith so that the prayers we offer will reach God first, and we will be able to get the good response we want from the Creator.

Our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ said in His Holy Gospel, "Whatever you ask in prayer, believe that you have received it, and it will be yours" (Mark 11:24).

Now our Lord Jesus Christ gave them this example that they should always pray without tiring: There was a judge in a city who did not fear God, and there was a widow who came to this judge and asked for judgment every day. It is recorded in the Gospel of Luke that because that widow or poor woman constantly argued with the judge, saying, "Grant me justice against my adversary," he finally accepted her request and gave her a verdict. (Luke 18:1-6)

1.7. Praying with a pure mind and a sincere spirit, free from resentment and revenge, allows us to see God by removing our evil deeds that are a barrier between us and God. Therefore, "the prayer of a spiteful man is like a seed that falls among thorns." It is recorded in the Book of Monks and other books.

1.8. You should gather your thoughts and pray with one heart. When the holy apostles and the first followers of the Lord (120 families) were praying in one place after the Lord's resurrection and ascension, before His death, our Lord Jesus Christ told them that He would send them the Holy Spirit to comfort them. According to the divine word that the Lord revealed to them, the Holy Bible tells us that they received the Holy Spirit while they were praying. (Acts 2:1-4)

1.9. During prayer, you should bow down. We bow when we start and finish the prayer. Also, when we reach a place or article in the word of God that talks about prostration, we should prostrate. (Judges 14) We bow down to the authorities and great people on this earth, let alone our Creator who is the heavenly King. This is because prayer is the line through which we can connect with the Creator through conscience, and we should pray while falling and standing up, saying that even though I am weak and sinful, accept my prayer in Your kindness and forgiveness.

1.10. We should pray for ourselves and others. We Orthodox Christians should pray not only for ourselves but also for our fellow citizens:

For those who are suffering in different ways: 

For those who are sick
For those living in prison
For those who live in the military to protect the peace of the country
For those who live in a foreign country
In general, we should pray for our people living in different challenges and for the country as well. When the prophet Jeremiah said that it is necessary to pray for the country, "But seek the welfare of the city where I have sent you into exile, and pray to the Lord on its behalf, for in its welfare you will find your welfare." (Jeremiah 29:7)

The Apostle Saint James also taught that we should pray for others, saying, "Therefore, confess your sins to one another and pray for one another, that you may be healed. The prayer of a righteous person has great power as it is working." (James 5:16) Moreover, God's word commands us to pray for all creatures, such as the animals, the plants, the flowing river

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
