Question፦ Hello I was wondering what are the steps a convert should take when still being new to the faith before and after baptism besides understanding the sacraments and the 5 pillars ?

Answer፦ Beloved in Christ, in the journey you embark upon towards and beyond the holy sacrament of baptism, let us recall the words of St. Paul to the Philippians: "Therefore, my beloved, as you have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your salvation with fear and trembling" (Philippians 2:12).

First and foremost, deepen your relationship with God through prayer. Daily conversation with the Lord opens your heart to His guidance and strengthens your faith. The Jesus Prayer ("Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner") is a precious gem in our tradition, embodying the essence of humility and repentance.

Immerse yourself in Holy Scripture and the writings of the Church Fathers. The Scriptures are the living Word of God, offering nourishment for your soul, while the Fathers provide wisdom that connects us to the early Church.

Attend the Divine Liturgy and other services regularly, as participation in the communal worship of the Church is vital for spiritual growth. This will also connect you with a community of believers who share your journey and can offer support and encouragement.

Seek a spiritual father who can guide you with wisdom and understanding. This mentorship is invaluable for navigating the challenges and questions that arise in your spiritual life.

Finally, involve yourself in the life of the Church through acts of charity and service. Our faith is lived not only through belief but through love expressed in action.

Beloved, what steps will you take today to draw closer to God and His Church, walking in the path of faith and repentance?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
