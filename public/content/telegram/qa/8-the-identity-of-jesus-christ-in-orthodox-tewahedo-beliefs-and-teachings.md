The identity of Jesus Christ in Orthodox Tewahedo beliefs and teachings

Beloved and respected, all our members who follow the orthodox education that we convey on John's Repentance website; Based on the topic, we have sent you the following basic lesson, so we entrust you all to read and learn from it.

Understanding the identity of Jesus Christ is the foundation of the Christian faith. Because "Christian" means a Christian, a disciple of Christ. Anyone who does not know Christ cannot be called a disciple of Christ. So who is Christ?

It was Jesus Christ himself who asked who the Christ was. He asked them the twelve psalms and the question and the answer are as follows.

Jesus came to the camp of Caesarea Philippi and his disciples. Who do people call the son of man? he asked them. But they said, some John the Baptist, others Elijah, others Jeremiah, or one of the prophets. Jesus said to them. Who do you say I am? And Simon Peter answered. You are the Christ, the Son of the living God. Jesus answered and said to him. O Simon, son of Jonah, blessed are you, for flesh and blood did not reveal this to you, but my Father in heaven. And I say unto thee, Thou art Peter. And upon this rock I will build my church, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it. And I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven. Whatever you bind on earth will be bound in heaven. Whatever you loose on earth will be loosed in heaven. (Matthew 16:13-19)

So, from this question and answer, we can understand that the people of the time when the Son of God called "Jesus" appeared in the flesh of Adam, more or less believed in Jesus as a prophet. It could be understood that he was not a prophet, apart from his work, even from the words he used to speak. When prophets teach, because they are sent from God and it is God who sent them, they say, "Thus saith God." But since Christ is the God who sent the prophets to tell you to speak like this, and the words he spoke were his own, he said, "But I tell you," "I tell you the truth, the truth." This is proof that he is not a prophet, but the God of prophets. In addition, he took three of his disciples (Peter, John, and James) and climbed Mount Tabor to show them that Jesus Christ is not a prophet, but the God of prophets. Among the prophets, he brought Moses from the grave and Elijah from the living. (Matthew 17:1-3) However, there are still many to whom the identity of Jesus has not been revealed.

So who is Jesus?

From the question and answer above, we clearly understand that Jesus is the son of man and the son of God. Because when Jesus asked about himself, he said, "Who do they call the Son of Man?" Saint Peter replied, "You are the son of the living God." Therefore, as Christ is the son of God, he is God. As a human being, he is human. The Orthodox Tewahedo Church is founded on the belief of St. Peter, the Only Son, so it teaches Christ as a perfect God and a perfect man. If the Son of Man is called a man, then the Son of God is God. If it is God, then it is God. For there is no god but God. He is the creator, for there is no creator other than God. He is a judge because there is no true judge other than God. Therefore, we believe that Jesus is: 1/ Perfect Man, Perfect God, 2/ Creator, 3/ God, 4/ God, 5/ Judge.
