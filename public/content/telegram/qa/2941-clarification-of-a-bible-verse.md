👉👉👉 Clarification of a #Bible #verse 

👉🏾 Question፦ Hello,

I have a quick question. In the bible it says, “If someone slaps you on one cheek, turn to them the other also. “ (Luke 6:29). 

Is this saying if someone is hurting you to keep letting them do that or what is the meaning of this verse?

Answer፦ Greetings in the peace of Christ,

Thank you for your thoughtful inquiry into the profound teaching of our Lord in Luke 6:29. This scripture counsels us not merely about physical retaliation but deeply emphasizes the virtue of patience and humility in the face of adversity or insult. In the Orthodox Christian perspective, this teaching encourages us to resist the instinct to retaliate with anger or vengeance, which can lead to greater sin. It is a call to embody Christ's own humility and patience, reflecting His divine love and forgiveness, even under provocation.

Saint Basil the Great, a revered father in the Orthodox tradition, elaborates on this concept by urging us to not repay evil with evil, but rather to overcome evil with good. By turning the other cheek, we are not consenting to injustice or harm, but rather choosing a path that leads to peace and holiness, avoiding the cycle of retribution.

In your daily encounters, how might you practice this Christ-like patience and humility, transforming potential conflicts into opportunities for grace and mercy?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
