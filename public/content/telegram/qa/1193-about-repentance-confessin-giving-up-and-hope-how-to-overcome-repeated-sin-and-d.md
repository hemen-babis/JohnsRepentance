About #repentance, #confessin, #giving #up and #hope 

How to overcome repeated sin and discouragement?

👉🏾Question: How does one overcome feelings of defeat and discouragement when falling into the same sin continually?
How to accept God's love and grace and not give up / lose hope with struggles that come with my journey with God? 
I tend to fall short of the goals I set for myself in spiritual life and the discouragement / feeling depressed about that holds me back from making progress. 

Thank you and May God bless your service! 

👉 Answer: This is a very important question because a lot of people confess their sins many times and go back to it many times. The main cause of this is not knowing what sin is and not understanding the depths of it's consequences. 

If a person confess their sins without understand what sin is, they will most likely fall back into it. If a person confesses their sins without understanding the benefits of repentance, it will be very hard for them to come back to repentance.

Hence we first need to learn about sin, it's consequences and the benefits of repentance. We need to learn from people that have fallen into sin, we need to know that sin separates us from God, sin deprives us from peace, sin brings curses to our lives, sin is the worst thing one can do. 

People who have fallen into sin have lost God, lost blessings, lost peace, lost life and can't even get alone with themselves. That is why Adam and Eve had a hard time living together after they fell into sin. Adam had asked Eve to get away from him, to depart form him accusing her to be the reason of his fall. People who have fallen into sin can't live with others nor be comfortable in their own skin. That is why Judas of Iscariot, who sold our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ with $30 thought that he was doing good for himself, that he would live but he couldn't. He threw away the money, hanged himself up on a tree and died after suffering for 40 days. Hence we need to understand that people trapped in sin can't live with God, with others nor with themselves. This, we have to believe before confessing. 

One needs to understand the depths of sin's consequences, and also understand the benefits of repentance. We need to learn from people who changed their lives and were healed through repentance. It is only then that we will have not repeatedly fall back to sin. 

We need to believe that everything is possible with God and nothing without Him. On Matthew 21:21, our Lord tells us "if you have faith and do not doubt, you will not only do what was done to the fig tree, but also if you say to this mountain, ‘Be removed and be cast into the sea,’ it will be done." Hence why do we give up? We are winners in Christ, we fall and we get back up but we should never give up, why do we give up? Are we not the ones He created in His own image? Are we not the ones whom He appointed as rulers over creatures? It is us that He has delivered from the bondage of the devil by becoming one of us, it is for us that He has given his Holy Blood and Holy Flesh. It is for us that is given the interception of the apostles, the prophets, the angels, the saints, the virgins, the monks, our Lady Virgin Mary and others. Hence how can we give up? It is for us that our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ established the mystery of Repentance so we can be reconciled with Him if we fall. Hence we do not give up. The holy water, the cross, the priests, the gospel, the prayer books, the advisors, the teachers, the fasts, prostration and countless more are ours. Hence how can we give up? We don't!
