👉👉👉 Question #8: How does one say when he repents if he goes out with ten women or men? Is it in detail or in general, only saying that he has committed adultery?

 Our questioner, although adultery is called adultery by its name, when it is defined by its work or sin, not only it is a very high type of sin, the number and level of the sin also varies.  For example, there is a difference in the sin between committing adultery with one person and committing adultery with many people. Therefore, if we are not ashamed to commit the sin, there is nothing to be ashamed of telling the priest that we have committed the sin of adultery with many people. It is important to list the types of adultery that we have committed for our confession father who we trust after God. This is because there is a canon that is given to us according to the amount and type of the sin of adultery that we have committed.
