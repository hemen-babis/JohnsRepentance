About #creation and #dinosaurs 

👉 Question፦ Hello, I was wondering what the church says about dinosaurs and tectonic plates in relation to the timeline of the earth/universe according to science and the events that occurred compared to biblical timeline of the universe. Did dinosaurs exist & if so, when did they actually exist if the world hasn't been existing for millions of years according to the bible. How long has the universe been existing? is it in thousands or in millions?  Someone asked me this questions & i am not sure how to respond. Thank you!

Answer፦ Dear seeker of truth,

Your inquiry touches upon the harmony between faith and science, a topic that has long intrigued both believers and scholars. The Orthodox Church, while deeply rooted in the spiritual understanding of creation as revealed in Scripture, does not oppose scientific discovery or the study of the natural world. In fact, the Church Fathers often encouraged the study of God's creation as a means to better understand and appreciate the Creator Himself.

Regarding dinosaurs and tectonic plates, the Church does not have an official position on scientific matters such as these. However, it is important to remember that the Bible, particularly the Book of Genesis, is not a scientific textbook but a theological and spiritual document. Its purpose is to convey the truth of God’s relationship with the world and humanity, rather than to provide a detailed account of the physical development of the universe.

Saint Basil the Great, in his "Hexaemeron," which is a series of homilies on the six days of creation, affirms that the creation of the world is an act of God, but he does not delve into specific scientific explanations. He writes, “What sort of day was it then? It was one without the succession of day and night, a day not measured by the sun, a day which had no need of any luminary.”

As for the age of the universe, the Church allows for a variety of understandings. While some may interpret the days of creation in Genesis as literal 24-hour periods, others view them as symbolic of longer epochs. The Church does not bind its faithful to a specific interpretation, as the essence of our faith lies in the Creator, not in the details of His creation.

Regarding the existence of dinosaurs, it is entirely possible, within the framework of Orthodox theology, to acknowledge their existence in the ancient past, as uncovered by scientific research. This does not conflict with our faith, as our understanding of God and His creation can encompass both the spiritual truths of the Bible and the discoveries of science.

In Psalm 104:24, it is said, “O Lord, how manifold are thy works! In wisdom hast thou made them all: the earth is full of thy riches.” This verse invites us to marvel at the vastness and complexity of God's creation, reminding us that our understanding is always limited compared to the infinite wisdom of God.

In your journey of faith and understanding, how might you find ways to harmonize the spiritual truths of our faith with the discoveries of the world God has created?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
