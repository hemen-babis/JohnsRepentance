About #fornication of eyes and thoughts

👉 Question፦ What is meant by fornication of thought and eyes?

Answer፦ The fornication of thought and eyes, my dear member, is a profound ailment of the soul, where one entertains impure thoughts and allows the gaze to fall upon things that inflame passions and lead one astray from the path of righteousness. In the Holy Scriptures, we are called to purity not only in deed but in heart and mind as well. Our Lord Jesus Christ said, “Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God” (Matthew 5:8).

This sin of the mind and eyes is a deviation from the sacred covenant we are to keep with our bodies and souls as temples of the Holy Spirit. The antidote to such temptation is vigilance in our thoughts and discipline in our sight. We are to "set no wicked thing before [our] eyes" (Psalm 101:3) and to take captive every thought to make it obedient to Christ (2 Corinthians 10:5).

To cultivate purity, one must immerse oneself in prayer, the reading of Holy Scripture, and the lives of the saints, whose examples inspire us to greater holiness. A spiritual father or guide, as provided through our ministry, is invaluable in this struggle, for they can help direct your gaze towards the “author and finisher of our faith” (Hebrews 12:2).

Remember, our battle is not against the flesh, but against “principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world” (Ephesians 6:12). Guarding our senses is not simply an act of personal piety but a warfare against these spiritual forces.

Now, dear soul, what step will you take to guard your heart and eyes, to draw nearer to the purity to which Christ calls you?

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.
