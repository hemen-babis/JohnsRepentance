Question፦ What is the difference in beliefs about Christ between #Athanasius and #Arius? Could you explain the term 'Tax Christ' and 'Common Christ' if they have any significance in Orthodox Christianity? If a person in a #Muslim #family converts to Christianity, what should be their approach in terms of living arrangements and family relationships?

Answer፦ In the grace and peace of our Lord Jesus Christ, I greet you, dear questioner, seeking to illuminate the path of faith for you.

The disagreement between Athanasius and Arius is fundamental in Christian theology, especially in the Orthodox tradition. Saint Athanasius, a staunch defender of Orthodoxy, upheld the belief in the full divinity of Christ. He asserted that Christ is of the same essence (homoousios) as the Father, co-eternal and co-equal. This is foundational to our understanding of the Holy Trinity. In contrast, Arius, a presbyter in Alexandria, argued that the Son was a creation of the Father, and therefore not co-eternal or of the same essence. This view, known as Arianism, was deemed heretical at the First Council of Nicaea in 325 AD.

As for the terms 'Tax Christ' and 'Common Christ', they are not recognized within the Orthodox Christian tradition. Our focus remains on the Christological teachings as affirmed by the Ecumenical Councils.

Regarding conversion from Islam to Christianity, this is a profound and delicate journey. In the Orthodox faith, we encourage love, respect, and patience in all family relationships. It is crucial to maintain peace and honor within the family while steadfastly adhering to one’s newfound faith in Christ. The approach to living arrangements and family relationships should be guided by prayer, the counsel of a spiritual father, and a heartfelt commitment to living a Christ-centered life.

As the Scripture teaches us, “God is love, and whoever abides in love abides in God, and God abides in him” (1 John 4:16). This love must be the cornerstone of all our relationships, especially within the family.

Now, beloved in Christ, how might you begin to approach these changes in your life with love and patience, while staying true to your faith in our Lord Jesus Christ?
