God Reveals in #Saints

(Part 6)

God has made His covenant with our forefathers like Abraham in terms of conduct and religion. 'I have made my covenant with my chosen one; And I will not profane my promise, nor change what comes out of my lips," says the Holy Word.

(Ps 88:3, 34): In the latter days, Christ Himself, the Savior of the world, said, "I am with you always until the end of the age (Matthew 28:20); He who receives you receives me" (Mathew 10:40), how wonderful is this promise that he gave to the saints? Remember that he said "heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not" (Matthew 24:35); "“Do not think that I came to destroy the Law or the Prophets. I did not come to destroy but to fulfill. For assuredly, I say to you, till heaven and earth pass away, one jot or one tittle will by no means pass from the law until all is fulfilled." (Matthew 5:17); "It is easier for heaven and earth to pass, than one tittle of the law to fail." (Luke 16:17) Are we to say to the true God who said this, You are not faithful to your word?
 
✍We know about God to the extent that He revealed Himself to the saints and our ancestors.
