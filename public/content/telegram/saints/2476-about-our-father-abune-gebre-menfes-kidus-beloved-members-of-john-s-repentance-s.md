About our father #Abune #Gebre_Menfes_Kidus

Beloved members of John's Repentance spiritual service, today was the annual feast of Abune Gebre Menfes Kidus where we commemorate his departure from the earth to the heavens. Hence we send you the following lesson so you can read and partake from the day's blessings. 

The life and teachings of St. Abune Gebre Menfes Kidus, a revered figure in Orthodox Christianity, offer a profound testament to the power of faith, asceticism, and divine intervention. His story begins in the land of Egypt, where his parents, Simeon and Aklesia, longed for a child for thirty years. In a miraculous occurrence, akin to the biblical stories of old, a divine voice promised them a son whose glory would surpass the heavens. This prophecy was fulfilled with the birth of Abune Gebre Menfes Kidus.

From his infancy, he exhibited signs of divine favor. His actions as a newborn, glorifying the Holy Trinity, and his angelic lifestyle, abstaining from earthly sustenance, marked him as a holy vessel. At the tender age of three, he was visited by Saint Gabriel, who brought blessings from the Virgin Mary and the celestial host. The Abbot Zemedeberhan, recognizing his sanctity, nurtured him in monastic life, where he flourished in spiritual wisdom and miraculous powers. His fame spread far and wide, leading to a pivotal moment when he healed a thousand blind men, a miracle reminiscent of Christ's own healings.

The spiritual journey of St. Abune Gebre Menfes Kidus was marked by profound solitude and communion with God. He was called to a life of seclusion in the forest, where his sustenance was of a heavenly nature, symbolic of man's reliance on divine grace rather than earthly provisions. His time in the wilderness was a testament to his unyielding faith and deep communion with God.

The saint's concern for humanity's salvation was paramount. His intercessions led to the liberation of souls from hell, showcasing his immense spiritual authority. His journey to Ethiopia, carried by Saint Gabriel, was a pivotal moment in his mission. There, he prayed fervently for the nation, an act of love and sacrifice, embodying the heart of a true shepherd.

St. Abune Gebre Menfes Kidus's life was not without trials. His encounter with Satan, who sought to hinder his mission, resulted in his temporary blindness. Yet, in this adversity, he was not forsaken; Saint Michael and Saint Gabriel came to his aid, a testimony to his favored status in the heavenly realms.

His ministry extended to Arabia, where he confronted idolatry and demonstrated the power of Christ through miraculous signs, converting many to Christianity. Even in the face of persecution, his faith remained unshaken, further attesting to his divine calling and steadfastness.

The life of St. Abune Gebre Menfes Kidus culminated in a glorious ascent, taken by angels to Jerusalem, where he was laid to rest. His earthly journey of 562 years, of which 262 was spent in Ethiopia, was marked by unwavering devotion, miraculous deeds, and a deep, transformative love for God and humanity.

This account of St. Abune Gebre Menfes Kidus, steeped in the traditions and teachings of Orthodox Christianity, serves as a beacon of spiritual guidance. His life exemplifies the virtues of faith, humility, and divine communion, offering a path to spiritual enlightenment and closeness to God. In his story, we find a profound reminder of the endless possibilities that faith in God can unlock, and the transformative power of living a life dedicated to divine service and love.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
