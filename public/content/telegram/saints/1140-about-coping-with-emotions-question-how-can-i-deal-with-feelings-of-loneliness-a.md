👉Question: How can I deal with feelings of loneliness and isolation and establish healthy relationships with others?

✍️ Answer፦ Beloved members of Joh’s repentance service, let us delve deeply into the profound topic of loneliness and isolation, and the path towards establishing healthy relationships with others. Understand, my dear ones, that loneliness in the context of our Orthodox faith transcends mere physical solitude. It encompasses a far-reaching spiritual state, where one has renounced the allure of worldly desires, material possessions, and all that displeases our Lord. However, it is vital to discern that this loneliness does not imply a lack of love for our fellow human beings or for our Creator. When we speak of loneliness, it is essential to distinguish it from the condition of one who rejects the ways of Christ and lives contrary to the teachings of our faith.

As devout Orthodox Christians, we firmly believe that we are never truly alone. We are an integral part of a vast and blessed spiritual community, encompassing the Holy Virgin Mary, the angelic hosts, the holy prophets, the noble apostles, the courageous martyrs, the pure virgins, the devoted monks, the reverent priests, and the diligent deacons. This holy assemblage, spanning both the earthly realm and the realm of eternity, stands united in communion with us and intercedes on our behalf. Let us always bear in mind that Christianity, is a faith rooted in unity—a faith that emphasizes the communion of saints over individualistic pursuits of personal salvation.

So, how do we confront the daunting specter of loneliness and isolation that oftentimes besets our hearts? How do we forge genuine and healthy relationships with others? The answer, my dear ones, lies in our steadfast commitment to unity—the bedrock of our faith. Christianity calls us to live in peace and harmony with all, recognizing that we are bound together as children of the Most High. True loneliness dissipates when we grasp the profound sense of belonging to a spiritual family that transcends physical boundaries and worldly limitations.
