✍1. What is religion?

For all those who ask what religion is, the first thing they should know is that 'religion' is the lens of conscience of all of us, in which we distinguish between creator and creature, righteousness and condemnation, light and darkness, life and death, heaven and hell. Since the creation of the human world; Religion is the true way to know and reach the invisible, intangible, abstract God (Creator).

Religion is a subtle way of believing and knowing God. Except through religion, we could not see it with our eyes, hear with our senses, think with our hearts, and listen with our senses. Therefore, religion is the secret by which we can accept the divine wisdom, which is distant and abstract to us, with faith that it will be done.In his message to the Hebrews about faith, the Apostle Saint Paul said, "Faith assures us of what we hope for; It explains what we do not see" and he said that 'religion' is a special grace by which we can see God and His subtle work that we cannot see with our eyes (Hebrews 11:1).

The foundation of religion is to accept your Creator. Acknowledging your Creator begins with believing in being a creature. Because there is no one who says that I was created by myself. If a person believes that he is created, then he believes that he has a creator. Then he begins to search for his creator. An example of this is Abraham, the great righteous man. Abraham was born in Karan, in what is now Turkey. His father was Tara and was a pot worshiper. It was not only worshiping, but it was also managed by carving and selling idols. Later, he realized that the material that was carved by his father's hand and carried to the market and sold could not be God and started searching for the creator. In the process of searching, he tried to worship rivers, winds, mountains and the sun. He affirmed that all these are creatures and not creators. In the end, he shouted, "O Creator, speak to me." At that time, God, the Creator of creation, appeared to him. He ordered him to leave Karan, where idols were worshipped, and go to the promised land of Canaan. (Genesis 12:1) From then on, Abraham believed God and lived faithfully to his word. This is to know that Abraham trusted God's word. Even today, what we mean by religion is believing in God and believing in God's commands like Abraham did.

2. What does Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo religion mean?

First of all, it is necessary to briefly understand the words 'ortho', 'dox', '/tewahdo', and 'religion', based on their mystical meaning and theological teaching style and style of expression.

"Ortho Dox" means; The word is Greek. It means "straight path (true religion)".

In the 4th century, ie in 325 AD. 318 scholars (Church Fathers) by making a religious assembly (Synod assembly) in the city of Nicaea; At that time, Arius, a man of disbelief, said, "The Creator; It is a creature”; He caused many to disbelieve with the doctrine of disbelief. Therefore; As the history of our church testifies; In this conference, scholars named it "orthodox (straight path) religion" in order to distinguish the true, direct unified religion from Arius' religion of denial and apostasy.

The Prophet Jeremiah, speaking about the true and straight path (religion), said, "Ask for the straight path that was before you, and walk in it, and you will find rest for your souls" (Jer 6:16).

Our Lord Jesus Christ said, "I am the way, the truth, the life" (John 14:6).
