About our Lord and Savior #Jesus #Christ, mistery of the #Trinity and mistery of #incarnation 

👉🏾 Question: I, as an Orthodox believe that Jesus Christ is God. There are also Bible verses that prove this. However, many have difficulty accepting that Jesus is God, and that he is one of the Holy Trinity, due to there still are other verses that say, "Hear my Father" when Jesus speaks. Therefore, how can we explain to those who do not believe in simple language? Becauseb this is difficult to understand it.

Answer: The question is related to theology and the mystery of the Trinity. He said not only 'my father', but 'he has begotten me'. To understand this, it is necessary to know about the mystical Trinity and the mystical incarnation. Previously, when we talked about the divinity of Jesus Christ, we talked extensively about the names of God. There are names by which the Lord was called in ancient times, that were unknown before His birth. And Jesus is his New Testament name. The names we call 'Jesus Christ', 'Jesus' are when he became flesh, became a perfect God (he was already God), a perfect Man; He is a Man and is God. He is God even after he dies, rises, and ascends. Therefore, when he becomes a man, what we speak about him, what He too speaks is that he speaks as if there is another father, another superior authority, to mean for the united body; The flesh that was a creature is saying that I have become God. (because he  was God before He united the flesh;   the flesh became absolute God when   Divinity united it; and He became absolute Man, absolute God) Jesus is the son of man and the son of God.

Our Lord Jesus is one of the Trinity Body, who was and is Glorified, praised equally together with the Almighty father & Holy spirit, before the creation of the world and forever. so, God the Son is God in a different form, he is the creator, he is almighty, he is pure and he is holy. When he was born from the virgin Mary, there is a body that arose from her, and he chose her as a pure saint for motherhood, starting from the womb, so that the sin of Adam would not be passed on to her. This is the teaching of our church, and this is what our holy fathers have revealed to them and said about her in all their books and common teachings.

In the holy book, when St. Gabriel announces her purity, virginity and is different from all women; he does not describe her femininity in the language used by other people. For example, a girl is called a 'woman' after losing her virginity; but  our Lady Virgin Mary is a virgin forever. She is a virgin before her conception, during her conception, and after her conception. She is a virgin before, during, and after giving birth.

Therefore, the flesh united from this mother is perfect God. What happened to the flesh that was the creature? He became a god. This is why:

1.  The expression "my father" refers to the birth of the pre-world without a mother. This is an inscrutable birth, it is a subtle birth, it is a birth beyond our imagination. Even we, let alone him, talk normally about the secret of reproduction from our mother and father,  but this even itself is abstract. Doctors confirm that a fetus lives in the mother's womb for nine months and five days before birth, and we know this from experience. But the secret of being born as a human being after this water and blood, as a boy or a girl, when he stayed in the mother's womb for 9 months and 5 days, what kind of food he was being fed, what kind of care, what kind of air he was getting, how he was born as a human being is amazing. After that, when we see how he comes from the darkness to the light world, from non-existence to existence, from the narrow to the wide world, this birth is a mystery. Hence, let alone the birth of God and the birth of man is even a subtle mystery.
