---Question፦ When I #pray, my thoughts are scattered, how should I pray for my #prayer to be heard?

Answer፦ Behoved in Christ, let us delve into the profound and mystical realm of prayer, a sacred dialogue with the Divine. Prayer, in its truest essence, is not a mere utterance of words but a celestial journey of the soul, transcending the mundane to commune with the Almighty.

1. The Essence of Prayer: Prayer is the divine art through which the earthly being ascends to their heavenly nature. It is akin to a reverent conversation with God. Just as a King governs with justice, so must our prayers be rooted in humility, sincerity, and unwavering faith. As we stand before the Most High, our hearts, not just our lips, must speak.

2. Unity of Heart and Mind: True prayer emanates from the heart, with the mind and soul in harmony. When our thoughts scatter like leaves in the wind, our prayers lose their essence. Gather your thoughts, center your heart, and rise above earthly distractions to embrace the celestial embrace of the Father.

3. Simplicity and Purity: As the Gospel of Matthew (6:7-8) teaches, our prayers need not be adorned with grandeur or verbosity. A simple, contrite heart is the altar upon which true prayer is offered. God heeds not the elegance of words but the purity of spirit.

4. Prayerful Wisdom: Approach the throne of grace with a humble heart and a spirit contrite. Seek not the fleeting glories of this world but the eternal virtues of the next. Let your speech reflect goodness, shun the company of the wicked, and align not with the adversary, for our battle is spiritual.

5. The Mystery of Prayer: True prayer aligns with the mystery of the New Covenant, guided by the Fatherhood of God and enlivened by the Spirit of the Son. As believers, we gaze eastward, not to a distant deity, but to an imminent, loving Father, offering worship in awe and reverence.

6. The Purpose of Prayer: Many pray for fleeting desires, yet such prayers often remain unheard. Remember, the wisdom of the Lord surpasses our understanding. Let us submit to His will, grateful for His providence, and never lose hope. In everything, give thanks, for God's plans are perfect.

7. The Heart of Prayer: He who harbors malice cannot truly pray. "Forgive, and you will be forgiven," commands the Lord. Let us emulate His mercy, forgiving others as He forgives us, thus making our prayers a pleasing fragrance to the heavens.

8. Perseverance in Prayer: Sometimes, God's silence is a call for heartfelt prayer. Let us cast aside vain repetitions and pray earnestly. Understand that the Lord's timing is impeccable, designed for our ultimate good. Do not despair but persevere in faith.

9. Constancy in Prayer: Continual prayer is the mark of a soul aflame with divine love. The saints, consumed by this love, ceaselessly commune with God. Their prayers, like breath, are inseparable from their being, a testament to their unquenchable love for the Almighty.

10. The Cross in Prayer: As we commence our prayers, let us envelop ourselves in the sanctity of the Cross. "Believe, and you shall receive," promised our Lord. Faith is the key that unlocks the treasures of heaven.

11. The Discipline of Prayer: A Christian is called to pray seven times a day, each moment a step closer to the divine. While circumstances may constrain us, let the morning and evening be times of sacred communion with God.

12. Repentance and Renewal: True prayer is born of a repentant heart. Tears of contrition pave the way back to God. St. John the Baptist proclaimed tears as both the parent and offspring of prayer, a profound truth revealing the depth of God's love for humanity.

In these teachings, let us find the path to a prayerful life, one that ascends beyond the temporal, reaching for the divine embrace of our loving Father. May our prayers be a sweet incense, pleasing to the Lord, and may He, in His infinite mercy, hear and bless us. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.
