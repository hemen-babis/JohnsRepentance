About #control of #mind and #throughts 

How to stop recurring bad thoughts in the mind permanently?

👉Question: I know that when bad thoughts come to our mind, we can choose not to accept them and not think about them, but is it possible to control our thoughts so that we don't have disturbing or bad thoughts that come to our mind in the first place? How can we permanently stop the bad thoughts that are repeatedly formed in our mind?

✍Answer:- The main reason for us to commit sin, either internally generated from ourselves or under the pressure of external forces, is the absence of righteousness or the deficiency of our inner spirituality. External sources of sin are trained against us when our intimate relationship with our God begins to falter.

Saint Paul, the light of the world, the teacher of the Gentiles, in his message to Ephesus, "Put on the whole armor of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil. For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places. Wherefore take unto you the whole armor of God, that ye may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand. Stand therefore, having your loins girt with truth, and having on the breastplate of righteousness; And your feet shod with the preparation of the gospel of peace; Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked. And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God: Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit, and watching thereunto with all perseverance and supplication for all saints; And for me, that utterance may be given unto me, that I may open my mouth boldly, to make known the mystery of the gospel," he told us (Ephesians 6:11-19)

Therefore, if we listen to God's word and obey God's commands by living in religion and morals, we can control our emotions, coordinate our minds with our free will, and destroy evil thoughts immediately. The one who has received the title 'heart of God', Saint David, in his psalms says, "You will be righteous with the righteous, you will be pure with the pure, you will be upright with the upright, and you will be crooked with the crooked" (Ps 17:25-27).

What makes man human and separates him from animals is not only his knowledge but also his free will. In other words, our soul has the freedom to be whatever it wants. In general, if freedom, reason, truth, knowledge and good will (choice) are properly integrated, our life can continue spiritually as our God wants. If we do this, our interests will be good. If we do not do this, we will easily fall into the trap of sin. The damage a sinner suffers from his sin is worse than the person on whom he committed the sin or the evil counselor who encouraged him to commit the sin.

Therefore, when we are still thinking about sin, we should return to the good free will as soon as possible, thinking about its final result and the damage it will cause. What helps us for this is to hate and abhor sin from its root, and when we oppose it, it should be with emotion, will, knowledge, and with all our strength. This is possible by thinking of the words, "Love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your strength." If this is the case, because the hatred will be faithful and perfect, then it will be difficult for us to even think about sin, and the thought will not come easily to us. Even if we think about it, the good spirit within us opposes it. Therefore, righteousness and sin can be seen and live together, fighting until one is built up and the other is completely defeated. But sin and righteousness do not continue to live together. In addition, if we regularly wash away sin with repentance, and practice planting good things in our minds little by little, the thought of sin will be replaced by righteousness.
