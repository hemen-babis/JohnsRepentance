Continued 👆🏽

A Day to Be Remembered

Therefore, this Friday, the first after the Resurrection, is not to be overlooked. For she is crowned with the glory of all that the Church possesses:

The Cross

The Resurrection

The Spirit

The Sacraments

The Preaching of the Apostles

The Teaching of the Truth

The Miraculous Power given to the Saints

And the Church herself, standing radiant as the Bride of the Lamb, clothed in white, born from Calvary.

Let us honor this day with reverence. Let us approach it not as another passing Friday, but as a living memorial of the Church’s birth and destiny.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
