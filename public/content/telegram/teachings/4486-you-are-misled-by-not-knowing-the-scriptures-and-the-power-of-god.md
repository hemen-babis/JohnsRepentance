👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾You Are #Misled by Not Knowing the #Scriptures and the Power of God:
(#Matthew 22:29 and the True #Knowledge of #Salvation)


Beloved brothers and sisters in Christ, those who drink from the living springs of the Holy Orthodox faith and walk in the light of the Church that is One, Holy, Apostolic, and true grace and peace be multiplied unto you. From the treasury of sacred teaching handed down by the apostles, guarded by the Fathers, and faithfully echoed by the voice of repentance, we set before you a flame to rekindle the mind and awaken the soul.

Today, we meditate upon the sacred words of our Lord, spoken to those who thought themselves wise but were in fact blind to truth. Our Lord Jesus Christ, when confronted by the Sadducees those who denied the resurrection of the dead and twisted the Scriptures to fit their own hollow reasoning rebuked them, saying:

“You are misled, not knowing the Scriptures nor the power of God.”(Matthew 22:29)

Oh, what thunder lies in these words! What warning for every generation! They are addressed not only to ancient deniers of the resurrection, but to all who fail to understand the true meaning of the Holy Scriptures and the immeasurable might of the living God.

The Mystery of Scripture and the Glory of Our Lady

He who does not know the Scriptures is easily misled. And not knowing the Scriptures means more than mere ignorance of words it is blindness to the mystery that lies within them. The sacred text is not a book of knowledge to be dissected, but a garment woven with divine wisdom, revealing God’s plan for the salvation of man.

Among the loftiest of these mysteries is the glory of the Virgin Mary, the Theotokos She who bore God in the flesh. The Scriptures, when rightly understood, reveal her not as a secondary character in the drama of redemption, but as its sacred gate. The Church, in its wisdom, calls her:

The Mother of God

The Faithful One

The Support of Our Nature


What does this mean? It is no poetic flourish. It is theology doctrine of salvation revealed by God. Those who know the Scriptures understand that these titles declare the power of God working through His handmaid, the Virgin. They do not diminish the glory of Christ; they manifest it more perfectly.

The Obedience That Heals the Fall

In Eve, disobedience. In Mary, obedience.
In Eve, the door to death was opened. In Mary, the path to life was restored.

Those who ignore this truth who belittle the reason for our salvation expose themselves as strangers to the mystery of Scripture. They can easily comprehend how death entered through Eve. But they are blind to how life entered through the Virgin. They are, as the Lord said, misleading, because they do not know the Scriptures, nor the power of God.

The Orthodox Church, preserved from distortion by the grace of the Holy Spirit, constantly teaches this mystery. In every hymn, in every prayer, she raises the name of Mary not as an idol, but as the vessel through which our Lord took flesh, the gateway through which salvation reentered the world. She is remembered as the foundation of our character, the one through whom the broken image of man was lifted once more toward its original glory.
