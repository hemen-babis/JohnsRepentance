However, some individuals get involved in matters that do not pertain to them or become concerned about an issue where they lack a complete understanding of the cause and consequences. This results in an emotional pain that resembles a physical ailment, where the individual is suffering without being able to express their distress without causing harm.

This intangible and invisible emotional pain cannot be treated by conventional medicine and is difficult to diagnose. Thus, it requires a spiritual solution to address this spiritual problem.

If we do not guide someone who is trapped in anxiety and temptation to the path of peace, even if we offer advice and quote biblical verses, they may not hear it. This is because once they are caught in the problem, it becomes difficult to escape, but if they had the proper understanding from the start, they would have been able to avoid it. For a person in depression, it may seem like there is no solution, but our Lord offers comfort and hope, "Come to me, all of you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls. For my yoke is easy and my burden is light" (Matthew 11:28).
