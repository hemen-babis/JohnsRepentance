👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾What does a #cloud #represent?”

(Part 3)

Beloved children of the covenant, heirs of the promise, and followers of the Orthodox faith, grace and peace be multiplied to you from God the Father and from our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. Today we set before our hearts a sacred mystery: What is the symbol of the cloud? The Scriptures, the Fathers, and the holy tradition all testify to its meaning, showing us that the cloud is not a mere vapor of the sky but a living sign of God’s mercy, His covenant, and His dwelling among His people.

1. The Cloud of Noah – The Rainbow of the Covenant

After the great flood, when the waters had subsided and the ark came to rest, Noah, the righteous man, offered a sacrifice of thanksgiving. God, moved with mercy, blessed Noah and his sons, commanding them: “Be fruitful and multiply and fill the earth” (Gen. 9:1). Yet Noah, full of sorrow, spoke: “Lord, my children will sin again, and will You not in Your justice destroy them once more?”

Then the Lord, who is good and merciful, established a covenant of peace. He said: “I will set My bow in the cloud, and it shall be for a sign of the covenant between Me and the earth. Never again shall the waters become a flood to destroy all flesh” (Gen. 9:12–15). Thus, the rainbow appeared in the heavens as the seal of God’s promise.

The rainbow is not only a reminder that God will not flood the earth again it is also a symbol of Christ and His Mother, the Holy Virgin Mary. Just as the rainbow shines with four colors, for it is four-faced, symbolizing the flesh of man made from the four elements, and prefiguring the fullness of Christ revealed through the four holy Gospels. And as the rainbow rests in the cloud, so too the covenant of God rests in His chosen vessel, the Holy Virgin Mary.

Our Lord Jesus, while hanging on the cross, entrusted her to us, saying to the beloved disciple: “Behold your mother” (John 19:27). She is, therefore, the Mother of the Covenant. Just as the rainbow stood as a sign that the flood of wrath would not return, so the Virgin is the sign that salvation has come into the world through her Son.

The holy scholar Abba Giorgis, in his book Enzira Sibhat, praises her: “You are the rainbow of the covenant, the sign that the rain of wrath will no longer fall upon the earth.” Likewise, Abba Tsige and Abba Gebre Mariam in Mahlete Tsige proclaim: “The rainbow of Noah’s covenant is fulfilled in you, O Virgin, for as the heavens are adorned with stars, so the earth is adorned with flowers and fruits through you.” Thus, the cloud that bore the rainbow becomes a symbol of Mary, who bore the Sun of Righteousness, Christ Himself.

2. The Cloud of Moses – The Shelter of Mercy

Let us now look to the days of Moses, the great lawgiver. He led Israel out of bondage in Egypt into the wilderness. Yet the people hardened their hearts, grumbling and rebelling, so that the journey of forty days stretched into forty years. Among the rebels were Korah, Dathan, and Abiram. The earth opened and swallowed them, and judgment fell upon their households. Fear and confusion seized the people, and they rose against Moses and Aaron, crying out to stone them.

But what says the Scripture?

Scripture says: “Moses and Aaron went into the tabernacle of the congregation, and the cloud covered it, and the glory of the Lord appeared” (Num. 16:42). The cloud descended, wrapping them in God’s protection and saving them from destruction.

This cloud, too, is a symbol of the Holy Virgin Mary. Just as the cloud shielded Moses and Aaron, so the Virgin protects all who call upon her with faith. She is a refuge and a covering for those who trust in her, guarding them from the attacks of the enemy and interceding before her Son on their behalf.
