👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾(#Anqetse #Bitsuan) #Blessed are the #poor in #spirit, for theirs is the #kingdom of #heaven(Matthew 5:3; Luke 6:20)

(Part 3)

Dear beloved in Christ, peace and blessings to you all.Today, through the mercy of our Lord, we continue our reflection on the divine saying:“Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven” (Matthew 5:3; Luke 6:20).

✍🏾God Exalts the Humble but Humbles the Proud

The Apostle James wrote, “God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble” (James 4:6), teaching us that pride is hateful before God, but humility is loved and honored by Him.

Our Lady, the Holy Virgin Mary, also said in her prayer, “He has shown strength with His arm; He has scattered the proud in the imagination of their hearts; He has brought down rulers from their thrones and exalted the lowly” (Luke 1:51–52). This shows that God humbles the proud in their hearts but raises the humble to honor.

✍🏾Christ’s Teaching on True Greatness

Our Lord said to His disciples:
“The scribes and Pharisees do all their works to be seen by men. They love the places of honor at feasts, the best seats in the synagogues, and to be called ‘Rabbi’ in the marketplaces. But you are not to be called ‘Rabbi,’ for One is your Teacher, and you are all brothers. Do not call anyone on earth your father, for One is your Father, who is in heaven. The greatest among you shall be your servant. For whoever exalts himself will be humbled, and whoever humbles himself will be exalted.”
(Matthew 23:5–12)

Christ teaches us that whoever lifts himself up will surely be brought low, but whoever humbles himself will be raised by God.

✍🏾The Testimony of King David

The Prophet David said, “You save the humble people, but You bring down the haughty eyes” (Psalm 18:27). He also wrote, “The Lord guides the humble in justice and teaches the humble His way” (Psalm 25:9).

David continued to glorify humility, saying:

“The humble shall inherit the earth and shall delight themselves in the abundance of peace” (Psalm 37:11).

“The Lord lifts up the humble; He casts the wicked to the ground” (Psalm 147:6).

“The Lord raises those who are bowed down; He loves the righteous” (Psalm 146:8).

“The Lord takes pleasure in His people; He will beautify the humble with salvation” (Psalm 149:4).

“Though the Lord is high, He regards the lowly, but the proud He knows from afar” (Psalm 138:6).

✍🏾The Voice of the Prophets and the Wise

The Prophet Isaiah declared, “The loftiness of man shall be bowed down, and the haughtiness of men shall be brought low; and the Lord alone shall be exalted in that day” (Isaiah 2:17).

King Nebuchadnezzar, after his pride was broken, confessed, “Now I, Nebuchadnezzar, praise and exalt the King of heaven, for all His works are truth, and His ways are justice; and those who walk in pride He is able to humble” (Daniel 4:37).
