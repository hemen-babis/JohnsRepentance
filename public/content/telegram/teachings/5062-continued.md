3. Midday Prayer (Noon) – When the sun burns hot, our bodies grow weary, our minds are scattered, and the demons press upon us. For this reason, we strengthen ourselves and cry to God (Psalm 54:17).

At this hour Adam ate of the forbidden tree and fell from glory (Gen. 3:6).

At this hour Enoch built the altar to the Lord (cf. Gen. 5:22).

Above all, it was at this hour that our Lord Jesus Christ was nailed to the Cross for our salvation (John 19:14).

Therefore, we pray that we may not fall into temptation but be strengthened by the Crucified One.
(and the remaining hours continue likewise, each filled with meaning and mystery Midafternoon, Evening, Sunset, and Midnight prayers all reminding us of God’s mighty works in creation and salvation.)

Conclusion

Beloved, prayer is the breath of the soul. If the righteous of old kept vigil and offered their hearts to God seven times daily, how much more should we, who have received the fullness of grace through Christ, be steadfast in prayer. Let us not leave our homes barren of prayer, but make them little churches filled with incense rising to heaven.
