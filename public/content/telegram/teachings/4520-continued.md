5. The Gibeonite Deception: Mercy in the Midst of Error

The Gibeonites came deceitfully, wearing torn garments and bearing moldy bread, pretending to be from a far land. Israel, failing to inquire of the Lord, made a covenant with them. When the truth came out, Joshua rebuked them  but still kept the oath.

Why? Because even in error, God's servant upholds integrity. Joshua declared: "Now you shall be servants in the house of the Lord." Thus, the deceit of men became a cause for their redemption, and mercy triumphed over judgment. Even in missteps, the Lord brings forth purpose, when His people walk in righteousness.

6. Victory by Divine Power: The Sun Stands Still

When the five Amorite kings rose against Gibeon, Joshua did not abandon them. He ascended with his warriors, and the Lord assured him: "Fear them not, for I have delivered them into thine hand." And as Israel fought, God Himself hurled down hailstones from the heavens, slaying more with His hand than the sword could strike.

But behold the wonder of wonders: when Joshua cried, "Sun, stand still upon Gibeon!" the heavens obeyed. The sun and moon stood frozen in their course, and Israel triumphed over her enemies. Nature itself submitted to the voice of the righteous. O what a lesson to us! The prayer of a faithful servant stills time and bends the cosmos, for the Creator hearkens to His obedient ones.

7. The Final Years and Eternal Legacy

Joshua continued faithfully, dividing the land, building seven cities of refuge for the innocent  sanctuaries of mercy in the midst of justice. And at the age of 120, like Moses before him, he gathered the tribes and charged them:

“Fear the Lord, keep His commandments. Worship not idols, for He alone is your Creator. If you obey Him, blessing shall be upon you. But if you forsake Him, you shall be destroyed.”

Such was his final sermon  not soft words of comfort, but a call to holiness and covenant fidelity. He rested peacefully and was buried in Shechem, the land he had purchased, a symbol of the promise fulfilled.

Conclusion: The Prophet as Pattern of Christ

Beloved, in the life of Joshua we see a foreshadowing of our Lord Jesus Christ:

Joshua brought the people into the Promised Land; Christ brings us into the Kingdom of Heaven.

Joshua broke down walls; Christ breaks the wall between God and man.

Joshua made the sun stand still; Christ made the sun of righteousness rise upon a dark world.

Joshua built cities of refuge; Christ Himself is our Refuge.

Let us walk in the spirit of Joshua: obedience, faith, courage, and trust in God's promises. Let us tear down every Jericho in our hearts, uphold every covenant we make, and march forward under the banner of Christ.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
