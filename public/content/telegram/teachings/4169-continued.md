6. The House of Bethany, a Living Temple

Let us not overlook the holy house that received Christ, not only in person but in sacrament. It was in the house of Lazarus that our Lord reclined before His Passion. It was among these pure-hearted siblings that He entrusted His Body and Blood, His Flesh and Spirit, to the apostles on the night of Maundy Thursday. O how sacred is that dwelling! A house not made with hands, but sanctified by humility and love.

Conclusion: Lessons for the Faithful

What then shall we learn, O brothers and sisters? That the Lord is not late, though He seems to delay. That death is not the end, though it wounds us deeply. That to be loved by God is to walk the path of faith, virginity, humility, and service. That to serve Christ in your home is to turn your dwelling into a temple.

Let the story of Lazarus be written upon your hearts:
A life once lost, restored by love.
A home once filled with sorrow, turned into a sanctuary.
A man once bound by death, sent forth to proclaim life.

Let us cry out with trembling and joy, “Glory to You, O Lord, who raises the dead! Glory to You, who weeps with us! Glory to You, who calls us forth!”

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
