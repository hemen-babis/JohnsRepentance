👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾 About the birth of our Lady, the Virgin #Mary. 

Dear esteemed members of the John’s Repentance telegram channel, we are pleased to send you this lesson in celebration of the anniversary of our Lady's birth, which is celebrated on May 9th.

The conception of the Holy Virgin Mary took place fifteen years before the Year of Mercy, in the year 5485 on the eve of the Year of Mercy. As the time for God to forgive His people drew near, the dove of peace appeared on earth to herald the end of destruction. Our Lady is descended from priests on her mother's side and kings on her father's side. Anyone who claims to know Christ but is ignorant of the Virgin Mary's identity, glory, holiness, and her role in the salvation of Adam's race is mistaken.

Some people wrongly claim that the Bible doesn't provide much information about the Virgin Mary. But what needs to be said? It was prophesied in advance that she would give birth to the Lord God, who made heaven, the throne, the earth, the footstool, the sanctity that is the money of nature, and all the creatures that were found by him in the past, and the infinite things that were found in him, the subtle and the giant that was held in the palm of his hand and appeared in front of his eyes. Later, the angel informed her that the time had come. And she gave birth to him, just as she was told. What could be more amazing than saying that she gave birth to the creator, nursed the milk of the all-giver, contained everything, carried Him in her womb, and carried Him on her back?

Our Lady, the Holy Virgin Mary, is the purest of all people, the city of God. Her sanctity is unmatched.

When we say,  “Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb!" we are acknowledging God's plan to save humankind through a human being who was a pure seed passed down through the loins of the fathers. Through this plan, we were saved from destruction. (Luke 1:42) "Unless the Lord of hosts Had left to us a very small remnant, We would have become like Sodom, We would have been made like Gomorrah." (Isaiah 1:9)

Therefore, Our Lady is a saint who has never been touched by the sin of Adam and Eve that was passed down to generations. As it is written in the Book of Negere Mariam(ነገረ ማርያም), the ancestors of Our Lady on her mother's side, Hana, were named Petrika Tekta(ጴጥሪቃ ቴክታ
), and both of them were pious and God-fearing people who trusted in their God with all their hearts without doubt. They were very rich at that time. 

One day, Petrikah's friend went into the archives and saw the amount of wealth and said to his wife, Tekta, "Even the money we collected would have been saved for our grandchildren. You and I are barren and we have no children to inherit." She thought that he meant that he wanted to have a child, so she said to him in sadness, "If the Lord Israel does not give you a child from me, he will give you someone else, marry someone else and have a child." He said, "The God of Israel knows that I will not do this, and that I will not think it in my heart."
