8. The Teachings of Master Kifle Wolde Tsadeq: “Tsige Zik”

✍🏾As recorded in Tsige Zik (pp. 1–75) by Master Kifle Wolde Tsadeq, the Tsige season commemorates not only the Virgin and her Son but also the angels, righteous ones, and martyrs.

✍🏾During this time, the Church celebrates:

👉🏾The Holy Trinity and Emmanuel

👉🏾The four beasts of Revelation

👉🏾Archangel Raphael

👉🏾John the Baptist, Stephen, and the Evangelists Matthew and Mark

👉🏾James and John, sons of Zebedee

👉🏾Thomas Zehndeke, Anna, Abune Aregawi, Kirkos, Abba Eustathius

👉🏾Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and many other saints

This harmony of remembrance shows that holiness, like flowers, does not bloom alone it grows in communion.

9. The Spiritual Lesson of Tsige

✍🏾In general, every Sunday during this period, a great celebration is held in all Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo churches, with the faithful participating in the Holy Liturgy . The forty-day celebration of the Feast of Wereha Tsige is dedicated to the Holy Virgin Mary and our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.

✍🏾A special service is conducted to commemorate the suffering, thirst, and persecution endured by the Holy Virgin Mary when she was taken to Egypt with her Son, Salome, and Joseph. The service also celebrates her return from Egypt to her homeland, Nazareth.

✍🏾During this period, the Sunday hymns, and liturgy, as well as the hymns of the Holy Spirit, are all centered on the Feast of zemene tsige, reminding the faithful of the trials, devotion, and divine protection surrounding the Holy Family.

Conclusion 

✍🏾Fasting without humility is an empty shadow. The true fast is not of the lips or stomach alone, but of the heart. The believer must fast with compassion, give alms to the poor, restrain anger, forgive offenses, and keep the tongue from deceit. Let fasting be united with mercy, prayer, and repentance, that it may ascend as sweet incense before the throne of God.

As the fathers teach: “When you fast, do not fast for vain praise, but that your fasting may be a fasting of mercy, wherein you find help, blessing, and the grace of God.”
