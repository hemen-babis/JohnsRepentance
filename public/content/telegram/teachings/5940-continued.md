✍🏾The Parable of the Two Sons: Doing the Will of the FatherOur Lord taught obedience through this parable:

👉🏾“A man had two sons. He came to the first and said, ‘Son, go and work in my vineyard today.’ He answered, ‘I will not,’ but afterward he repented and went. Then he came to the second and said the same. He answered, ‘Yes, Lord,’ but he did not go. Which of the two did the will of his father?”
(Matthew 21:28–31).

Jesus then said:
“Truly I say to you, the tax collectors and the prostitutes are entering the kingdom of God before you”(Matthew 21:31).

Through this parable, Our Lord calls us not to do our own will, but the will of our Father in heaven. Therefore, if you know this and practice obedience in love, you are truly blessed. And if you know it and live by it, you are worthy of honor before God.
