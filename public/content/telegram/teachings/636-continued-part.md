(Continued part)👆👇

Therefore, we believe that God's word is hope. It is proper to go to church regularly, listen to the word and read the scriptures, know the gospel, understand the faith and practice it. As we have seen above, our righteous fathers who understood the word, obeyed the law and the commandments, not only had hope, but they are also the hope of salvation for others. We beseech them to think of us in their prayers, and to beg for God's goodness and mercy through their intercession.

Besides, it is very important for us to follow the example of our fathers in our lives and beliefs, as the main reason why their stories are written for us is so that we can learn from them and imitate them in our religion. It is good for us to see and learn how our fathers lived their lives. Next, there is something that God commanded us, Matthew chapter 10 verse 16 "Be wise as serpents and gentle as doves." Therefore, an orthodox Christian who is united by doing these things means that even if he faces obstacles, he will overcome them with his gentleness and wisdom.
 
May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
