Conclusion

The cloud in Scripture is no ordinary vapor of the sky. It is the veil of divine majesty, the sign of God’s presence, the dwelling of His glory, the symbol of His saints, and the most perfect image of the Virgin Mother of God. Let us, therefore, look to the cloud not with earthly eyes, but with the eyes of faith, seeing in it the mercy, protection, and glory of the Lord who comes to save His people.
