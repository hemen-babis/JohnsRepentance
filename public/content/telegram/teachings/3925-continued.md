The Path to Salvation through the Covenant of Mercy

Come now, faithful ones, and behold the great gift bestowed upon us! Shall we neglect so great a salvation? Shall we turn our hearts from such mercy? The Covenant of Mercy is not an excuse for iniquity but a call to righteousness. Without good works, without obedience to the divine law, can one expect salvation? No! For faith without works is dead, and those who invoke her name must also walk in the path of holiness.

Take heed, O Christian soul, and rejoice in this wondrous mystery! The Virgin’s intercession is a bridge from the depths of sin to the heights of divine grace. Those who honor her, who practice charity, and who live righteously according to the commandments of the Lord, shall taste the fruit of this holy covenant.

As the thief on the cross found mercy in his final breath, so too shall those who repent and cling to the Mother of Mercy find grace in their hour of need. For she is not merely the bearer of Christ; she is the bearer of divine compassion, the refuge of sinners, the mother of all who call upon her name.

Conclusion: A Call to Honor the Covenant

Brethren, as we commemorate this sacred feast, let us renew our devotion to the Virgin Mother. Let our lips sing forth her praises, let our hands be stretched forth in works of mercy, let our hearts be set ablaze with love for Christ.

May all who call upon her name find favor in the sight of the Lord. May her intercession be our shield in times of trial, our light in times of darkness, and our refuge in the hour of judgment. And may we, through her Covenant of Mercy, be found worthy to inherit the kingdom prepared for the righteous from the foundation of the world.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
