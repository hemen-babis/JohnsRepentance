4. The Path of Purity and Holiness

Isaiah calls this the “pure path,” the “holy way.” It is not for the profane or careless. It is for the righteous, for those who follow Christ with a pure heart, guided not by ambition or pride, but by humility, love, and perseverance.

Let no one say, “This way is too narrow.” Did not the Lord Himself say:

 “Narrow is the gate and difficult is the way that leads to life, and there are few who find it”? (Matt. 7:14)


Do not expect the world to applaud your journey. They begged our Lord to leave their country; they accused Him, insulted Him, condemned Him. If they did this to the Master, will they treat the servant better?

Yet, brethren, let us not be shaken. For just as Christ was raised from the dead and ascended in glory, so too shall we who walk in His steps. The destination is sure: eternal life, eternal joy, eternal communion with God.


5. The Example of the Wise Serpent and the Innocent Dove

Our Lord, in His divine wisdom, counseled us:

“Be wise as serpents and harmless as doves.” (Matt. 10:16)


The serpent, to preserve its head, exposes its body to danger. In the same way, let us be willing to sacrifice all temporal things—wealth, health, even life itself—for the sake of preserving the treasure of our faith. This is not foolishness, but the highest wisdom. For what is a man profited if he gains the whole world and loses his soul?

If we give all, even our very bodies, for Christ, He will restore it all to us in glory. For the Lord is no debtor. He rewards those who lose for His sake with riches eternal, incorruptible, and divine.


Conclusion: Let Us Walk Without Doubt

Beloved, let us walk this path without doubt. It is a path well-trodden by the prophets, apostles, martyrs, and saints. It is the path of Christ, who went before us, blazing the trail through suffering into victory. Let us not falter when we suffer for His name. Let us not grow weary when the journey is long. For the journey is Christ, the truth is Christ, and the reward is Christ.

Let all glory, dominion, and power be to our Lord Jesus Christ, who is the image of the invisible God, the life of the world, and the path of salvation. May He guide us by His Spirit along this holy way, until we enter into the mansions He has prepared in the house of His Father.


“May our Lord Jesus Christ… lead us on the path of righteousness so that we may enter into the many mansions in His Father’s house in His goodness and love for mankind.”

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
