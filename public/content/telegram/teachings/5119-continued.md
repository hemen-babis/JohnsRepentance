Conclusion

Therefore, beloved, when you see the clouds in the heavens, lift up your hearts to the Creator. Do not regard them as mere accidents of nature, but as instruments of God’s wisdom. See in them both His providence for the body and His mystery for the soul.

The cloud teaches us of rain that refreshes the earth, and of Christ who refreshes mankind. The cloud reveals to us that our God is both Creator and Redeemer, who provides bread for the body and grace for the spirit.
