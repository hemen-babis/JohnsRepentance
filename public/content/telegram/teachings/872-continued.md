(Continued) 👆

In summary, the church is an essential part of our salvation, and it is through the church that we come to know and love Christ. The church is the mystical body of Christ, and whoever enters it becomes a member of Christ. Therefore, let us strive to participate fully in the life of the church, to receive the sacraments regularly, to pray and worship with the community of believers, and to live a life of love and service to one another, as Christ Himself taught us. By doing so, we will grow in our unity with Christ and with one another, and we will experience the joy and peace that come from living in communion with God.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
