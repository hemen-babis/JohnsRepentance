V. The Legacy of the Saint: Judgment, Healing, and Veneration

After Saint Arsema was martyred with the twenty-seven virgins, King Dirtades fell seriously ill. Saint Gregory had already prisoned by him. (Tradition says he was her elder brother.) Later he released Saint Gregory and cured him of his illness. The holy virgins, Saint Arsema, and the holy martyrs were to be buried here, in Saint Gregory, on the sixth day of Tahisas. Likewise, the one hundred and nineteen martyrs who are called her companions are commemorated. Then they built a beautiful church in her name and honored it there, so we should honor them.

The feast day of Saint Arsema, is celebrated on this day every month. Her story serves as a testament to the strength of faith, the preservation of purity, and the power of spiritual devotion in the face of adversity and temptation.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
