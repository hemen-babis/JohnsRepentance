III. The Cross by Service

👉🏾 Etse Meskel (ዕፀ መስቀል): Represents Christ Himself, our Lord and Savior.

👉🏾 Mestor Meskel (የመፆር መስቀል): The cross carried by the deacon during the Divine Liturgy.

👉🏾 Erfe Meskel (ዕርፈ መስቀል): The cross used by deacons when offering the Precious Blood to the faithful in the Holy Eucharist.

👉🏾Hand Meskel( የእጅ መስቀል): The cross held by bishops and priests, through which they bless the faithful.

👉🏾 Neck Cross(የአንገት መስቀል): Worn by the faithful as a sign of their love for Christ and their commitment to Him.

👉🏾 Kerne Begi-e Meskel (ቀርነ በግዕ መስቀል): A cross shaped like a ram’s horn, mentioned in the Book of "Diguset", symbolizing the victory and triumph of Christ.

Conclusion

✍🏾Let no man despise the variety of its forms, for every shape is a sermon, every material a proclamation, every usage a participation in the one mystery of our salvation. And may Christ, the Crucified and Risen Lord, engrave His Cross upon our hearts, that we may walk in its power and inherit the kingdom prepared for those who love Him.
