Continued 👆🏽

2. “Today you will be with Me in Paradise.”
Between two thieves He hung. One mocked, one believed. And to that repentant thief He opened Paradise, showing that even in agony, mercy overflows.

3. “Father, into Your hands I commit My spirit.”
No one took His life; He offered it. These words show His divine authority, His unity with the Father, and His willing surrender.

4. “Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do.”
In the very moment of cruelty, when nails tore flesh and mockers laughed, He interceded for His murderers. Behold the ocean of His mercy.

The Cross: From Curse to Crown

In Roman law, the cross was for criminals, for the shamed and defeated. But see how the Cross becomes the throne of the King, the ladder of Jacob, the key to Paradise, the sword that struck down Hades! The instrument of death becomes the fountain of life.

For on this very Friday, humanity that once groped in the dark was flooded with light. What the first Adam lost in Eden, the second Adam reclaimed on Calvary.

What Have We Gained?

O soul, ponder what we have received:

Forgiveness, by the One who was sinless.

Freedom, by the One who was bound.

Life, by the One who died.

Victory, by the One who appeared defeated.

The heavens wept, the earth quaked, the sun hid its face, and the veil of the temple was torn because the Creator died for the creature.

Let us then live not as those who forget this mystery, but as those marked by it. Let us kiss the Cross, embrace suffering for His sake, and proclaim with trembling joy:

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
