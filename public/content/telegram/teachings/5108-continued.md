Continued 👇🏾

Her Glory and Her Rest

The Lord Jesus Christ promised her a place of rest at the right hand of the Holy Virgin Mary, and He gave her the name:

“The Staff of Mary.”

The Holy Virgin appeared to her and blessed her with words of praise:

“Blessed is the womb that bore you, and blessed the breasts that nursed you. Blessed are the eyes that beheld you, and blessed are those who celebrate your struggle.”

At last, she prepared for her departure. Digging a pit equal to her height, she set spears around her and endured twelve years in this manner of worship, pierced and suffering, yet unwavering. Then, on Nehase 24, her soul was received by the angels, and her body was laid to rest in Geye Monastery, where her holy relics remain for the blessing of the faithful.

The Lord’s Promise

The Lord Himself declared to her:

“I will bless twelve generations of those who love you, who call upon your name, who honor your memory, and who celebrate your feast.”

Thus, her name is glorified, her prayers intercede for us, and her memory remains a fountain of blessing until the end of the age.

Conclusion

O beloved faithful, let us imitate the steadfastness of Holy Kristos Semra—her courage in trial, her compassion even for her enemies, her ceaseless prayer, and her trust in the Lord who crowned her.

May her prayers, her intercession, and her unfailing aid be with us all, now and forever.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
