👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾A Homily on #1John 3:8  “The# Son of #God Was #Revealed to #Destroy the #Works of the #Devil”

Part 1

Brethren beloved in the Lord,
Grace and peace be multiplied unto you, who hunger not for earthly bread, but for the Bread of Life, which came down from heaven. Today, as we meditate upon the sacred mystery revealed in 1 John 3:8 “For this reason the Son of God was revealed: to destroy the works of the devil” let us tread reverently into the holy ground of divine economy, and examine this ineffable condescension of the Son, who bowed the heavens and descended for our salvation.

The Word Became Flesh: The Manifestation of the Divine Plan

The eternal Word, begotten of the Father before all ages, has been revealed. Not by voice only, nor by written commandment alone, but by becoming man by becoming flesh without ceasing to be God. This is no metaphor, no poetic flourish, but the heart of the mystery of our salvation.

Revelation, in this sense, is not mere communication, but divine manifestation. The Invisible became visible; the Eternal entered time; the Infinite embraced finitude and yet remained unchanged. He who was without body took on a body, and He who was beyond suffering became capable of suffering, not for His own sake, but for ours.

“The subtle God became the limited flesh, and the flesh, without leaving its limitations, became the subtle divinity.”
O what mystery! O what love unspeakable!

The Divine Paradox: Death Clothed in Life

Let the mind of flesh be silent, for this mystery is not grasped by reason alone, but by faith enlightened by grace. Consider what the Church teaches us through the Scriptures and the saints:

The First and the Last He who said “I am the Alpha and the Omega” became one who tasted death.

He who is outside time entered into time, yet time could not contain Him.

The divine and the human were united in one Person without confusion, without change, without division, and without separation.

This is why the glorified Son could declare, “I am alive, and I was dead, and behold I am alive forevermore” (Rev. 1:17). Death could not conquer Him. He entered death voluntarily, shattered its gates, and rose, trampling Hades beneath His feet.

The Mystery Hidden Through Ages Now Revealed

St. Paul declares this most wondrous mystery to Timothy:

“Without doubt, great is the mystery of godliness: God was manifested in the flesh...” (1 Tim. 3:16)

This is not merely a theological notion, but the very heart of our worship and life. The Word was made flesh and dwelt among us not as a shadow, but in fullness, from the Holy Virgin Mary, born not of man but of the Holy Spirit. He united Himself wholly to our human nature body and soul to redeem us entirely.

Three Great Revelations of the Godhead

Let us now consider how the Lord unfolded Himself throughout history:

1. In the Law of the Heart: From the beginning, in Eden and beyond, He spoke inwardly to the heart of man, revealing His voice through visions and miracles (Genesis 2:8-18; Genesis 3:9; Genesis 12:1).


2. In the Torah: Through Moses and the prophets, He declared His will in word, vision, miracle, and law  guiding Israel like a loving shepherd.


3. In the Gospel: At last, He revealed Himself perfectly in the Incarnate Son teaching as one having authority, healing the broken, and forgiving sins  revealing that He is both perfect man and perfect God.

And the Evangelist testifies:

 “No one has ever seen God; the only Son, who is in the bosom of the Father, He has declared Him” (John 1:18).
He did not come to speak about God, but to reveal God Himself for He is God.
