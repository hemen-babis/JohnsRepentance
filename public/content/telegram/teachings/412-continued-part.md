3.   "I and the Father are one. He who has seen me has seen the Father. Do you not believe that I am in the Father and the Father is in me?" (John 14:9:10)

4.  Prophet Malachi said in his prophecy, "Do we not all have one father?" Didn't one God create us? He said that God is one in creating. (Mal 2:10)

5.  The prophet Iyasas said, "He is the God who created the heavens, who made the earth and established it... God says this. "I am God and there is no other besides me," he wrote emphasizing that God is one in creating and ruling.

A Christian who does not believe in the unity and trinity of the Trinity will never find the forgiveness of sins and the hope of heaven.

The Trinity brought all visible and invisible creations from non-existence into existence and created them.
The Trinity, was existing before the world, their charactor praising one another, and still is, and will forever. However, it is explained in the holy books that they created men and angels to inherit their glory in order to praise their name so that their glory would not remain in their character. But we understand the other creation to be created for memory, memory, food, meat, and soul. They appointed the human being as the first-born ruler of all creatures on earth. (Ps 8:6)

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always

May God's mother, the virgin, and the cross remain glorious, Amen.
