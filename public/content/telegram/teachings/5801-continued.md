4. Living a Righteous Life

Our father David asks in his psalm:

“Who shall ascend into the hill of the Lord? Who shall stand in His holy place? He who has clean hands and a pure heart, who has not lifted up his soul to vanity nor sworn deceitfully. He shall receive blessing from the Lord and righteousness from the God of his salvation.” (Psalm 24:3-5)

To stand before God requires purity of heart and a life striving for righteousness.

Saint Paul teaches his spiritual son Timothy:

“Flee youthful desires and pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace, with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart.” (2 Timothy 2:22)

And again:

“The goal of the commandment is love from a pure heart, a good conscience, and sincere faith.” (1 Timothy 1:5)

Righteousness grows out of a pure heart.

👉🏾Saint John also teaches:
“Whoever abides in Him does not sin. Whoever sins has neither seen Him nor known Him. Let no one deceive you: He who practices righteousness is righteous.” (1 John 3:6-7)

Purity of heart means abiding in Christ and living in righteousness.
