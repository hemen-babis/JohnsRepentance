 👉👉👉Question #5 What are the solutions and spiritual tools to get rid of adultery?

Repentance

The first and only solution for people who have fallen into the sin of adultery is to repent. Repentance will make the sinner righteous and the adulterer a virgin. When we get sick we go to a doctor and get medical care and follow-up. Just like that, since the spirit of adultery is deadly not only in the flesh but also in our souls, to get out of it:

 1) We should have a heart willing to be saved and a sincere repentance for the sin of adultery we have committed, like the leper. The foundation and main requirement for true repentance is when our conscience admits and repents of the wrong we have done.

 2) We need a good penitent father who is a doctor for our souls, who gives us the medicine called canon according to the type and extent of our illness, and regularly monitors us until we are completely cured, heals and advises us. “And I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven;  Whatever you bind on earth will be bound in heaven, and whatever you loose on earth will be loosed in heaven." (Mathew 16᎓19). Therefore, repenting and having a confession father is not a command of man, but an instruction of the Creator.

 3) We must take the right remedy called repentance that God has commanded us through our confession father, and we must carry out the canons given to us properly. Even though we have been committing sins for centuries, the righteousness that we have done in a short time of the canon will wipe out all that innumerable destruction and help to enter the kingdom of heaven.

 4) After repenting and fulfilling our canon, we have passed from eternal death to eternal life. So that we don't fall into the sin again, the healing medicines or our spiritual weapons are fasting more, praying more, and prostrating more. Although each of them has a wide range of lessons, we have briefly explained it as follows, thinking that the more we do these, the less we understand the concept behind them and their significance. The main thing is that the type of adultery is different for each one of us, and we should examine our how and why we are tempted and apply the general remedies accordingly and speficifically.
