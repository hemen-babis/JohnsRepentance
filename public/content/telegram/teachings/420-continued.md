My son who I said was dead has been saved and put a ring on his finger and shoes for his feet. The word "shoe" means "teach him religion, teach him the gospel, preach to him." This is a great example, those who were at home did not sin. Therefore, our questioner, you must realize above all else that while it is good if we do not commit sin, if we do, our last option and the way to be reconciled with God is repentance. Basically, there will never be a sin that has never been committed by a human being, and we are asking you to understand that what happened to you is a type of sin that a person commits and is forgiven in a life of repentance. In general, we recommend the following to solve your problem:

  - Father of Repentance: Repentance is done through fathers and priests who have been given priesthood authority by God to be shepherds of souls, so there should be no shameful or unspeakable secret to tell your repentant father, so we say you should dare to tell your sin. And God is not a God of fear, but a God of love and peace, who raises up those who have fallen in sin and says, "Take heart, my son," and find us where we are lost. Instead, what scares us and makes us feel naked is living in sin, and what hides our shame before our God is not clothes, but repentance. Spiritual clothing is honor and covers privacy. - Delaying and aggravating fear for reasons is one of Satan's battles, and God is not the God of fear, but the God of love and peace. Even if you believe that you have committed so many sins, there is no wrongdoing that cannot be erased by God, so we advise you to stay away from reason and fear and come closer to the forgiving God.
