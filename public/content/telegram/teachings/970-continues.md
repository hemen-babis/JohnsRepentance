(Continues) 👆

Let us seek the truth and separate it from falsehood. Let us do the work that God loves and remain steadfast in His house until the end of our lives. Let us repent of our sinful nature, accept His holy body and His precious blood, and inherit the everlasting inheritance of the kingdom of heaven.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
