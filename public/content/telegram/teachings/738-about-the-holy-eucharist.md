About the Holy Eucharist  
 
(Part 5)

The precautions a Christian should take on the day he receives Holy Communion, and the Christian life he should have in his future life:
 
1.       On the day a Christian receives Holy Communion, he should not wash his body with water, he should not take off his clothes and be naked, he should not draw blood from his body.
 
2.        He should not do hard work that can cause fluid (sweat) on our body, he should not walk a long way (because this is also considered work).
 
3.       Even if we live in a married life, it is necessary to abstain from sexual intercourse 3 days before receiving Holy Communion and 2 days after receiving Holy Communion. 
 
4.       He should not eat too much, drink intoxicating drinks that disturb what we have eaten and drunk.
 
5.       On the day we received Holy Communion, it is forbidden to cut nails, shave hair, bow and kneel, spit, bathe in water, and change clothes before the end of the day. (Liturgy No. 166) We can change our cloths at the evening after 6:00pm, as it is considered as the next day.

6.       if a person experiences nose bleeding , vomit, bodily bleeding, wounds, and a gnat that enters his mouth by Being careless or due to not taking appropriate care on the day of receiving the Holy Communion,  he should tell this to his penitent  father and  he should repent. And he cannot receive the Holy Communion again without performing his penance.  
 
7.       It is forbidden to go to Court and argue on the day you receive Holy Communion. (Council of Nicaea) In general, one should not go to a public place where there is an argument, argue with someone, talk with hard words, do work that provokes anger, and such other things after communion on that day.
 

8.       Before we receive Holy Communion, we should ask for forgiveness and be cleared of our sins of enmitity and revenge through repetance; and promise not to return back on it. So, now after receiving Holy Communion, we should refreain by far from argument, dispute and grudge. We should  be more committed to stay back from being offended, rebuked, or quarreled with. Even if it happens being out of control, we should ask for forgiveness, let go of grudges, repent and accept it as it should be. Be it from your family or social life, you should stay away from this type of situation as much as possible and spend everything patiently so that you don't have ideas that bring resentment and quarrels. We should live in love with everyone.
 
9.       After receiving Holy Communion, we should refrain from any sinful activity, such as adultery, murder, theft, gossip, addiction, fraud, lying, and the like. 
 
10.   After receiving Holy Communion, it is appropriate to listen to the word of God, go to church regularly, pray, fast, prostrate, and generally live a spiritual life and Christian behavior.

11.   It is necessary for us to have a regular prayer program in our personal life. Because the main spiritual struggle that we can do is through prayer so that Satan does not fight and deprive us of the blessings we have received through the Holy Communion.
 
12.   In general, it is appropriate to know that when we live bounded by the Holy Communion, it is never appropriate to go to a place that is contrary to spiritual life and to associate with a situation that exposes us to sin. We should fight against all the evil temptations and sin.
