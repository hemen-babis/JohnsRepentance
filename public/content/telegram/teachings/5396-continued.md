2.4.Paradise, the Seraph, and the unique role of Christ and the Virgin

👉🏾“Without You, O Lord, there is no creature that can open the garden guarded by the Seraphim except Christ.”
(Genesis 3:24)

✍🏾Eve, who was restored to her former glory and place by Christ, rejoiced as one who finds again her mother’s embrace.

✍🏾 This teaches us that no one can open the gate of Paradise except Christ, who redeemed mankind by His cross. It was Christ who opened the closed door of Paradise and restored the grace of sonship that humanity had lost and this same Christ was born of our Lady, the Virgin Mary.

✍🏾 God said through Isaiah: “My salvation will not delay; I will bring salvation to Israel that I may be glorified in Zion.” (Isaiah 46:13)

✍🏾The Savior, Jesus Christ, through whom fallen mankind was glorified and saved, came forth from our Lady called Zion the Holy Virgin Mary. She is the one who gave this medicine of life to the world.

Conclusion 

✍🏾Fasting without humility is an empty shadow. The true fast is not of the lips or stomach alone, but of the heart. The believer must fast with compassion, give alms to the poor, restrain anger, forgive offenses, and keep the tongue from deceit. Let fasting be united with mercy, prayer, and repentance, that it may ascend as sweet incense before the throne of God.

As the fathers teach: “When you fast, do not fast for vain praise, but that your fasting may be a fasting of mercy, wherein you find help, blessing, and the grace of God.”
