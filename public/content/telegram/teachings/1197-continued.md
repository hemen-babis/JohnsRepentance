(Continued) 👆

Remember, our dear member, that your story has the potential to provide comfort and inspiration to others who may be facing similar trials.

Let us spread the lessons we learn from our own experiences so that they may serve as a beacon of hope for those in need.

May God bless you abundantly, grant you comfort and assurance within your home, and lead you to a path of reconciliation and redemption. Reach out to us, and together, we will strive to rebuild your family and your life with the guidance and grace of God.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
