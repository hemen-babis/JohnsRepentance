✍🏾Nolawi – The True Shepherd 

✍🏾Meaning of Nolawi

The third Sunday of the Zemene sibket  is called “Nolawi.”
The word Nolawi means “Shepherd” or “Guardian.”

On this day, the Church remembers how the holy prophets foretold that the Son of God would appear as the true Shepherd and Guardian of humanity. Jesus Christ is proclaimed as the true Shepherd, and on this day the Church sings, sanctifies, and gives thanks.

✍🏾 The Guardian of Israel

The prophets saw the people of Israel, and indeed all humanity, as sheep without a shepherd. They prophesied about the coming of the true Shepherd who would gather, protect, and guide His flock.

On this holy Sabbath, the Church reflects on the fourteen generations from the Babylonian exile to the birth of Christ, showing how God prepared the world for the coming of the Good Shepherd.

On this holy Sabbath, the Church teaches that the prophets prayed, saying:

“Hear, O Shepherd of Israel,You who lead Joseph like a flock,
You who dwell between the cherubim, shine forth.”(Psalm 80:1)

Through this prayer, they were crying out for the coming of the true Shepherd, so that:

👉🏾the scattered sheep would be gathered,

👉🏾the wolf would not destroy the flock, and

👉🏾the human race, separated from the faithful Shepherd because of sin, would be restored and fed.

By this, the Church proclaims that Jesus Christ is the true Shepherd, who gathers, protects, and feeds His people.

✍🏾The Good Shepherd 

When the prophecies were fulfilled, our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ revealed Himself as the true Shepherd, saying:

 “I am the Good Shepherd. The Good Shepherd gives His life for the sheep.”(John 10:11)

He also declared:

“I am the Good Shepherd. I know My sheep, and am known by My own, just as the Father knows Me and I know the Father; and I lay down My life for the sheep.”
(John 10:15)

By the Spirit of God, the righteous King David proclaimed:

“The Lord is my Shepherd; I shall not want.
He makes me lie down in green pastures.”(Psalm 23:1–2)


During this holy fasting season, the Church lifts its prayer before God:

👉🏾Lord, send out Your Light and Your righteousness.

👉🏾O Good Shepherd, remember us, for we have wandered.

👉🏾Restore the love in our hearts that has grown cold.

👉🏾Grant us Your true peace, that we may return to our true Shepherd,
our Savior Jesus Christ, who never abandons His flock.

✍🏾Beware of Wwolves 

1. The True Protector of the Flock

The faithful protector of all Christians is our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. He is the true Shepherd and the true Guardian of the Church.

The priests of the Church are also protectors of the flock, because they received the command given through Saint Peter:

“Feed My sheep.”

Yet, in an age of deception and false teaching, believers must learn to distinguish between true shepherds and wolves.

Our Lord warned:

 “Beware of false prophets, who come to you in sheep’s clothing, but inwardly they are ravenous wolves.”
(Matthew 7:15)

We recognize them by their fruits, for as a good tree bears good fruit, so a true shepherd brings good to his flock.

2. The Danger of False Shepherds

A wolf does not come to protect the sheep, but to destroy and devour them. In the same way, false shepherds do not seek the salvation of souls but their own gain. Their end is destruction, because they lead many away from the truth.

Therefore, the faithful must be watchful, faithful in doctrine, and firm in their spiritual life.
