(Blessed are those who are persecuted for #righteousness, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven, #Matthew 5:10)

(Part 3)

Beloved children of the Light,
Grace and peace be multiplied to you in the name of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. Today we bring to you a holy teaching filled with divine wisdom a reflection on those who are persecuted for righteousness’ sake, whose reward is none other than the kingdom of heaven itself. Let us open our hearts to this mystery, that we may understand the blessing hidden in suffering and the glory that follows the cross.

Blessedness in Persecution

Our Lord teaches in the Gospel of Matthew:

“Blessed are those who are persecuted for righteousness’ sake, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.”

Righteousness refers to truth, integrity, and honesty. It is the good and virtuous work that flows from faith in God and brings honor and glory to Him. In the original Gë'ëz ( Ele Yiseddu), the word translated as “persecuted” literally means “those who suffer” or “those who are mistreated.” Thus, “persecuted for righteousness’ sake” describes all who suffer because they uphold the truth, speak the truth, stand for justice, perform good deeds, or earnestly seek the truth.

✍🏾 There is only one true righteousness: Jesus Christ. He is the source and author of righteousness (Jeremiah 23:6; 2 Corinthians 5:21). Therefore, those who are persecuted for righteousness’ sake are those who suffer for believing in and following Christ, for obeying His commandments, for preaching His Word, or for serving Him faithfully.

The Apostle Paul also teaches this truth:

"You know the persecutions that happened to me at Antioch, at Iconium, and at Lystra, which I endured; but out of them all, the Lord delivered me. Indeed, all who desire to live godly in Christ Jesus will be persecuted." (2 Timothy 3:11-12)

This reminds us that persecution is not a sign of failure, but a confirmation that we are walking in the way of Christ, living righteously, and upholding the truth in a world that often opposes it.

✍🏾Blessed Are Those Who Suffer for Righteousness’ Sake

Therefore, those who suffer for the sake of the true Christ are noble, honored, and blessed; the kingdom of heaven belongs to them. As Saint John the Baptist explained, our Lord did not mention merely those who are causes the suffering  the main focus is the purpose ‘for righteousness’ sake’ not the identity of the persecutors. 

As long as the persecution comes with the intention of righteousness, whether from Gentiles, heretics, believers, or unbelievers, the reward is the kingdom of heaven. Rather, one who rebukes others and suffers for the sins of fellow believers will receive the blessing, as Saint John the Baptist promised

✍🏾 The phrase “for righteousness’ sake” is profound, as it distinguishes a martyr from a criminal. Though the external punishment may appear the same, the purpose makes all the difference:

The martyr suffers for righteousness, upholding the truth and the will of God.

The criminal suffers as punishment for wrongdoing.

The martyr will receive eternal reward, while the thief has no hope of blessing unless he repents.
