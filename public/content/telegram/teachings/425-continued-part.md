Therefore, with the love of God and the love of neighbor, the ten words can be kept and fulfilled (Matthew 22:40). (John 10:11) The manifestation of God's love is keeping the law.

It is his love for man that raised Christ from the dead. "For there is no greater love than to give oneself as a ransom for the Son of Man" (John 15:13).

B/ the 6 words of the Gospel

1. Do not be angry with your brother in vain (Matthew 5:22-26)

2. Do not look at a woman, and do not commit adultery in your heart (Matthew 5:27).

3. Do not divorce your wife for any other reason other than adultery (Matthew 5:32).

4. Never swear (Matthew 5:34)

5. You do not oppose evil with evil. (Matthew 5:39)

6. Love your enemy (Matthew 5:45)
