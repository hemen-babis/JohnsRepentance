---Lesson about St. #Gabriel the Archangel 

"Behold, I am Gabriel who stand in the presence of God."
(Luke 1:19)

Dear members of John's Repentance spiritual service, today is the annual feast of the great Archangel Gabriel, our divine intercessor, and our revered martyrs, Saint Yelutan and the young Saint Kirkos. This writing is intended to enhance our understanding of Archangel Gabriel so that we may receive his heavenly blessings.

Gabriel, a name signifying 'God's strength' or 'God's messenger', is a title bestowed upon this radiant being who stands as a divine emissary between the Creator and humankind, often clothed in the semblance of a man. Archangel Gabriel holds the 2nd position in the celestial hierarchy, just after the venerable Archangel Michael, among the seven great Archangels. Angels, those luminous beings created by God on the first day, exist in eight classes. They are birthed from the Word of God, their substance being both fiery and ethereal. God has arrayed 99 tribes of angels across the celestial sphere, divided among three heavens.

1st/ In Yore, God has designated 4 chiefs over 40 angelic tribes.

2nd/ In the Heaven of Light known as Rama, He has ordained 3 chiefs, with 30 tribes inhabiting 3 heavenly cities.

3rd/ In the Heaven known as Err, God has divided them into 3 divisions, 30 tribes, 3 cities, each division under a chief.

The extensive and intricate angelic order bears profound lessons, which, God willing, shall be expounded upon in future discourses. Coming back to our subject, Archangel Gabriel presides as the chief over the 30 tribes of angels residing in Rama's heaven of light, along with 4 tribes in Arbab. In the Holy Scriptures, Saint Gabriel's intercessory and healing roles are akin to those of Saint Michael.

When the angels pondered the mystery of their origin, the Father of Lies, the Devil, claimed to have created them. It was Gabriel who counselled steadfastness, admonishing the angels to remain faithful to their divine origin.

1st/ To the prophet Daniel, Gabriel was a guide and an expositor of divine mysteries. He appeared before Daniel, saying, "Understand, O son of man, for at the time of the end shall be the vision." (Daniel 8:16)

2nd/ Once more, in a subsequent vision, Daniel saw Gabriel, who explained to him, "O Daniel, I have now come forth to give you wisdom and understanding." (Daniel 9:22)

3rd/ Gabriel was the divine deliverer who saved Daniel from the lions' den.

4th/ The Three Youths were spared the fiery furnace through Gabriel's intervention.

5th/ He foretold to the priest Zechariah and his wife, Saint Elizabeth, the birth of their son, John the Baptist, despite their old age.

6th/ It was Gabriel who announced to the Virgin Mary the impending birth of the World's Savior, our Lord Jesus Christ.

7th/ Saint Yelutan and young Kirkos were saved from perishing in an iron vessel by his divine intervention.

8th/ He guided the Magi to Bethlehem, where the Savior was born, by the sign of a star.

9th/ He instructed Joseph in a dream to take the Virgin Mary and the infant Jesus and flee to Egypt.

Throughout the sacred texts, we witness Gabriel's mediation and guidance. We shall continue to explore these divine interactions in future homilies. On this feast day, let us ask for the intercession of Archangel Gabriel, that he may bless us and keep us safe. For if the Lord had not sent His angels to guard us day and night, at home and on our journeys, we would be vulnerable to the snares of Satan, who seeks to undermine both our earthly life and our eternal salvation.

As the holy Psalmist and Prophet David declared, "He shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways." (Psalm 91:11)

And again, "The angel of the Lord encamps all around those who fear Him, And delivers them." (Psalm 34:7)

Discourse on the Divine Mystery of #Holy #Communion

1. To those who faithfully partake of Holy Communion

"Whoever endures to the end will be saved" Matthew 24:13

The essence of the Christian life resides not only in professing faith but in preserving it until our last breath. This was the counsel of our Lord to His disciples, whom He ordained and sent forth to proclaim the Gospel. He did not promise them a path strewn with roses, but a journey filled with trials, strewn with fiery tests and bearing the sharp edge of persecution.

It is of utmost importance to keep in mind that your victor, the Lord who has overcome the world, is by your side. Despite manifold hardships, remember to remain steadfast in your faith and love of God, and never to let your spiritual crown tumble.

Learn to treat the mundane affairs of this world with equanimity, cherishing the knowledge that all things on earth are but fleeting. With perseverance, transform every aspect of your life, be it commerce, agriculture, learning, marrying, rearing children, into an act of dedication to God. Remember that you do not bear the weight of your life alone; your God is by your side, transforming all your deeds into blessings. 

Heed the wisdom imparted in the scriptures to combat the devil's ploys. Do not disregard the tribulations you face, for these trials temper your faith. After savouring the grace of God, pray fervently that any worldly enticements that could wrench you from God's glory be quashed beneath your feet. 

2. To those desiring to faithfully partake of Holy Communion 

"Now is the day of salvation" /2 Corinthians 6:2/

The Apostle's exhortation that today is the day of our salvation should spur our determination to prepare for Holy Communion. 

The desire to partake in Holy Communion must be birthed from the Holy Spirit. It requires self-examination and contemplation: Why do I desire Communion? How deeply do I comprehend its significance? What are my expectations for life post-Communion? 

Saint Peter proclaimed, "Lord, to whom shall we go?" (John 6:68), emphasizing the enduring, life-giving truth that Christ alone has the words of eternal life. However, many stumbled at Christ's claim, "My flesh is real food, and my blood is real drink." Such people could not fathom how one could consume Christ's flesh and blood, and thus, they fell away. To prevent a similar downfall, ensure that your motivation for Communion stems not from anything else than the depth of your faith and from guidance provided by your spiritual leader.

As you approach Holy Communion, beware of the whispers of Satan, who seeks to fill your heart with doubts and fears.

3. To those distant from Holy Communion 

"If they still refuse to listen, tell it to the church" /Matthew 18:17/

No Christian who truly grasps the significance of Holy Communion would shun it. Are you hesitant to receive Communion due to personal conflicts or issues? Address these problems openly within the church, as instructed by the Gospel. 

Those who believe they can traverse this world unscathed by troubles are victims of a devilish delusion. Indeed, as followers of Christ, we should expect the world's disdain and hostility, for our Lord warned, "If the world hates you, keep in mind that it hated me first" (John 15:18).

Consequently, if we avoid approaching God due to our problems, our error is grave. For no matter the chaos in our lives, we should never consider ourselves unworthy to draw near to God. 

Every Christian has the capacity to dedicate their life to the worship and glorification of God, just as they have the potential to prepare for eternal glory through Holy Communion.
