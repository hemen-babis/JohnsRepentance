👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾#Anqetse #Bitsuan (“Blessed are those who #hunger and #thirst for #righteousness, for they shall be filled.”)
#Matthew 5:6; #Luke 6:21

(Part 17)

Dear beloved in Christ, followers of the Orthodox faith, we greet you in the name of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. Continuing our meditation on the holy Beatitude, “Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they shall be filled,” we learn that the true satisfaction of the soul comes only from God, who rewards those who seek Him with faith and sincerity.

✍🏾Those Who Seek God Wholeheartedly Will Find Him

The Holy Scripture testifies:

“From there you will seek the Lord your God, and you will find Him if you seek Him with all your heart and with all your soul.” (Deuteronomy 4:29)

Again, the prophet Jeremiah declares:

“You will seek Me and find Me when you seek Me with all your heart.” (Jeremiah 29:13)

And the book of Proverbs proclaims:

“I love those who love Me, and those who seek Me diligently will find Me.” (Proverbs 8:17)

✍🏾 These holy words confirm that God reveals Himself to those who earnestly search for Him, not with divided hearts but with full devotion, love, and perseverance.

✍🏾How God Satisfies the Hungry and Thirsty Soul

The Lord, in His mercy, satisfies the spiritual hunger and thirst of His children through divine means:

👉🏾 He satisfies the hunger and thirst for His Holy Word through the true teachers of the Gospel who proclaim His truth faithfully.

👉🏾 He satisfies the hunger and thirst for His Holy Body and Precious Blood through the holy priests who serve the Divine Liturgy.

👉🏾 He establishes those who hunger and thirst in His house through prayer and fellowship.

👉🏾 He builds up the faithful by strengthening their faith and guiding their conduct according to His will.

✍🏾The covenant He gave us “They shall be filled” is not temporary but perfect and eternal. It assures us that all who seek Him in truth will never remain empty.

The Boundless Goodness of God

The promise “they shall be filled” reveals the immeasurable kindness and generosity of God. He not only satisfies the needs of His people but fills them with overflowing grace and joy.

As our Lord said:

“I have come that they may have life, and have it more abundantly.” (John 10:10)

He knows what our souls need and provides far more than we could ever ask or imagine.

Saint Paul, the apostle, also teaches:

“My God shall supply all your needs according to His riches in glory in Christ Jesus.” (Philippians 4:19)

And again:

“Now to Him who is able to do exceedingly abundantly above all that we ask or think, according to the power that works in us.” (Ephesians 3:20)

✍🏾 God’s generosity has no limit. When He gives, He gives in fullness both in this life and in the life to come. Therefore, those who hunger and thirst for righteousness shall be filled with divine wisdom, holiness, and eternal joy.
