👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾The #Thirty-Three #Feasts of the Holy Virgin

Nehase 7 – The day of her conception.

Nehase 16 – The day her Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, rose from the dead and ascended into heaven.

Nehase 17–21 – Her holy resurrection is celebrated for five days, in likeness to the Resurrection of her Son, just as the Lord’s Resurrection is honored for fifty days.

Nehase 18 – Memorial of her Resurrection.

Nehase 19 – Memorial of her Resurrection.

Nehase 20 – Memorial of her Resurrection.

Nehase 21 – Memorial of her Resurrection.

Meskerem 10 – The day the image of Our Lady, who appeared incarnate and performed many miracles, entered and was established in the land of Tsedenia.

Meskerem 21 – Celebrated as Meskerem Maryam, connected with the Council of Nicaea (326 AD) which condemned Arius, and also with Emperor Zera Yacob, who brought the Holy Cross of Christ to the monastery of Gishen Debre Kerbe.

Tikimt 21 – Feast of Our Lady.

Hidar 6 – The day she returned from exile in Egypt and rested in the land of Qusqam.

Hidar 21 – The day the Temple of Moses was built in Axum; also the day when the priest Zechariah saw the lamp burning, a symbol of Our Lady.

Tahsas 3 – The day she entered the Temple at the age of three.

Tahsas 21 – Feast of Our Lady.

Tahsas 22 – The Annunciation of Gabriel (as Saint Deksios marked Megabit 29, the day she conceived the Lord).

Tahsas 28 – “The Feast of the Nativity of Our Lord is celebrated once every four years on the 6th day of Pagume, while in other years it is celebrated on Tahsas 28.”

Tahsas 29 – The feast of the Virgin, Birth of our Lord.

Tir 21 – The day Our Lady departed this life at the age of 64.

Yekatit 16 – The day she received a great promise from her Son.

Yekatit 21 – Feast of  our lady

Megabit 21 – Feast of our lady.

Megabit 29 – Feast of Our Lady and the feast of the Conception of Our Lord

Miyazya 21 – Feast of Our Lady.

Ginbot 1 – The Nativity of the Virgin Mary.

Ginbot 21 – She appeared at Debre Mitmaq and performed a great miracle.

Ginbot 22 – She spoke clearly and visibly to the faithful gathered at Debre Mitmaq.

Ginbot 23 – She again spoke to the faithful gathered at Debre Mitmaq.

Ginbot 24 – The day she entered Egypt with her beloved Son, Saint Joseph, and Salome.

Ginbot 25 – The day her Son planted the dry staff, and it miraculously blossomed.

Sene 8 – The day her Son brought forth water from the dry rock.

Sene 20 – The Lord appeared, gathered the apostles, and built a church in her name.

Sene 21 – the day of her liturgy house celebrated 

Hamle 21 – Feast of Our Lady.

Conclusion

The thirty-three feasts of Our Lady, the Holy Virgin Mary, remind us of her holiness, her intercession, and her closeness to Christ our God. Let us honor these days with faith and devotion, that through her prayers we may receive mercy and inherit the Kingdom of Heaven. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
