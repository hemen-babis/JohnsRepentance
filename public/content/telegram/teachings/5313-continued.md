3. The Daily Therapy (Spiritual Struggle):

 After the Physician has set the bone, the patient must still do the exercises to regain strength. The healing of the soul is a lifelong cooperation between God's grace and our effort. This is the daily struggle, and it involves:

Prayer: This is the breath of the new heart. A heart that does not pray is a heart that is suffocating. Speak to God simply, honestly, as a child to a Father. Recite the Psalms, especially Psalm 50 (51), the great prayer of repentance: "Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me."

Fasting: By denying the body, we strengthen the spirit. We learn to control our passions rather than be controlled by them. We empty ourselves of worldly things to make more room for God.

Reading Holy Scripture: This is feeding the new mind with the thoughts of God. Meditate on the Gospels. See the life of Christ and let it be the pattern for your own.

Almsgiving and Works of Mercy: A healed heart is a heart that loves. This love must be expressed in action. When you give to the poor, when you comfort the sorrowful, when you forgive those who wrong you, you are exercising the muscles of your new heart, making it stronger and more like Christ's.

Do not be discouraged, my child. This path is not easy, and you will stumble. But our God is a God of mercy, a loving Father who runs to embrace His repentant children. The healing begins the moment you turn to Him.

I urge you, find a spiritual father, a priest to whom you can go for confession. He will be your guide on this blessed journey. Place your hope not in your own strength, but entirely in the boundless love and mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ, who came "not to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance" (Luke 5:32).

May the Holy Theotokos intercede for you, and may the grace of our Lord be your strength and your healing. I will keep you in my prayers.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
