Conclusion

Blessed indeed are those who weep before God not for worldly sorrow, but for love, repentance, and longing for the Kingdom. Their tears are seen by the angels and remembered before the throne of the Lord.

May our hearts be softened like theirs, and may our tears wash away all pride and sin, so that we too may be comforted by the mercy of our Savior, Jesus Christ.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
