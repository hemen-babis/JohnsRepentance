👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾#Blessed are the #pure in #heart, for they shall #see #God.(#Matthew 5:8)

(Part 29)

Dear followers of the Orthodox teachings .we greet you with the peace of the Lord. Today, based on the topic mentioned above, we present to you the following important lesson. We encourage everyone to read it carefully and learn from it.

2.Protecting Yourself From Sin

Just as we wash our bodies daily to preserve physical cleanliness, we must also guard the purity of our hearts every day. Repentance is not a one-time act but a continual labor. Loving God with a pure heart leaves no room for loving the passing things of this world.

This is why the Apostle James warns us:
“You adulterers! Do you not know that friendship with the world is enmity with God? Whoever wishes to be a friend of the world becomes an enemy of God.” (James 4:4)

Worldly love separates us from God. Purity of heart means turning away from the world and following God wholly.

Our Lord also teaches:
“He who loves father or mother more than Me is not worthy of Me; and he who loves son or daughter more than Me is not worthy of Me. And he who does not take up his cross and follow Me is not worthy of Me.” (Matthew 10:37-38)

These words are addressed to those who choose to follow Him with an undivided, pure heart.

Saint Peter, whose heart was sincere, once said to the Lord:
“Behold, we have left everything and followed You. What shall we have?”
And the Lord replied:
“Truly I say to you, in the regeneration, when the Son of Man sits on the throne of His glory, you who have followed Me will also sit on twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel.” (Matthew 19:27-28)

Those who follow Christ with a pure heart will behold His glory and share in His kingdom.

3.Living a Life Filled With Love

When we speak of a pure heart, we mean an inner life free from sin, free from wrongdoing, free from doubt, and filled with holy love. A clean house is not defined by emptiness but by what is kept within it. In the same way, purity of heart is not only freedom from sin but the presence of genuine love.

Saint Paul teaches that love is the foundation and fulfillment of every virtue:

👉🏾“If I give all my possessions to feed the poor, and if I surrender my body to be burned, but do not have love, I gain nothing.” (1 Corinthians 13:3)A pure heart, therefore, is a heart saturated with holy love.

Our Lord, when revealing the two greatest commandments, said:
“You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind. This is the first and greatest commandment. And the second is like it: You shall love your neighbor as yourself. On these two commandments hang all the Law and the Prophets.” (Matthew 22:37-40)

✍🏾He taught this so that these two commandments of love might take root in our hearts and shape our entire lives.

It is indeed a blessing to dwell in the love of God and to live in love toward others. The Lord said:
“Blessed are those who hear the word of God and keep it.” (Luke 11:28)

This blessing belongs to those who hear His word with a pure heart and obey it with a pure heart.

In the Gospel of John, Christ teaches:

“If you love Me, keep My commandments.” (John 14:15)
And again:

👉🏾“This is My commandment, that you love one another as I have loved you.” (John 15:12)
These words show clearly that a pure heart is never empty; it is filled with divine love.

Saint John also declares in his revelation:

👉🏾“Blessed is he who reads, hears, and keeps the words of this prophecy.” (Revelation 1:3)
And later:“Behold, I am coming quickly. Blessed is he who keeps the words of the prophecy of this book.” (Revelation 22:7)
The pure in heart are those who hear the word of God and keep it faithfully.

Blessed indeed are those whose hearts are filled with pure love, the love of God and the grace of the Holy Spirit, for they shall see God.
