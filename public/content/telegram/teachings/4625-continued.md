VI. Both Were Crowned with Martyrdom in the Same Year

Let us not forget the supreme testimony of their love martyrdom.

Peter was crucified in Rome under Nero in A.D. 67. Out of humility, he requested to be crucified upside down, deeming himself unworthy to die as his Lord.

Paul, being a Roman citizen, was not crucified but was beheaded, also under Nero’s persecution.

Both drank from the same cup of suffering and were baptized with the same baptism of blood. One the fisherman, one the Pharisee yet both now reign with Christ.

Conclusion: Unity in Diversity, Harmony in the Spirit

These two apostles Peter, the pillar of the circumcision, and Paul, the apostle to the Gentiles stand together in the iconography of the Church, not as rivals, but as brothers. They are the two olive trees and two lampstands spoken of in Revelation (Rev. 11:4), who pour out oil and shine with light for the Church throughout the ages.

Let us then imitate their faith, their zeal, their humility, and their love for Christ. Let us not measure ourselves by background or knowledge or worldly honor, but by the cross we are willing to bear and the Gospel we are willing to proclaim.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
