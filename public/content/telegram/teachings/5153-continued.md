Continued 👇🏾

Conclusion

Beloved, let us understand with fear and joy: the cloud in Scripture always points us to the same mystery. To Noah, the cloud bore the rainbow of the covenant. To Moses, it covered the tabernacle with God’s glory. To Elijah, it appeared as a small hand bringing rain. To Isaiah, it was the swift cloud carrying the Lord into Egypt.

In all these, the cloud reveals the Holy Virgin Mary. She is the cloud who bore the Sun of Righteousness, the hand through which the mercy of God was poured, the vessel chosen to carry the Savior into the world. Through her, the famine of sin is ended, and the drought of mercy is filled with abundant rain.

Therefore, let us cry out with faith: “O Virgin Mary, our covenant and our cloud, remember us before your Son!”

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
