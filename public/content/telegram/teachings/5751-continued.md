3.God's Love and Providence for All:

Our Lord Himself taught that not a single sparrow falls to the ground without our Father's knowledge (Matthew 10:29). This speaks to God's immense providence and love for all His creatures. While we may not fully comprehend the ultimate destiny of individual animals, we trust in God's perfect wisdom and goodness, knowing that His plan for the restoration of creation is comprehensive and glorious.

So, to summarize, my dear child: the Holy Scriptures and the wisdom of the Fathers point to a future where all creation, including the animal kingdom, will be freed from the corruption and suffering caused by sin, participating in the glorious liberty of the children of God in the new heavens and new earth. While this is not understood as individual animals having an immortal soul and conscious eternal life like humans, it is a profound affirmation of God's love and ultimate redemption for everything He has made.

May this bring comfort to your heart and to your friend's, knowing that God's mercy extends to all His works.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
