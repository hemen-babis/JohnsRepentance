👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾(#Anqetse #Bitsuan)#Blessed are the #merciful, for they will be shown #mercy (#Matthew 5:7)

(Part 20)

Dear followers of the Orthodox teachings ,we greet you with the peace of God. Today, based on the the above mentioned title, we present to you the following teaching and entrust all of you to read and learn from it.

✍🏾“Blessed are the merciful, for they will find mercy”

Our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ taught in the fifth Beatitude:
“Blessed are the merciful, for they will obtain mercy.” (Matthew 5:7)

✍🏾 The Meaning of Mercy

Mercy is a union of three virtues:

👉🏾 Compassion

👉🏾 Kindness

👉🏾 Forgiveness

👉🏾Compassion is the feeling of the heart that understands others, feels for them, makes their pain our own, and moves us to help them.

👉🏾Kindness is sharing what we have wealth, knowledge, strength, and other blessings with those who lack them, expecting nothing in return.

👉🏾Forgiveness means pardoning those who have harmed us, even when they do not deserve forgiveness, and letting go of anger or punishment.

Kindness and forgiveness flow from a compassionate heart.

Church scholars teach that mercy is of two kinds:

👉🏾 Physical mercy

👉🏾 Spiritual mercy

Physical mercy is providing what a person in need lacks.

Spiritual mercy is helping a person repent from sin and return to God.

✍🏾The Square of Mercy

The place where our Lord Jesus Christ healed the sick in body through miracles and healed the sick in soul through His teachings is known as “Awde Mihret,” meaning “the Square of Mercy.”
Even today, the platform from which the Gospel is proclaimed in the church bears this name to show that the Church is the place of mercy.

The Church, redeemed by the precious blood of Christ, is where people show mercy to one another and where God shows mercy to His people. From the days of our forefathers until now, the Church has preserved this identity as the Square of Mercy.

✍🏾 God, the Source of Mercy

May the God of Mercy be the One who grants us mercy throughout our lives. In this fifth beatitude(Anketse Bitsuan), we reflect on:

👉🏾 the mercy of God

👉🏾 the power of mercy

👉🏾 and the righteous leaders who became examples of mercy.

✍🏾God is a God of mercy. He is kind and compassionate, slow to anger and full of steadfast love. Showing mercy is His nature. For this reason, the Church praises Him saying:

“Glory to the King,Glory to the God of mercy.”To show mercy is to imitate God.

King David, the one after God’s own heart, said:

👉🏾“The Lord is merciful and forgiving, slow to anger and abundant in kindness.He will not always rebuke, nor will He keep His anger forever.He has not dealt with us according to our sins, nor repaid us according to our iniquities.As the heavens are high above the earth, so great is His mercy toward those who fear Him.As far as the east is from the west, so far has He removed our transgressions from us.”

✍🏾The prophet Jeremiah also declared:

✍🏾“It is because of the Lord’s steadfast love that we are not consumed,for His mercies never fail.” (Lamentations 3:22)

👉🏾This teaches us that we live only because of His unfailing compassion.
