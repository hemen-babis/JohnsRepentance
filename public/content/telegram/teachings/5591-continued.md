Continued 👇🏾

f. The Mediator Between God and Man

The Virgin Mary is both the relative of mankind by the flesh and the Mother of the Creator by grace. She stands as the mediator between humanity and God, bringing human supplications before God and conveying God’s mercy to mankind.

Through her intercession, we receive both the salvation and blessing of soul and body.
