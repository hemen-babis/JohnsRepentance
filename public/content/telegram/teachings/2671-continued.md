Conclusion: The New Eden

Good Friday invites us to stand at the threshold of the new Eden, the restored paradise where Christ, the new Adam, restores what the first Adam lost. Here, in the shadow of the cross, we find the highest expression of divine love—a love that suffers, forgives, and triumphs over death. As we reflect on the profound mysteries of this day, let us approach the cross not as distant observers, but as participants in the divine drama of redemption, where every heart is called to the foot of the cross, to gaze upon the One who loved us to the end.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of The Virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
