Using #piano, #guitar and other instruments in the Church. 

Question: I have a question…
Why do we not use instruments like piano, guitars/electric guitars, trumpets, etc. in our Orthodox Tewahdo Church for Mezmurs? I want to understand it better for myself and to have an answer for others when I’m asked. Thank you!

Answer: Every country has its own traditional instruments, whether it is a traditional song or musical instrument.

Firstly, we need to examine each case separately. For instance, in our country, there are instruments used by musicians. Many of them may be the same, but we know how to use the tools in a professional style and adapt them to what is needed. Therefore, we cannot simply judge the instrument with a single perspective.

We all know why these musical instruments, which are products of the age and technology, are in demand. They are evocative and there are many occasions when we are drawn into the emotions of the instruments, to the extent that we are forced into that emotion without even wanting to.

Psalms are praises to God. When we offer a delightful song to God, we submit to God with one heart and one spirit. We cry with inspiration if we can, but we do not laugh, have fun, move our physical bodies or show ourselves to other emotions.

Therefore, the one who submits more conscience to God should submit all of their feelings and spirit to God. For this reason, songs, praises and all the musical instruments used in worship should not be anything other than a mystery that makes us feel connected to God, and that God hears us and we also hear from God. If we all understand this, singing instruments do not need to be modified as we are told in the scriptures. Nor can we say that they need improvement.

If we use these instruments properly, they are sufficient if we apply them according to the commandments and laws of the Holy Book. The important thing is not whether it is an electric instrument, but that we use it to save energy, save time, and make songs that are more audible and influential.

There are harps, lyres, flutes, drums, cymbals, and other musical instruments mentioned in the Bible. However, most of these instruments are not currently being made or used for singing. Nonetheless, it is written in the book, "I will praise God with harps, lyres, flutes, and drums." However, most of these instruments are not currently being made, or used, for singing.
