(Part 30)

Dear followers of the Orthodox teachings on John's Repentance website .we greet you with the peace of the Lord. Today, based on the topic mentioned above, we present to you the following important lesson. We encourage everyone to read it carefully and learn from it.

5. Imitating God

God Himself is pure in His nature, and all His works are pure. As King David declared:
“The way of God is perfect; the word of the Lord is tested; He is a shield to all who trust in Him.”
(Psalm 18:30)

A pure heart resembles God by speaking pure words and rejecting evil thoughts. David teaches us:
“My heart overflows with a good theme; I speak the things concerning the King; my tongue is the pen of a ready writer.”
(Psalm 45:1)

A heart purified by grace produces good works: thanksgiving, humility, mercy, and spiritual wisdom.

God is patient, and a pure heart becomes the storehouse of patience. Saint Paul teaches:
“Our mouth is open to you, our heart is enlarged. You are not restricted by us… I speak to you as to my children: be enlarged also.”
(2 Corinthians 6:11-13)

A pure heart is wide and compassionate, able to bear others gently.

David also wrote:
“He has not dealt with us according to our sins, nor repaid us according to our iniquities. For as the heavens are high above the earth, so great is His mercy toward those who fear Him. As far as the east is from the west, so far has He removed our transgressions from us.”
(Psalm 103:10-12)

Those who imitate God must therefore treasure patience.

Our Lord Himself revealed the height of godliness:
“Love your enemies, bless those who curse you, do good to those who hate you, and pray for those who persecute you… that you may be children of your Father in heaven. For He makes His sun rise on the evil and on the good, and sends rain on the righteous and the unrighteous.”
(Matthew 5:44-45)

Purity of heart is to imitate God’s mercy.

6. Being the Temple of God

The Apostle Saint Paul teaches:
“Do you not know that you are the temple of God, and that the Spirit of God dwells in you? If anyone destroys the temple of God, God will destroy him; for the temple of God is holy, and that temple is you.”
(1 Corinthians 3:16-17)

The heart of a Christian is the dwelling place of God.

Saint Peter also exhorts us:
“Sanctify Christ the Lord in your hearts.”
(1 Peter 3:15)

Paul further warns the faithful regarding purity of life:
“Every sin a person commits is outside the body, but the one who commits sexual immorality sins against his own body. Do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit who is in you, whom you have from God? You are not your own; you were bought with a price. Therefore glorify God in your body.”
(1 Corinthians 6:18–20)

To honor the heart is to honor the temple of God.

He also writes:
“What agreement has the temple of God with idols? For we are the temple of the living God. As God has said: ‘I will dwell in them and walk among them; I will be their God, and they shall be my people.’”
(2 Corinthians 6:16-17)

God dwells in the purified heart.

To the Ephesians, Paul further teaches the unity and growth of this spiritual temple:
“You are built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Christ Jesus Himself being the chief cornerstone. In Him the whole building is joined together and grows into a holy temple in the Lord. In Him you also are being built together for a dwelling place of God in the Spirit.”
(Ephesians 2:20–22)

A heart rooted in Christ becomes a holy dwelling prepared for God.
