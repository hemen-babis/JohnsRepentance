(Continued)☝️

May the intercession of St. Raphael bring healing to those who are ill, peace to those who are troubled, and guidance to all who seek to walk in the light of God's mercy. Let us, with faith and devotion, call upon this mighty archangel, trusting in his constant prayers and protection, knowing that, through him, God’s love and healing are made manifest in our lives.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
