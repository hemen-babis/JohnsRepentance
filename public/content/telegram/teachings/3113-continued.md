Continued ☝️

In conclusion, the lives of the apostles, as chronicled in sacred scripture and tradition, are not mere historical accounts but are vibrant exhortations to live a life of profound faith, radical hope, and enduring love. May we, inspired by their example, continue to walk faithfully in the footsteps of those who first walked with Christ.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
