Conclusion

Behold then, beloved, the Sea of Thoughts: vast, deep, and mysterious, a reflection of God’s eternal order. And behold the sage, Saint Demetrius of Alexandria, who by the grace of the Holy Spirit reached its depths and gave to the Church the gift of time’s reckoning.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
