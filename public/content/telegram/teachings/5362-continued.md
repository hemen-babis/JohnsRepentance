V. The Cross as the Pattern of Christian Life

The Cross is the law of love written in suffering. It is the path through which obedience becomes joy and death becomes life. Christ, by humbling Himself unto death, fulfilled all righteousness. His obedience on the Cross became the cure for Adam’s disobedience. Hence, the Cross stands as the everlasting covenant between heaven and earth, abolishing the curse of sin and restoring the soul’s harmony with its Maker.

The believer who bears his own cross walks in the footsteps of the Savior.Our marriages, too, should be filled with the perfect love of the Cross compassionate, gentle, always ready to forgive, and full of hope for eternal life. May God bless us with a steadfast love for Him and an unwavering spiritual devotion, so that our lives may shine with spiritual awareness and continually remind us of the love of the Cross.


VI. The Cross as Healing and Resurrection

After His resurrection, the Lord continued His work through the Holy Cross healing the sick and raising the dead. Those who witnessed these wonders and experienced His grace recognized that the Cross is not mere wood, but the source of life and redemption. They worshiped Him with awe and devotion, proclaiming: “The Cross is power, the Cross is redemption, the Cross is the salvation of the soul.”

Indeed, whoever stands at the Cross with steadfast faith finds rest for every affliction. The Cross is not merely a relic of the past; it is the living presence of divine grace in the world. From its wood flows the strength to endure temptation, the courage to forgive, and the hope that does not fade.

VII. Conclusion: The Call to the Love of the Cross

Saint Yared designated the period from the 17th to the 25th of Meskerem as the Time of the Cross. The Cross is the supreme symbol of love, planted between God and mankind to bring reconciliation. Through the Cross, the curse of the soul and of the body was abolished. Whoever stands steadfast in the hope of the Cross can find comfort for their troubles and salvation for their soul (John 19:25).

The Cross is also the perfect symbol of obedience. Our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, by His obedience unto death, bore the sins of many and fulfilled the role of the suffering servant, bringing righteousness to all who believe.

May our merciful God grant us the understanding of the meaning and love of the Cross. May He help us dwell in His house, live in His grace, and inherit His eternal kingdom.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
