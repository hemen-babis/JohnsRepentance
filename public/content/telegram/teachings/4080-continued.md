Continued👆🏽

Thus, dear brethren, let us walk in this love, for upon it hangs the Law and the Prophets. Without love, all virtue is void, all righteousness is but dust, and all knowledge is but vanity. May the Most Holy Trinity strengthen us in this divine love so that we may be found worthy of His kingdom.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
