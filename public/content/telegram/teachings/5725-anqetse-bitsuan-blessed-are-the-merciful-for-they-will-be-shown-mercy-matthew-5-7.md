👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾 (#Anqetse #Bitsuan)“#Blessed are the #merciful, for they will be shown #mercy” (Matthew 5:7)

(Part 21)

Dear followers of the Orthodox teachings ,we greet you with the peace of God. Today, based on the the above mentioned title, we present to you the following teaching and entrust all of you to read and learn from it.

✍🏾God’s Mercy Revealed on the Cross

✍🏾First, the prophet Isaiah testified to the boundless mercy of God shown through the suffering of Christ:

“Surely He has borne our griefs and carried our sorrows; yet we considered Him stricken, smitten by God, and afflicted. But He was wounded for our transgressions, He was bruised for our iniquities; the chastisement for our peace was upon Him, and by His stripes we are healed.” (Isaiah 53:4-5)
Through His suffering, He carried our sins and revealed the depth of His mercy.

✍🏾Again, through the prophet Ezekiel, the Lord declared His desire not for destruction, but for repentance and life:

“Do I have any pleasure in the death of the wicked, says the Lord God? Is it not that he should turn from his way and live?” (Ezekiel 18:23)
Christ accepted the cross so that humanity might turn back from its wicked ways.

✍🏾God, who seeks not the ruin of humanity but its restoration, showed mercy to the people of Nineveh. When they returned through fasting and prayer,
“God saw their works, that they turned from their evil way; and God relented from the disaster He had said He would bring upon them.” (Jonah 3:10)

This same mercy reached its fullness at the Cross, when our Lord prayed,

“Father, forgive them, for they know not what they do.” (Luke 23:34)
And as He taught,“Greater love has no one than this, than to lay down one’s life for his friends.” (John 15:13)

He revealed His perfect love by giving His life for all.

✍🏾God’s Mercy Toward the Repentant

👉🏾The Lord forgives all who come to Him with repentance, for His heart is rich in compassion. Scripture says of Him:

“When He saw the multitudes, He had compassion on them, because they were harassed and helpless, like sheep without a shepherd.” (Matthew 9:36)

✍🏾When the prophet Nathan rebuked King David, David replied:
“I am in great distress; let me fall into the hand of the Lord, for His mercies are great; but let me not fall into the hand of man.”
(2 Samuel 24:14)

For truly, “The Lord desires mercy and not sacrifice, and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings.” (Hosea 6:6)
Therefore, we also must hold mercy in the highest honor.

✍🏾God Gives Mercy to Every Heart That Truly Asks

God gives mercy to anyone who asks with a sincere heart. Our Lord says in the Gospel, “Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and the door will be opened to you. For everyone who asks receives, and he who seeks finds, and to him who knocks it will be opened.” Matthew 7:7 to 8 shows that He grants mercy to all who ask with their whole heart. This is why we pray, “Lord, Christ, the Merciful.”

👉🏾David understood this well. He prayed, “Have mercy on me, O Lord, according to your lovingkindness; according to the multitude of your tender mercies blot out my transgressions. Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin. For I acknowledge my transgressions, and my sin is ever before me. Against you, you only, have I sinned, and done evil in your sight.” Psalm 51:1 to 4 gives us a model of honest repentance. With the same spirit he said, “But as for me, in the multitude of your lovingkindness I will come into your house; in your fear I will worship toward your holy temple,” Psalm 5:7. We also rise early, drawn by His mercy, and come before Him.

👉🏾Luke tells us of the tax collector who “stood far off, would not even lift his eyes to heaven, but beat his breast and said, God, be merciful to me a sinner.” Luke 18:13 shows a humility we are meant to learn from. When we seek mercy with this kind of heart, we should also show mercy to those who have wronged us.
