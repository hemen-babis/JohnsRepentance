(Part 11)

Dear beloved in Christ, peace and grace to you.Today we continue our spiritual reflection on one of the beautiful mysteries of our faith the intercession of the holy angels.
Let us therefore open our hearts and learn how the angels stand before God as intercessors.

The Holy Angels as Intercessors

The Holy Bible clearly teaches that the angels of God intercede for humanity. When we read the Scriptures carefully and interpret them in their proper context, we understand that the ministry of the angels includes prayer, intercession, and service before God on behalf of mankind.

To understand this mystery correctly, let us examine the meaning of the word “stand”, which is often used in the Bible when describing the presence of angels before God.

The Meaning of “Stand” in Scripture

The word “stand” appears in many places in the Holy Scriptures, and its meaning changes according to the context. It can mean to rise, to be upright, to remain firm, or to serve in dignity.

Some examples include:

“And the flow of her blood stopped at once.” (Luke 8:44)

“The pillar of cloud stood still.” (Deuteronomy 31:15; Numbers 10:12)

“Stand firm in the faith.” (1 Corinthians 16:13)

Each of these verses shows a different use of the same word, reminding us that the meaning must be understood spiritually and contextually.

Standing as a Sign of Intercession

In the Book of Exodus, when the Israelites provoked God by worshiping idols, the prophet Moses pleaded before the Lord, saying:

“Blot me out of Your book which You have written.” (Exodus 32:32)

Through this intercession, Moses turned away God’s anger and saved his people. The Psalmist later recalled this, saying:

“If Moses His chosen one had not stood before Him in the breach, to turn away His wrath, lest He destroy them.” (Psalm 106:23)

Here, the word “stood” means to intercede to pray earnestly on behalf of others. Thus, “standing before God” in Scripture often refers to the act of intercession.

The Angels Who Stand Before God

In the Book of Job, it is written:

“The sons of God came to present themselves before the Lord, and Satan also came among them.” (Job 1:6)

The phrase “came to stand before the Lord” reveals that the angels came to intercede and present petitions before God. Even Satan appeared to request permission to test Job. Therefore, the angels’ “standing” signifies their intercessory service before God for the sake of humanity.

In Scripture, words often carry meanings that differ from, or even oppose, their common usage. This is why it is essential to study their meaning according to the style and hidden mysteries of the Bible. Many fail to understand the true spirit of the angels’ intercession because they do not grasp the deeper sense of the words used in the Scriptures.

Likewise, Saint Gabriel said to the priest Zechariah:

“I am Gabriel, who stands in the presence of God.” (Luke 1:19)

This declaration means more than physical standing. Angels do not possess physical bodies as humans do; they do not sit, sleep, or move as we do. Therefore, Gabriel’s words “I stand before God” express his constant presence before the Lord in prayer and intercession.

He did not say this to describe his position like a guard standing before a king, but to reveal his ministry as one who intercedes before God continually for mankind. The angels’ standing before God is a sign of their service of thanksgiving, praise, and intercession.
