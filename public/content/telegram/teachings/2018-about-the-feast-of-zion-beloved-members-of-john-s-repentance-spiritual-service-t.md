About the feast of #Zion

Beloved members of John's Repentance spiritual service, today is the anual feast of Zion. Hence, let us delve into the profound significance of the Feast of Zion, a pivotal celebration in the Orthodox Tewahedo Church, marked annually on December 1st. This feast, deeply rooted in scriptural and historical contexts, intertwines the sanctity of the Ark of Zion with the reverence for the Virgin Mary, embodying the profound mysteries of faith and divine providence.

The Ark of Zion: A Testament to God's Presence

At the heart of this celebration lies the Ark of Zion, a sacred symbol of God's covenant with His people. The Ark, as recounted in the Holy Scriptures, was entrusted to Moses and later came to reside in the great city of King David. Here, it stood as a beacon of hope and justice for the suffering Israelis, a divine presence amidst their trials and tribulations.

The Feast of Zion: A Historical Perspective

The narrative of the Ark's journey, particularly its capture and eventual return, is central to the Feast of Zion. This story unfolds with the sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, whose sacrilegious actions in the temple led to dire consequences. Their disrespect towards the sacred rituals, the extinguishing of the temple lamp, and their irreverence in the presence of the Ark invoked divine wrath, fulfilling the prophecy of their simultaneous demise.

The capture of the Ark by the Philistines, a pivotal event, demonstrated the power of God even in foreign lands. The Philistine god Dagon was reduced to rubble in the presence of the Ark, and plagues afflicted the Philistines until they returned the Ark to the Israelites. This return was marked by miraculous events, reinforcing the Ark's divine nature.

The Virgin Mary: The New Ark

In Orthodox theology, the Virgin Mary is venerated as the New Ark, a living sanctuary who bore Christ, the Word made flesh. The Feast of Zion, therefore, is not only a commemoration of the historical Ark but also a celebration of Mary as the Mother of God. This linkage is beautifully encapsulated in the Psalms and the prophecies of Isaiah, which prefigure Mary as Zion, the chosen dwelling of God.

The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church and the Ark

The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church holds a unique connection to the Ark, believing it came to Ethiopia under the auspices of Queen Sheba and King Solomon. This historical belief intertwines with the scriptural narratives, enriching the spiritual tapestry of the Feast of Zion.

The Feast of Zion: A Call to Spiritual Renewal

The Feast of Zion is more than a historical commemoration; it is a call to spiritual renewal, urging the faithful to embody the virtues exemplified by the Virgin Mary. It's a time to reflect on divine justice, mercy, and the transformative power of God's presence in our lives.

Concluding Exhortation

As we observe this sacred feast, let us embrace the virtues of love, humility, and service, inspired by the Virgin Mary, the New Ark of the Covenant. May her intercession guide us, and may we find solace and strength in the enduring legacy of the Feast of Zion.

In this manner, the Feast of Zion stands as a profound testament to God's enduring presence and His unyielding commitment to His covenant with humanity. It beckons us to a deeper understanding of our faith and a renewed commitment to live out its precepts in our daily lives.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of The Virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
