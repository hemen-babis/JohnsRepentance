7. The Martyrdom of Saint George

Despite the many miracles, King Dodianus and his council, unable to subdue the saint’s spirit, condemned him to death by beheading. Before his execution, our Lord Jesus Christ appeared to Saint George, promising that all who commemorate his name and call upon Christ in his memory would be delivered from their troubles. This divine assurance filled Saint George with joy as he offered his life for his Savior. His body was taken to Lydda, where a church was built in his honor, and countless miracles continued to occur through his intercession.

---
8. Lessons from Saint George’s Life

The life of Saint George offers timeless lessons for every believer:

1. Faith Above All: Saint George’s refusal to compromise his faith, even in the face of death, reminds us to hold steadfastly to Christ.

2. Generosity and Sacrifice: By giving away his earthly possessions, he teaches us to value eternal treasures over worldly wealth.

3. Divine Power: His miracles demonstrate that God’s power is unmatched and that He strengthens His faithful servants in their trials.

4. Witness through Suffering: Saint George’s suffering became a testimony that led many to salvation.

5. The Triumph of Truth: The defeat of idols and the conversion of multitudes testify to the ultimate victory of God’s truth.

---
9. The Promises of Christ

As Christ promised Saint George, those who commemorate him in faith will find deliverance from their afflictions. This assurance calls us to honor the memory of this great martyr, seek his intercession, and trust in the boundless mercy of our Lord.

---
Conclusion

On this sacred day of Tir 18 we celebrate not only the life of Saint George but also the triumph of God’s grace through him. His story calls us to greater faith, deeper devotion, and a commitment to the truth of the Gospel. May his intercessions protect us from all harm, and may we, like him, boldly proclaim Christ’s glory to the ends of the earth.

In the words of our Lord Jesus Christ to Saint George: “I will erase all the sins of everyone who does your remembrance on earth, and whoever calls on me in your name in trouble... I will quickly deliver them.”

May the blessings of Saint George be upon us all!

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
