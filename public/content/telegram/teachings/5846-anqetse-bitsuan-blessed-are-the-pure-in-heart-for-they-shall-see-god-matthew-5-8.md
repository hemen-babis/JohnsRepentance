✍🏾Our Father Abraham

Genesis 18:1–15

At the heat of the day, while Abraham sat at the door of his tent in the plains of Mamre, the Lord appeared to him. When he lifted his eyes, he saw three men standing nearby. Recognizing the divine presence, he ran from the tent door to meet them and bowed himself to the ground in deep reverence.

Abraham said, “Lord, if I have found grace in Your sight, do not pass by Your servant. Let a little water be brought, so that Your feet may be washed, and rest yourselves under the tree. I will bring a morsel of bread for your refreshment; after that, you may continue on your way, since you have come to your servant.”
And they replied, “Do as you have said.”

✍🏾The Righteous Job

Job 19:23–27; Job 42:1–6

Job cried out in the midst of deep suffering:
“Oh, that my words were written! Oh, that they were inscribed in a book, engraved with an iron tool and lead, carved forever in the rock! But I know that my Redeemer lives, and that He will stand upon the earth at the end of time. After my skin has been destroyed, yet in my flesh I shall see God; I myself will behold Him, and not another. My heart longs with desire.”
(Job 19:23–27)

Later, when God answered him from the whirlwind, Job humbled himself and said:
“I know that You can do all things, and that no purpose of Yours can be withheld. Who is the one who speaks without knowledge? I spoke of things I did not understand, things too wonderful for me to know. Hear me, and I will speak; I will ask You, and You make me to understand. I had heard of You with my ears, but now my eyes have seen You. Therefore I humble myself in dust and ashes and repent.”
(Job 42:1–6)

✍🏾Our Father Jacob

Genesis 28:10–17; Genesis 32:30

Jacob left Beersheba and journeyed toward Haran. When the sun had set, he found a place to rest for the night. He took a stone, set it under his head, and lay down to sleep.

In his dream he saw a ladder set upon the earth, reaching up to heaven. The angels of God were ascending and descending upon it, and the Lord stood above it and said:

“I am the God of your father Abraham and the God of Isaac. The land on which you are lying, I will give to you and to your descendants. Your offspring shall be like the sand of the earth, spreading to the west, the east, the north, and the south. Through you and through your seed all the nations of the earth shall be blessed. I am with you, and I will keep you wherever you go. I will bring you back to this land, for I will not leave you until I have fulfilled all that I have spoken to you.”

Jacob awoke from his sleep and said, “Surely God is in this place, and I did not know it.” He was filled with awe and declared, “How awesome is this place! This is the house of God; this is the gate of heaven.”(Genesis 28:10–17)

 Jacob said, “I have seen God face to face, and my life has been preserved.”and named the place Peniel(Genesis 32:30)
