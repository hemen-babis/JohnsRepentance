5. Hungering and Thirsting for the Will of God

Hungering and thirsting for righteousness also means striving to do the will of God in all things.

When our Lord passed through Samaria, His disciples brought Him food, saying, “Rabbi, eat.” But He replied,

“I have food to eat that you do not know about.”
The disciples said to one another, “Has anyone brought Him something to eat?”
Then Jesus said to them,
“My food is to do the will of Him who sent Me, and to finish His work.”(John 4:32–34)

In this, our Lord teaches that the true food of a Christian is to fulfill the will of God and to serve Him faithfully. This is what we must hunger and thirst for daily to please God through obedience and holy deeds.

Hungering for righteousness also means to labor in service that leads to holiness and justice. The Lord said,

“Blessed is that servant whom his master, when he comes, finds so doing.”(Matthew 24:46; Luke 12:43)

He also said,

“Blessed are those servants whom the master finds watching when he comes; truly I tell you, he will gird himself, have them recline at table, and will come and serve them.
And if he comes in the second or third watch and finds them so, blessed are those servants.”
(Luke 12:37–38)

Therefore, blessed are those who hunger and thirst to do the will of God, who serve Him diligently and remain steadfast in their calling until the Lord comes.

Those who hunger in this way shall be filled, for our Lord has promised,

“Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they shall be filled.”(Matthew 5:6)
