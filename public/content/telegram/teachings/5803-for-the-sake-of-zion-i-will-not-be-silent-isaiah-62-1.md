✍🏾Understanding the Name “Zion”

In the prophecy of Isaiah, God declares that He will gather those who are far and reveal what was hidden. The term “Zion” appears throughout Scripture and is interpreted according to its context. The Holy Scriptures are explained in an ordered, faithful, and traditional manner. One word may carry different meanings depending on where and how it appears, so careful examination is required.

The word Zion can mean:

👉🏾The Virgin Mary

👉🏾Jerusalem

👉🏾The Church

👉🏾The Kingdom of Heaven

👉🏾The Temple

Likewise, Jerusalem can mean:

👉🏾The House of Israel (House of Jacob)

👉🏾The Temple

👉🏾The Virgin Mary

👉🏾The Cross

👉🏾Christ Himself

If we fail to understand the context, and attempt to interpret these terms carelessly, we will lose the mystery instead of uncovering it. To avoid confusion, we must examine Scripture carefully, learn from the Fathers, and pray with humility for God’s enlightenment.

1. Zion Refers to the Virgin Mary

“The Virgin Mary is called Zion
“For the Lord has chosen Zion; He has desired her for His dwelling place. ‘This is My resting place forever; here I will dwell, for I have desired her. I will abundantly bless her provision; I will satisfy her poor with bread. I will clothe her priests with salvation, and her saints shall shout for joy.’” (Ps. 132:13-16)

In this mystery, the Virgin Mary, who is Zion, reveals the blessings promised to those who believe in her covenant, her motherhood, and her intercession:

👉🏾 She grants honor to her faithful.

👉🏾 She fills her poor with gladness and grace.

👉🏾 She provides nourishment for body and soul.

👉🏾 She grants new birth through baptism, making believers children of God, sharers of one Body and Blood.

👉🏾 She strengthens her priests and servants with life and spiritual vitality.

👉🏾 The saints who trust in her purity receive the joy of the Holy Spirit.

Mary, the dwelling place of the Prince of Peace
She bore the Son of God in her womb after nine months and five days, fulfilling the prophecy:
“For unto us a Child is born, unto us a Son is given…” (Isaiah 9:6)
Christ, who is our peace (Eph. 2:14), dwelt in her, the true land of peace. As the psalm says: “Glorious things are spoken of you, O city of God.” (Ps. 86:3)

✍🏾The Scripture that says, “I am appointed as King on the mountain of praise” (Ps. 2:6) is understood by the Church as a prophecy pointing to the mystery of the Incarnation. The Virgin Mary is called Mount Zion because, just as Mount Zion was a strong and trusted refuge for Israel, so the Virgin Mary is a refuge for all who believe. The name “Zion” itself means refuge, and our Lady truly becomes the place of safety for sinners seeking mercy, for the righteous seeking protection, and for all Christians who trust in the covenant of compassion given to her through her motherhood and intercession.

✍🏾Because she became the living dwelling place of God, she is called the Mountain of the Temple, the Cradle of the Flesh, the Cradle of Righteousness, and the Cradle of Salvation. For nine months and five days she bore Christ, the Savior of the world, in her holy womb. she carried Christ with humility and endured suffering for the salvation of humanity. She inclined her head toward her womb, for within it the angels were glorifying their Creator, and she listened to their praise as one hears a sacred liturgy. (John 1:42 ).
