An explanation of #depression, the challenge of #loneliness and perfect #peace and #light

👉🏾 Question from member: I often feel depressed and lonely. Life has no meaning to me, I just want to die and live with Jesus, I've been looking for happiness all my life but I can't find it, I feel like my mind is going to explode, please pray for me, I need prayer, what do you recommend?

✍️Answer:- Why does a person choose loneliness? Why does a person hide? In essence, man is not happy without God, because there is no peace without God. Jesus Christ said in the Gospel of John, chapter 14, verse 27, "Peace I leave with you, and my peace I give you, the peace I give you is not as the world gives; do not let your hearts be troubled, do not be afraid."
 
The world kisses us today then bites tomorrow; gives today then takes away tomorrow. But God does not take away what He has given, no matter what, there is no peace without God. What is life? Life is the opposite of death, life is living. Life is God, it is written, "Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life."
 
Therefore, if we believe in the Son, we live with Jesus who is the life. It is Jesus Christ who gave life when human beings could not live according to the commandment, it is Jesus Christ who enlightened the dark world. In fact, this Christmas (birth) season is called the light. And if it is said who is the light: it is Jesus Christ.

This world was dark before God the Son became a man. The world lived in darkness. It is called light because God became a man and enlightened the world by making us members of His body. Many people's lives are in darkness. Darkness is sin, darkness is betrayal, darkness is hatred, darkness is lack of love; On the other hand, light is Jesus Christ, light is life, light is religion, light is peace, light is help, blessing and love. Because it is our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ who gave us that, there is no life without Jesus Christ.
 
As the owner himself told us, John Gospel chapter 15 verse 5 said "without me you can do nothing", so without Jesus Christ nothing can be done. How can peace be found without Jesus Christ? How can you live? Absolutely not possible. When Judas betrayed Jesus Christ for 30 dollars he died because he was separated from Jesus Christ who is life. We remember that when the devil was once the archangel, when he forgot God and said, "I am the creator," and then failed and separated from God who is life.

Also, when Adam lived peacefully in paradise for seven years and did what he was told not to do, he lost peace from God who is life, and found it difficult to live. but then we remember that he found peace when he returned to God, when he reconciled with God, so there is no peace without God.
 
Our questioner said that they want to die and live with Jesus Christ. So, those of us who do not have peace on earth, those who have fallen into sin on earth, those who have not tried to repent to find Jesus Christ, how can we find peace if we die? What qualifies us for the kingdom of heaven is the work we have done on earth to live with Jesus Christ and enable us to live in heaven. If we die on earth covered in sin, without repentance, confession, and reconciliation with God, we will not be able to live with Jesus. Therefore, it means that we cannot live with our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ before we repent.
