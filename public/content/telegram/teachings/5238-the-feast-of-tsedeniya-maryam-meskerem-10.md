👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾The Feast of #Tsedeniya #Maryam (#Meskerem 10)

Beloved brethren in Christ, children of the holy Orthodox faith, let us rejoice and give thanks to God, for today we recall the wondrous miracle of Tsedeniya Maryam, the day sanctified by the appearance of the holy icon of the Mother of God, the Virgin Mary, on the tenth of Meskerem.

This is no ordinary remembrance but a holy testimony of the providence of God and the loving intercession of the Queen of Heaven.

Martha of Tsedeniya and the Desire for Eternal Riches

In the land called Tsedeniya, there lived a woman named Martha, chosen and honored by God. She was wealthy, but unlike many who cling to riches, she considered the vanity of worldly possessions. In her heart she said:

“What shall it profit a man to gain the whole world, but lose his soul?” (Mark 8:36).

Inspired by this truth, Martha resolved to turn her wealth into a work greater than all earthly works the work of hospitality, which Scripture so often exalts. She remembered how Abraham welcomed the Holy Trinity beneath the oak of Mamre, how Lot received the holy angels, and how Martha of Bethany, the sister of Lazarus, made her home a resting place for Christ Himself.

Following their example, Martha built a great guest-house and prepared a table as for a king’s banquet, daily receiving strangers as though they were the Lord Himself. In doing so, she prepared her heart to be a dwelling place for God.

Martha’s Desire for the Holy Image

One evening, as she conversed with her guests, she heard of a sacred image of the Holy Virgin Mary, strong in flesh and soul, said to be painted by St. Luke the Evangelist. It was renowned for answering prayers and working miracles through her Son. Hearing this, Martha’s heart burned like fire with love for the Virgin. In tears, she pleaded:

“Is there anyone among you who will go to Jerusalem and bring me this holy image? I will give all that I have for this treasure.”

A holy monk, moved by her devotion, promised to fulfill her request. He traveled to Jerusalem, purchased the holy icon, and began his journey back.

Miracles Along the Journey

On the way to Tsedeniya, the monk encountered grave dangers.

A lion, fierce and roaring, leapt upon him to devour him. In terror, he fell to the ground. At that moment, the holy icon cried out with a voice seven times louder than the lion’s roar, and the beast fled in fear.

Later, bandits surrounded him with intent to kill. Again, the image resounded with a voice like thunder, and the robbers scattered in confusion, not knowing whence the cry came.

Seeing these wonders, the monk marveled at the power of the image of the Mother of God and thought to himself: “I will take her to Ethiopia, that she may be my helper.” Yet he erred, not knowing that the Virgin herself had chosen to dwell with Martha, who loved her with all her heart.

The Virgin’s Choice and the Monk’s Struggle

When the monk sought to avoid Martha and sail directly to Ethiopia, the winds of heaven rose against him, driving his ship back to the shores of Tsedeniya. There he entered Martha’s house, lodging with travelers.

Three times he attempted to leave, but a miraculous door open to all others remained shut before him. Martha, seeing his strange affliction, asked what troubled him. In shame, the monk confessed, recounting the miracles of the icon. At last, he surrendered it to her, saying:

“Behold, a picture of wondrous nature. Truly, it belongs to you, for she has chosen your home as her dwelling.”
