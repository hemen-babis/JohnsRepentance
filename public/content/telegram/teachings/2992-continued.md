Continued 👆🏾

Reflecting on the gravity of receiving the Eucharist unworthily, we are reminded of the Apostle Paul’s admonitions: to partake without proper reverence and preparation is to incur judgment. This solemn warning urges us to approach the holy mysteries with the utmost seriousness and sanctity, fully aware of the profound transformation they effect in us.

Conclusion

In conclusion, beloved in Christ, let us approach the Holy Eucharist with the awe and reverence it deserves. Let it be a true encounter with Christ, transforming us into His likeness. May our participation in this divine mystery be with pure hearts and minds, so that we may truly live out the union with Christ that it signifies, and thus be transformed into beacons of His light in this world.

May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all, guiding and preserving you in the true faith until the day of His glorious return. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
