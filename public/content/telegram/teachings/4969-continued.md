Continued 👇🏾

7. Mary—The Fragrant Flower

You are the flower from the root of Jesse (Isa. 11:1)Christ Himself, the sweet fragrance to the saints and the terror of demons.

8. Greater than All the Saints

O Lady full of grace, you are greater in honor than patriarchs, prophets, apostles, and even the highest angels. The cherubim gaze in awe at the mystery of God taking flesh from you. The seraphim bow before your purity. You are the support of our human nature, the intercessor for our salvation, the vessel through which grace flows to mankind.

Conclusion

Therefore, on this Sunday the day when the world was renewed in the Resurrection we unite our praise of Christ with our veneration of His Mother. Her intercession is a shield for the faithful. Her glory is the adornment of the Church. Her prayers obtain for us the grace and glory of her Son, lest we be deprived of the life He offers.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
