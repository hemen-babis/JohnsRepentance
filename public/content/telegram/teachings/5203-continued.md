Continued 👇🏾

Final Word

Behold, brethren, how the Ethiopian reckoning of time is not a mere calculation, but a sacred order, teaching the faithful when to fast, when to mourn, and when to rejoice. 

Therefore, let us not consider these matters lightly, but receive them with reverence, seeing in them the hand of Divine Providence. For in every count, in every division, in every feast, Christ Himself is revealed the Alpha and the Omega, the Beginning and the End.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
