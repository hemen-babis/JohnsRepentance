(Part 37)

Dear followers of the Orthodox teachings on John's Repentance website .we greet you with the peace of the Lord. Today, based on the topic mentioned above, we present to you the following important lesson. We encourage everyone to read it carefully and learn from it.

✍🏾Our Lord taught in the seventh Beatitude:
“Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called the children of God.” (Matthew 5:9)

Peace means tranquility, calmness, rest, and contentment.
It refers to a right and holy relationship with God, oneself, and other people, founded on:

• Love
• Unity
• Harmony

Gideon declared, “The Lord is Peace” (Judges 6:24), teaching that God Himself is the source of peace. God is truly a God of peace.

Peace is one of the Fruits of the Holy Spirit, and its foundation is love (Galatians 5:22).

The Holy Gospel is called the “Gospel of Peace” because it is the message of reconciliation that God sent to humanity, and which our Lord Jesus Christ preached to the whole world (Acts 10:36; Ephesians 6:15).

Peacemakers are the living fruits of the Gospel, beloved by God.
They actively work to establish peace:

• Between human beings and God
• Between God who dwells in the heart and the inner life of a person
• Between the soul and the body

✍🏾Peacemakers as Ministers of Reconciliation

(2 Corinthians 5:18–20 )
Active peacemakers establish peace between people and God by first reconciling themselves with God. Through repentance, confession, prayer, and obedience, they become vessels of peace.

After making peace with God, they help others to become steadfast in faith, to repent, and to return to God.

👉🏾The Apostle Paul teaches:

“All things are of God, who has reconciled us to Himself through Jesus Christ, and has given us the ministry of reconciliation; that is, in Christ God was reconciling the world to Himself, not counting their trespasses against them, and has committed to us the word of reconciliation. Therefore, we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God were pleading through us. We implore you on behalf of Christ, be reconciled to God.”(2 Corinthians 5:18–20)

This holy work of reconciliation is also carried out through the intercessions of the saints, by which many souls are drawn back to God.

✍🏾How Peacemakers Establish Peace Among People

Peacemakers first live in peace with others themselves. A person who is not at peace with people cannot bring peace between people.

After establishing peace in their own relationships, peacemakers work to create peace among others in three ways:

👉🏾First, they do not create conflict, spread rumors, or increase misunderstandings and hatred.

👉🏾Second, they actively help to resolve the problems that cause division, anger, and hostility.

👉🏾Finally, they allow love, forgiveness, and compassion to govern relationships between people.

Peace among people can exist at different levels:

• Between individuals
• Between families
• Between communities
• Between nations

A true peacemaker does not divide people but unites them through wisdom, patience, and love.
