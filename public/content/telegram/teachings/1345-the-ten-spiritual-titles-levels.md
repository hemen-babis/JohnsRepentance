This is the first step practiced by our holy fathers.  They speak the word of God, they say good things, they have no place for bad things, it means that their food is the word of God.
 
2 “ልባዌ” ( heart, insight, knowledge)

‘ልባዌ’ (Lbawe)  it means heart, insight, knowledge, opinion of the heart. Saints who have reached this rank prostrate more when they are  tempted by desire and evil thoughts . They don't want their conscience to separate them from thinking about the cross so that they don't want their grace to be lifted up by thinking about physical things. If they are steadfast in their promise, God will quickly give them everything they need. They will also see where the soul separates from the body. When angels ascend and descend, they know their mission and even their names. He gives them the grace to see the demons and to know why they were sent. They understand without asking anything that is under the surface of the earth except some things that God hides. / Dan 9:11-13; 2 Cor 12:1-7; Galatians 6:4/.

It is also a way of knowing what God has done, knowing and noticing what is being said and what is being done. For example, there are the so-called "Five Pillar Mysteries",  and it means to understand and know . To describe in an example, we hear a sound and we hear that sound, but we do not understand the essence of it. For example, he hears a car screeching, but doesn't know what to say. But we call it " Lbawe " to understand the essence, to understand everything that has been done, to pay attention, to know the secret.
 
Third: It is called 'Anbie' (crying), it means to cry, (to shed tears)" It means to cry while thinking of the suffering of Christ, the torture of the martyrs and everything that Christ has done for us. But when they reach this rank, they know it, so it means to cry while thinking of his merits, this is called 'Anbie', it means to cry while thinking of Christ's suffering.

3. ጣዕመ ዝማሬ (Tastful Song).

‘ጣዕመ ዝማሬ’   it means 'Tastful Song'. Fathers know the secret of the prayers they pray when they reach this rank. They never tire of fasting, prayer, worship, spiritual service. Their voices are like angels when they sing. They give thanks day and night without rest. They don't get bored of their own voice as it is like the singing of angels. They do not eat food unless they want to hide their dignity. They don't sleep. For fathers who have reached this rank, the devils will bring a test of conscience to them that they will enter the city instead of where you live disguised as a squirrel in this cave and live there, sanctify, sing, and teach. At this time, they will be given the title of a pure soul if their heart is freed from vain praise.

4. ‘የነፍስ ንጽሕና’ (Purity of Soul)

‘የነፍስ ንጽሕና’ it means Purity of Soul (Centralists) The title of a pure soul is the time when the body is forgotten, and the soul is free of sins. God blesses the person who lives steadfastly in the time of pure soul. He sees the light and mystery of the Supreme, which the people of this world cannot see with the physical body. But he will be subjected to a special test. Leaving a legacy, despairing, doubting that I should give up and leave, he will not forgive me, he will not save me. At this time, because of the number of fasts, without wavering from his steadfastness, if he perseveres in religion, saying that God will save him, his glory (grace) will not separate him. Those who are in the title of pure soul are likened to the seed that produced 60 (sixty) fruits. The virtues of a pure soul are tear of repentance, condemnation, love, and humility.

'አንብዕ' (Tear of repentance) it means lots of tears for repetance. 'Saints who reach this rank are given the wealth of tears. They recite without ceasing (tears of repentance); But their eyes are not lost; Their tears do not stop.
