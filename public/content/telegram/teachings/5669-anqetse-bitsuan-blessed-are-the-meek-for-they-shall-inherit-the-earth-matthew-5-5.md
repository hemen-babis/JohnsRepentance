👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾(#Anqetse #bitsuan) #Blessed are the #meek, for they shall #inherit the #earth" (Matthew 5:5 )

( Part 12)

Beloved in Christ,Let us reflect today on the teaching of our Lord Jesus Christ, who said: “Blessed are the meek, for they shall inherit the earth.” (Matthew 5:5)

In these words, our Savior calls us to a life of humility, gentleness, and trust in God. He is Himself the perfect example of meekness, and through His life, suffering, and victory, He shows us how to walk in meekness and inherit both the blessings of this world and the eternal Kingdom of Heaven.

✍🏾
Our Lord Jesus Christ: The Perfect Example of Meekness

The perfect example of meekness in the New Testament is our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, the crown of all the saints. He said in His Gospel:

“Learn from Me, for I am gentle and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls.”
(Matthew 11:29)

Long before His coming, the Prophet Zechariah had already foretold this, saying:

“Say to the daughter of Zion, ‘Behold, your King is coming to you, meek and riding on a donkey, on a colt, the foal of a donkey.’”
(Zechariah 9:9; Matthew 21:4)

Christ, therefore, is the perfect and complete example of meekness and gentleness.

✍🏾Testimony of Saint Peter

Saint Peter bore witness to the meekness of Christ, saying:

“For to this you were called, because Christ also suffered for you, leaving you an example, that you should follow in His steps.
He committed no sin, nor was deceit found in His mouth.
When He was reviled, He did not revile in return; when He suffered, He did not threaten, but committed Himself to Him who judges righteously.
He Himself bore our sins in His body on the tree, that we, having died to sins, might live for righteousness.”(1 Peter 2:21–24)

Through His meekness, Christ endured suffering without complaint and revealed the true nature of divine love and humility.

✍🏾Testimony of Saint Paul

Saint Paul likewise testified, saying:

“I, Paul, entreat you by the meekness and gentleness of Christ.”
(2 Corinthians 10:1)

Thus, the apostles confirm that Christ Himself is the model of perfect meekness the One who humbled Himself for our salvation, that we might follow in His steps and find peace in His example.

✍🏾They Shall Inherit the Earth

The early Church Fathers explained the Beatitude, “Blessed are the meek, for they shall inherit the earth” (Matthew 5:5), in four ways. The first is the new earth.

1. The New Earth – The Kingdom of Heaven

Meekness is true wisdom the wisdom by which the righteous inherit the Kingdom of Heaven. God promises the meek this “earth,” meaning the eternal Kingdom.

King David, called a man after God’s own heart, said:

“But the meek shall inherit the earth, and shall delight themselves in the abundance of peace.”
(Psalm 37:11)

This refers to the Kingdom of Heaven. Those who choose God’s kingdom above all else on earth will faithfully receive it.

David longed for this heavenly land with steadfast faith, saying:

“I would have despaired unless I had believed that I would see the goodness of the Lord in the land of the living.”(Psalm 27:13)

Similarly, Saint Peter reminds us of this promise:

“But according to His promise, we look for new heavens and a new earth, in which righteousness dwells.”(2 Peter 3:13)

Saint John, in his divine vision, also revealed this new earth:

“Then I saw a new heaven and a new earth, for the first heaven and the first earth had passed away; and there was no more sea.”
(Revelation 21:1)

Thus, the “earth” that the meek shall inherit is the new, eternal earth, which will be inhabited after the present world passes away.
