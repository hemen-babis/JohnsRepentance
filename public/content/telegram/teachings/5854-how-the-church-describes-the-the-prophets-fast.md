Dear followers of the Orthodox teachings on John's Repentance website .we greet you with the peace of the Lord. Today, based on the topic mentioned above, we present to you the following important lesson. We encourage everyone to read it carefully and learn from it.

✍🏾The Second Sunday – “Light” 

The second Sunday of the preaching season is called “Light.”

On this day, the Church remembers how the holy prophets preached that the Light of our souls would be born, and that the true Light would come into the world. This Light is our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, who is the Light of faith and the Light of life.

Before the coming of Christ, humanity lived in spiritual suffering. Both body and soul were separated from God, who is the true Light. Therefore, this week is a time to remember how the prophets proclaimed in advance that the Light of our souls would be born and that the true Light would shine upon the world.

During this week, the Church also remembers the fourteen generations from the birth of King David to the Babylonian exile, teaching how God prepared the way for the coming of Christ through history.

✍🏾 The Prayer of the Prophets

One of the ways the holy prophets awaited, guarded, and revealed God’s salvation was through their longing for Light.

King David prayed:

“Send out Your light and Your righteousness; let them lead me.”(Psalm 43:3)

Through this prayer, he was not only asking for guidance, but also crying out for the coming of the Son of God. The meaning of this prayer is: “Send us Your Son, who is Light, righteousness, and truth, so that He may lead us into the Kingdom of Heaven.”

The Prophet Isaiah also proclaimed this hope of salvation (Isaiah 49).

✍🏾The True Light

As Saint John the Evangelist testified:

 “The true Light, which gives light to everyone, was coming into the world.”(John 1:9)

This Sabbath is dedicated to preaching the Light of Jesus Christ, who alone is the True Light that enlightens every human soul.

Our Lord Himself proclaimed:

 “I am the Light of the world. Whoever follows Me will not walk in darkness, but will have the Light of life.”
(John 8:12)

On this holy Sabbath, the Church preaches about His divine Light and teaches believers to make Christ the Light of their lives, that they may walk in truth and inherit the Kingdom of Heaven.

✍🏾The Light of Our Lives 

On this day, the Church proclaims Jesus Christ as “Light from Light” and “the ineffable Light,” the true and eternal Light.

In the hymn of Saint Yared, we sing:

“The Light has come into the world.”

Following the teaching of our holy fathers and the instruction of Saint Ephrem in the Monday Praise of the Virgin Mary, the Church proclaims:

 “The true Light that shines upon all who live in this world, all creation rejoices in Your coming for the love of humanity. You saved Adam from his error, You freed Eve from the curse of death, You gave us the Spirit by whom we are born anew, and with the angels we praise You.”

Therefore, we give thanks to our God and Savior Jesus Christ, who is the Light of our lives and the salvation of our souls.
