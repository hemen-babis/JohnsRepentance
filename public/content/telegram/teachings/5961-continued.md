5. Christianity and the Church were founded in the East.

The first Church was founded in Jerusalem, and the Gospel was preached from there. Therefore, when we turn to the East, we remember the journey of the Church, the spread of the Gospel, and the salvation of the people and the world.

6. The East is a manifestation of the glory of God.

God glorified the God of Israel in the East, and His name in the islands of the sea (Isaiah 24:15).

The glory of the God of Israel came from the East (Ezekiel 43:2).

👉🏾 The Second Coming of the Lord will also be from the East (Zechariah 14:4).

👉🏾The East is the dwelling place of God.

👉🏾 To ask whether the West is the dwelling place of the devil.
