The causes of fornication and the eves of adultery are mostly kissing between a woman and a man outside of legal marriage, passionately touching each other, sleeping together, watching or reading pornographic movies and the Internet, talking too much about this matter, giving conscience and focus to related activities. This is when the flesh leads him to the thought of adultery, he keeps playing and practicing the idea secretly in his mind, and when it gets out of control, he falls into the original sin of adultery.

 👉👉👉Question # 4: What should we do to get out of the danger of adultery?

This is a wake-up call that contains the voice of God, supported by biblical evidence, about the dangers of all forms of adultery and the damage they can cause to our social life and our souls. This text should wake us up from the deep adultery sin that we have fallen into and get away to never go back to it again. Basically, many of us say we don't know how to get out when the exit door is closed to us because it is our inner desire and we think that it will bring us happiness, even temporarily. We give it more importance than food and think that we will not live if we leave it. But if we are determined with a purpose and control our emotions and do not do this adultery, we will not die or get injured. If a person is determined and perseveres for his purpose on this earth, he decides to honor his people and country and gives his chest and forehead to die on the battlefield, let alone fight his fleeting physical feelings that only last for a few minutes. We don't have to fight or die in the battle against adultery. The secret is that the spirit of fornication is a battle of the evil spirit that fights us abstractly with our intangible and invisible inner thoughts. So, how can we fail to fight and win with the guidance of our fathers and the spiritual weapons given to us by God? Christians, don't give Satan a place!  Although the development of our spiritual life, the type of our sins, and the amount of our sins are different from each of us, the main thing is that we strive to improve our spiritual life step by step without stopping to carry out the solutions listed below and God will help us.
