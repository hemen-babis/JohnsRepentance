Beloved brothers and sisters in Christ,

The hour has come for us to gather in fellowship and prayer. As the Psalmist proclaims, "Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity!" (Psalm 133:1, LXX).  This session will be blessed by the guidance of Dn. Kidus, who will lead us in our reflections. Let us therefore hasten to join our meeting, that we may strengthen one another in faith and love under his direction. May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all.

Come, let us enter His presence with thanksgiving!
