Concluding Reflection

The Transfiguration is not merely a historical event; it is a profound theological statement about Christ's nature and a promise of the glory we too can partake in. As we reflect upon this divine mystery, let us aspire to transform our lives, to radiate the love and light of Christ, and to ascend our own spiritual mountains to meet God.

May the grace of the Transfigured Christ enlighten our paths and lead us to the eternal glory of His kingdom. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
