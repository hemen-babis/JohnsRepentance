(Continued) ☝️

In summary, the Orthodox perspective on marriage and Christian living is deeply rooted in scripture, tradition, and a profound understanding of human nature. It emphasizes the importance of a Christ-centered marriage, mutual respect, spiritual growth, and a life of love and humility. These teachings, if embraced and practiced, can lead to a fulfilling and resilient marriage that not only withstands the trials of this world but also progresses towards eternal salvation.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
