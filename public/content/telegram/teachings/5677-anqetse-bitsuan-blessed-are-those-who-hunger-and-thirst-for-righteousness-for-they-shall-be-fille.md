👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾#Anqetse #Bitsuan (“Blessed are those who #hunger and #thirst for #righteousness, for they shall be filled.”)
#Matthew 5:6; #Luke 6:21

(Part 14)

Beloved in Christ, just as our bodies long for food and water, our souls long for God, His Word, and His salvation. We invite you to read, reflect, and learn how to hunger and thirst for righteousness, 

Hungering and Thirsting for Righteousness

Our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, in the fourth Beatitude, teaches us that “Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they shall be filled.”

✍🏾 Righteousness is truth and holiness the life we live with God, who Himself is Truth, and with all His creation. It is to live on earth according to the will of God, and then to live eternally with Him and His saints in the Kingdom of Heaven.

✍🏾 In the spiritual life, to hunger and thirst for righteousness means to long for and to obtain righteousness to “feed” upon it continually through:

👉🏾Fasting

👉🏾 Prayer

👉🏾 Worship

👉🏾 Almsgiving

👉🏾 Service

This does not only mean obeying God’s commandments outwardly, but also striving with all our heart for the inner life of holiness. True righteousness is not the mere fulfillment of the law, but the longing to be united with the God of righteousness Himself.

✍🏾 When we speak of struggling for righteousness, we mean:

👉🏾To earnestly desire righteousness within our hearts,

👉🏾To pray fervently with our lips to obtain it, and

👉🏾To labor throughout our lives in performing deeds that are good and pleasing to God.

✍🏾 As Saint John Afework taught, our Lord described “those who hunger and thirst for righteousness” as those whose desire arises from within. Therefore, true hunger for righteousness is not something we display to impress others or imitate human appearance it is a deep, spiritual yearning that flows from the heart and the conscience.

When our Lord used both words hunger and thirst He showed that righteousness is not a casual wish, but an urgent and essential need for the soul.

Just as the body cannot live without food and water, the soul cannot live without righteousness. Hunger and thirst drive us to seek what sustains life; likewise, hunger and thirst for righteousness drive the faithful to seek holiness with all their strength, until they are filled with the grace and peace of God.

Hunger and Thirst for Righteousness

✍🏾 Just as physical hunger and thirst compel us to seek food and water with all our strength, so spiritual hunger and thirst compel us to seek righteousness with all our heart and mind. The Lord teaches us that just as our physical life cannot continue without food and water, our spiritual life cannot be sustained without righteous deeds.

✍🏾When we feel spiritual hunger, we must long for righteousness as we long for bread; when we feel spiritual thirst, we must seek righteousness as we seek water. Only then can our souls be nourished, refreshed, and strengthened in the grace of God.

To “hunger and thirst for righteousness” means to desire and strive for:

👉🏾 God Himself,( the source of all righteousness )

👉🏾 His Holy Word, (which enlightens and strengthens the soul)

👉🏾 His Holy Flesh and Precious Blood, (which give life and salvation)

👉🏾 Dwelling continually in His holy house and rejoicing in the goodness found in Him;

👉🏾 Loving and following His divine law and way;

👉🏾 And ultimately, inheriting the kingdom of heaven prepared for those who live in righteousness.

In this way, the faithful grow in spiritual life, being filled not with earthly food, but with the true nourishment that comes from God alone.
