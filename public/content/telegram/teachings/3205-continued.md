(Continued) ☝️

Let us, therefore, embrace the grace offered during this holy fast, engaging in prayer, fasting, and the sacraments, so that we might be drawn closer to the holiness of Mary and through her, to Jesus Christ our Savior. In doing so, we reaffirm our commitment to the Christian life, striving to embody the virtues of Mary, who by her assumption, has shown us the promise of our own resurrection and glorification in Christ.

May the intercession of Our Lady the Holy Virgin Mary bring us closer to the realization of our heavenly inheritance, as we partake of this sacred tradition handed down through the generations by our forebears in faith. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
