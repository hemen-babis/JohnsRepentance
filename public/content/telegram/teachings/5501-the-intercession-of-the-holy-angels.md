👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾The #Intercession of the Holy #Angels

(Part 10)

Beloved in Christ,Let us open our hearts to understand the divine mystery of the intercession of the holy angels, as revealed by our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. In His parables, He not only taught about repentance and mercy but also unveiled the hidden ministry of His heavenly servants, who intercede on behalf of humankind with love and compassion.

The Creation and Ministry of the Holy Angels

The holy angels were created by God on the first Sunday of creation. From the beginning, they have continually glorified Him, crying out, “Holy, holy, holy, Lord God of Hosts.”

These pure angels are sent by God to protect humankind. They guard us from the power of evil and stand as defenders of those who love God. Through the grace of intercession which God has given them, they reconcile us to Him when we humbly pray, calling upon their help in faith.

The Intercession of the Holy Angels in the Scriptures

The Holy Bible gives us clear examples of angelic intercession times when the angels pleaded before God for His mercy and protection over His people.

The Angel Who Pleaded for Jerusalem (Zechariah 1:12)

When Jerusalem and the cities of Judah were captured and the people suffered under their enemies, the angel of the Lord interceded before God, saying:

“O Lord of hosts, how long will You not have mercy on Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, against which You have been angry these seventy years?”

God accepted this angelic plea and responded with mercy:

“I have returned to Jerusalem with compassion.” (Zechariah 1:16)

Here we see that the angels not only serve God but also plead for mankind. They present our prayers before Him, asking for forgiveness and deliverance from affliction.

The Protection of the Faithful (Psalm 91:11–12)
The Prophet David bears witness to this divine ministry, saying:

“For He shall give His angels charge over you, to keep you in all your ways. They shall bear you up in their hands, lest you dash your foot against a stone.”

This shows that God has appointed His angels to guard His faithful ones in every path of life. In danger, temptation, or sorrow, the angels are present by divine command to defend those who love and obey the Lord.

The Intercession of the Holy Angels in the Book of Daniel

One of the clearest parts of the Holy Bible that reveals the intercession and protection of the holy angels is found in the Book of Daniel.

In his prophecy about the end of times, the Prophet Daniel speaks of the Archangel Michael, the great prince and guardian of God’s people. Daniel writes:

“At that time Michael, the great prince who stands watch over the children of your people, shall arise.” (Daniel 12:1)

Through this prophecy, we understand that the Archangel Michael (Mika’el) stands as a mighty intercessor and protector for those who believe in God. In times of tribulation and spiritual warfare, he pleads before God on behalf of the faithful and shields them from stumbling in their faith.

The Teaching of Our Lord on the Intercession of the Holy Angels

Our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ revealed to us the mystery of the holy angels’ intercession through His parable of the barren fig tree, saying:

“A man had a fig tree planted in his vineyard, and he came seeking fruit on it and found none. And he said to the vinedresser, ‘Behold, for three years I have come seeking fruit on this fig tree and found none. Cut it down; why should it use up the ground?’
But the vinedresser answered, ‘Lord, let it alone this year also, until I dig around it and fertilize it. If it bears fruit, well; but if not, then you can cut it down.’”
(Luke 13:6–9)
