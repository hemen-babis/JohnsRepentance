5. Jesus is the judge

(continued part)👆👇

Therefore, the apostle St. John wrote to us what the Lord had revealed to him in a vision: "Behold, I am coming quickly and my reward is with me to repay each one according to his work." (Revelation 22:12) When St. Paul said that at the end of his earthly life, he would receive the price of his service from the hands of Christ, "There is a crown prepared for me in the future, and the Lord, who is the righteous judge, will hand it over to me on that day; and he also said that it is not only for me, but for all who love His revelation." (2 Tim 4:8) It is judgment to give the righteous the price of their righteousness and the sinner the price of their iniquity. Therefore, it means that Jesus is the judge, and if he is the judge, he is God, and if he is God, then he is the creator and God.
