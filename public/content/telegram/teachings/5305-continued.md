5. The Cross is our open confession

✍🏾When we make the sign of the cross ( moving our hand from the forehead to the chest, and from the left shoulder to the right shoulder.), we show that we belong to the cross. By carrying the cross on our necks, designing it in our jewelry, and placing it high in our places of faith, we declare openly that we are the people of Jesus Christ, who was crucified for us. We are not ashamed of the cross before others; instead, we are proud of it and hold firmly to it. Even without words,The sign of the cross becomes a witness of our faith to all who see us.

6. Worshiping God with Spirit, Mind, and Body

✍🏾Man is a spirit, mind, and physical being. The cross is understood not only by thought but also through our senses seeing and touching it helps us remember Christ’s crucifixion.

✍🏾We worship God with our spirit, mind, and body. Every time when we sign ourselves with the Cross and say In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit", we express our belief in the Holy Trinity. Making the sign of the cross unites our body, mind, and spirit in devotion, reminding us of Christ’s sacrifice and victory.

Conclusion

✍🏾Behold then, beloved, the glory of the Cross. Angels proclaim it, apostles preach it, prophets foretell it, martyrs embrace it, and the Church raises it high as her boast. Through it we are reconciled, by it we are sealed, in it we are glorified.

Let us therefore venerate the Cross, not as mere wood, but as the throne of the Lamb, the ladder of Jacob, the key to Paradise, and the banner of victory.
