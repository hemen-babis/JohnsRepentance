👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾(#Anqetse #bitsuan) #Blessed are the #meek, for they shall #inherit the #earth"
(Matthew 5:5 )

( Part 10)

Dear followers of Orthodox teachings . we greet you in the name of God. Today, we share an important lesson on the blessedness of the meek, and we encourage you to read, reflect, and learn from it.

✍🏾The Meek Put Others First

Out of sincere love, the meek place others before themselves. They are not a burden to anyone, nor do they cause others to stumble, but rather they carry one another’s burdens with patience and compassion. As Saint Paul teaches,

“Bear one another’s burdens, and so fulfill the law of Christ.” (Galatians 6:2)

The meek honor and respect everyone, as it is written,

“Be kindly affectionate to one another with brotherly love; in honor preferring one another.” (Romans 12:10)

Saint Paul also says,

“We who are strong ought to bear the weaknesses of the weak, and not to please ourselves. Let each of us please his neighbor for his good, leading to edification.” (Romans 15:1–2)

Thus, the meek seek the good and happiness of others more than their own. In humility, they follow the command:

“Do nothing out of selfish ambition or vain conceit, but in humility count others better than yourselves. Let each of you look not only to his own interests, but also to the interests of others.” (Philippians 2:3–4)

Therefore, the meek do not act for their own advantage but strive for the benefit and salvation of their neighbors, fulfilling the perfect law of love taught by Christ.

✍🏾The Meek Do Not Seek Earthly Glory

The meek do not seek glory for themselves, but desire that God may be glorified in all they do. They are joyful when others are honored rather than themselves.
As our Lord taught in His Gospel:

“Everyone who exalts himself will be humbled, and he who humbles himself will be exalted.” (Matthew 23:12)

The meek guard their hearts against vain praise because they know that their true glory is stored up in heaven.
King Solomon also warns,

“It is not good to eat much honey; nor is it glorious to seek one’s own honor.” (Proverbs 25:27)

Our Lord, who is the perfect model of meekness, said:

“I do not accept glory from men.” (John 5:41)

“I do not seek My own glory; there is One who seeks it and judges.” (John 8:50)

“He who speaks on his own seeks his own glory; but he who seeks the glory of the One who sent him is true, and in him there is no unrighteousness.” (John 7:18)
“If I honor Myself, My honor is nothing; it is My Father who honors Me.” (John 8:54)

Thus, the meek glorify God in all things and refuse the empty praise of the world.
