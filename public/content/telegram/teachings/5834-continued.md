3. Synagogue

The third week of the Zemene Astemhro is called “Synagogue.” It includes the seven days from Hidar 20 to 26.

Hymns of the Day (Sunday)

👉🏾 Amlak Ftsum behlawehu ...: “God is perfect in His existence, before the world was created…; He who existed before all creation is perfect in His being.”

👉🏾 Misbak:Kirebu Habehu Weyaberh Lekmu “Draw near to Him and He will shine upon you; your face will never be ashamed. This poor man cried out, and God heard him.” (Ps. 33:5-6)

Readings

👉🏾 Gospel: Matthew 8:28 to the end
👉🏾 Liturgy: Kidase Egzi-e
Teaching of the Week

✍🏾 This week, it is taught that our Lord, God, and Savior Jesus Christ gathered the people in the Jewish synagogue and instructed them; that He rebuked the sea and the winds; and that He cast out demons from those who were possessed.

Synagogue in the Great Fast

👉🏾 Hymn: “Boa Eyesus, Mkurabomu Le-ayhud.”

👉🏾 Sermon Verse(Misbak): Esme kinate betke... “For the zeal of Your house has consumed me; the reproaches of those who reproached You fell on me. I humbled my soul with fasting.” (Ps. 68:9-10)

👉🏾 Gospel: John 2:12 to the end
👉🏾 Liturgy: Kidase Egzi-e

✍🏾 This week presents the lesson of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ teaching in the synagogue and driving out those who were selling worldly goods in the temple.
