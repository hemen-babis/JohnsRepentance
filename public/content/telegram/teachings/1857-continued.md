(Continued) 👆🏾

Let us, therefore, beseech his holy intercessions, that we too may be granted the strength to renounce the world, the courage to embrace the ascetic struggle, and the grace to attain the heavenly kingdom. May his life inspire us to seek the things above, to live in accordance with the Gospel, and to become partakers of the divine nature.

As we commemorate his holy memory, let us exclaim with joy, “O holy Abune Aregawi, pray to God for us, that He may save our souls!”

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.
