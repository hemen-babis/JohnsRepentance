Continued ☝️ 

Let us follow her example of faith, humility, and obedience, and let us always seek her intercession, for she is not only the Mother of God but the mother of all who believe in Christ. May her prayers protect us, may her example inspire us, and may her Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, grant us the grace to walk in the path of righteousness all the days of our lives.

To Him be the glory, honor, and worship, with the Father and the Holy Spirit, now and ever, and unto ages of ages. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
