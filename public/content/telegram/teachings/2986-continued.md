(Continued) ☝️

The Holy Eucharist stands as a profound testament to God’s infinite mercy and love. It is a sacrament that encapsulates the entire Christian mystery, offering not only forgiveness and salvation but also a continual renewal of life through Christ. As we partake of the Holy Eucharist, may we always be mindful of its sanctity and the immense grace it bestows upon us, aiding us on our journey towards eternal communion with God.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
