Let us draw inspiration from the Blessed Virgin’s unwavering faith, from Joseph’s steadfast obedience, and from the miraculous interventions of Christ, even as a child, who guided and protected His earthly parents. And may we, like Titos, the repentant thief, seize the moments of grace that God gives us, repenting of our sins and placing our trust in the crucified and risen Lord.

May this sacred time renew our hearts, strengthen our faith, and deepen our love for Christ, who in His infancy fled persecution, and in His divinity reigns forever as the King of kings and Lord of lords. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every language that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
