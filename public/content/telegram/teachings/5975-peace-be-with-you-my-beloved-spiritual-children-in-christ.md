Peace be with you, my beloved spiritual children in Christ.

As we stand at the threshold of a New Year (2026), all hearts of the admin team is filled with profound prayers and fervent blessings for each and every one of you, my cherished members on this spiritual journey.

May the boundless and uncreated grace of our Lord, God, and Savior Jesus Christ illuminate your path in the days and months to come. Let us embrace this turning of the year not merely as a change on the calendar, but as a divine call for spiritual renewal, deeper repentance, and unwavering dedication to our Holy Orthodox Faith.

May the Lord grant you:
   Peace in your homes and within your souls.
   Health and strength in your bodies, to serve Him diligently.
   Joy that surpasses worldly understanding, rooted in His divine love.
   Wisdom to discern His will in all your decisions.
   And above all, spiritual growth that draws you ever closer to Him, the source of all life and light.

Let us cast aside the burdens, shortcomings, and anxieties of the past year, offering them to our merciful God, and step into this new beginning with renewed zeal for good works, fervent prayer, and steadfast faith.

Through the abundant intercessions of His All-Holy Mother, our Lady the Ever-Virgin Mary, the Holy Archangels Michael and Gabriel, Saint George, and all the Saints who intercede for us before the Throne of Grace, may God protect you, guide you, and keep you safe from all evil.

A truly blessed and spiritually fruitful New Year to you all! Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
