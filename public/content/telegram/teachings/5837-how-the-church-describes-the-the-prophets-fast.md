4. Metsagu

The fourth week of the Fast, covering the days from Hidar 27 to Tahsas 7, is called Metsagu, meaning “a sick or bedridden person.”

✍🏾Why the Day Is Called Metsagu

It commemorates the day when Our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ opened the eyes of the man who was born blind, revealing His divine power and mercy.

👉🏾Hymn of the Day (Sunday)

The hymn composed by Saint Yared begins with:
“Yebelu Israel…”(“The people of Israel said, ‘We have neither seen nor heard of the man who was born blind and whose eyes were opened on the Sabbath)
It recounts the disbelief of the Israelites who said they had never seen or heard of a miracle such as the healing of the man born blind.

👉🏾Psalm of the Day

Psalm 4:2–3
"O sons of men, how long will you remain in darkness? Why do you love vanity, and why do you seek falsehood? Know that God is revealed in His righteousness.”
This psalm teaches us to recognize and believe in the wondrous works God performs for His faithful ones.

👉🏾Gospel Reading

John 9:1to the end.
The Gospel narrates the healing of the young man who was born blind. Through this miracle, Christ reveals His divine authority to heal both physical afflictions and the sicknesses of the soul. The Liturgy of the day is the Mass of God.

✍🏾Spiritual Meaning of the Week

During this week, we reflect on both the healing of the body through Christ’s miracles and the healing of the soul through His teachings.

Many people today suffer not only from physical illnesses but from spiritual blindness that keeps them from seeing the light of righteousness. These diseases include:

👉🏾Gossip

👉🏾Heresy

👉🏾Hatred

👉🏾Violence

👉🏾Theft

👉🏾Lying

👉🏾Adultery

👉🏾Immorality of all forms

👉🏾Corruption, even among those entrusted with sacred duties

These sins are deeper wounds than any bodily disease.

✍🏾The True Cure

Physical illnesses may be healed by holy water or medicine. But the sickness of sin has only one remedy: repentance.
Every person afflicted by these spiritual diseases is called to return to our Lord Jesus Christ, the eternal Healer of both body and soul.

5. Debre Zeyt

The fifth and final week of the Fast is known as Debre Zeyt, covering Tahsas 4 to 6.

Hymn of the Day (Sunday)

The hymn for this week is:
“Ser'a Senbete le-seb’e…”(“God created the Sabbath as a day of rest for all creatures in heaven and on earth.”)It praises God who established the Sabbath for all creatures in heaven and on earth.

👉🏾Psalm of the Day

Psalm 131:15–16
"I will bless her widows, I will satisfy her poor with bread, I will clothe her priests with peace, and her righteous ones will rejoice greatly."
This psalm proclaims God’s care for His Church and His people.

👉🏾Gospel Reading

Luke 12:32–41
Christ teaches that since no one knows the hour of their death or the passing of the world, every Christian must live in constant spiritual readiness and Instead of gathering perishable wealth, we must increase righteous deeds that prepare the soul for the Kingdom of God.
The Liturgy of the week is the Liturgy of Saint Athanasius.
