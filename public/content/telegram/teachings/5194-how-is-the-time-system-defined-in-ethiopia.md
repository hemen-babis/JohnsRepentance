👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾How is the #Time System Defined in #Ethiopia?

(Part 6)

Beloved brethren, hear with reverence the wisdom of the fathers, for the times and seasons of the Church are not arranged by men according to their own will, but are preserved by divine tradition. The calendar of Ethiopia is not a mere reckoning of days, but a sacred order by which the mysteries of salvation, the fasts, and the feasts of our Lord and His saints are revealed to us. To neglect this wisdom is to walk in darkness, but to understand it is to walk in the light of God’s appointed times.

1. The Order of the Fasts

The Church has received from the fathers seven fasts. Four of these change their dates each year according to the calculation of Mebaja Hamer, while three are fixed and never change.

The four fasts that vary are:

1. The Great Fast (Abiy tsom),

2. The Fast of the Resurrection,

3. The Fast of Nineveh(Tsome Nenewey),

4. The Fast of the Apostles.

The three fasts that are fixed each year are:

1. The Fast of the Prophets (Christmas Fast),

2. The Fast of ("Gehad")or( "tsome Gehad"),

3. The Fast of the Virgin Mary.

Each of these is bound to its own mystery of salvation.

The Fast of the Prophets begins on the 15th of "Hidar", as a preparation for the Feast of the Nativity of Christ.

The Fast of ("Gehad")or( "tsome Gehad"), is observed as a substitute fast. It always falls on the eve of the Feast of the Lord’s Baptism. If Baptism is on Friday, then Thursday is kept as the fast; if on Saturday, then Friday is kept. The fathers have decreed that this fast must always precede the feast, even if the day itself would not ordinarily be a fast.

The Fast of the Assumption of the Virgin Mary is the remembrance of the apostolic fast, when the holy apostles fasted two weeks in longing to behold the resurrection of the Theotokos. This fast begins on Nehase 1st, and two weeks later the Dormition of our Lady is celebrated.


2. The Mystery of "Tewsak"

"Tewsak" is a special number, or code, assigned to a particular fast or holy day. By using this number, the dates of holidays and festivals can be determined. The count of Tewsak begins on Saturday.

Saturday = 8

Monday = 6

Tuesday = 5

Wednesday = 4

Thursday = 3

Friday = 2

By these numbers, the fasts and feasts are aligned with the mysteries of salvation.

3. The Mystery of Mebaja Hamer

At the center of this calculation lies Mebaja Hamer, which is defined as:

Mebaja Hamer = (መጥቅዕ)Metike' + the day of tewsak which also beale metike is performed.

Metike'(መጥቅዕ)= Last Year Metike'+ Tinte Metike' (ጥንተ መጥቅዕ).

Here, Tinte Metike' is always the fixed number 19.

The fathers handed down this rule:

If Metike' is greater than 14, it falls in Meskerem.

If it is less than 14, it falls in Tikimt.
This law never changes.

4. Worked Example: The Year 2018 (E.C.)

To understand how this system works, let us consider the year 2018 (E.C.).

First, the “class”(መደብ) (calculation) is found 

{5500} + 2018) ÷ 19

= 7518 ÷ 19 = 395

 { remainder } =13

Thus the class is 13.

The Seat(ወንበር) = Class – 1 = 12.

From this, the Day is calculated:

Metike'= seat ÷ 30

12 × 19 = 228

 228 ÷ 30 = 7 

{ remainder } 18

So Metike' = 18.

Now, Mebaja Hamer is found by adding this number to the Metik E' of the corresponding day of the Tewsak.

Since the 18th falls on a Sunday, and the Tewsak of Sunday is 7:

Mebaja Hamer = 18 + 7 = 25

Thus, the Mebaja Hamer of 2018 is 25, and from this number the fasts and feasts of the year are determined.

The main function of the Mebaja Hamer is to determine the dates of feasts and festivals in conjunction with the Tewsak. Therefore, it is necessary to know the Tewsak assigned to each feast and festival. By using the Mebaja Hamer together with the Tewsak of the festivals, the exact dates of the celebrations can be calculated.
