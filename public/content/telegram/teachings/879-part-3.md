The nine major festivals are Jesus Christ's conception, birth, baptism, Hosanna, crucifixion, resurrection, and ascension. It is Jesus Christ who will come again, he is the one we said appeared in Debretabor, our time is with him, there is a book  about Jesus Christ that is repeated (read) after Mass.
 
It is God who is praised every day, every day at Mahlet, when the hours stop. The Orthodox Tewahedo Church gives its full time to Jesus Christ. Day and night, always, we do not have Christianity without Christ. When we say, "In the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit," it is Christ, Torah Leviticus, Chapter 19, Verse 2. As I am holy, so be ye holy." It is Jesus Christ who is sanctified.
 
When we give time to the saints, we are also giving time to Jesus Christ. The festivals of Jesus Christ are celebrated every year. There is no Christianity without Jesus Christ. May God help us to understand this and be saved.
