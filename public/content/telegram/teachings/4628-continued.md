IV. Lover of the Theotokos, the One Who Magnified Her Praise

But what shall we say of his devotion to Our Lady, the Virgin Mother of God? This love was not fleshly, but burning with the fire of the Spirit. While others debated, he adored. While others were content with doctrine, he burst into hymn.

He praised Her not once, nor seven times, but sixty-four times each day. He drew his inspiration from Saint Luke’s Gospel and from her own lips, the Magnificat. He wished her name to be praised like the stars that twinkle without ceasing, like the sands that no man can number, like food that nourishes, and like water that quenches.

Because he poured his heart into her praise, the Theotokos herself appeared to him. Not in silence, not in shadow, but openly and gloriously, clothed in light. She greeted him, saying:

“Peace be upon you, beloved son of Ephraim.”

The ground became a carpet of light, the air a robe of glory. She commanded, “Praise me,” and he, filled with the Holy Spirit, obeyed. He lifted his hands; the angels joined him; the martyrs sang with him.

What mystery is this, O faithful! That a man who praises the Virgin with sincerity will be visited by her in person!

She granted him fourteen thousand hymns and exhortations, and from these the Church still drinks. Most of these were taken by the angels for their own thanksgiving. Others were given to Saint Yared, the golden voice of Ethiopia, who received the seven-day hymns of Our Lady, sung in Axum Zion with Saint Ephraim and Abba Hiriakos, under the light of her presence.

She blessed them with the Cross of Light, and whispered to them her eternal hope. These were not dreams, but mystical realities gifts to those who burn with holy love.

V. His Blessed End and Everlasting Reward

After seventy years of pleasing God, Saint Ephraim departed this life in peace on Sene 15/ 370 A.D in a holy monastery in Edessa. But he did not die as men die. He was greeted at the veil of eternity by Christ Himself, by the Virgin Theotokos, and by the assembly of saints and martyrs.

He who praised in time now praises eternally. He who sang from the dust now sings from glory. He who was filled with the Spirit now dwells in the bosom of the Holy Trinity.

Conclusion: A Call to Follow the Harp of the Spirit

Let us not merely admire, but imitate. Let us not be content with applause, but seek communion. For what Saint Ephraim has done, the Church is called to do. Let our lips be like his praising Our Lady, defending the truth, and loving the Word with fire.

O beloved of God, pray today:

“Saint Ephraim, intercede for us! You who saw the Mother of Light, obtain for us purity of heart. You who received the hymns of heaven, teach us how to pray. You who walked with saints and angels, help us walk with God. Amen.”

By the intercessions of Saint Ephraim the Syrian, Abba Hiriakos, Saint Yared, and Abba Giorgis Zegascha, may the Most Holy Virgin grant us wisdom and understanding unto eternal life.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
