Continued 👆🏽

 A Final Exhortation

To those who are married, and to those preparing for marriage, know this: your attitude is the soil in which your love will either flourish or wither. If you cultivate an attitude of gratitude, humility, and faith, your marriage will be a source of unshakable joy. If you allow pride, selfishness, and resentment to take root, no amount of external effort will save it.

Therefore, examine your hearts, shape your perspectives through prayer, and build your marriage upon the firm foundation of Christ. In doing so, you will not only experience peace in your own home but will reflect the love of God to the world.

Glory to God, the Ever-Born Virgin, and the Glorious Cross!

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
