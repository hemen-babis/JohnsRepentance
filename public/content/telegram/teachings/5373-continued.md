Conclusion

✍🏾Beloved, let us revere holy icons with the spirit of piety, understanding that the glory lies not in the material, but in the holiness of the one depicted and in the grace of God that works through them. Let us avoid idolatry, recognizing the danger of images that exalt falsehood. And let us use worldly images wisely.
