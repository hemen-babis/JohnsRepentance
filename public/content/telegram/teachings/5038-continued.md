V. The Praise of the Virgin Across the Ages

With the saints of old and the faithful of today, we glorify her. She is the pure perfume that fills the house of God without dust, the dwelling prepared by the Holy Spirit from her mother’s womb, free from all sin.

Saint Ephraim likens her to the burning bush that Moses saw, aflame yet unconsumed (Ex. 3:2–3)  a type of the Virgin who bore the fire of the Godhead without being destroyed.

VI. Greater than the Cherubim and the Seraphim

Abba Giorgis teaches that she is more exalted than the highest angels: they bore the Lord upon fiery chariots, but she bore Him in her womb, in her arms, upon her knees her very limbs became chariots of fire.

Through her, knowledge came to those in darkness, faith to the unbelieving, life to the dead.

VII. The Eternal Word in Time

"He who was before all ages became man without ceasing to be God. The Word was united with flesh, the timeless One entered time, the invisible was seen, the impassible suffered. Jesus Christ, one essence with the Father and the Holy Spirit, born of the Virgin in purity let us worship Him and seek the intercession of our holy Lady, that His grace and glory be upon us forever."

VIII. The Prophecy of the Closed Gate

Ezekiel saw her in prophecy as the eastern gate that remains shut (Ezek. 44:1–2). No man entered by it; only the Lord God of Israel came and went, leaving it still sealed. This is the mystery of her perpetual virginity inviolate before, during, and after giving birth.

IX. The Triumph Over Sin

She bore the One who came not by angel or mediator, but by Himself, to save us with His own blood. At the fullness of time, He came to destroy the thorns of sin, granting us salvation.

X. The Supremacy of Her Glory

Her glory surpasses all the saints: she received the Word into her womb, feared by the angels, praised by the saints, worshiped by all creation. Her Son, one with the Father and the Spirit, is the source of her honor.

On this Wednesday of glory, the Church joins the angels in unending praise:

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
