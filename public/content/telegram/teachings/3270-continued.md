(Continued)☝️

In conclusion, brethren, let us heed the exhortation of the Scriptures and the wisdom of our Fathers. We must recognize and utilize the graces bestowed upon us, not as entitlements to squander but as tools for our sanctification. In doing so, we shall escape the snares of sin and walk steadfastly toward our heavenly inheritance. Let us, therefore, choose life, that we and our progeny may live in the light of Christ. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue, today and always.
