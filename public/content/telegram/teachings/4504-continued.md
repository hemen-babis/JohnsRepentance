8. Spiritual Fruits and Our Call

O children of repentance, hear this: Moses’s life reveals that:

1. No one is beyond grace. From idol worship and violence, salvation springs eternal.

2. Humility is the ladder of ascent. Moses fell prostrate before monks, not arrogantly ruling but serving.

3. Prayer and obedience weld the spirit. By tears, fasting, solitude and labor, true transformation was wrought.

4. Martyrdom crowns the path of repentance. The robber’s sword surrendered, replaced by the sword of Christ.

Let Us Pray and Imitate

O Lord, through the intercession of Thy Ethiopian saint Moses, grant us hearts aflame with repentance. Let us serve without witness, pray without ceasing, and await our hour with courage may our sins fall behind us, as we strive for the crown set before us.

May the voice of Abune Moses thunder in our souls: "My sins have fled behind me; I judge none who stand today." On this holy day, let us embrace his spirit turning from idols to service from darkness to light.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
