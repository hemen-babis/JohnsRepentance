Continued ☝🏽 

Prayers and Blessings: Offered in the name of the Trinity.

Baptism: Administered “in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit” (Matthew 28:19).

Sacraments and Rituals: Rooted in the unity and diversity of the Triune God.
A Call to Commemorate the Trinity
The Feast of the Holy Trinity serves as a reminder to witness and partake in the divine mystery. By celebrating this festival, we are invited to:

Reaffirm Our Faith: Recognize the power and mercy of the Triune God.
Seek Unity in Diversity: Reflect the harmony of the Trinity in our relationships and communities.
Live in Holiness: Remain steadfast in repentance and faithful to the teachings of Christ.

Let us therefore celebrate the Feast of the Holy Trinity with reverence and awe, glorifying the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, one God, now and forever. Amen

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
