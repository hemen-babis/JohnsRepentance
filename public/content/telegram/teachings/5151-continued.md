Conclusion

Beloved, let us understand clearly: the cloud is a symbol of God’s covenant and His mercy. In the days of Noah, the cloud carried the rainbow, a promise of peace. In the days of Moses, the cloud covered the tabernacle, a sign of God’s protection. In both, the Holy Virgin Mary is revealed as the living cloud our Mother, our refuge, and our intercessor.

Therefore, let us glorify God who gave us such a sign, honor the Virgin who bore the Savior, and rejoice in the Cross by which we are redeemed.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
