3. The Atronach (Throne of Intercession)

Here, the priests lift their hearts in prayer and place the Missal the holy book of the divine service. The Atronach is a symbolic throne of our Lord Jesus Christ and also of the Theotokos, for in them dwells the fullness of grace and divine mediation.

4. Holy Images (Icons) – Windows into the Kingdom of God

Icons, my beloved, are not mere decorations. They are revelations. They depict the lives, martyrdoms, and miracles of the saints, drawing our hearts toward imitation and intercession. And more still icons of our Lord are not simply remembered; they are worshipped, for He is truly present in His image.

Let us then approach these images not with idle gazes but with bowed heads, with tears, with prayers, with trembling. They stand not as art but as presence divine presence manifest through grace.

5. The Unveiled Mass Garments – Robes of Holiness

The garments worn by priests during the sacraments are not common apparel. As the Lord commanded Moses, so did Aaron wear robes of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, adorned with twelve golden tassels. Their sound yes, even their sound healed the people. (Exodus 28:35; 39:1–25)

Likewise today, the priest, when robed in these garments, becomes not a mere man but a vessel of divine operation. Through these garments, the sacred mysteries are revealed; and through their consecrated symbolism, the faith of the people is deepened.

Conclusion: The Incomparable Glory of the Holy Relics

What then shall we say? Shall we treat these relics lightly? Shall we grow numb in our hearts toward the divine tools that minister to our salvation?

God forbid.

These Nwaye Kedsat, these consecrated treasures, stand as witnesses of the eternal covenant. They are the Ark of the Covenant, the chair of divine presence, the book of intercession, the image of sanctity, the robe of grace. They are not of man, but of God.

Let us therefore revere them with the utmost solemnity. Let us teach our children their worth. Let us, like the people of old, journey with joy to the places where they dwell. For in doing so, we approach the Holy One of Israel Himself.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
