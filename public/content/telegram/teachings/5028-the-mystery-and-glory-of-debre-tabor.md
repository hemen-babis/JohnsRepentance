👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾The #Mystery and Glory of #Debre #Tabor

(Part1)

“Beloved brethren in Christ, children of the holy Church, let us turn our gaze to the mystery of Debre Tabor, that radiant height where earth was clothed in heaven, where prophecy was fulfilled, and where the eternal light of divinity burst forth before the eyes of men.”

For what is Debre Tabor? The very name itself reveals its depth. Debre means mountain, and Tabor is the name of the mountain. The prophet David proclaimed with joy: “Tabor and Hermon shall rejoice in Your Name, O Lord” (Ps. 88:12). Thus, this feast is not of man’s imagination, but rooted in prophecy, sealed by divine revelation, and celebrated by the saints of old.

How the Feast is Kept

Our holy Orthodox Tewahedo Church counts this day among the nine great feasts of the Lord. With solemn fasting, with unceasing prayer, with long vigils, with almsgiving, and with the consecration of the holy Qurban, the faithful give honor to Him who revealed His glory on that height. This is not a feast of earthly merriment, but of divine majesty. Here the faithful stand in awe, hearts lifted like incense, beholding with the eyes of faith the vision once granted to the apostles.

The Mysteries Revealed

Why is Debre Tabor numbered among the great feasts? Because here the Lord unveiled the hidden mysteries of His kingdom.

He who raised the dead in the house of Lazarus, He who restored life in the house of Jairus, He who foretold His coming on the Mount of Olives this same Lord revealed His majesty once and for all on Tabor. Yet not all mysteries were alike: some were witnessed by all the disciples, but the mystery of His kingdom was disclosed only to Peter, James, and John, with Andrew.

And not apostles only! Moses the lawgiver and Elijah the prophet stood there also. What greater testimony could be given than that the law and the prophets themselves bore witness to Christ, conversing with Him as friend with friend? Thus was shown the unity of the covenants: the ancient and the new, the prophetic and the apostolic, joined in one voice and one heart.

The Voice of the Father and the Glory of the Son

Here the Father’s voice thundered from the cloud: “This is My beloved Son, whom I have chosen, in whom I delight; listen to Him.” Here the Son shone in the majesty of His divinity, His face brighter than the sun, His garments whiter than snow. Here the Holy Spirit descended in the form of a dove. At Tabor, the unity of the Trinity was made manifest: Father speaking, Son revealed, Spirit descending.

No wonder Peter, enraptured, longed to dwell there forever, desiring to build tabernacles for the Lord and His prophets. For who would not desire to remain where heaven touches earth, where divine light swallows human darkness, where death itself trembles at the glory of the immortal King?

Why Tabor?

Why was Tabor chosen from among all the mountains? Because it is the mountain of victory. Recall how Deborah, the prophetess, summoned Barak to ascend this very height with ten thousand men, and there, though Sisera’s chariots were iron and his army vast, the Lord gave victory to Israel. Upon this mountain, hymns of triumph were sung, as once Miriam sang by the Red Sea.

Thus, as Israel of old conquered Sisera at Tabor, so too Christ on this same mountain displayed His eternal triumph over sin, death, and the devil. Prophecy itself declared: “Tabor and Hermon shall rejoice in Your Name.” And again: “The mountain of the Lord is a green mountain, a strong mountain, chosen as His dwelling” (Ps. 67:15). Therefore, this mountain was appointed as the place where divine light would shine forth for all generations.
