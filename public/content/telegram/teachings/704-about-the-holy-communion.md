About the Holy Communion 

Part  3 (Cont'd)

2.       If those receiving Holy Communion are married, they should abstain from sexual intercourse for 3 days before receiving Holy Communion. Sexual intercourse on permitted days in marriage is not a sin, but since it is an act of the flesh , so this period is also one of the forbidden days in terms of giving respect to the sacrament.
 
3.       Anyone who repents and is freed from his sins should keep his heart away from revenge and his body away from wrongdoing.

4.       In order to receive Holy Communion, one should fast for 18 hours without food and water, and wait until the feeling of food being removed from the stomach.

5.       If one sees inappropriate dreams, they will not receive Holy Communion that day. Also, if there is bleeding on anypart of a body, bleeding wound,  or if vomited, or if flys like a mosquito bite entered our the mouth or nose, it is not possible to receive on that day.

6. On the eve of the day we plan to receive Holy Communion, we should take a bath and clean what we wear.

7. Before starting the mass, it is necessary to attend the church and properly follow /consecrate the mass. We should be present before Liturgy starts, and ideally attend the services before that as well. 

8. A woman should  not receive until 7th days of her menstruation. Then she washes her body and can receives on the 8th day or after then. Also at the time of childbirth she does not receive it until he day of the Christening of the child. "If a woman gives birth, it means 80 days, if she gives birth to a boy, it means 40 days" (Leviticus 12:1-5)

Points we need to know when we present at Church to receive Holy Communion

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
