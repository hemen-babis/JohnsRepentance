4. 4 The Flower in the Time of Harvest

There is no harvest or gathering during the flowering season, because their times are different. For example, in Meskerem, which is the season of flowers, there is no harvest or gathering.

✍🏾 “You are the harvest of prophecy, O Mary, who has brought to fruition the promises of the prophets and removed the time of famine.” Just as in the days of famine the prophecy of Joel the prophet of Elda spoke of blessings mountains flowing with honey and milk the seed of prophecy spoken by the prophets was fulfilled in our Lady. The prophets foretold the coming of the Messiah in various ways, and she became the fulfillment of these divine words.

✍🏾 The prophecies spoken by the Lord’s prophets were fulfilled, and the apostles, chosen to witness this fulfillment, understood:

“One sows and another reaps. I sent you to reap that for which you have not labored. Others have labored, and you have entered into their labors.” (John 4:37)

✍🏾 Thus, the prophets sowed their words, and in the time of the apostles, their labor bore fruit. This fruit of prophecy is Mary. Moreover, it is written:

“A rod shall come forth from the root of Jesse, and the fruit of Christ will appear.”

Therefore, our Lady is like a flower appearing in the harvest season. Since flowers do not usually bloom at this time, she is called “a strange flower; a strange flower.” This is also a fulfillment of the prophecy of Joel.

✍🏾 In Joel’s time, the people suffered a severe famine. Yet the prophet declared that God would visit them with mercy and bring an end to the famine:

“You shall eat in plenty and be satisfied. After this, the mountains shall drip with wine, and the hills shall flow with milk, and all the springs of Judah shall flow with water.” (Joel 3:18)

Through Mary, the seed of prophecy was fulfilled, and the mercy of God flowed into the world as promised.

Conclusion

The Virgin Mary is the sealed garden, the fountain of life, and the strange flower that blooms in the harvest season. In her, the prophets’ words are fulfilled, and the promise of salvation is realized. Through her, heaven was opened, and God came to dwell among men.
