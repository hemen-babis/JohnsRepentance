(Continued)👆🏾

How long will you neglect the commands of your Lord and fail to comprehend the depth of your need for Him? Be aware that any attempt to navigate life's trials alone will inevitably lead to your downfall. Instead, strive to partake of the covenant of the Holy Communion, for unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink His blood, you have no life in you (John 6:53). Before your mortal body returns to the dust and your spirit to God who gave it (Ecclesiastes 12:7), engage deeply with this divine mystery. Remember, you are but a transient guest in this world. Partake of the Holy Communion and ensure your eternal dwelling with God.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
