Part 3

Saints who reach this rank usually pray with conscience; Not through fatigue. And because this is a subtle grace that walks in their conscience, it removes their sins.

Just like if you throw wet wood on the fire, burning water will come out, so the saints who are at the level of tears of repentance will shed tears of grace because of the fire of spiritual grace burning in their hearts while thinking of the death of God and the bitter hellfire. / Matthew 5:4; Luke 6:11; Acts 10:1; Romans 12:11.
 
5. 'ኩነኔ' ( Condemnation)

'ኩነኔ' ( Condemnation) it means rule, dominion. Saints who have reached this rank, their soul's will rules their body's will; He will win. In human life, the will of the body and the soul are always in conflict. /Galatians 5:16-17/. The title of 'condemnation' is where the will of the soul overcomes and takes control of the body, where the soul begins by doing only the will of the Holy Spirit. The will of their flesh has no effect on the will of their soul. They are also protected from abuse. They grieve thinking of the damnation of hell and hellfire. Holy Fathers, a person who reaches this rank will think of the death of the Son of God and will be ready to suffer; They explain. / Romans 7:15-18; 1 Cor 9:17; Galatians 5:14-16; Eph 2:1/.
 
6 ‘ፍቅር’ (Love)
 
‘ፍቅር’ (Love)  it means to love everyone; regardless of whether they are sinners, righteous, believers, heretics, relatives or strangers. This is a big thing we are getting trouble in this world.   We humans love only those who loves us, who did something or favor to us; but the saints do not do that.  Fathers who reach this rank are given the wealth of loving all. And he loves them all. Among these fathers, this one is rich, this one is poor, this one is in authority, this one is male, this one is female, this one is kind, this one is evil, this one is handsome, this one is ugly. These saints love all people as Christ loved the world. They have compassion for everyone. They pray for people, to step from apostasy to religion, from active sin to active righteousness. In general, birds and animals do not attack them. Even rebellious demons obey them. / Psalm 90:13; Matthew 5:33-38; Luke 6:16-20; 10:17; 1 Cor 13:1-13/.

7 ሑሰት
 
ሑሰት (Husset) it means that they know what is happening all over the world at the same time from where they are. Like leg of sun (light of sun) , reaching and seeing wherever they want to travel or see things just by staying where they are. That is, the presence of everything you think of as the sun (sunlight) of the feet; It means being able to see what is happening in one place. This title is the last title for the centralists. Saints who have reached this rank are not hidden from them in everything that happens in the world. You can see them all in one place. For example, you know instantly what is happening all over the world at the same time. For the abstract in this title, the Holy Spirit unites them, so they become abstract. Even though they are huge in body, they can pass through it like the light of a mirror without stopping or hindering them. /2 Kings 6:8-13; Isaiah 30:17-21; Acts 5:1-11; 1 Cor 12:1-5/.
These saints can come and visit the world, angels, heaven, living nations, blessed nations. He gives them a light wheel (chariot) and wings.
