3. Additional Names of the Virgin Mary

The Church venerates her under many glorious titles, reflecting her role in the divine plan of salvation:

Mother of God (Theotokos) – she bore the Word made flesh.

Ever-Virgin – holy before, during, and after the birth of Christ.

Kidane Mihret (Covenant of Mercy) – the faithful intercessor and refuge of sinners.

Redeemer of the World – for through her came the Savior of humanity.

Conclusion

Beloved, the story of Mary—Negere Maryam—is not merely a tale of a holy woman but a revelation of divine wisdom and mercy. Through her, Christ entered the world, the grace of God was restored to mankind, and the path to salvation was opened. Let us honor her, seek her intercession, and follow her obedience and humility, that we too may be made worthy of eternal life through her Son, our Lord Jesus Christ.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
