Continued 👇🏾

When the disciples were unable to cast out a demon, the Lord explained to them the reason for their failure:

“This kind can come out by nothing except by prayer and fasting” (Mark 9:29).
Without fasting and prayer, spiritual victory is impossible.

Therefore, it is important to recognize that those who deny the need for fasting or claim to cast out demons without it are misled. Such teachings are deceitful tricks of the enemy, meant to weaken the faithful and separate them from the spiritual tools given by God.

Fasting, united with prayer, is a true act of worship, a source of spiritual power, and a shield of protection for all who follow Christ
