Continued ☝️ 

As we celebrate this sacred day, we are called to:

- Reflect on the purity and holiness of the Virgin Mary.
- Recognize her pivotal role as the bearer of the Incarnate Word.
- Seek her intercession as the refuge of humanity.
- Celebrate the historical and spiritual legacy of the Ark of Zion in the life of the Church.

Conclusion: A Call to Devotion

Let us join in the blessings of the Holy Virgin Mary, the Ark of Zion, who stands as the intercessor for all who seek her. With fervent prayer, we echo the words of the faithful: “Our Lady, her love, her intercessor, do not separate us!”

This Hedär 21, may our hearts be filled with gratitude and reverence for the Virgin Mary, whose feast reminds us of the unbroken link between the divine and human, the heavenly and earthly, and the eternal covenant of salvation.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
