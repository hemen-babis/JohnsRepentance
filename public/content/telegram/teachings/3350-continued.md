Continued ☝️  

May the Lord bless this New Year, granting us the grace to repent, to bear thirty, sixty, and a hundredfold fruit for His kingdom. And may we, through His mercy, inherit the eternal life promised to those who walk in His ways. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us. Amen.
