👉🏽👉🏽👉🏽The Law and the Prophets Stand By These Two Commandments (Matthew 22:40 )

Part 2

Beloved brethren, seekers of the divine truth, we extend to you the peace and grace of God. In continuation of our sacred teaching, we delve deeper into the profound mystery of the Law and the Prophets as they stand upon the foundation of two great commandments, as spoken by our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. These commandments are the axis upon which the entire revelation of God turns, the pillars that uphold the divine covenant with humanity.

The Love of God

To understand the Law and the Prophets, we must first comprehend what it means to love God. Love, in its highest form, is not a sentiment bound by human frailty but a divine ordinance, a calling that transcends mere affection and demands total surrender.

The love of God manifests in two ways:

1. God’s love for mankind
2. Man’s love for God

God’s Love for Mankind

“For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have eternal life” (John 3:16). Behold, the unparalleled love of the Father! What earthly father, even in the depths of his affection, would lay down his life for his child? Yet God, in His infinite mercy, fashioned man in His own image and likeness, bestowing upon him holiness and purity, intending him to be an heir of divine glory.

Saint Paul, a vessel of divine wisdom, elucidates this eternal decree: “He chose us for Himself before the foundation of the world, that we might be holy and blameless before Him in love, having predestined us for adoption as sons through Jesus Christ, according to the good pleasure of His will” (Ephesians 1:4-5).

God’s love is not reactionary; it is proactive, uncaused by any merit of our own. He loved us even when we were unworthy, and when man transgressed, He did not abandon him to destruction but humbled Himself, shedding His own blood, breaking His own flesh, and offering His life as a ransom for our redemption. Such love cannot be found in any other example.

Man’s Love for God

If the first facet of divine love is God’s unfathomable mercy, the second is man’s response to it. Our duty is made plain in the words of the Lord Himself: “Love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your strength. This is the great and first commandment” (Matthew 22:37).

The love of God is not a mere declaration of faith but a lived reality, evidenced by obedience to His divine will. Thus, the soul that truly loves God will:

Worship no other god – for to love God is to serve Him alone in spirit and truth.

Honor His commandments – for love is not lawlessness but joyful obedience.

Live according to His law – for His law is not burdensome but the pathway to life eternal.

Our Lord affirmed this with a solemn declaration: “If you love Me, keep My commandments” (John 14:15).

The Supreme Law of Love
All that exists in the heavens and on earth was created within the eternal wisdom of the Holy Trinity, and the first law binding upon all creation is love. Christ Himself testified to this, declaring it the greatest of all commandments. This love is not an option but the very essence of our being.

Consider the paradox of our fallen nature:

We love Him who has judged us with true justice and yet set us free.

We love Him who was insulted on our behalf yet has honored us.

We love Him who was persecuted for our sins yet has made us heirs of His kingdom.

O mystery beyond comprehension! O love that cannot be measured! How can we withhold our hearts from such a God? He who has given us life, He who has redeemed us from the abyss, He who has called us sons and daughters should we not then love Him with all our heart, all our soul, and all our strength?
