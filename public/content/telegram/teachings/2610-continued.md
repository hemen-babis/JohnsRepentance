(Continued) ☝️

Let us walk in the steps of Nicodemus, moving from night into day, from query to faith, and from solitude to the sacred community of the church. May our journey through Lent lead us into the bright dawn of Pascha, reborn and renewed in the image of Christ. Amen.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always.

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
