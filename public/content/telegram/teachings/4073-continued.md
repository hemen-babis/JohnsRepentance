Continued👆🏽

The Warning Against Idleness

Many young people, instead of using their talents for the glory of God, are consumed by:

Addictions and drunkenness

Lust and adultery

The fleeting pleasures of the world

Time passes, and salvation is neglected. The Lord calls us to wake up and labor while we still can.

The Final Judgment: The Reward of the Faithful

When Christ, the Son of the Virgin Mary, returns in glory, He will judge every servant according to their works. To those who have been faithful, He will say:

👉 “Enter into the kingdom of my Father with joy.”

But to those who have squandered their talents, He will declare:

👉 “Depart from me, you wicked and slothful servant.”

Let us, therefore, strive to be found among the faithful, multiplying the grace given to us, that we may enter into the joy of our Master.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever in every tongue that He has created, today and always. 

May the blessings of God's mother, the virgin, and the cross be upon us.
Amen.
