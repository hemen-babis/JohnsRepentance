5. How We Should Live in Prayer

Keep the appointed times of prayer faithfully.

Pray with the words of the psalms, hymns, and intercessions.

Be present in the temple, not just alone at home.

Unite your heart to the prayers of the Liturgy daily, speaking with God as His child.

Conclusion

To pray little or not at all is to weaken the soul and expose ourselves to temptation. To pray faithfully at home, in church, and especially in the liturgy is to dwell in the protection of God.

Therefore, let us never grow lazy in prayer. Let us speak daily with our Maker, lifting our voices with His Church, He who prays dwells with God; he who abandons prayer strays into the hands of the evil one.
