Continued 👇🏾

✍🏾God shows mercy to the oppressed, the downtrodden, and the needy

✍🏾God saw the suffering of the Israelites in Egypt and said, “I have surely seen the affliction of my people who are in Egypt, and have heard their cry because of their taskmasters, and I know their sorrows. And I have come down to deliver them out of the hand of the Egyptians, and to bring them up out of that land unto a land flowing with milk and honey, unto a large and good land, unto the place of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites.”

✍🏾King David said, “Because of the affliction of the poor, because of the cry of the needy, the Lord says, Now I will arise. I will save and show them my face.” Psalm 12:5 gives this promise that God comes quickly to the needy.

👉🏾David also said, 

✍🏾“He who judges the oppressed gives food to the hungry. The Lord sets free the prisoners, the Lord raises up the fallen, the Lord makes the blind wise, the Lord loves the righteous, the Lord protects the exiles, he welcomes the orphans and the widows, he destroys the way of the wicked.” Psalm 146:7 to 9 speaks of God’s steady care for the helpless.

✍🏾God brought justice for Naboth. “Thus says the Lord, Have you killed and inherited? In the place where dogs licked up Naboth’s blood, dogs will lick up your blood.” 

✍🏾Haman prepared a cross for Mordecai, hoping to destroy him, but the sentence turned on him. The cross he built for another became his own, while Mordecai received mercy. Esther 7:9 to 10 shows how God defends the innocent.
