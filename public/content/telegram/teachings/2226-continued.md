(Continued) ☝️

As we reflect on this feast, may we be drawn ever closer to the heart of God, embracing the fullness of His revelation in Christ Jesus our Lord, who, in His infinite wisdom and love, has shown us the path to salvation.

May the name of the Holy God be praised forever and ever, today and always.

May the blessings of the Holy Mother of God, the Virgin, and the Cross be upon us. Amen.
